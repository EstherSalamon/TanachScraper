**1**	**these.** these incidents that related the kings’ decrees.

**1**	**the chiefs.** of Israel.

**1**	**saying.** And so they said to me.



**2**	**was first in this treachery.** for they commenced to deal treacherously by intermarrying with the peoples of the lands.



**3**	**I rent my garment.** out of the great pain due to this treachery. bewildered Heb. מְשּׁוֹמֵם miserable and bewildered, like (Ez. 3:15): “… seven days bewildered among them.”



**5**	**from my fast.** for I had not eaten on that day.



**7**	**we were delivered.** Heb. נִתַּנוּ, like נִתַּנְנוּ. The “dagesh” in the “nun” is instead of another “nun,” like (II Chron. 14:10): “for we rely (תִשְּׁעַנוּ) on You,” like נִשְּׁעַנְנוּ. (Gen. 34:16): “… then we will give (וְנָתַנוּ) our daughters,” like וְנָתַנְנוּ.

**7**	**as of this day.** as appears this day, for the Ten Tribes have already been exiled, and many of the exiles still remain in Babylon.



**8**	**And now, for a short moment.** temporarily.

**8**	**favor.** Heb. תְּחִנָה, like חֲנִינָה

**8**	**and to give us a peg.** to establish us in the land of Israel.

**8**	**a peg.** יָתֵד, like (Isa. 22:23): “And I will thrust him like a peg (יָתֵד) in a sure place.”

**8**	**a little life.** [as translated,] a little life.



**9**	**For we are slaves.** to Darius, king of Persia.

**9**	**has not forsaken us.** Heb. לֹא עֲזָבָנוּ, a combination of לֹא עָזַב אוֹתָנוּ.

**9**	**and He has extended.** Heb. וַיַט, an expression of the causative, like מַטֶה. He extended His loving-kindness upon us.

**9**	**a fence.** for they had created a fence to keep His commandments.



**11**	**unclean.** Heb. נִדָה, unclean and repulsive.



**12**	**and you shall cause… to inherit.** You shall give the land to your children as an inheritance to eternity.



**13**	**And after all that has come upon us.** after all the calamity that has befallen us because of our evil deeds.

**13**	**You have punished less.** You have held back from our iniquities until below them [i.e., less than them], for our iniquity was lessened by the expiation effected by our exile. We may also interpret it in this manner: You held Yourself back from collecting from us all our iniquity, and You collected from us below [less than] the iniquities, and You requited us according to all our sins.



**14**	**If we revert.** Heb. הֲנָשּׁוּב, if we return.

**14**	**will You not be wroth with us.** a wonder with an affirmative answer.



**15**	**for we cannot.** lit. for there is nothing; i.e., we do not have the power of good deeds to stand and exist before You, because of this transgression.
