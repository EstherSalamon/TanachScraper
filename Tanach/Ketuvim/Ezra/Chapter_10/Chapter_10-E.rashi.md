**1**	**weeping.** Heb. בּכֶה, like בֶּכִי.



**2**	**and we have taken.** And we took them in.

**2**	**there is hope.** There is hope for Israel concerning this, for they can cast out their foreign wives and their offspring and cast out all the children born from these foreign wives.



**3**	**by the counsel of the Lord.** by the counsel and the will of the Holy One, blessed be He.

**3**	**and those who hasten to [perform] the commandment of our God.** and the God-fearers, they shall cast them out.

**3**	**and according to the Law it shall be done.** he who performs this act, who will cast out his gentile wife and children.



**4**	**and we are with you.** to agree with you.



**7**	**a proclamation.** Heb. קוֹל, lit. a voice, an announcement.



**8**	**by three days.** at the end of three days.



**9**	**the ninth month.** That is the month of Kislev.

**9**	**quaking over the matter.** They were afraid over the matter. They were afraid of the stumbling block of this transgression.

**9**	**and because of the rains.** And because of the rains that were falling upon them there, they were shivering and miserable.



**11**	**confess.** Heb. תּוֹדָה, [which, in this case, means] וִדּוּי, confession.



**13**	**and it is the rainy season.** for the rains are falling upon us.

**13**	**and the work.** And this separation from sin cannot be executed so quickly.



**14**	**with the entire congregation.** Heb. לְכָל הַקָהָל

**14**	**who took in.** Heb. הַהשִּׁיב equivalent to אֲשֶּׁר הוֹשִּׁיב.

**14**	**until we avert.** until the burning anger of the Holy One, blessed be He, returns from upon us.



**15**	**stood up against this.** to argue concerning this matter.

**15**	**supported them.** strengthened their hands.



**16**	**to investigate.** Heb. לְדַרְיוֹשּׁ, like לִדְרוֹשּׁ.



**17**	**And they completed everything.** And they completed the rectification of the matter of the men who had brought in heathen wives, for they cast them out.

**17**	**of the first month.** until the first day of Nissan.



**19**	**And they advised them.** Heb. וַיִתְּנוּ יָדָם, lit. and they gave their hand, their advice, like (II Sam. 14:19): “Behold Joab’s advice (יַד יוֹאָב) is with you in all this (sic).”

**19**	**for their guilt.** This was a temporary ruling.



**20**	**And of the sons of Immer.** All these are priests until (verse 23): “And of the Levites, etc.”



**44**	**and some of them had wives by whom they had children.** And some of them had heathen wives by whom they had children. They cast out all of them, both the wives and the children.
