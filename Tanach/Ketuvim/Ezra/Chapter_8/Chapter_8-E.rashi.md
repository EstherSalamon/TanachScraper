**15**	**to the flowing river.** Heb. הַבָּא אֶל הַנָהָר, lit. to the coming river.

**15**	**to Ahava.** to that river.

**15**	**and we encamped there.** and we were camping there.

**15**	**and I surveyed the people.** I directed my attention to survey the people who had gathered with me.



**16**	**And I sent to Eliezer… the heads.** the great men.

**16**	**the wise men.** Heb. מְבִינִים, lit. understanding. They were wise men.



**17**	**And I commanded them, etc..** I sent them and commanded them to speak to Iddo, who was the greatest and most esteemed in Casiphia.

**17**	**to Iddo.** Heb. עַל אִדוֹ, lit. about Iddo.

**17**	**and I put… into their mouth.** I put into their mouth words to speak as my emissary to Iddo and his brother, who were placed and settled in Casiphia, to bring us servants to serve in the Temple.

**17**	**in Casiphia.** the name of a place in Babylon.

**17**	**Ahiv.** the name of a man.

**17**	**who were settled.** Heb. הַנְתִינִים, like הַנְתוּנִים, those who were settled. Therefore, it is written with a “vav.”

**18**	**a man of intellect.** a wise man.

**18**	**of the sons of Mahli….** All these were Levites.



**20**	**And of the Nethinites.** And of the slaves, the Nethnites that King David had appointed, and the chefs of Israel ascended with them.



**21**	**to fast.** Heb. לְהִתְעַנוֹת to fast.

**21**	**a straight way.** that the Omnipresent lead us in peace.



**22**	**For I was ashamed.** For I was ashamed of the king, and I did not wish to request of him chariots and horsemen in order to guide us.

**22**	**for we had said.** For we had already said to King Darius that the Holy One, blessed be He, benefits those who seek Him and pours out His wrath and His anger upon those who forsake Him.



**23**	**and He accepted our prayer.** for we arrived safely in Jerusalem.



**24**	**And I separated.** And when I was there by the river Ahava, I separated some of the chiefs of the priests and weighed out the silver and the gold for them to deposit into the treasuries of the House of the Lord.



**25**	**which… had offered.** which those living in Babylon had offered.



**26**	**and one hundred silver vessels by talents.** one talent for each vessel.



**27**	**And… sprinkling bowls.** bowls for sprinkling [the blood of the sacrifices].

**27**	**of one thousand darics.** a type of coin. There were one thousand darics in these twenty sprinkling bowls of gold.

**27**	**golden.** of golden copper (or) a type of copper coin.

**27**	**fine.** Heb. טוֹבָה, the feminine form, modifying copper (נְחֹשֶּׁת).

**27**	**two.** two vessels.

**27**	**precious as gold.** Heb. חֲמוּדוֹת כַּזָהָב. He calls the vessels חֲמוּדוֹת in the feminine form.



**28**	**to them.** to these chiefs of the priests.



**29**	**the chambers of the House of the Lord.** in order to deposit them in the chambers of the Temple.



**31**	**on the twelfth of the first month.** on the twelfth of Nissan

**31**	**the hand of our God was upon us.** to cause us to succeed in the way that we traversed.



**34**	**Everything was counted and weighed.** The number of the vessels and their weights was the same when they received them as when they itemized them.



**35**	**Those coming from captivity, the people of the exile.** those who came with Ezra.

**35**	**offered up burnt offerings.** all were burnt offerings, and it was a temporary ruling.



**36**	**And they gave the king’s decrees.** These returnees from exile related King Darius’s mandate to the officers of the king, those who see his face.

**36**	**and the governors.** Heb. וּפַחֲווֹת, like וְפַחוֹת.

**36**	**the land beyond the river.** those on the side of the land of Israel, who are considered as being beyond the river of the inhabitants of Babylon.

**36**	**and they exalted.** They were exalting and assisting.
