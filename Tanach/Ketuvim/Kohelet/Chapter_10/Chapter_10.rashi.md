**1 [Hebrew]**	**זְבוּבֵי מָוֶת יַבְאִישׁ יַבִּיעַ וְגוֹ'.** כְּגוֹן בִּימֵי הַחֹרֶף, שֶׁאֵין בִּזְבוּבִין כֹּחַ, וְהֵם קְרוֹבִים לָמוּת, גַּם אִם בָּא לְתוֹךְ שֶׁמֶן רוֹקֵחַ וּמִתְעָרֵב בִּבְשָׂמִים, הוּא מַבְאִישׁוֹ, וְהוּא מַעֲלֶה קֶצֶף, שֶׁקּוֹרִין אשקו"א בְלַעַ"ז, וְנִרְאֶה בוֹ כְמִין אֲבַעְבּוּעוֹת, וְזֶהוּ מַשְׁמָעוֹ שֶׁל "יַבִּיעַ". הֲרֵי דָבָר קַל שֶׁהִפְסִיד דָּבָר חָשׁוּב. כַּךְ, יָקָר מֵחָכְמָה וּמִכָּבוֹד הַסִּכְלוּת מְעָט, שֶׁהֲרֵי הִכְרִיעַ אֶת כֻּלָּם. הֲרֵי שֶׁהָיָה אָדָם זֶה שָׁקוּל בְּמֶחֱצָה עֲבֵרוֹת וּמֶחֱצָה זְכֻיּוֹת, וּבָא וְעָבַר עֲבֵרָה אַחַת וְהִכְרִיעַתּוּ לְכַף חוֹבָה, נִמְצֵאת סִכְלוּת זֶה שֶׁהוּא דָבָר מֻעָט, יָקָר וְשָׁקוּל וְכָבֵד יוֹתֵר מִכָּל הַחָכְמָה וְהַכָּבוֹד שֶׁהָיָה בוֹ, שֶׁהֲרֵי הִכְרִיעַ אֶת כֻּלָּם. יָקָר לְשׁוֹן כּוֹבֶד. הוּא כָבֵד וְשָׁקוּל יוֹתֵר מִן הַחָכְמָה שֶׁבּוֹ. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה מוֹשְׁלִים יֵצֶר הָרַע לִזְבוּבֵי מָוֶת<br>
**1 [English]**	**Dying flies make putrid and foamy, etc..** e.g., in the winter season, when the flies have no strength, and they are near death: if it falls into the oil of a perfumer and becomes mingled with the perfumes, it causes it to become putrid and it assumes a foam, which is called zecume in Old French, foam, froth, bubbles, and a sort of bubbles (אֲבַעְבּוּעוֹת) appears in it, and this is the meaning of יַבִּיעַ. Hence we have an insignificant thing spoiling an important thing. So does a little folly outweigh wisdom and honor, for it outweighed them all. Let us suppose that this man was equal with half transgressions and half merits, and he came and committed one transgression, which weighed him down to the scale of demerit. It is found that this folly, which is a small thing, is heavy and weighs and is heavier than all the wisdom and the honor that were in him, for behold, it outweighed them all. יָקָר is an expression of weight. It is heavy and weighs more than the wisdom and the honor in him. The Midrash Aggadah (Ber. 61a) states: Scripture compares the evil inclination to dying flies.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**יַבְאִישׁ יַבִּיעַ.** שֵׁם טוֹב, שֶׁהוּא עָרֵב מִשֶּׁמֶן רוֹקֵחַ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**makes putrid and foamy.** a good name, which is more pleasant than the oil of a perfumer.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**לֵב חָכָם לִימִינוֹ.** חָכְמָתוֹ מְזֻמֶּנֶת לְהַטּוֹתוֹ אֶל דֶּרֶךְ הַמְּיֻמֶּנֶת לְטוֹבָתוֹ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**The heart of a wise man is at his right.** His wisdom is ready to turn him to the way that is “righted” for his good.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֵב כְּסִיל לִשְׂמֹאלוֹ.** לְעַקְּשׁוֹ מִן הַדֶּרֶךְ הַמְּיֻמֶּנֶת שֶׁהִיא תִפְאֶרֶת וְנוֹחָה לוֹ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and the heart of a fool is at his left.** to turn him in a crooked way from the right way, which is a glory and a comfort for him.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְאָמַר לַכֹּל סָכָל הוּא.** בַּהֲלִיכָתוֹ וּבְדִבּוּרוֹ, הַכֹּל מַכִּירִין שֶׁהוּא שׁוֹטֶה:<br>
**3 [English]**	**and he says to all that he is a fool.** with his walk and with his speech, everyone recognizes that he is a fool.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**אִם רוּחַ הַמּוֹשֵׁל.** מוֹשֵׁל הָעוֹלָם תַּעֲלֶה עָלֶיךָ לְדַקְדֵּק אַחֲרֶיךָ בְמִדַּת הַדִּין:<br>
**4 [English]**	**If the spirit of the Ruler.** the Ruler of the World, ascends upon you to scrutinize you with the Divine Standard of Justice.

**4 [Hebrew]**	**מְקוֹמְךָ אַל תַּנַּח.** מִדָּתְךָ הַטּוֹבָה אַל תַּנַּח, לוֹמַר לוֹ: "מַה יוֹעִיל צִדְקָתִי לִי"?<br>
**4 [English]**	**do not leave your place.** Do not leave your virtue to say to Him, “What will my righteousness avail me?”

**4 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי מַרְפֵּא.** דִּקְדּוּקֵי הַדִּין בְּיִסּוּרִין הַבָּאִין עָלֶיךָ, מַרְפֵּא הוּא לַעֲוֹנוֹתֶיךָ, וְיַנִּיחַ לְךָ חֲטָאִים הַגְּדוֹלִים:<br>
**4 [English]**	**for a cure.** The stringencies of the judgment with the afflictions that come upon you are a cure for your iniquities, and will assuage great sins for you.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**כִּשְׁגָגָה שֶׁיֹּצָא מִלִּפְנֵי הַשַּׁלִּיט.** דּוֹמֶה הוּא לְשַׁלִּיט שֶׁהוֹצִיא דְבַר שְׁגָגָה מִפִּיו שׁוֹגֵג, וְאִי אֶפְשָׁר לַחֲזוֹר, כַּךְ אוֹמֵר הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא, וְאֵין לְהָשִׁיב:<br>
**5 [English]**	**like an error that goes forth from before the ruler.** It is like a ruler who let an error out of his mouth unintentionally, and it cannot be retracted. So does the Holy One, blessed be He, speak, and it cannot be retracted.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**נִתַּן הַסֶּכֶל בַּמְּרוֹמִים רַבִּים.** זוּ הִיא הָרָעָה שֶׁהִיא כִּשְׁגָגָה שֶׁיּוֹצָא מִלִּפְנֵי הַשַּׁלִּיט, שֶׁנִּתַּן הַשְּׁטוּת וְהָרֶשַׁע בִּמְרוֹמֵי גֹבַהּ, שֶׁהִגְבִּיהַּ הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא, אֶת הַכְּסִילִים וְאֶת הָרְשָׁעִים, שֶׁאֲנִי רוֹאֶה בְרוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ שֶׁהֵם עֲתִידִים לִפְשֹׁט יָד בְּהֵיכָלוֹ וְלָשׂוּם אוֹתוֹתָם אוֹתוֹת:<br>
**6 [English]**	**Folly was set at great heights.** This is the evil that is like an error which goes forth from before the ruler: that folly and wickedness are set at the loftiest heights, for the Holy One, blessed be He, raised the fools and the wicked, for I see with the holy spirit, that they are destined to stretch out a hand upon His Temple and to make their signs for signs." (Psalms 74:4; see also Gittin 56b.)

**6 [Hebrew]**	**וַעֲשִׁירִים בַּשֵּׁפֶל יֵשֵׁבוּ.** יִשְׂרָאֵל, שֶׁכָּל הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַכָּבוֹד שֶׁיֵּשׁ לָהֶם עַכְשָׁיו בְּיָמַי, עֲתִידִים לֵישֵׁב בַּשֵּׁפֶל, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, "יֵשְׁבוּ לָאָרֶץ יִדְּמוּ":<br>
**6 [English]**	**and the rich sit in a low place.** Israel, for despite all the greatness and honor that they have now in my days, they are destined to sit in a low place, as it is said (Lam. 2:10): “sit on the ground in silence.”



**7 [Hebrew]**	**רָאִיתִי עֲבָדִים עַל סוּסִים.** הֵם כַּשְׂדִּים, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בָּהֶם, "זֶה הָעָם לֹא הָיָה". יִתְעַלּוּ לִהְיוֹת עַל סוּסִים, מוֹלִיכִים אֶת שְׁבוּיֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲסוּרִים בְּקוֹלָרִין:<br>
**7 [English]**	**I saw slaves on horses.** These are the Chaldeans, about whom it is said (Isa. 23:13): “this people has never been.” [They] will be elevated to be on horses, leading the captives of Israel, bound with neck irons.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**הֹלְכִים כַּעֲבָדִים עַל הָאָרֶץ.** לִפְנֵי קְרוֹנִין שֶׁל כַּשְׂדִּים:<br>
**7 [English]**	**and princes walking like slaves on the ground.** before the chariots of the Chaldeans.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**חֹפֵר גּוּמָּץ.** שׁוּחָה:<br>
**8 [English]**	**.**

**8 [Hebrew]**	**בּוֹ יִפּוֹל.** פְּעָמִים שֶׁהוּא נוֹפֵל בּוֹ. כְּלוֹמַר, יֵשׁ לְךָ חוֹרֵשׁ רָעָה וְסוֹפוֹ לָשׁוּב עָלָיו בְּסוֹף, שֶׁכָּלָה זַרְעוֹ שֶׁל נְבוּכַדְנֶאצַּר עַל יְדֵי כְלֵי בֵית הַמִּקְדָּשׁ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, "וְעַל מָרֵא שְׁמַיָּא הִתְרוֹמַמְתָּ":<br>
**8 [English]**	**fall therein.** Sometimes he falls into it, i.e., sometimes you have someone plotting evil and it ultimately returns upon him in the end, for Nebuchadnezzar’s seed was destroyed through the Temple vessels, as it is said (Dan. 5:23): “but over the Lord of Heaven you exalted yourself,” [and the vessels of His House they brought before you, and you, your dignitaries, your queen, and your concubines drank wine in them, etc.].

**8 [Hebrew]**	**וּפֹרֵץ גָּדֵר.** סְיָג שֶׁל חֲכָמִים, לַעֲבֹר עַל דִּבְרֵיהֶם:<br>
**8 [English]**	**and one who breaks a fence.** the fence of the Sages, to transgress their words.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**יִשְּׁכֶנּוּ נָחָשׁ.** מִיתָה בִידֵי שָׁמָיִם, וּלְפִי שֶׁדִּבֵּר בִּלְשׁוֹן פְּרִיצַת גָּדֵר, הִזְכִּיר בְּתַשְׁלוּמִין לְשׁוֹן נְשִׁיכַת נָחָשׁ, שֶׁהוּא דָר בְּחוֹרֵי נִקְבֵי כָתְלֵי בָתִּים פְּרוּצִים:<br>
**8 [English]**	**a snake shall bite him.** death by the hands of Heaven, and since he spoke with an expression of breaking a fence, he mentions his recompense with an expression of the biting of a snake, which occupies the holes of the broken walls of houses.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**מַסִיעַ אֲבָנִים יֵעָצֵב בָּהֶם.** מַסִּיעַ אֲבָנִים מִמַּחְצַבְתָּם בֶּהָרִים מִתְיַגֵּעַ בָּהֶם יֵעָצֵב לְשׁוֹן יְגִיעָה, כְּמוֹ, "בְּעִצָּבוֹן תֹּאכֲלֶנָּה". כְּלוֹמַר, כָּל אָדָם לְפִי מְלַאכְתּוֹ עִצְּבוֹנוֹ, אַף עוֹשֵׂה רָעָה, לְפִי זְרִיעָתוֹ יִקְצוֹר:<br>
**9 [English]**	**One who quarries stones shall be wearied by them.** One who takes stones from their quarry in the mountains is fatigued by them. יִעָצֵב is an expression of weariness, like (Gen. 3:17): “with toil (בְּעִצָבוֹן) shall you eat it,” i.e., every man, according to his work is his weariness. So will he who commits evil reap according to what he sows.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**יִסָּכֶן בָּם.** יִתְחַמֵּם בָּהֶם, כְּמוֹ "וּתְהִי לוֹ סֹכֶנֶת". אַף הָעוֹסֵק בַּתּוֹרָה וּבְמִצְוֹת, סוֹפוֹ לֵהָנוֹת מֵהֶם:<br>
**9 [English]**	**shall be warmed by it.** יִסָכֶן בָּם, shall be warmed by it, like (I Kings 1:2): “and she shall be to him a warmer (סֹכֶנֶת).” So will one who engages in the Torah and in the precepts ultimately benefit from them.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**אִם קֵהָה הַבַּרְזֶל.** חַרְבוֹת צוּרִים, שֶׁקֵּהָה פִּיהֶם וְחִדּוּדָם:<br>
**10 [English]**	**If the iron is dull.** sharp swords, whose edges have become dull.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְהוּא לֹא פָנִים קִלְקַל.** וְאֵינָם לְטוּשִׁים וּמְרוּטִים: "לְמַעַן הֱיֵה לָה בָּרָק." אַף עַל פִּי כֵן "וַחֲיָלִים יְגַבֵּר". מַגְבִּיר הוּא בְמִלְחָמָה אֶת גִּבּוֹרֵי הַחֲיָלִים לְנַצֵּחַ:<br>
**10 [English]**	**and he did not sharpen the edge.** And they are not sharpened and burnished (Ezek. 21:15): “in order that they may glitter;” nevertheless, it still strengthens the armies. It strengthens the mighty men of the armies to be victorious in battle.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְיִתְרוֹן הַכְשֵׁיר חָכְמָה.** וּמַעֲלַת כִּשְׁרוֹן יֵשׁ עוֹד לַחָכְמָה יוֹתֵר מִן הַבַּרְזֶל. אִם תַּלְמִיד חָכָם מַשְׁחִיר פָּנָיו בְּרָעָב, וְאַתָּה רוֹאֵהוּ מִסְכֵּן בֵּין הָעֲשִׁירִים, הַרְבֵּה חֲיָלִים מִתְגַּבְּרִים עַל יָדוֹ. וְאַל תִּתְמַהּ עַל וָי"ו "וַחֲיָלִים", כִּי הַרְבֵּה וָוִי"ן נוֹפְלִים כֵּן בְּלָשׁוֹן עִבְרִית, כְּמוֹ "אִם רָאִיתָ גַנָּב וַתִּרֶץ עִמּוֹ": "עָזִּי וְזִמְרָת יָהּ וַיְהִי לִי לִישׁוּעָה", וְהַרְבֵּה מְפֹרָשִׁים כָּזֶה:<br>
**10 [English]**	**but wisdom has a greater advantage.** But wisdom has another advantage over iron, namely, that if a Torah scholar blackens his face with hunger, and you see him as a poor man among the rich, many armies are strengthened by him. Do not wonder about the “vav” of וַחֲיָלִים, for many “vavin” appear like that in Hebrew, like (Ps. 50:18): “If you saw a thief, you agreed (וַתִּרֶץ) with him;” (Exod. 15:2): “The strength and the cutting of the Lord was (וַיְהִי) to me a salvation,” and many are explained in this manner.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**אִם יִשֹּׁךְ הַנָּחָשׁ.** אֶת אִישׁ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**If the snake bites.** a man.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**בְּלוֹא לָחַשׁ.** מֵחֲמַת שֶׁלֹּא לָחְשׁוֹ הֶחָבֵר שֶׁלֹּא יִשֹּׁךְ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**it is because it was not charmed.** lit. without charming; because the charmer did not charm him so that he should not bite.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵין יִתְרוֹן.** לֶחָבֵר הָרָשָׁע שֶׁהָיָה רָגִיל לְלָחְשׁוֹ, בְּלֹא לָחַשׁ, כַּךְ, אִם בְנֵי עִירְךָ נִכְשָׁלִים בְּאִסּוּרִין מֵחֲמַת שֶׁאֵין חָכָם דּוֹרֵשׁ לָהֶם וּמְלַמְּדָם אֶת חֻקֵּי הַתּוֹרָה, אֵין יִתְרוֹן לוֹ בִשְׁתִיקָתוֹ, וְלֹא יִשְׂתַּכֵר:<br>
**11 [English]**	**and there is no advantage.** to the wicked charmer who was accustomed to charm it, if he does not charm it. So, if the people of your city stumble over prohibitions because the wise man does not preach to them or teach them the statutes of the Torah, he has no advantage with his silence, and he will not profit.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**דִּבְרֵי פִי חָכָם חֵן.** עַל שׁוֹמְעֵיהֶם וְנִשְׁמָעִים לוֹ, וְטוֹב לוֹ שֶׁנּוֹטֵל גְּדֻלָּה עֲלֵיהֶם:<br>
**12 [English]**	**The words of a wise man’s mouth find favor.** in the eyes of their listeners, and they listen to him, and it is good for him that he assumes greatness over them.

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְשִׁפְתוֹת כְּסִיל תְּבַלְּעֶנּוּ.** זֶה הַמֵּסִית אֶת חֲבֵרוֹ מִדֶּרֶךְ טוֹבָה, כְּגוֹן בִּלְעָם, שֶׁפָּרַץ גְּדֵרָן שֶׁל עַכּוּ"ם שֶׁגָּדְרוּ עַצְמָן מִן הָעֲרָיוֹת מִדּוֹר הַמַּבּוּל וְאֵילֵךְ, וְהוּא יָעַץ לָהֶם לִהְיוֹת מַפְקִירִין בְּנוֹתֵיהֶם לִזְנוּת:<br>
**12 [English]**	**but a fool’s lips will destroy him.** This refers to someone who misleads his fellow man from the good way to the bad way; e.g., Balaam, who broke the restrictions that the nations had imposed upon themselves, that they restricted themselves from immorality from the time of the Generation of the Flood and onwards, and he advised them to offer their daughters for prostitution.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**תְּחִלַּת דִּבְרֵי פִיהוּ סִכְלוּת.** כְּשֶׁאָמַר לוֹ הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא: "מִי הָאֲנָשִׁים הָאֵלֶּה עִמָּךְ"? הָיָה לוֹ לְהָשִׁיב "ה', אַתָּה יוֹדֵעַ" וְהוּא אָמַר "בָּלָק בֶּן צִפּוֹר מֶלֶךְ מוֹאָב שָׁלַח אֵלָי". כְּלוֹמַר: אִם בְּעֵינֶיךָ אֲנִי נִבְזֶה, חָשׁוּב אֲנִי בְעֵינֵי מַלְכֵי הָאָרֶץ:<br>
**13 [English]**	**The beginning of the words of his mouth is folly.** When the Holy One, blessed be He, said to him, (Num. 22:9): “Who are these men with you?” he should have replied, “O Lord, You know,” but he said, “Balak ben Zippor the king of Moab sent them to me,” meaning that if I am despised in Your eyes, I am esteemed in the eyes of the Earthly kings.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַחֲרִית פִּיהוּ הוֹלֵלוּת.** שִׁעְמוּם וְעִרְבּוּב: "לְכָה אִיעָצְךָ" זִמַּת אִוֶּלֶת:<br>
**13 [English]**	**and the end of his speech is… madness.** Heb. הוֹלֵלוּת, madness and confusion, “Come I will counsel you with foolish lewdness.”



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַסָּכָל יַרְבֶּה דְּבָרִים.** שֶׁהָיָה מִתְפָּאֵר בְּעַצְמוֹ שֶׁהָיָה יוֹדֵעַ דַּעַת עֶלְיוֹן:<br>
**14 [English]**	**And the fool increases words.** for he was boasting that he (ibid. 24:16): “knows the thoughts of the Most High.”

**14 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא יֵדַע הָאָדָם מַה שֶׁיִּהְיֶה.** לוֹ לְאַחַר זְמַן. שֶׁהֲרֵי הָלַךְ לִיטֹּל שְׂכָרוֹ בְמִדְיָן, וְלֹא יָדַע שֶׁיִּפּוֹל בֶּחָרֶב.<br>
**14 [English]**	**a man does not know what will be.** to him after a while, for he went to take his pay in Midian, and he did not know that he would fall by the sword. According to its simple meaning:

**14 [Hebrew]**	**הַסָּכָל יַרְבֶּה דְבָרִים.** גּוֹזֵר וְאוֹמֵר: "מָחָר אֶעֱשֶׂה כֵן לִפְלוֹנִי", וְאֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ מַה יִּהְיֶה מָחָר:<br>
**14 [English]**	**the fool increases words.** He declares and says, “Tomorrow I will do this to so-and- so,” but he does not know what will be tomorrow.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**וַאֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה מֵאַחֲרָיו, מִי יַגִּיד לוֹ.** כְּלוֹמַר: לֹא סוֹף דָּבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ מַה יִּהְיֶה לְאַחַר זְמַן, אֶלָּא אַף הַהֹוֶה עַכְשָׁיו מֵאַחֲרָיו, שֶׁלֹּא כְנֶגֶד עֵינָיו אֶלָּא מֵאֲחוֹרֵי עָרְפּוֹ מֵרָחוֹק, צָרִיךְ הוּא שֶׁיְּהֵא מִי יַגִּיד לוֹ:<br>
**14 [English]**	**and what will be behind him, who will tell him.** i.e., not only does he not know what will be after a time, but even what is present behind him, which is not in front of his eyes, but behind his back, from afar, he needs someone to tell him [what is happening].



**15 [Hebrew]**	**עֲמַל הַכְּסִילִים תְּיַגְּעֶנּוּ.** כְּסִילוּתָם גּוֹרֵם לָהֶם עָמָל הַמְיַגְּעָן, אֲשֶׁר לֹא לָמְדוּ דַרְכֵי מְבוֹאוֹת הָעִיר וּמִתְיַגֵּעַ לִכְנוֹס דֶּרֶךְ פְּתָחִים וּבִצְעֵי הַמַּיִם, וְיָגֵעַ בִּטְבִיעוּת רַגְלָיו בַּבּוּץ. כְּלוֹמַר: עַצְלוּת שֶׁל עוֹזְבֵי הַתּוֹרָה גוֹרֵם לָהֶם יְגִיעַת עָמָל בְּגֵיהִנֹּם:<br>
**15 [English]**	**The toil of the fools wearies him.** Their folly causes them toil, which wearies them, for they did not learn the ways of the approaches to the city, and he wearies himself by entering by way of pits and marshes, and he is wearied by his feet sinking into the mire; i.e., the laziness of those who abandon the Torah causes them wearying toil in Gehinnom.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא יָדַע לָלֶכֶת.** אֶל נְתִיב אֱמֶת לִפְרוֹשׁ מִן הָעֲבֵרָה, לְפִי שֶׁלֹּא לָמַד תּוֹרָה, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, "נֵר לְרַגְלִי דְבָרֶיךָ":<br>
**15 [English]**	**for he does not know to go to the city.** to the path of truth, to part from transgression, because he did not learn Torah, as it is said (Ps. 119:105): “Your words are a lamp for my feet.”



**16 [Hebrew]**	**אִי לָךְ אֶרֶץ שֶׁמַּלְכֵּךְ.** בִּזְמַן שֶׁמַּלְכֵּךְ וְדַּיָּנַיִךְ מִתְנַהֲגִים בְּנַעֲרוּת:<br>
**16 [English]**	**Woe to you, O land whose king.** when your king and your judges behave childishly.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**בִּגְבוּרָה וְלֹא בַשְּׁתִי.** שֶׁעוֹסְקִין בִּגְבוּרַת הַחָכְמָה וְהַבִּינָה, וְלֹא בִשְׁתִיַּת יָיִן:<br>
**17 [English]**	**in might and not in drinking.** They engage in the might of wisdom and understanding and not in drinking wine.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**בַּעֲצַלְתַּיִם יִמַּךְ הַמְּקָרֶה.** כְּשֶׁאָדָם מִתְעַצֵּל וְאֵינוֹ מְתַקֵּן הַפְּרִיצָה קְטַנָּה שֶׁבְּתִקְרַת הַבָּיִת:<br>
**18 [English]**	**Through laziness the rafter sinks.** when a person is lazy and does not repair a small breach in the ceiling of the house.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**יִמַּךְ הַמְּקָרֶה.** יִשְׁפַּל הַבִּנְיָן הַמְּקָרֶה אֶת הַבַּיִת וְהַמְּסַכֵּךְ עָלָיו:<br>
**18 [English]**	**the rafter sinks.** The structure that covers the house and protects it will sink.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**יִדְלֹף.** יְטַפְטֵף דֶּלֶךְ גְּשָׁמִים, כְּלוֹמַר: כְּשֶׁיִּשְׂרָאֵל מִתְעַצְּלִים בַּתּוֹרָה, הֵם נְמַקִּים, וּבֵית גְּאוֹן עֻזָּם חָרֵב וּמָךְ:<br>
**18 [English]**	**leaks.** The dripping rain drips, i.e., when the Israelites are lazy with the Torah, they melt, (i.e., they are defeated) and the House of the pride of their strength (viz. the Temple) becomes destroyed and impoverished.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**לִשְׂחוֹק עֹשִׂים לֶחֶם.** לְחֶדְוַת מִזְמוּטֵי חֲתָנִים וְכַלּוֹת, עוֹשִׂים סְעוּדָה, וּסְתָם סְעוּדָה גְדוֹלָה קְרוּיָה לֶחֶם, כְּמוֹ דְאַתְּ אָמַר: "בֵּלְשַׁצַּר מַלְכָּא עֲבַד לֶחֶם רַב":<br>
**19 [English]**	**On joyous occasions, a feast is made.** For the joy of the musical entertainment at a wedding, we make a grand feast, and an undefined grand feast is called לֶחֶם, as it is stated (Dan. 5:1): “King Belshazzar made a great feast (לְחֶם).”

**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְיַיִן.** מַשְׁקִין בִּסְעוּדָה, אֲשֶׁר יְשַׂמַּח הַחַיִּים:<br>
**19 [English]**	**and wine.** they give to drink at the feast, which gladdens the living.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַכֶּסֶף יַעֲנֶה אֶת הַכֹּל.** אִם אֵין כֶּסֶף, אֵין סְעוּדָה. לְפִיכָךְ, לֹא יִתְעַצֵּל אָדָם מִן הַמְלָאכָה, כְּדֵי שֶׁיְּהֵא לוֹ מַה לְהוֹצִיא:<br>
**19 [English]**	**and money answers everything.** If there is no money, there is no feast; therefore, a person should not neglect work in order that he should have what to spend.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**גַּם בְּמַדָּעֲךָ מֶלֶךְ.** אֲפִילּוּ בְמַחְשְׁבוֹתֶיךָ, בְּלֹא דִבּוּר. "גַּם" אֲפִילוּ. מֶלֶךְ אַל תְּקַלֵּל אַל תַּרְגִּיז לְמַלְכּוֹ שֶׁל עוֹלָם. דָּבָר אַחֵר: כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ, מֶלֶךְ בָּשָׂר וָדָם:<br>
**20 [English]**	**Even in your thought… a king.** even in your thoughts, without speech. גַּם means “even.” You shall not curse a king; do not provoke the King of the World. Another interpretation is that this is a king, according to its apparent meaning, a mortal king.

**20 [Hebrew]**	**עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם.** נְשָׁמָה הַנְּתוּנָה בְךָ, שֶׁסּוֹפָה לָעוּף עַל הַשָּׁמָיִם:<br>
**20 [English]**	**the bird of the heaven.** the soul, which is placed within you, which will ultimately fly up to the heaven.

**20 [Hebrew]**	**וּבַעַל כְּנָפַיִם.** מַלְאָךְ הַמְלַוֶּה אוֹתְךָ, כְּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: "כִּי מַלְאָכָיו יְצַוֶּה לָךְ", וּלְפִי מַשְׁמָעוֹ, הָעוֹבְרִים וְהַשָּׁבִים. יֵשׁ לְךָ לִדְאֹג מִכָּל בִּרְיָה, שֶׁמָּא יֵשׁ שׁוֹמְעִין וְיַגִּידוּ לַאֲחֵרִים:<br>
**20 [English]**	**and the winged creature.** the angel who escorts you, as it is stated: (Ps. 91:11): “For He will command His angels on your behalf,” and according to its apparent meaning, the passersby. You should worry about every person, perhaps there are people listening, and they will tell the matter to others.
