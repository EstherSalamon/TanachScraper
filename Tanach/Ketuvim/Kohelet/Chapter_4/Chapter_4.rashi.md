**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְשַׁבְתִּי אֲנִי וָאֶרְאֶה.** בְּרוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**But I returned and saw.** with the holy spirit.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־כָּל־הָעֲשֻׁקִים.** הַנַּעֲשִׂים עֲשׁוּקִים בְּגֵיהִנֹּם בַּמַּעֲשִׂים אֲשֶׁר נַעֲשִׂים:<br>
**1 [English]**	**all the oppressed.** who were made to be oppressed in Gehinnom for the deeds that are done.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**תַּחַת הַשָּׁמֶשׁ.** תַּחַת חֲלִיפִים שֶׁל הַתּוֹרָה:<br>
**1 [English]**	**under the sun.** instead of the exchange for Torah.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִנֵּה דִּמְעַת הָעֲשֻׁקִים.** בּוֹכִים עַל נַפְשׁוֹתֵיהֶם הָעֲשׁוּקוֹת בְּיַד מַלְאֲכֵי מַשְׁחִית וְאַכְזָרִים, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר, "עֹבְרֵי בְּעֵמֶק הַבָּכָא מַעְיָן יְשִׁיתוּהוּ"; אֵלּוּ יוֹרְדֵי גֵיהִנֹּם. וְאַף מִקְרָא זֶה כַּךְ נִדְרָשׁ בְּסִפְרֵי:<br>
**1 [English]**	**and behold, the tears of the oppressed.** weeping for their souls, which are oppressed in the hand of the destructive and cruel angels, and so it says (Ps. 84:7): “Transgressors in the valley of weeping make it into a fountain.” These are the ones who descend to Gehinnom (Mid. Ps. 84:3). And this verse too is expounded in this manner in Sifrei (Deut. 11:26).

**1 [Hebrew]**	**וּמִיַּד עשְׁקֵיהֶם כֹּחַ.** עוֹשְׁקֵיהֶם מַכְרִיחִים וְתוֹקְפִים אוֹתָם בְּכֹחַ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**and from the hand of their oppressors there is power.** Their oppressors overpower them and attack them with force.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**שֶׁכְּבָר מֵתוּ.** עַד שֶׁלֹּא שָׁלַט בָּהֶם יֵצֶר הָרַע הַזֶּה לִדְחוֹתָם מִן הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא, כְּגוֹן אָבוֹת הָרִאשׁוֹנִים, שֶׁלֹּא נַעֲנָה משֶׁה אֶלָּא עַל יְדֵיהֶן, וּכְגוֹן דָּוִד אָבִי, שֶׁלֹּא נַעֲנֵיתִי אֲנִי בְכ"ד רְנָנוֹת עַד שֶׁאָמַרְתִּי, "זָכְרָה לְחַסְדֵי דָּוִד עַבְדֶּךָ":<br>
**2 [English]**	**who have already died.** before the evil inclination overwhelmed them to repel them from the Holy One, blessed be He, like the early Patriarchs, for Moses was not answered except through them, and like my father David, for I was not answered with twenty-four praises until I said, (II Chron. 6:42): “Remember the kind deeds of David Your servant.”



**3 [Hebrew]**	**עֲדֶן.** עֲדַיִן:<br>
**3 [English]**	**yet.** Heb. עֲדֶן, [like] עֲדַיִן.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא רָאָה אֶת־הַמַּעֲשֶׂה.** רָאִיתִי בְמִדְרַשׁ קֹהֶלֶת, אֵלּוּ תתקע"ד דּוֹרוֹת שֶׁקֻּמְּטוּ לְהִבָּרְאוֹת וְלֹא נִבְרְאוּ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**who has not seen.** I saw in Midrash Koheleth (Zuta pp. 117, 125): These are the 974 generations that were decreed to be created, but were not created.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**וְרָאִיתִי אֲנִי אֶת־כָּל־עָמָל.** הֵן הָעֲבֵרוֹת, שֶׁהֵן עָמָל בְּעֵינֵי הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא:<br>
**4 [English]**	**And I saw all the toil.** These are the sins, which are toil in the eyes of the Holy One, blessed be He.

**4 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵת כָּל־כִּשְׁרוֹן הַמַּעֲשֶׂה.** שֶׁאֵינוּ לְשֵׁם שָׁמַיִם, אֶלָּא לְקִנְאַת אִישׁ מֵרֵעֵהוּ, שֶׁשְּׁנֵיהֶם הָבֶל:<br>
**4 [English]**	**and all the excellence of work.** which is not for the sake of Heaven, but for one’s envy of his friend, both of which are vanity.

**4 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי הִיא קִנְאַת אִישׁ.** אֲשֶׁר הִיא קִנְאַת אִישׁ מֵרֵעֵהוּ:<br>
**4 [English]**	**which is a man’s envy.** [as translated,] which is a man’s envy of his friend.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**הַכְּסִיל.** הָרָשָׁע חוֹבֵק אֶת־יָדָיו וְאֵינוֹ יָגֵעַ, וְאֵינוֹ אוֹכֵל אֶלָּא מִן הַגָּזֶל:<br>
**5 [English]**	**The fool.** The wicked man folds his hands and does not toil, and he does not eat except from robbery.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְאוֹכֵל אֶת־בְּשָׂרוֹ.** לְיוֹם הַדִּין שֶׁרוֹאֶה צַדִּיקִים בְּכָבוֹד, וְהוּא נִדּוֹן. כַּךְ נִדְרָשׁ בְּסִפְרֵי:<br>
**5 [English]**	**and eats his own flesh.** on the Day of Judgment, when he sees righteous men experiencing honor, while he is being judged. It is explained in this manner in Sifrei (Deut. 11:26).



**6 [Hebrew]**	**טוֹב מְלֹא כַף נָחַת.** לִהְיוֹת קוֹנֶה נְכָסִים מְעַט מִיגִיעוֹ, שֶׁיְּהֵא בָהֶן נַחַת רוּחַ לְיוֹצְרוֹ:<br>
**6 [English]**	**Better is a handful of ease.** to acquire few possessions, but with his toil, so that his Creator should have satisfaction therefrom.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**מִמְּלֹא חָפְנַיִם.** נְכָסִים הַרְבֵּה בַּעֲבֵרָה, שֶׁהוּא עָמָל וְעַצְבוּת רוּחַ לִפְנֵי הַמָּקוֹם:<br>
**6 [English]**	**than two handfuls.** many possessions through sin, which is toil and grief to the Omnipresent.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**תַּחַת הַשָּׁמֶשׁ.** כְּמוֹ "תַּחַת הַשָּׁמָיִם":<br>
**7 [English]**	**under the sun.** like, “under the heaven.”



**8 [Hebrew]**	**יֵשׁ אֶחָד וְאֵין שֵׁנִי.** יֵשׁ לְךָ אָדָם שֶׁעוֹשֶׂה דְבָרָיו בִּיחִידִי:<br>
**8 [English]**	**There is one, and there is no second.** There is a man who does his work alone.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**גַּם בֵּן וָאָח אֵין לוֹ.** אִם תַּלְמִיד חָכָם הוּא, אֵינוֹ קוֹנֶה לוֹ תַלְמִיד שֶׁהוּא כְבֵן, וְלֹא חָבֵר שֶׁהוּא כְּאָח. וְאִם רַוָּק הוּא, אֵינוּ נוֹשֵׂא אִשָּׁה לִהְיוֹת לוֹ כְאָח לְעֵזֶר וּלְהוֹלִיד בֵּן. וְאִם סוֹחֵר הוּא, אֵינוֹ קוֹנֶה לוֹ שֻׁתָּפִים, וְיוֹצֵא לְדֶרֶךְ יְחִידִי:<br>
**8 [English]**	**yea, he has neither son nor brother.** If he is a Torah scholar, he does not acquire for himself a disciple, who is like a son, or a companion, who is like a brother. And if he is a bachelor, he does not take a wife, to be to him like a brother, and to beget a son. And if he is a merchant, he does not acquire partners for himself, but he goes out on the way alone.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵין קֵץ לְכָל־עֲמָלוֹ.** יָגֵעַ בְּגִירְסָא, וְאִם סוֹחֵר הוּא, עָמֵל בִּפְרַקְמַטְיָא:<br>
**8 [English]**	**and there is no end to all his toil.** He toils in study, and if he is a merchant, he toils with merchandise.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**גַּם־עֵינוֹ לֹא תִשְׂבַּע עשֶׁר.** לֹא יְהֵא שָׂבֵעַ בְּטַעֲמֵי תוֹרָה, שֶׁהַרְבֵּה תוֹרָה לוֹמֵד אָדָם מִתַּלְמִידָיו. וּלְעִנְיַן הַמָּמוֹן, רוֹדֵף תָּמִיד אַחַר הַמָּמוֹן:<br>
**8 [English]**	**neither is his eye sated from wealth.** He will not be sated from the insights of Torah, for a person learns much Torah from his pupils, and regarding money, he constantly pursues money.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**וּלְמִי אֲנִי עָמֵל.** מֵאַחַר שֶׁאֵינִי מַעֲמִיד תַּלְמִידִים, וְאֵינִי נוֹשֵׂא אִשָּׁה לְהוֹלִיד בָּנִים:<br>
**8 [English]**	**Now for whom do I toil.** since I do not raise disciples, and I do not take a wife to beget children?



**9 [Hebrew]**	**טוֹבִים הַשְּׁנַיִם.** לְכָל־דָּבָר:<br>
**9 [English]**	**Two are better.** in all respects.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**מִן־הָאֶחָד.** לְפִיכָךְ, יִקְנֶה לוֹ אָדָם חָבֵר וְיִשָּׂא אִשָּׁה, אֲשֶׁר יֵשׁ לָהֶם יוֹתֵר רֶוַח בַּעֲמָלָם. הַרְבֵּה מְלָאכָה נַעֲשֵׂית בִּשְׁנַיִם, שֶׁאֵין הַיָּחִיד מַתְחִיל בָּהּ לְבַדּוֹ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**than one.** Therefore, a person should acquire for himself a companion and take his wife, for they have more profit in their toil. Much work is done by two, which the individual does not begin alone.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי אִם־יִפֹּלוּ.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ. וּלְעִנְיַן הַמִּשְׁנָה, אִם תָּקְפָה עָלָיו מִשְׁנָה שֶׁלּוֹ, חֲבֵרוֹ מַחֲזִירָהּ לוֹ, אוֹ אִם יִכָּשֵׁל וְלֹא דִקְדֵּק אֶת־אֲשֶׁר שָׁמַע מִפִּי רַבּוֹ, בָּא חֲבֵירוֹ וּמַעֲמִידוֹ עַל הָאֱמֶת:<br>
**10 [English]**	**For if they fall.** [To be explained] according to its apparent meaning. And regarding studies, if his studies were too difficult for him, his companion will restore them to him, or if he stumbles and was not exact in what he heard from his mentor, his companion will set him on the truthful course.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִי לוֹ.** וְאוֹי לוֹ:<br>
**10 [English]**	**but woe.** Heb. וְאִילוּ, but woe is to him.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְחַם לָהֶם.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ, וּלְעִנְיַן זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה, מִתְחַמְּמִים זֶה מִזֶּה וּמוֹלִידִים:<br>
**11 [English]**	**they will have warmth.** according to its apparent meaning, and regarding male and female, they are aroused by one another and reproduce.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִם יִתְקְפוֹ הָאֶחָד.** אִם בָּאוּ לִסְטִים עָלָיו לְתָקְפּוֹ אִם שְׁנַיִם הֵם, יַעַמְדוּ לְנֶגְדוֹ, וְכָל־שֶׁכֵּן אִם ג' הֵם, וְהַחוּט הַמְשֻׁלָּשׁ לֹא בִמְהֵרָה יִנָּתֵק. דָּבָר אַחֵר: מִי שֶׁהוּא תַּלְמִיד חָכָם, וּבְנוֹ וּבֶן־בְּנוֹ, שׁוּב אֵין תּוֹרָה פוֹסֶקֶת מִזַּרְעוֹ, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר, "לֹא יָמוּשׁוּ מִפִּיךָ וּמִפִּי זַרְעֲךָ וּמִפִּי זֶרַע זַרְעֲךָ". דָּבָר אַחֵר:<br>
**12 [English]**	**And if a man prevails against the one.** If bandits came upon him to prevail against him, if they are two, they will stand against him, and surely, if they are three, for a three-stranded cord will not easily be broken. Another explanation: Whoever is a Torah scholar, as well as his son and his grandson, the Torah will never cease from his seed, and so Scripture states (Isa. 59:21): “shall not move from your mouth or from the mouth of your seed or from the mouth of your seed’s seed.” Another explanation:

**12 [Hebrew]**	**חוּט הַמְשֻׁלָּשׁ.** בְּמִקְרָא וּבְמִשְׁנָה וּבְדֶרֶךְ אֶרֶץ לֹא בִמְהֵרָה הוּא חוֹטֵא. בְּפָנִים אֲחֵרִים נִדְרָשׁ בַּמִּדְרָשׁ: "יֵשׁ אֶחָד וְאֵין שֵׁנִי" לוֹ, אֲבָל אֵין סֵדֶר כָּל־הַמִּקְרָאוֹת הַלָּלוּ מִתְיַשֵּׁב עֲלֵיהֶם:<br>
**12 [English]**	**and a three-stranded cord.** in Bible, Mishnah, and good manners-will not quickly sin. “There is one, and there is no second,” is expounded in the Midrash in other ways, but the sequence of all these verses does not fit in with them.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**טוֹב יֶלֶד מִסְכֵּן וְחָכָם.** זֶה יֵצֶר טוֹב, וְלָמָּה נִקְרָא שְׁמוֹ יֶלֶד? שֶׁאֵינוֹ בָא בְאָדָם עַד י"ג שָׁנָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**Better a poor and wise child.** This is the good inclination, and why is it called a child? Because it does not enter man until thirteen years.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**מִסְכֵּן.** שֶׁאֵין הָאֵבָרִים שׁוֹמְעִים לוֹ כְּמוֹ לְיֵצֶר הָרָע:<br>
**13 [English]**	**poor.** because the limbs do not obey it, as [they do] the evil inclination.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**חָכָם.** שֶׁמַּשְׂכִּיל אֶת־הָאָדָם לְדֶרֶךְ טוֹבָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**wise.** which gives a person intelligence to [follow] the good way.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**מִמֶּלֶךְ זָקֵן וּכְסִיל.** זֶה יֵצֶר הָרַע, שֶׁהוּא שַׁלִּיט עַל כָּל־הָאֵבָרִים:<br>
**13 [English]**	**than an old and foolish king.** the evil inclination, which rules over all the limbs.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**זָקֵן.** שֶׁמִּשָּׁעָה שֶׁנּוֹלָד הַוְּלָד, הוּא נָתוּן בּוֹ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, "לַפֶּתַח חַטָּאת רֹבֵץ":<br>
**13 [English]**	**old.** for when the child is born, it is put into him, as it is said (Gen. 4:7): “sin lies at the opening.”

**13 [Hebrew]**	**וּכְסִיל.** שֶׁמַּתְעֵהוּ בְּדֶרֶךְ רָעָה, כַּךְ נִדְרָשׁ בַּמִּדְרָשׁ:<br>
**13 [English]**	**and foolish.** for it misleads him in the way of evil. In this manner, it is interpreted in the Midrash (Ecc. Rabbah).

**13 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָדַע לְהִזָּהֵר עוֹד.** שֶׁהֲרֵי הִזְקִין, וְאֵינוֹ מְקַבֵּל תּוֹכֵחָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**who no longer knows to receive admonition.** for he has become old and does not accept reproof.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי־מִבֵּית הָסוּרִים יָצָא לִמְלֹךְ.** מִמְּקוֹם הַטִּנֹּפֶת וְסִרְחוֹן, כְּדִמְתַרְגְּמִינָן "וַיִּבְאַשׁ" וּסְרִי:<br>
**14 [English]**	**For out of the prison he has come to reign.** Heb. הָסוּרִים; from the place of filth and stench, as we translate: (Exod. 16:29) וַיִבְאַשּׁ, (and it became putrid) as וּסְרִי.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי גַּם בְּמַלְכוּתוֹ.** מִּשֶׁמָּלַךְ בְּאָדָם, נוֹלַד הָרָשׁ הַמִּסְכֵּן הַטּוֹב מִמֶּנּוּ, וּבָא מִתּוֹךְ טָהֳרָה וְלֹא מִתּוֹךְ טִנֹּפֶת הָרֶחֶם, כַּךְ דְּרָשׁוּהוּ בַמִּדְרָשׁ. דָבָר אַחֵר:<br>
**14 [English]**	**for even in his kingdom.** Since he reigned over man, the poor one was born; the poor one, who was better than he, and who came out of purity, not out of the filth of the womb. So did our Rabbis interpret it in the Midrash. [See Ecc. Rabbah.] Another explanation:

**14 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי טוֹב יֶלֶד מִסְכֵּן וְגוֹ'.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ:<br>
**14 [English]**	**[13] Better a poor and wise child, etc..** according to its apparent meaning.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָדַע לְהִזָּהֵר עוֹד.** שֶׁכְּבָר הִזְקִין בְּרִשְׁעוֹ וּכְסִילוּתו‍ֹ<br>
**14 [English]**	**who no longer knows to receive admonition.** for he has already aged in his wickedness and foolishness.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי מִבֵּית הָאֲסוּרִים יָצָא לִמְלֹךְ.** כִּי הַיֶּלֶד הַמִּסְכֵּן, סוֹפוֹ שֶׁיֹּאמְרוּ עָלָיו שֶׁיָּצָא לִמְלֹךְ מִתּוֹךְ עָנְיוֹ וּמִבֵּית אֲסוּרָיו, שֶׁהֲרֵי סַמֶּ"ךְ שֶׁל הָסוּרִים נָקוּד רָפֵי, וַהֲרֵי הוּא כְּמוֹ "הָאֲסוּרִים". (סְפָרִים אֲחֵרִים: כְּמוֹ, "וְלֹא־יַהֵל שָׁם עֲרָבִי", כְּמוֹ יַאֲהֵל) שֶׁכֵּן מָצִינוּ בְיוֹסֵף, שֶׁמָּלַךְ מִתּוֹךְ יְצִיאַת בֵּית הָאֲסוּרִים. וְכֵן דָּוִד, "אֲנִי לְקַחְתִּיךָ מִן־הַנָּוֶה מֵאַחַר הַצֹּאן"<br>
**14 [English]**	**[14] For out of the prison he has come to reign.** For the poor child-it will ultimately be said about him that he came to reign from the midst of his affliction and from the place of his imprisonment, for the “sammech” of הָסוּרִים is punctuated without a “dagesh,” which is equivalent to הָאֲסוּרִים. [Some editions: like (Isa. 13:20): “and no Arab shall pitch his tent (יַהֵל) there, like יֶאֶהַל], for so we found that Joseph reigned following his being freed from prison, and so David, (II Sam. 7:8):” I took you from the sheepcote, from following the sheep."

**14 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי גַּם בְּמַלְכוּתוֹ נוֹלַד רָשׁ.** כִּי כָשֵׁר וְהָגוּן הוּא שֶׁיִּמְלֹךְ, כִּי גַּם בְּמַלְכוּתוֹ הוּא נֶהְפַּךְ מִמִּנְהַג הַשְּׂרָרָה וּמַקְטִין עַצְמוֹ אֵצֶל הַחֲכָמִים כְּמִדַּת הָרָשִׁים, וְכֵן, "וְעַיִר פֶּרֶא אָדָם יִוָּלֵד", שֶׁיֵּהָפֵךְ וְיִשְׁתַּנֶּה לוֹ מִמַּה שֶׁהָיָה כְעַיִר פֶּרֶא, וְיֵעָשֶׂה אָדָם נוֹלַד נַעֲשָׂה, וּלְשׁוֹן הֹוֶה הוּא:<br>
**14 [English]**	**for even in his kingdom he becomes humble.** For it is fitting and proper that he should reign, because even in his kingdom, he changed from the custom of the ruling class and he humbled himself to the sages, like the custom of the poor. And so (Job 11:12): “and [from] a wild donkey a man will be born (יִוָלֵד),” that he will be changed and converted from what he was, resembling a wild donkey, and he will become a man. נוֹלַד means “becomes,” and it is in the present tense.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**רָאִיתִי אֶת־כָּל־הַחַיִּים וְגוֹ'.** מָצָאתִי בְמִדְרַשׁ הַסֵּפֶר הַזֶּה: "זֶה דוֹר הַמַּבּוּל, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בָּהֶם, ,וּמִכָּל־הָחַי'":<br>
**15 [English]**	**I saw all the living, etc..** I found in the Midrash of this Book (Ecc. Zuta): This refers to the Generation of the Flood, about which it says (Gen. 6:19): “And of every living thing.”

**15 [Hebrew]**	**עִם הַיֶּלֶד הַשֵּׁנִי.** אֲשֶׁר יִתְקַיֵּם תַּחַת אוֹתוֹ הַדּוֹר, שֶׁהֵם נֹחַ וּבָנָיו:<br>
**15 [English]**	**with the second child.** who will exist instead of that generation, who were Noah and his sons.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**אֵין־קֵץ לְכָל־הָעָם.** פָּרִים וְרָבִים הָיוּ יוֹתֵר מִדַּאי, כְּמוֹ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, "זַרְעָם נָכוֹן לִפְנֵיהֶם". אִשָּׁה מִתְעַבֶּרֶת וְיוֹלֶדֶת לִשְׁלשָׁה יָמִים. "יְשַׁלְּחוּ כַצֹּאן עֲוִילֵיהֶם":<br>
**16 [English]**	**There is no end to all the people.** They were fruitful and multiplied too much, as it is said (Job 21:8): “Their children are well established in their sight.” A woman would conceive and bear in three days; (ibid. verse 11): “They send forth their infants like sheep.”

**16 [Hebrew]**	**לְכֹל אֲשֶׁר־הָיָה לִפְנֵיהֶם.** אֵין קֵץ לְכָל־טוֹב שֶׁהָיָה לִפְנֵיהֶם, וְהַכֹּל אָבַד, וּבְרֶגַע שְׁאוֹל יֵחַתּוּ:<br>
**16 [English]**	**to all that were before them.** There was no end to all the good that was before them, but all was lost, and in a moment descended to the grave.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**גַּם הָאַחֲרוֹנִים.** דּוֹר הַפְלָגָה:<br>
**16 [English]**	**also the last ones.** the Generation of the Dispersion.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא יִשְׂמְחוּ.** גַּם הֵם בַּטּוֹב הַנִּתָּן בְּיָדָם:<br>
**16 [English]**	**will not rejoice.** with the good that was given into their hands.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי־גַּם־זֶה.** סוֹפוֹ שֶׁל הֶבֶל וְרַעְיוֹן רוּחַ כַּאֲשֶׁר אָדָם מְהַלֵּךְ אַחַר יִצְרוֹ:<br>
**16 [English]**	**for this too.** Its end is that of vanity and frustration, when one follows his evil inclination.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁמֹר רַגְלְךָ כַּאֲשֶׁר תֵּלֵךְ אֶל־בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים.** הֵיאַךְ תֵּלֵךְ. אִם תָּבִיא תוֹדָה וְנִדְבַת שְׁלָמִים, הוּא הַטּוֹב, וּשְׁמֹר עַצְמְךָ, שֶׁלֹּא תִצְטָרֵךְ לֵילֵךְ בַּהֲבָאַת חַטָּאוֹת וַאֲשָׁמוֹת:<br>
**17 [English]**	**Watch your feet when you go the House of God.** [Watch] how you go: if you bring a thanksgiving offering or a voluntary peace offering, that is good, but watch yourself that you should not have to bring sin offerings or guilt offerings.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**וְקָרוֹב.** הֱוֵי לִשְׁמֹעַ דִּבְרֵי הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא, וֶהֱוֵי טוֹב וְקָרוֹב לְהַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא:<br>
**17 [English]**	**and be ready.** Be ready to obey the words of the Holy One, blessed be He.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**מִתֵּת הַכְּסִילִים זָבַח.** שֶׁיֶּחֱטָא וְיָבִיא קָרְבָּן:<br>
**17 [English]**	**rather than fools should give sacrifice.** that he should sin and bring a sacrifice.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי־אֵינָם יוֹדְעִים לַעֲשׂוֹת רָע.** אֵין הַכְּסִיל מֵבִין שֶׁהוּא עוֹשֶׂה רַע לְעַצְמוֹ:<br>
**17 [English]**	**for they know not that they do evil.** The fool does not realize that he is doing harm to himself.
