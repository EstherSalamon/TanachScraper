**1**	**My soul quarrels.** Heb. נקטה. My soul quarrels about the fact that I am alive. That is an expression of (Ezek. 20:43), "and you shall quarrel (ונקטתם) with yourselves.

**1**	**I will leave my speech.** i.e., my trouble, to cry and lament, and I cannot forget it or restrain it.



**3**	**Is it good.** Heb. הטוב, an interrogative form. [Is it good to You] that You should oppress and rob the righteous man of his righteousness, [while] upon the council of the wicked, who vex You, You shine Your splendor to show them a friendly countenance?



**5**	**Are Your days like the days of a mortal.** to provoke him and pursue [him]?



**7**	**It is in Your knowledge.** You know.



**8**	**shaped me.** Heb. עצבוני, They made me a shape, a mold, like “nor straighten (מעצבין) an infant’s limbs,” in Tractate Shabbath (22:6). But now Your hosts are together round about.

**8**	**and You destroy me.** These are the worms.



**9**	**You will return me.** in the end.



**10**	**Did you not pour me like milk.** Concerning the drop from which I was born.



**12**	**and Your providence.** The providence of Your watch, for You appointed watchers for me.

**12**	**watched my spirit.** in my mother’s womb, and afterwards…



**13**	**But You hid these in Your heart.** to destroy me as though You did not remember them, but I knew that this was with You, everything as it was delineated, and You did not forget.



**14**	**If I sinned, You watched me.** You laid Your eyes on me so as not to ignore [my sins] nor to forbear retaliation, and [from now on] You are not cleansing me of my iniquity but are requiting me for everything.



**15**	**sated with disgrace.** am I.

**15**	**and seeing my affliction.** Heb. וראה. This is not an expression of the imperative, but is an adjective. וראה עניי is badlace (?) in Old French, “that I see it all the time.” As you say (below, at the end of the Book), “ וּשְׂבַע יָמִים, and I am sated with days,” like “וְשָׂבֵע יָמִים” (with a “kamatz” under the “sin”) so do you say; וּרְאֵה, like וְרוֹאֶה, “and seeing my affliction.”



**16**	**And it is so great that You hunt me like a lion.** And it is so great in Your eyes to be to You as a burden and a disappointment, that You hunt me like a lion; You spread out a net for me as though I was as mighty as a lion.

**16**	**and You repeatedly.** from day to day.

**16**	**pronounce a verdict upon me.** Heb. תתפלא, to exact and to pronounce judgment, an expression of (Num. 6:2).“shall pronounce (יפלא).”



**17**	**Your witnesses.** to testify about the changes of the ailments and the pains.

**17**	**with set times are with me.** Times set for these my tortures, which serve (once) and [then] repeatedly shift with changes different from these, and serve again.



**18**	**Would that I had died and no eye had seen me.** If only I had died [upon emerging] from the womb.



**19**	**would that I was brought from the womb to the grave.** Would that I was brought.



**20**	**withdraw from me.** Withdraw Yourself from me and from upon me, like (Isa. 65:5), “Keep to yourself” Remain by yourself and distance yourself from me.



**21**	**before I go.** While I am still alive, and the time for me to die has not yet arrived, desist from me and I will rest.



**22**	**darkness.** Heb. עפתה. This is a noun meaning darkness, like (Amos 4: 13) “dawn and darkness (ועיפתה).”

**22**	**the shadow of death.** it is, without orders in it (that there are no orders of man; i.e., there are no customs, for there is no civilization, and its light is like darkness).

**22**	**and where the light is as darkness.** The illuminated place [therein] is like darkness.
