**1**	Afterwards, Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.

**2**	Now Job raised his voice and said,

**3**	"Would the day in which I was to be born be lost, and the night when one said, 'A man has impregnated.'

**4**	May that day be dark; let God not seek it from above, and let no light shine upon it.

**5**	May darkness and the shadow of death defile it; let pitch darkness dwell upon it; let them frighten it like demons of the day.

**6**	That night-may pitch darkness take it; it shall not rejoice among the days of the year; in the number of months it shall not come.

**7**	Behold that night shall be lonely; no joyful singing shall come therein.

**8**	May those who curse the day, curse it-those destined to be childless in their union.

**9**	May the stars of its evening be darkened; it shall hope for light but have none, neither shall it see the rays of dawn.

**10**	For He did not shut the doors of my womb, neither did He hide trouble from my eyes.

**11**	Why did I not die from the womb? [Why did I not] emerge from the belly and perish?

**12**	Why did knees receive me and why [were there] breasts that I should suck?

**13**	For now, I would be lying tranquilly; I would sleep, then it would be restful for me.

**14**	With kings and counselors of the earth, who build ruins for themselves,

**15**	Or with princes who have gold, who fill their houses with silver.

**16**	Or [why] was I not like a hidden stillborn child, like infants who did not see the light?

**17**	There the wicked cease from anger, and there the weary rest.

**18**	Together prisoners are tranquil; they hear not the voice of the taskmaster.

**19**	Small and great are there, and a slave is free from his master.

**20**	Why does He give the toiler light and life to those of bitter spirit?

**21**	Who await death and it is not here, and they spy after it more than after hidden treasures.

**22**	Those who are happy over joyous occurrences, rejoice when they find a grave

**23**	for a man whose way is hidden, and God hedged him in.

**24**	For before my meal comes my sigh, and my moans pour like water.

**25**	For the thing I feared has befallen me, and what I dreaded is coming upon me.

**26**	I was not at ease, neither was I quiet, and I did not rest, yet trouble came.
