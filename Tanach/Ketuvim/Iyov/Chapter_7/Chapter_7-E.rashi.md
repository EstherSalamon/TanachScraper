**1**	**Is not man on Earth for a limited time.** This is what I said to you. ‘Agree and turn to me.’ because how can I be silent from crying out about my misfortune? Do you not know that the time a man has to live is limited?

**1**	**and… like the days of a hireling.** and I-that time that is allotted to me…



**2**	**As a slave who longs for the shadow.** As a slave, who toils all day through and longs and yearns, “When will the shadow of evening come?” And as a hireling, who hopes for his wages at eventide, because the whole day was to him for toil, and he yearns for the sunset.



**3**	**So was I made to possess.** that allotted time that was given to man on Earth, with months of futility and torments.

**3**	**and they appointed wearisome nights for me.** (from Heaven).



**4**	**If I would lie down.** at night, I would hope and say, etc.

**4**	**and the evening depart.** When will it become light and when will the time of arising come, and when will the evening depart? [The word מדד] is an expression of (Gen. 31:40) “and sleep was banished (ותדד).” The “mem” is a defective radical, as in מאמר, statement; משמר, watch.

**4**	**And I was sated with restlessness.** I was sated with restlessness on my bed by day until twilight, because, due to the torments I could not sleep until the twilight of day, since my flesh was clothed with worms.



**5**	**and clods of earth.** Clods of earth (?) Now this is the development of rust, because it is the custom of clods of earth to moisten and cause rust.

**5**	**wrinkled.** Heb. רגע wrinkled, as in (Isa. 51:15), “Who wrinkles (רגע) the sea.” Another explanation: עוֹרִי רָגַע is an expression of resting a wave after a wave, like wrinkles (?).



**6**	**My days are swifter.** The days of my prosperity hastened to go away.

**6**	**than a weaver’s shuttle.** Than weaving that is done swiftly. So said Hezekiah (Isa. 38:12), “I severed, like a weaver, my life.”

**6**	**without hope.** I no longer hope for good.



**7**	**my eye will not return.** after death. Here Job denies the dogma of the Resurrection of the Dead.



**8**	**shall see me no more.** The eye that wishes to see me shall see me no more after I die.

**8**	**set Your eyes upon me and I will be here no longer.** He is addressing the Holy One, blessed be He: “Why did You have to finish me off and crush me with pains? With one look that you set Your eyes on me, I am no longer in the world.”



**11**	**Neither will I restrain my mouth.** Since You do not desist from me, neither will I restrain my speech from complaining about Your ways.



**12**	**Am I a sea.** that You placed sand over me as a guard?

**12**	**or a sea monster.** A huge fish that You imprisoned in the depths of the sea, that You place this Adversary as a guard over me, to watch me lest my soul depart?



**13**	**that my couch shall bear my speech.** The couch upon which I sleep at night will bear my trouble somewhat, and I will be able to restrain my speech.



**15**	**death rather than these my bones.** I choose death rather than these limbs in my body.



**16**	**I despised.** my life, because ultimately I will not live forever.

**16**	**desist from me.** From harming me, because my days are futile and few.



**17**	**that You should give him importance.** to pay attention to him, to visit his deeds every morning and test him every moment?



**19**	**How long.** For what length of time will You not desist from me, like (Exod. 5:9), “and let them not turn (ישעו) to false words.” Similarly (below 14:6), “Turn away (שעה) from him and desist.”

**19**	**until I swallow my spit.** long enough to swallow my spit.



**20**	**as a mark for You.** As an object that a man strikes when he is angry and vents upon it the entire act of his hatred (other editions: his sin).

**20**	**so that I have become a burden to myself.** [Etz Chayim ms: Like “and I have become a burden to You.”] This is one of the words in which Scripture euphemized, and they are known as the emendation of the scribes.



**21**	**Now why do You not forgive my transgression.** What is this matter that You do not forgive my transgression?

**21**	**and You shall seek me.** You shall seek me and not find me.
