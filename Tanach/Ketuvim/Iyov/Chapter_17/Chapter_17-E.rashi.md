**1**	**flicker.** Heb. נזעכו have sprung away, like (above 6:17) “they jump (נדעכו).”

**1**	**graves are ready for me.** I am ready for the grave.



**2**	**even if there were no.** [Even if] this had not befallen me, that scornful people had gathered with me in my consolations to mock me.

**2**	**in their provocation.** Heb. ובהמרותם, like (Ezek. 20:8, 13, 21), “And they provoked (וימרו) me;” (Deut. 9:7), “you have been provoking (ממרים).” [This is] an expression meaning: in their provocation.

**2**	**my eye… abide.** My eye, my heart, and my thought abide in them.



**3**	**Pay heed now.** O Creator.

**3**	**give me surety.** by shaking the hands to debate together, for who is it of these who will shake my hand that it should be good for me?



**4**	**For.** Behold.

**4**	**You have hidden their hearts from understanding.** Therefore, Your glory will not be exalted through them.



**5**	**flattery.** Heb. לְחֵלֶק, flattery (חלקלקות).

**5**	**speaks.** Each one of them [speaks] to his friend, and this will come to them: their children’s eyes will fail.



**6**	**and… a drum.** Heb. תפת, like תּוֹף, a drum.

**6**	**before them.** lit. to the face; [i. e., to the face] of the people, I am [a drum].



**7**	**and my limbs.** namely, the limbs with which I was formed.



**8**	**will be appalled.** they will wonder.

**8**	**and an innocent one.** If there is a righteous [man] who hears the mockery of these hypocrites.

**8**	**will be aroused.** to quarrel with you.



**9**	**Yet the righteous holds on his way.** i.e., every righteous man holds on his way to stand up against the company of the hypocrites.



**10**	**But all of them.** I say to you, “Return now and come now.”

**10**	**and I will not find any wise man among you.** insofar as you mock my lamentation.



**11**	**My days etc..** Behold, the days of my prosperity, to which I became accustomed, have passed.

**11**	**my purposes.** Which I had expected to last with prosperity.

**11**	**the thoughts of my heart have been broken off.** Heb. מורשי, lit. the inheritances of. The thoughts which my heart gave me to possess, for my heart said that I would inherit good because of my [righteous] way.



**12**	**They turn night into day.** These mockers turn night into day for me, for I cannot sleep at night because of the trouble of the mockings.

**12**	**the light is short.** lit. קרוב, near, and short for me because of the trouble of the darkness of the night. The light is short; when the sun sets, it appears to me as though a short time ago the day dawned, was shortened, and left. This is like (Gen. 19:20), “this city is recent (קרובה)” it was newly settled recently and like (below 20:5), “That the triumph of the wicked is short (מקרוב),” and like (Deut. 32:17), “new ones that came up of late (מקרוב).”



**13**	**Since I hope for the grave.** Heb. אם, like (above 14:5), “If (אם) his days are limited” [See Rashi ad loc.], i.e., since I hope for the grave as my house.

**13**	**in the darkness I have spread out my couch.** I have spread out my couch in the grave.



**14**	**To the pit I have called, ‘You are my father,’.** to lodge there many days as though it was my father, since this is my hope.



**15**	**Where then is my hope.** [Where then is] this [my hope]? Why is it deferred, and who will see it to hasten it to me?



**16**	**The limbs of the grave shall descend.** Those limbs [of my body] that will ultimately descend to the grave, for indeed they will all rest on the dust.

**16**	**they rest on the dust.** Heb. נחת, will lodge, an expression of (Num. 11:26), “the spirit rested (ותנח) upon them.”
