**10**	**the camels.** Heb. הָאֲחַשְּׁתְּרָנִים. a type of camel that runs swiftly.



**11**	**and to take their spoils for plunder.** as was written in the first [letters], but they “ -upon the spoils they did not lay their hands,” for they showed everyone that it was not done for the sake of money.

**14**	**hastened.** They hastened them to act swiftly, because they had no time, since they had to get ahead of the first couriers to overtake them.



**15**	**and a wrap of linen.** Heb. וְתַכְרִיך בּוּץ, a wrap of linen, a shawl in which to enwrap oneself.



**17**	**became Jews.** מִתְיַהֲדִים, became proselytes.
