**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהִי בִּימֵי אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ.** מֶלֶךְ פָּרַס הָיָה, שֶׁמָּלַךְ תַּחַת כּוֹרֶשׁ לְסוֹף שִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה שֶׁל גָּלוּת בָּבֶל:<br>
**1 [English]**	**Now it came to pass in the days of Ahasuerus.** He was the king of Persia who reigned instead of Cyrus at the end of the seventy years of the Babylonian exile.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**הוּא אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ.** הוּא בְרִשְׁעוֹ מִתְּחִלָּתוֹ וְעַד סוֹפוֹ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**he was the Ahasuerus.** He was equally wicked from beginning to end.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**הַמֹּלֵךְ.** שֶׁמָּלַךְ מֵעַצְמוֹ וְלֹא הָיָה מִזֶּרַע הַמְּלוּכָה:<br>
**1 [English]**	**who reigned.** He reigned on his own, and was not of royal seed.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**מֵהֹדּוּ וְעַד כּוּשׁ, וגו'.** הַמּוֹלֵךְ עַל מֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים וְשֶׁבַע מְדִינוֹת כְּמוֹ שֶׁמָּלַךְ מֵהֹדוּ וְעַד כּוּשׁ שֶׁעוֹמְדִים זֶה אֵצֶל זֶה. וְכֵן "כִּי הוּא רֹדֶה בְּכָל עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר מִתִּפְסַח וְעַד עַזָּה", שֶׁהָיָה רוֹדֶה בְּכָל עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר כְּמוֹ שֶׁהוּא רוֹדֶה מִתִּפְסַח עַד עַזָּה:<br>
**1 [English]**	**from Hodu to Cush, etc..** He reigned over one hundred twenty-seven provinces as he reigned from Hodu to Cush, which are situated alongside one another, and so [we explain] (I Kings 5:4): “For he had dominion over all [the inhabitants of] this side of the river, from Tiphsah even to Gaza,” meaning that he had dominion over all the inhabitants of this side of the river, just as he had dominion from Tiphsah to Gaza.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**כְּשֶׁבֶת הַמֶּלֶךְ אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ, וגו'.** כְּשֶׁנִּתְקַיֵּם הַמַּלְכוּת בְּיָדוֹ. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ פֵּרְשׁוּהוּ בְּעִנְיָן אַחֵר בְּמַסֶּכֶת מְגִילָּה:<br>
**2 [English]**	**when King Ahasuerus sat, etc..** when the kingdom was firmly established in his hand. Our Sages, however, explained it differently in Tractate Megillah (11b).



**3 [Hebrew]**	**הַפַּרְתְּמִים.** שִׁלְטוֹנִים בִּלְשׁוֹן פָּרַס:<br>
**3 [English]**	**the nobles.** Heb. הַפַּרְתְּמִים, governors in Persian.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**יָמִים רַבִּים.** עָשָׂה לָהֶם מִשְׁתֶּה:<br>
**4 [English]**	**many days.** He made a banquet for them.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**גִּנַּת.** מְקוֹם זֵרְעוֹנֵי יְרָקוֹת:<br>
**5 [English]**	**the garden.** a place where vegetables are sown.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**בִּיתַן.** נָטוּעַ בְּאִילָנוֹת:<br>
**5 [English]**	**orchard.** where trees are planted.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**חוּר כַּרְפַּס וּתְכֵלֶת.** מִינֵי בְגָדִים צִבְעוֹנִים פֵּרַס לָהֶם לְמַצָּעוֹת:<br>
**6 [English]**	**white, fine cotton, and blue.** He spread out various types of colored garments for them for spreads.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**אָחוּז בְּחַבְלֵי בוּץ וְאַרְגָּמָן.** מְרֻקָּמִים בִּפְתִילֵי בוּץ וְאַרְגָּמָן. אוֹתָן פֵּרַס לָהֶם עַל גְּלִילֵי כֶסֶף וְעַל עַמּוּדֵי שֵׁשׁ:<br>
**6 [English]**	**embroidered with cords of linen and purple.** embroidered with threads of linen and purple; he spread these out for them on rods of silver and on columns of marble.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**מִטּוֹת זָהָב וָכֶסֶף.** עָרַךְ לֵישֵׁב עֲלֵיהֶם לִסְעוּדָה:<br>
**6 [English]**	**couches of gold and silver.** he set [them] up to sit upon for the feast.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**עַל רִצְפַת.** קַרְקָעוֹת שֶׁל בַּהַט וָשֵׁשׁ, וגו'. מִינֵי אֲבָנִים טוֹבוֹת פֵּרְשׁוּ רַבּוֹתֵינוּ. וּלְפִי מַשְׁמָעוּת הַמִּקְרָא כַּךְ שְׁמָם:<br>
**6 [English]**	**on a pavement of.** floors of green and white, etc. Our Sages identified them as kinds of precious stones, and according to the apparent meaning of the verse, these were their names.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַשְׁקוֹת בִּכְלֵי זָהָב.** כְּמוֹ וּלְהַשְׁקוֹת:<br>
**7 [English]**	**And they gave them to drink in golden vessels.** Heb., like וּלְהַשְּׁקוֹת, and to give to drink.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**שׁוֹנִים.** מְשֻׁנִּים זֶה מִזֶּה, וְכֵן "וְדָתֵיהֶם שֹׁנוֹת". וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ דָרְשׁוּ מַה שֶׁדָּרְשׁוּ:<br>
**7 [English]**	**and the vessels differed from one another.** Heb. שּׁוֹנִים, different from each other, and likewise, (below 3:8) “and their laws differ (שּׁנוֹת),” and our Sages expounded what they expounded.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**וְיֵין מַלְכוּת רָב.** הַרְבֵּה. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ אָמְרוּ: שֶׁהִשְׁקָה אוֹתָם כָּל אֶחָד וְאֶחָד יַיִן שֶׁהוּא זָקֵן מִמֶּנּוּ:<br>
**7 [English]**	**and royal wine was plentiful.** It was abundant; and our Sages said that he gave each one to drink wine that was older than he.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**כַדָּת.** לְפִי שֶׁיֵּשׁ סְעוּדוֹת שֶׁכּוֹפִין אֶת הַמְּסֻבִּין לִשְׁתּוֹת כְּלִי גָדוֹל, וְיֵשׁ שֶׁאֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לִשְׁתּוֹתוֹ כִּי אִם בְּקֹשִׁי, אֲבָל כַּאן: "אֵין אוֹנֵס":<br>
**8 [English]**	**according to the law.** Because there are feasts in which they coerce those seated to drink [the contents] of a large vessel, and some can drink it only with difficulty, but here, no one coerced [anyone].

**8 [Hebrew]**	**יִסַּד.** לְשׁוֹן יְסוֹד, כְּלוֹמַר, כֵּן תִּקֵּן וְצִוָּה:<br>
**8 [English]**	**ordained.** Heb. יִסַד, an expression referring to a foundation (יְסוֹד); i.e., so he instituted and ordained.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**עַל כָּל רַב בֵּיתוֹ.** עַל כָּל שָׂרֵי הַסְּעוּדָה, שַׂר הָאוֹפִים וְשַׂר הַטַּבָּחִים וְשַׂר הַמַּשְׁקִים:<br>
**8 [English]**	**upon every steward of his house.** upon all the stewards of the feast: the chief baker, the chief butcher, and the chief butler.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**לַעֲשׂוֹת כִּרְצוֹן אִישׁ וָאִישׁ.** לְכָל אֶחָד וְאֶחָד רְצוֹנוֹ:<br>
**8 [English]**	**to do according to every man’s wish.** Heb. אִישּׁ וָאִישּׁ, lit. man and man, for each one his desire.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי.** רַבּוֹתֵינוּ אָמְרוּ: שַׁבָּת הָיָה:<br>
**10 [English]**	**On the seventh day.** Our Rabbis said that it was the Sabbath.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַתְּמָאֵן הַמַּלְכָּה וַשְׁתִּי.** רַבּוֹתֵינוּ אָמְרוּ: לְפִי שֶׁפָּרְחָה בָהּ צָרַעַת כְּדֵי שֶׁתְּמָאֵן וְתֵהָרֵג. לְפִי שֶׁהָיְתָה מַפְשֶׁטֶת בְּנוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל עֲרֻמּוֹת וְעוֹשָׂה בָהֶן מְלָאכָה בַּשַּׁבָּת, נִגְזַר עָלֶיהָ שֶׁתִּפָּשֵׁט עֲרֻמָּה בַּשַּׁבָּת:<br>
**12 [English]**	**But Queen Vashti refused.** Our Rabbis said (Meg. 12b): Because leprosy broke out on her, in order that she should refuse and be killed. Because she would force Jewish girls to undress, and work on the Sabbath, it was decreed that she should be stripped naked on the Sabbath.

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקְצֹף.** שֶׁשָּׁלְחָה לוֹ דִבְרֵי גְנַאי:<br>
**12 [English]**	**became very wroth.** because she sent him shameful words.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי כֵן דְּבַר הַמֶּלֶךְ.** כִּי כֵן מִנְהַג הַמֶּלֶךְ בְּכָל מִשְׁפָּט לָשׂוּם אֶת הַדָּבָר "לִפְנֵי כָּל יוֹדְעֵי דָּת וָדִין":<br>
**13 [English]**	**for so was the king’s custom.** For it was the king’s custom in every judgment to present the matter before all who knew law and judgment.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַקָּרֹב אֵלָיו.** לַעֲרֹךְ דְּבָרָיו לִפְנֵיהֶם. אֵלּוּ הֵם: כַּרְשְׁנָא שֵׁתָר, וגו':<br>
**14 [English]**	**And the nearest to him.** to present his words before them were the following: Carshena, Shethar, etc.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**כְּדָת מַה לַּעֲשׂוֹת.** מוּסָב עַל "וַיֹּאמֶר הַמֶּלֶךְ לַחֲכָמִים":<br>
**15 [English]**	**According to the law what [is there] to do.** This refers back to “And the king said to the wise men.”



**16 [Hebrew]**	**עָוְתָה.** לְשׁוֹן עָו‍ֹן:<br>
**16 [English]**	**has… done wrong.** Heb. עָוְתֳה, an expression of iniquity (עָוֹן).



**17 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי יֵצֵא דְבַר הַמַּלְכָּה עַל כָּל הַנָּשִׁים.** זֶה שֶׁבִּזְּתָה אֶת הַמֶּלֶךְ עַל כָּל הַנָּשִׁים לְהַבְזוֹת אַף הֵן אֶת בַּעֲלֵיהֶן:<br>
**17 [English]**	**For the word of the queen will spread to all the women.** that she disgraced the king- [this will cause] all the women to despise their husbands as well.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**תּאמַרְנָה שָׂרוֹת פָּרַס וּמָדַי וגו'.** לְכֹל שָׂרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ אֶת הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה. וַהֲרֵי זֶה מִקְרָא קָצֵר:<br>
**18 [English]**	**the princesses of Persia and Media, etc. will say.** this word to all the princes; this is an ellipsis.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**וּכְדַי בִּזָּיוֹן.** וְיֵשׁ בַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה הַרְבֵּה בִזָּיוֹן וָקָצֶף:<br>
**18 [English]**	**and much contempt and wrath.** And in this matter, there is much contempt and wrath.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**דְבַר מַלְכוּת.** גְּזֵרַת מַלְכוּת שֶׁל נְקָמָה שֶׁצִּוָּה לְהָרְגָהּ:<br>
**19 [English]**	**a royal edict.** a royal edict of revenge, i.e., that he commanded to execute her.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְיִכָּתֵב בְּדָתֵי פָרַס וּמָדַי.** בְּסִפְרֵי חֹק וּמִנְהַג הַמַּלְכוּת:<br>
**19 [English]**	**and let it be inscribed in the laws of Persia and Media.** in the books of the statutes and the customs of the kingdom.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא יַעֲבוֹר.** חוֹק זֶה מִבֵּינֵיהֶם שֶׁיְּהֵא זֶה חוֹק וְדָת לְכָל הַבּוֹזָה אֶת בַּעְלָהּ:<br>
**19 [English]**	**and let it not be revoked.** this statute from among them; this should be a statute and a law for anyone who shows contempt for her husband.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא תָבוֹא וַשְׁתִּי.** וּלְכַךְ נֶהֶרְגָה:<br>
**19 [English]**	**that Vashti did not come.** and therefore, she was executed.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**וּמְדַבֵּר כִּלְשׁוֹן עַמּוֹ.** כּוֹפֶה אֶת אִשְׁתּוֹ לִלְמֹד אֶת לְשׁוֹנוֹ אִם הִיא בַּת לָשׁוֹן אַחֵר:<br>
**22 [English]**	**and speak according to the language of his nationality.** He can compel his wife to learn his language if her native tongue is different.
