**1**	**he remembered Vashti.** her beauty, and he became sad.



**3**	**And let the king appoint commissioners.** since each commissioner is familiar with the beautiful women in his province.

**3**	**their ointments.** These are things that cleanse, like (Lev. 6:21): “it shall be scoured (וּמֹרַק) and rinsed.” [This includes] perfumed oil, various ingredients and spices that cleanse and smoothen the skin.



**5**	**a Judean man.** because he was exiled with the exile of Judah; all those who were exiled with the kings of Judah were called יְהוּדִים [Judeans, Jews] among the nations, even if they were from another tribe.

**5**	**a Benjamite.** He was from Benjamin. That is the simple meaning, but our Sages explained what they explained.



**7**	**for a daughter.** Heb. לְבַת. Our Sages explained: לבית, for a house, meaning for a wife.



**9**	**and he hastened her ointments.** He was more diligent and quicker with bringing her [ointments] than those of all of them.

**9**	**fitting to give to her.** to serve her, and so they would do for all of them; and our Sages explained what they explained.

**10**	**not to reveal.** so that they should say that she was from an ignoble family and dismiss her, for if they knew that she was of the family of King Saul, they would detain her.



**11**	**and what would be done to her.** He was one of two righteous men to whom a hint of salvation was given: David and Mordecai. David-as it is said (I Sam. 17:36): “Both the lion and the bear has your bondsman smote.” He said [to himself], “This incident happened to me only to teach me to have faith that I can battle with this one [Goliath].” Likewise, Mordecai said [to himself], “The only reason that this righteous woman was taken to the bed of a gentile was because she is destined to arise and save Israel.” He therefore went around to find out what would be her fate.



**12**	**turn.** Heb. תֹּר, her time.



**13**	**whatever she would request.** any entertainment or types of music.



**14**	**to the second house of the women.** Heb. שֵּׁנִי [Like the usual form] הַשֵּׁנִי.



**16**	**in the tenth month.** the cold season, when one body enjoys [the warmth of] another body. The Holy One, blessed be He, designated that cold season in order to endear her to him.



**17**	**more than all the women.** who had been with men, for they were also gathered.



**18**	**and he granted a release.** In her honor, he released them from the tax that was [levied] upon them.

**18**	**and gave gifts.** He sent them gifts, and everything was in order to entice her, perhaps she would tell her nationality. But nevertheless-Esther would not tell.



**20**	**Esther would not tell her lineage.** because Mordecai was sitting in the king’s gate; he was encouraging her and hinting to her about it.



**21**	**to lay a hand.** to give him poison to drink.



**22**	**And the matter became known to Mordecai.** For they were discussing their affairs before him in the Tarsean language, and they did not know that Mordecai was familiar with seventy languages, for he was one of those who sat in the Chamber of Hewn Stone.



**23**	**and it was written in the diary.** the favor that Mordecai did for the king.
