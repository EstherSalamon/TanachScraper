**1 [Hebrew]**	**זָכַר אֶת וַשְׁתִּי.** אֶת יָפְיָהּ וְנֶעֱצַב:<br>
**1 [English]**	**he remembered Vashti.** her beauty, and he became sad.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְיַפְקֵד הַמֶּלֶךְ פְּקִידִים.** לְפִי שֶׁכָּל פָּקִיד וּפָקִיד יְדוּעוֹת לוֹ נָשִׁים הַיָּפוֹת שֶׁבִּמְדִינָתוֹ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**And let the king appoint commissioners.** since each commissioner is familiar with the beautiful women in his province.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**תַּמְרֻקֵיהֶן.** הֵן דְּבָרִים הַמְצַחְצְחִין כְּמוֹ "וּמֹרַק וְשֻׁטַּף." שֶׁמֶן עָרֵב וּמִינֵי סַמָּנִים וּבְשָׂמִים הַמְטַהֲרִין וּמְעַדְּנִין אֶת הַבָּשָׂר:<br>
**3 [English]**	**their ointments.** These are things that cleanse, like (Lev. 6:21): “it shall be scoured (וּמֹרַק) and rinsed.” [This includes] perfumed oil, various ingredients and spices that cleanse and smoothen the skin.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**אִישׁ יְהוּדִי.** עַל שֶׁגָּלָה עִם גָּלוּת יְהוּדָה. כָּל אוֹתָן שֶׁגָּלוּ עִם מַלְכֵי יְהוּדָה הָיוּ קְרוּיִים "יְהוּדִים" בֵּין הַגּוֹיִם, וַאֲפִילוּ מִשֵּׁבֶט אַחֵר הֵם:<br>
**5 [English]**	**a Judean man.** because he was exiled with the exile of Judah; all those who were exiled with the kings of Judah were called יְהוּדִים [Judeans, Jews] among the nations, even if they were from another tribe.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**אִישׁ יְמִינִי.** מִבִּנְיָמִין הָיָה, כַּךְ פְּשׁוּטוֹ. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ דָרְשׁוּ מַה שֶׁדָּרְשׁוּ:<br>
**5 [English]**	**a Benjamite.** He was from Benjamin. That is the simple meaning, but our Sages explained what they explained.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**לוֹ לְבַת.** רַבּוֹתֵינוּ פֵּרְשׁוּ "לְבַיִת", לְאִשָּׁה:<br>
**7 [English]**	**for a daughter.** Heb. לְבַת. Our Sages explained: לבית, for a house, meaning for a wife.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְבַהֵל אֶת תַּמְרוּקֶיהָ.** זָרִיז וּמְמַהֵר בְּשֶׁלָּהּ, מִשֶּׁל כֻּלָּן:<br>
**9 [English]**	**and he hastened her ointments.** He was more diligent and quicker with bringing her [ointments] than those of all of them.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**הָרְאֻיוֹת לָתֶת לָהּ.** לְשָׁרְתָהּ, וְכֵן עוֹשִׂין לְכֻלָּן. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ דָרְשׁוּ מַה שֶּׁדָּרְשׁוּ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**fitting to give to her.** to serve her, and so they would do for all of them; and our Sages explained what they explained.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְשַׁנֶּהָ.** שִׁנָּה אוֹתָהּ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**.**



**10 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא תַגִּיד.** כְּדֵי שֶׁיֹּאמְרוּ שֶׁהִיא מִמִּשְׁפָּחָה בְזוּיָה וִישַׁלְּחוּהָ, שֶׁאִם יֵדְעוּ שֶׁהִיא מִמִּשְׁפַּחַת שָׁאוּל הַמֶּלֶךְ הָיוּ מַחֲזִיקִים בָּהּ:<br>
**10 [English]**	**not to reveal.** so that they should say that she was from an ignoble family and dismiss her, for if they knew that she was of the family of King Saul, they would detain her.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**וּמַה יֵּעָשֶׂה בָּהּ.** זֶה אֶחָד מִשְּׁנֵי צַדִּיקִים שֶׁנִּתַּן לָהֶם רֶמֶז יְשׁוּעָה: דָּוִד וּמָרְדְּכַי. דָּוִד, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "גַּם אֶת הָאֲרִי גַּם אֶת הַדּוֹב הִכָּה עַבְדֶּךָ". אָמַר: "לֹא בָא לְיָדִי דָבָר זֶה אֶלָּא לִסְמֹךְ עָלָיו לְהִלָּחֵם עִם זֶה". וְכֵן מָרְדְּכַי אָמַר: "לֹא אֵרַע לְצַדֶּקֶת זוּ שֶׁתִּלָּקַח לְמִשְׁכַּב נָכְרִי אֶלָּא שֶׁעֲתִידָה לָקוּם לְהוֹשִׁיעַ לְיִשְׂרָאֵל". לְפִיכָךְ, הָיָה מְחַזֵּר לָדַעַת מַה יְּהֵא בְסוֹפָהּ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**and what would be done to her.** He was one of two righteous men to whom a hint of salvation was given: David and Mordecai. David-as it is said (I Sam. 17:36): “Both the lion and the bear has your bondsman smote.” He said [to himself], “This incident happened to me only to teach me to have faith that I can battle with this one [Goliath].” Likewise, Mordecai said [to himself], “The only reason that this righteous woman was taken to the bed of a gentile was because she is destined to arise and save Israel.” He therefore went around to find out what would be her fate.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**תּר.** זְמַן:<br>
**12 [English]**	**turn.** Heb. תֹּר, her time.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**כָּל אֲשֶׁר תּאמַר.** כָּל שְׂחוֹק וּמִינֵי זֶמֶר:<br>
**13 [English]**	**whatever she would request.** any entertainment or types of music.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל בֵּית הַנָּשִׁים שֵׁנִי.** הַשֵּׁנִי:<br>
**14 [English]**	**to the second house of the women.** Heb. שֵּׁנִי [Like the usual form] הַשֵּׁנִי.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**בַּחֹדֶשׁ הָעֲשִׂירִי.** עֵת צִנָּה שֶׁהַגּוּף נֶהֱנֶה מִן הַגּוּף. זִמֵּן הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא אוֹתוֹ עֵת צִנָּה כְּדֵי לְחַבְּבָהּ עָלָיו:<br>
**16 [English]**	**in the tenth month.** the cold season, when one body enjoys [the warmth of] another body. The Holy One, blessed be He, designated that cold season in order to endear her to him.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**מִכָּל הַנָּשִׁים.** הַבְּעוּלוֹת, שֶׁאַף נָשִׁים הַבְּעוּלוֹת קִבֵּץ:<br>
**17 [English]**	**more than all the women.** who had been with men, for they were also gathered.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**וַהֲנָחָה לַמְּדִינוֹת עָשָׂה.** לִכְבוֹדָהּ הֵנִיחַ לָהֶם מִן הַמַּס שֶׁעֲלֵיהֶם:<br>
**18 [English]**	**and he granted a release.** In her honor, he released them from the tax that was [levied] upon them.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּתֵּן מַשְׂאֵת.** שָׁלַח דּוֹרוֹנוֹת לָהֶם. וְהַכֹּל כְּדֵי לְפַתּוֹתָהּ אוּלַי תַּגִּיד מוֹלַדְתָּהּ. וְאַף עַל פִּי כֵן:<br>
**18 [English]**	**and gave gifts.** He sent them gifts, and everything was in order to entice her, perhaps she would tell her nationality. But nevertheless-Esther would not tell.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**אֵין אֶסְתֵּר מַגֶּדֶת מוֹלַדְתָּהּ.** לְפִי שֶׁמָּרְדְּכַי יוֹשֵׁב בְּשַׁעַר הַמֶּלֶךְ, הַמְזָרְזָהּ וְהַמְרַמְּזָהּ עַל כָּךְ:<br>
**20 [English]**	**Esther would not tell her lineage.** because Mordecai was sitting in the king’s gate; he was encouraging her and hinting to her about it.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**לִשְׁלֹחַ יָד.** לְהַשְׁקוֹתוֹ סַם הַמָּוֶת:<br>
**21 [English]**	**to lay a hand.** to give him poison to drink.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּוָּדַע הַדָּבָר לְמָרְדְּכַי.** שֶׁהָיוּ מְסַפְּרִים דִּבְרֵיהֶם לְפָנָיו בְּלָשׁוֹן טוּרְסִי, וְאֵין יוֹדְעִים שֶׁהָיָה מָרְדְּכַי מַכִּיר בְּשִׁבְעִים לְשׁוֹנוֹת שֶׁהָיָה מִיּוֹשְׁבֵי לִשְׁכַּת הַגָּזִית:<br>
**22 [English]**	**And the matter became known to Mordecai.** For they were discussing their affairs before him in the Tarsean language, and they did not know that Mordecai was familiar with seventy languages, for he was one of those who sat in the Chamber of Hewn Stone.



**23 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּכָּתֵב בְּסֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים.** הַטּוֹבָה שֶׁעָשָׂה מָרְדְּכַי לַמֶּלֶךְ:<br>
**23 [English]**	**and it was written in the diary.** the favor that Mordecai did for the king.
