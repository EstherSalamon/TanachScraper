**3 [Hebrew]**	**לְרֹב אֶחָיו.** וְלֹא לְכָל אֶחָיו, מְלַמֵּד שֶׁפֵּרְשׁוּ מִמֶּנּוּ מִקְצַת סַנְהֶדְרִין, לְפִי שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂה קָרוֹב לַמַּלְכוּת וְהָיָה בָטֵל מִתַּלְמוּדוֹ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**and accepted by most of his brethren.** but not by all his brethren, for part of the Sanhedrin disassociated from him when he became close to the government and neglected his studies.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**לְכָל זַרְעוֹ.** מֻסָּב עַל עַמּוֹ לְכָל זֶרַע עַמּוֹ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**to all their seed.** This refers back to “all his people,” to all the seed of his people.
