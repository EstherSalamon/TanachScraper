**1**	**made an image.** He had an urge to make an image of gold.

**1**	**its width six cubits; he set it up etc..** If its thickness was only six [cubits], how could it stand at a height of sixty cubits? Said Rav Bibi: They would set it up and it would fall, until they brought all the gold of Jerusalem and poured a base around its feet, to fulfill what was said (Ezek. 7:19): “and their gold will be for a repugnant thing.”

**1**	**in the plain of Dura.** the name of a place.



**2**	**the satraps.** Aram. לַאֲחַשְּׁדַרְפְּנַיָּא dukes.

**2**	**governors.** rulers.

**2**	**the judges, the treasurers, etc..** They are all names of nations.

**2**	**the dedication of the image.** the beginning of its worship.



**4**	**And the announcement was issued aloud.** [as translated,] and the announcement was issued aloud.

**4**	**To you we are saying, O peoples.** [as translated,] To you, all peoples and nations, we are announcing the command of the king as a message from the king.



**5**	**At the time you hear the sound of the whistling horn.** Aram. קָל קַרְנָא מַשְּׁרוֹקֵיתָא, the sound of the whistling horn, like (Zech. 10:8) “I will whistle (אֶשְּׁרְקָא) to them, and I will gather them.”

**5**	**the clavichord, the harp, etc..** They are all musical instruments.

**5**	**kinds of music.** Aram. זְנֵי זְמָרָא kinds of music. (Gen. 1:11) “After its kind,” Onkelos renders: לִזְנוֹהִי.



**6**	**a fiery furnace.** a pit that is made to burn rocks into lime, and that is כִּבְשָּׁן in all the Scriptures.



**7**	**In view of this.** in view of this, that they took heed to observe the king’s command concerning this. bagpipes would be played.

**7**	**at that time.** At the time that they hear the sound of the music, which is a sign that those who prostrate themselves should come to prostrate, all peoples would fall etc.



**8**	**In view of this.** in view of this, that their enemies saw that there is a time to denounce them.

**8**	**some Chaldean men approached and denounced.** Aram. וַאֲכַלוּ קַרְצֵיהוֹן informed about them. (Lev. 19:16) “Do not go as a talebearer among your people,” is translated by Targum as קוּרצִין לָא תֵיכוּ I say that it was the custom of talebearers to eat a meal in the place where they informed, and that was a sign of the verification of their words; for at that time it was incumbent upon them to clarify their words and to verify the slander that they spoke, and that meal was called eating קוּרצִין, an expression of (Prov. 6:13) “he winks (קוֹרֵץ) with his eyes,” which is a sign of slander.



**9**	**They spoke up and said.** Aram. עֲנוֹ, they shouted. Every [expression of] עֲנִיָה in Scriptures is an expression of raising the voice.



**10**	**gave an order.** issued a decree.



**12**	**There are.** Aram. אִיתַי

**12**	**Judahite men.** of the tribe of Judah.

**12**	**these.** Aram. אִלֵּךְ

**12**	**did not take counsel to follow your decree.** Aram. עֲלָיִךְ מַלְכָּא טְעֵם לָא שָּׂמוּ. They did not care to give themselves counsel to care for your decrees.



**13**	**ordered to bring.** [as translated,] ordered to bring.

**13**	**were brought.** [as translated,] were brought.



**14**	**Is it meaningless.** Aram. הַצְדָא. Are my decrees void and desolate and an empty thing in your eyes, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, that you do not worship my god? The “hey” of הַצְדָא is used as an interrogative expression.

**14**	**that I have set up.** [as translated,] that I have set up.



**15**	**Now, behold you are destined.** Now, behold you are destined against your will to accept this upon yourselves.

**15**	**and if you do not prostrate yourselves.** and if you do not prostrate yourselves, you shall be cast.

**15**	**into.** into the midst of.

**15**	**and who is a god.** and who is a god who will save you from my hands?



**16**	**to the king, ‘Nebuchadnezzar.** Why is his name mentioned? But this is what they said to him: “If [it concerns] taking upon ourselves to pay taxes, either the tax from the crops or the head taxes, you are king over us, [but] if [it concerns] denying the Holy One, blessed be He, [you are merely] Nebuchadnezzar. In our eyes, you are a lowly person and the most despised of the people. You and a dog are equal.”

**16**	**we do not care.** We do not care to take counsel on what to answer you, for the answer is ready and fluent in our mouths.



**17**	**He can save us.** He can save us from all our troubles.

**17**	**From the burning fiery furnace and from your hands, O king, He will save us.** from the furnace and from your hands He will save.



**18**	**And if not.** And if He will not wish to save our bodies, let it be known to you, etc.



**19**	**was filled with wrath.** [as translated,] was filled with wrath.

**19**	**and the features of his face changed.** [as translated,] and the features of his face changed.

**19**	**to heat the furnace.** to light the fiery furnace and to heat it.

**19**	**seven times as much as it should be heated.** instead of once, seven times as much as they calculated to heat it up; and it was as though they would kindle it seven times as much as it required.

**19**	**to be heated.** lit. to heat it.



**20**	**certain mighty men in his army.** [as translated,] certain mighty men in his army.

**20**	**And he commanded… to bind.** He commanded to bind them because he saw that they were strengthening themselves to answer him harshly, and he said, “These are mighty”; so he commanded the mighty men in his army to bind them.



**21**	**these men.** [as translated,] these men.

**21**	**were bound in their cloaks.** They were bound in the garments in which they enwrapped themselves.

**21**	**their trousers, their robes.** types of royal raiment that they had; and although they were going forth to be judged before Nebuchadnezzar regarding their death sentence, they did not change their clothes to humble their greatness. From here the Sages learned that even in times of danger, a person should not change from the ruling position to which he has become accustomed.

**21**	**their robes.** Aram. וְכַרְבְּלָתְהוֹן. There is a word similar to this (in I Chron. 15:27): "And David was clothed (מְכֻרְבָּל).

**21**	**and they were cast into the burning, fiery furnce.** [as translated,] and they were cast into the burning, fiery furnace.



**22**	**In view of this.** in view of this, that the king’s command was strong.

**22**	**and the furnace was heated excessively.** and the furnace was heated to a great degree.

**22**	**these men.** these mighty men who had bound Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah and had cast them into the furnace.

**22**	**were killed by a spark of fire.** The power of a spark of fire burned them.



**23**	**bound.** [as translated,] bound.



**24**	**was bewildered.** Aram. תְּוַהּ, wondered.

**24**	**and stood up in haste.** Aram. וְקָם בְּהִתְבְּהָלָה.

**24**	**answered and said.** Aram. עָנַיִן וְאָמְרִין They answered and said to the king.

**24**	**The king is true.** Aram. יַצִיבָא מַלְכָּא The king has spoken the truth.



**25**	**free men walking in the midst of the fire.** freed from their bonds and walking in the midst of the fire.

**25**	**and there is no wound upon them.** [as translated,] and there is no wound upon them.

**25**	**and the form of the fourth.** [as translated,] and the form of the fourth.

**25**	**is like [that of] an angel.** It is like the angel I saw when I was with Sannecherib, when his armies were burned, as it is said (II Kings 19:35): “[And an angel of the Lord went forth] and slew… of the camp of Assyria.” And Nebuchadnezzar was there and fled among the ten who fled from them.



**26**	**approached the gate of the… fiery furnace.** He approached the gate of the court, in which the furnace was situated, to speak with them, and at first, he saw them from afar.

**26**	**Step out and come.** “step out and come,” but he did not say to them, “step out and ascend.” From here we learn that the floor of the furnace floated up and became even with the ground, so that they should not be troubled to go up.

**26**	**Then… came out.** Although they were confident of the miracle that had been performed for them, they did not dare to treat the king lightly, to go out without permission.



**27**	**And the satraps… gathered.** Four nations were counted above in the first gathering who were not counted here: Adargazeria, Dethaveria, Gedaveria, Tiftaei. They are the mighty men whom the spark of fire had killed when they cast Hananiah and his colleagues into the fire.

**27**	**saw these men.** saw these men: Hananiah and his colleagues.

**27**	**that the fire had taken no effect on their bodies.** [as translated,] that the fire had taken no effect on their bodies.

**27**	**on their bodies.** בְּגֶשְּׁמְהוֹן, their bodies, in Aramaic, and there are many [instances of it] in this Book.

**27**	**the hair of their heads was not singed.** [as translated,] the hair of their heads was not singed.

**27**	**their robes were not changed.** They did not change from their appearance.

**27**	**and the smell of fire had not been absorbed by them.** Aram. לָא עֲדַת בְּהוֹן, did not pass into their midst.



**28**	**Nebuchadnezzar cried out.** [as translated,] Nebuchadnezzar cried out.

**28**	**who trusted him.** [as translated,] who trusted him.

**28**	**deviated from the command of the king.** They deviated from the king’s command by not fulfilling it.

**28**	**and risked their lives.** lit. delivered their bodies.

**28**	**except to their God.** [as translated,] except to their God.



**29**	**Now an order is issued by me.** [as translated,] Now an order is issued by me.

**29**	**that will speak amiss.** that will speak in error, i.e., any disrespectful word.

**29**	**limb from limb.** Aram הַּדָּמִין limbs; i.e., they will cut him into limbs.

**29**	**shall be made a dungheap.** [as translated,] shall be made a dungheap.

**29**	**that can save in this manner.** [as translated,] that can save in this manner.



**30**	**made… prosper.** made them prosper and aggrandized them.



**31**	**King Nebuchadnezzar [says] to all peoples.** This is the text of the missive: King Nebuchadnezzar greets all peoples.



**32**	**it behooves me to recite.** It is good for me to tell.
