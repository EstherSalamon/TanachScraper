**1**	**Will not wisdom call out.** Does not the Torah announce for you the things mentioned below in this section?



**3**	**at the entrance of the roof.** Heb. קרת, the ceiling above the gate, where people sit.

**3**	**she cries.** Heb. תרנה, she cries, and what does she say? “To you, O men, I call.”



**6**	**Hearken for I will speak noble things.** Words of nobility and importance.



**8**	**there is nothing twisted or crooked in them.** There is no crookedness.



**10**	**gold.** Heb. חרוץ. A type of gold.



**11**	**than pearls.** Heb. מפנינים, pearls.

**11**	**cannot be compared to it.** They will not equal its value.



**12**	**I dwelt [beside] cunning.** Beside cunning, for since a man has learned Torah, cunning about every matter enters into him.



**13**	**Fear of the Lord is to hate evil.** This is the discipline that wisdom announces to the people.



**15**	**Kings reign with me.** for the judges and the judgments I teach them.



**17**	**I will love those who love me.** Heb. אהבי. [This is the reading.] The text is written: אוהביה, those who love her. Said the Holy One, blessed be He: “I will love those who love the Torah.” I heard this from Rabbi Aaron in the name of Rabbi Nathan.

**17**	**I will love.** Heb. אהב, like אאהב.

**17**	**will find me.** Heb. ימצאנני. There is an extra “nun,” denoting fifty. I will allow him to find the fifty gates of understanding.



**21**	**There is substance to give inheritance.** Heb. יש, lit. there is. There is with me a great inheritance.



**22**	**at the beginning of His way.** before the creation of the world.



**23**	**I was enthroned, from the beginning.** Heb. נסכתי, an expression of (Ezek. 35): “Princes (נסיכי) of the sons of man.”



**24**	**I was created.** Heb. חוללתי.



**25**	**before the mountains were sunk.** within the water.



**26**	**the land and the outsides.** The land of Israel and other lands.

**26**	**and the beginning of the dust of the earth.** The first man.



**27**	**when He drew a circle over the face of the deep.** When He drew the circle of the earth over the water, to draw a boundary that it may not pass. חוג is an expression of encircling, as in (Isa. 44:13): “and with a compass (ובמחוגה) he rounds it.” (Compas in French, zirkel in German, as in Gen. 29:17, Isa. 5:22, 44:13, and Job 22:14.)



**28**	**when He strengthened the fountains of the deep.** When He made mighty the fountains of the deep.



**29**	**when He gave the sea its boundary.** and decreed upon the Reed Sea when He created it, on the condition that it split before Moses.

**29**	**when He established the foundations of the earth.** Heb. בחקו from an expression of engraving (חקוי), as in (Isa. 49:16): “Behold on [My] hands I have engraved you (חקתיך),” and so (Ezek. 43:14): “And from the bottom (מחיק) upon the ground.”



**30**	**a nursling.** Heb. אמון, one that was reared beside Him, an expression of (Lam. 4:5): "They that were reared (האמנים) amid crimson.

**30**	**every day.** יום יום, lit. a day a day, two thousand years.



**31**	**playing in the habitable world of His earth.** All the generations of the wicked that were from Adam to Noah and from Noah to Abraham, I was laughing at them.

**31**	**and [having] my delights.** I waited until the generation of the desert came and accepted me.



**33**	**and do not put it to naught.** Heb. תפרעו, and do not put my discipline to naught.



**34**	**to watch.** Heb. לשקד

**34**	**by my doors.** [to be the] first to enter into the study hall and the synagogue and [the] last to leave.
