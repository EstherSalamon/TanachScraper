**4**	**with stringed instrument and flute.** Heb. במנים ועגב. They are kinds of musical instruments.



**5**	**with resounding cymbals.** Heb. בצלצלי שמע, cymbals that let a sound be heard. שָׁמַע is a noun, like שֶׁמַע, with a “segol,” but because of the “ethnactha” [which denotes a pause], it is vowelized with a long “kamatz,” and therefore, its accent is above, under the “shin.”
