**9**	**to the young ravens.** Our Sages explained that he [the raven] is cruel to his offspring, and the Holy One, blessed be He, has pity on them and prepares for them gnats from their excrement, which enter their mouths.



**10**	**He does not desire the might of the horse.** [i.e.,] the Holy One, blessed be He.

**10**	**nor does He take pleasure in the legs of man.** nor does He want the swiftness of the running of the legs of man.



**14**	**the best of the wheat.** Heb. חלב, lit. fat. The best, fat wheat kernels.



**16**	**hoarfrost.** Heb. כפור, jelede in Old French.



**17**	**He hurls His ice like crumbs.** The water congeals and becomes crumbs. [According to] Midrash Aggadah like crumbs Everything [is] according to the burden of the people: the poor man, according to his lack of clothing, He is lenient with him:

**17**	**who can stand.** that he should not become chilled from His cold?



**18**	**He sends His word and melts them.** those crumbs.

**18**	**He blows His wind.** The west wind, to get rid of the ice, and water runs.
