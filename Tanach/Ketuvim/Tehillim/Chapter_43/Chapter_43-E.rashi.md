**1**	**Avenge me.** Heb. שפטני, lit. judge me.

**1**	**against an unkind nation.** That is Edom (Ishmael - based on a different version of Rashi's commentary), who dwelt between two righteous men [Abraham and Isaac] and did not learn from their deeds.



**3**	**Send Your light and Your truth.** The King Messiah, who is compared to light, as it is stated (below 132:17): “I have set up a lamp for my anointed,” and Elijah the prophet, who is true, a faithful prophet.

**3**	**that they may lead me.** Heb. ינחוני, menoront moi in old French, they shall lead me.



**4**	**to the God.** To the Holy One, blessed be He, who is the joy of my exultation.

**4**	**and I will thank You with a lyre.** because You took me out of my exile.
