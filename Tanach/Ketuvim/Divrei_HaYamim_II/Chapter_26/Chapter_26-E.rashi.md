**4**	**And he did what was right in the eyes of the Lord.** like all the proper things that his father did, but not like his wickedness.



**5**	**in the days of Zechariah.** This refers to Uzziah, and he was called by three names: Uzziah, Zechariah, and Azariah, similar to (II Kings 14): Yehoahaz/Ahaziah.

**5**	**who understood the visions of God.** who understood that the fear of God is good, and feared the Lord.



**9**	**And Uzziah built towers, etc..** He had to strengthen the parts of the wall they breached in his father’s days.



**10**	**plowmen.** Heb. אִכָּרִים, aratkori in Italian, plowmen.

**10**	**.**

**10**	**for he loved the soil.** It is explained in Genesis Rabbah (See 22:3): Two had a passion for agriculture, and no good was found in them: he and Cain.



**15**	**engines, invented by skillful men.** Heb. חִשְּׁבֹנוֹת מַחֲשֶׁבֶת חוֹשֵׁב, similar to (Exod. 28:6): “expert work (מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב.)” He made war engines with craftsmanship, such as siege mounds and battering rams, as it is written (Prov. 24:6): “For you shall wage war with strategies.”



**16**	**and he trespassed against the Lord, his God.** Heb. וַיִּמְעַל. Here it is appropriate to say מְעִילָה (trespass), for he sinned inadvertently, similar to (Lev. 5:15): “If a person commits a trespass (תִמְעֹל מַעַל) and sins inadvertently.”

**16**	**and he came into the Temple of the Lord.** for he said, “It is proper for a king to serve the King of glory.”



**19**	**and in his hand was a censer to burn.** and he wished to strike the priest who prevented him from burning incense.



**21**	**in a house of retirement.** Heb. בֵּית הַחָפְשִׁית. And in Yerushalmi (unknown) it is explained that he made himself a house in the cemetery, reminiscent of (Ps. 88:6): “Among the dead, a free man (חָפְשִׁי).”

**21**	**for it had been decreed.** upon him to come no longer among people, similar to (Esther 2:1): “and that which was decreed upon her.”



**23**	**in the cemetery.** but not in the cave with the kings.
