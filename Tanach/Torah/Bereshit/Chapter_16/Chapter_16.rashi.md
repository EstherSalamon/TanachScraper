**1 [Hebrew]**	**שׁפחה מִצְרִית.** בַּת פַּרְעֹה הָיְתָה, כְּשֶׁרָאָה נִסִּים שֶׁנַּעֲשׂוּ לְשָׂרָה אָמַר: מוּטָב שֶׁתְּהֵא בִתִּי שִׁפְחָה בְּבַיִת זֶה וְלֹא גְבִירָה בְּבַיִת אַחֵר:<br>
**1 [English]**	**an Egyptian handmaid.** She was Pharaoh’s daughter. When he (Pharaoh) saw the miracles that were wrought for Sarah, he said, “It is better that my daughter be a handmaid in this household, than a mistress in another household.” - [from Gen. Rabbah 45:1]



**2 [Hebrew]**	**אוּלַי אִבָּנֶה מִמֶּנָּה.** לִמֵּד עַל מִי שֶׁאֵין לוֹ בָּנִים שֶׁאֵינוֹ בָנוּי אֶלָּא הָרוּס:<br>
**2 [English]**	**perhaps I will be built up from her.** This teaches that whoever has no children is not built up but demolished. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:2]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**אִבָּנֶה מִמֶּנָּה.** בִּזְכוּת שֶׁאַכְנִיס צָרָתִי לְתוֹךְ בֵּיתִי:<br>
**2 [English]**	**I will be built up from her.** in the merit that I will bring my rival into my house. — [from Gen. Rabbah 71:7, Aggadath Bereishith 52]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**לְקוֹל שָׂרַי.** לְרוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ שֶׁבָּהּ (בראשית רבה):<br>
**2 [English]**	**to Sarai’s voice.** to the Divine Spirit within her. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:2]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**וַתִּקַּח שָׂרַי.** לְקָחַתָּה בִּדְבָרִים, אַשְׁרַיִךְ שֶׁזָּכִית לִדָּבֵק בְּגוּף קָדוֹשׁ כָּזֶה (בראשית רבה):<br>
**3 [English]**	**So Sarai, Abram’s wife, took.** She took her with words, “You are fortunate that you have merited to cleave to a holy body such as this one.” - [from Gen. Rabbah 45:3]

**3 [Hebrew]**	**מִקֵּץ עֶשֶׂר שָׁנִים.** מוֹעֵד הַקָּבוּעַ לְאִשָּׁה שֶׁשָּׁהֲתָה עֶשֶׂר שָׁנִים וְלֹא יָלְדָה לְבַעֲלָהּ, חַיָּב לִשָֹּא אַחֶרֶת:<br>
**3 [English]**	**at the end of ten years.** This is the allotted time for a woman who has lived ten years with her husband and has not borne children to him, when he is obligated to marry another. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:3]

**3 [Hebrew]**	**לְשֶׁבֶת אַבְרָם וגו'.** מַגִּיד שֶׁאֵין יְשִׁיבַת חוּצָה לָאָרֶץ עוֹלָה לוֹ מִן הַמִּנְיָן (יב' ס"ד), לְפִי שֶׁלֹּא נֶאֱמַר לוֹ וְאֶעֶשְׂךָ לְגוֹי גָּדוֹל, עַד שֶׁיָּבֹא לְאֶרֶץ יִשְֹרָאֵל:<br>
**3 [English]**	**of Abram’s dwelling.** This tells us that the time they dwelled outside the Land does not count in the number [ten years], because it was not said to him, “and I will make you into a great nation,” [i.e., this promise would not be fulfilled] until he would come to the Land of Israel. — [from above source]



**4 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּבֹא אֶל־הָגָר וַתַּהַר.** מִבִּיאָה רִאשׁוֹנָה (בראשית רבה):<br>
**4 [English]**	**And he came to Hagar, and she conceived.** from the first union. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:4]

**4 [Hebrew]**	**וַתֵּקַל גְּבִרְתָּהּ בְּעֵינֶֽיהָ.** אָמְרָה שָׂרַי זוֹ אֵין סִתְרָהּ כִּגְלוּיָהּ מַרְאָה עַצְמָהּ כְּאִלּוּ הִיא צַדֶּקֶת וְאֵינָהּ צַדֶּקֶת, שֶׁלֹּא זָכְתָה לְהֵרָיוֹן כָּל הַשָּׁנִים הַלָּלוּ, וַאֲנִי נִתְעַבַּרְתִּי מִבִּיאָה רִאשׁוֹנָה (בראשית רבה):<br>
**4 [English]**	**and her mistress became unimportant in her eyes.** She said, “This Sarai her conduct in secret is not like her conduct in public. She shows herself as if she is a righteous woman, but she is not a righteous woman, for she did not merit to conceive all these years, whereas I have conceived from the first union.” - [from aforementioned source]



**5 [Hebrew]**	**חֲמָסִי עָלֶיךָ.** חָמָס הֶעָשׂוּי לִי, עָלֶיךָ אֲנִי מֵטִיל הָעֹנֶשׁ; כְּשֶׁהִתְפַּלַּלְתָּ לְהַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא מַה תִּתֶּן לִי וְאָנֹכִי הוֹלֵךְ עֲרִירִי, לֹא הִתְפַּלַּלְתָּ אֶלָּא עָלֶיךָ, וְהָיָה לְךָ לְהִתְפַּלֵּל עַל שְׁנֵינוּ, וְהָיִיתִי אֲנִי נִפְקֶדֶת עִמְּךָ, וְעוֹד דְּבָרֶיךָ אַתָּה חוֹמֵס מִמֶּנִי, שֶׁאַתָּה שׁוֹמֵעַ בִּזְיוֹנִי וְשׁוֹתֵק (בראשית רבה):<br>
**5 [English]**	**May my injustice be upon you.** [For] the injustice that has been done to me, I lay the punishment upon you. When you prayed to God, “What will You give me, since I am going childless?” you prayed only for yourself, whereas you should have prayed for both of us, and I would have been remembered with you. Moreover, you are stealing from me your [protective] words, for you hear my degradation, and you remain silent (i.e., you are depriving me of the words you should have spoken to Hagar to reprimand her on my behalf). — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:5]

**5 [Hebrew]**	**אָֽנֹכִי נָתַתִּי שִׁפְחָתִי וגו' בֵּינִי וּבֵינֶֽיֹךָ.** כָּל בֵּינֶיךָ שֶׁבַּמִּקְרָא חָסֵר, וְזֶה מָלֵא, קְרִי בֵיהּ וּבָנַיִךְ, שֶׁהִכְנִיסָה עַיִן הָרָע בְּעִבּוּרָהּ שֶׁל הָגָר וְהִפִּילָה עֻבָּרָהּ; הוּא שֶׁהַמַּלְאָךְ אוֹמֵר לְהָגָר הִנָּךְ הָרָה, וְהֲלֹא כְבָר הָרְתָה, וְהוּא מְבַשֵּׁר לָהּ שֶׁתַּהַר? אֶלָּא מְלַמֵּד שֶׁהִפִּילָה הֵרָיוֹן הָרִאשׁוֹן:<br>
**5 [English]**	**I gave my handmaid, etc. between me and you.** Every בֶּינֶיךָ in Scripture is spelled defectively (without the second yud), but this one is spelled plene. It may thus also be read וּבֵינַיִךְ (second person feminine), for she cast an evil eye on Hagar’s pregnancy, and she miscarried her fetus. That is why the angel said to Hagar, “Behold, you will conceive.” But was she not already pregnant? Yet he announces to her that she will conceive? But this teaches that she miscarried her first pregnancy. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:5]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וַתְּעַנֶּהָ שׁרי.** הָיְתָה מְשַׁעְבֶּדֶת בָּהּ בְּקֹשִׁי (בראשית רבה):<br>
**6 [English]**	**And Sarai afflicted her.** She enslaved her harshly. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:6]



**8 [Hebrew]**	**מִזֶּה בָאת.** מֵהֵיכָן בָּאת? יוֹדֵע הָיָה, אֶלָּא לִתֵּן לָהּ פֶּתַח לִכָּנֵס עִמָּה בִּדְבָרִים. וּלְשׁוֹן אֵי מִזֶּה: אַיֵּה הַמָּקוֹם שֶׁתֹּאמַר עָלָיו מִזֶּה אֲנִי בָאָה?<br>
**8 [English]**	**where are you coming from.** [meaning]: “Where have you come from?” He knew [where she was coming from] but he wished to give her an opening to commence speaking with her. Now the אֵי מִזֶּה [lit. where from this] means: “Where is the place about which you can say, ‘From this place I have come.’”



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמֶר לָהּ מַלְאַךְ וגו'.** עַל כָּל אֲמִירָה הָיָה שָׁלוּחַ לָהּ מַלְאָךְ אַחֵר, לְכָךְ נֶאֱמַר מַלְאָךְ בְּכָל אֲמִירָה וַאֲמִירָה:<br>
**9 [English]**	**And the angel of the Lord said to her, etc..** For each statement, another angel was sent to her. Therefore, the word מַלְאָךְ, angel, is used with each statement. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:7]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**הִנָּךְ הָרָה.** כְּשֶׁתָּשׁוּבִי תַּהֲרִי, כְּמוֹ הִנָּךְ הָרָה דְאֵשֶׁת מָנוֹחַ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**Behold, you will conceive.** When you return, you will conceive, like (Jud. 13:5): Behold you shall conceive, stated concerning the wife of Manoah.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְיֹלַדְתְּ בֵּן.** כְּמוֹ וְיוֹלֶדֶת, וְדוֹמֶה לוֹ יֹשַׁבְתְּי בַּלְּבָנוֹן מְקֻנַּנְתְּי בָּאֲרָזִים (ירמיה כ"ב):<br>
**11 [English]**	**and bear a son.** וְיֹלַדְתְּ is וְיוֹלֶדֶת, and similar to this (Jer. 22:23): You, who abide (ישַׁבְתְּ) in the Lebanon, (יוֹשֶׁבֶת) who nest (מְקוּנַנְתְּ) in the cedars [like, מְקוֹנֶנֶת].

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְקָרָאת שְׁמוֹ.** צִוּוּי הוּא, כְּמוֹ שֶׁאוֹמֵר לְזָכָר וְקָרָאתָ אֶת שְׁמוֹ יִצְחָק (בר' י"ז):<br>
**11 [English]**	**and you shall name him.** This is the imperative [feminine], as the text states for the masculine: (below 17:19): “and you shall name him (וְקָראתָ אֶת שְׁמוֹ) Isaac.”



**12 [Hebrew]**	**פֶּרֶא אָדָם.** אוֹהֵב מִדְבָּרוֹת לָצוּד חַיּוֹת, כְּמוֹ שֶׁכָּתוּב וַיֵּשֶׁב בַּמִּדְבָּר וַיְהִי רֹבֶה קַשָּׁת (בראשית כ"א):<br>
**12 [English]**	**A wild donkey of a man.** who loves the wilderness to hunt beasts, as it is written (below 21:20f): “And he was an archer; and he dwelt in the desert of Paran.”

**12 [Hebrew]**	**יָדוֹ בַכֹּל.** לִסְטִים:<br>
**12 [English]**	**his hand will be upon all.** [He will be] a bandit. — [from Tan. Shemot]

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְיַד כֹּל בּוֹ.** הַכֹּל שׂוֹנְאִין אוֹתוֹ וּמִתְגָּרִין בּוֹ:<br>
**12 [English]**	**and everyone’s hand upon him.** Everyone will hate him and attack him.

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְעַל־פְּנֵי כֹּל אֶחָיו ישכון.** שֶׁיִּהְיֶה זַרְעוֹ גָּדוֹל (בראשית רבה):<br>
**12 [English]**	**and before all his brothers he will dwell.** for his seed will be numerous.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אַתָּה אֵל רֳאִי.** נָקוּד חֲטַף קָמָץ, מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא שֵׁם דָּבָר, אֱלוֹהַּ הָרְאִיָּה, שֶׁרוֹאֶה בְעֶלְבּוֹן שֶׁל עֲלוּבִין:<br>
**13 [English]**	**You are the God of seeing.** רֳאִי is vowelized with a “chataf kamatz” because it is a noun, i.e., the God of seeing, Who sees the humiliation of the humiliated. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:10] (Other editions: Another explanation: “You are the God of seeing” meaning that He sees all, but no one sees Him. Targum Jonathan).

**13 [Hebrew]**	**הֲגַם הֲלֹם.** לְשׁוֹן תֵּמַהּ; וְכִי סְבוּרָה הָיִיתִי שֶׁאַף הֲלוֹם בַּמִּדְבָּרוֹת רָאִיתִי שְׁלוּחוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם אַחֲרֵי רֹאִי אוֹתָם בְּבֵיתוֹ שֶׁל אַבְרָהָם שֶׁשָּׁם הָיִיתִי רְגִילָה לִרְאות מַלְאָכִים? וְתֵדַע שֶׁהָיְתָה רְגִילָה לִרְאוֹתָם, שֶׁהֲרֵי מָנוֹחַ רָאָה אֶת הַמַּלְאָךְ פַּעַם אַחַת וְאָמַר מוֹת נָמוּת (שופ' י"ג), וְזוֹ רָאֲתָה ד' זֶה אַחַר זֶה וְלֹא חָרְדָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**Have I seen here also.** הֲגַם הֲלֹם is an expression of wonderment. Would I have thought that even here in the desert I would see the emissary of the Omnipresent after I had seen them in the house of Abraham, where I was accustomed to seeing angels? And you should know that she was accustomed to seeing them, because Manoah saw the angel once and said, “We will surely die,” and this one saw four, one after the other, and she was not frightened. — [from Gen. Rabbah 45:7]



**14 [Hebrew]**	**בְּאֵר לַחַי.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ:<br>
**14 [English]**	**Be’er Lachai Ro’i.** As the Targum renders: a well upon which the living angel appeared.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקְרָא אַבְרָם שֶׁם־וגו'.** אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁלֹּא שָׁמַע אַבְרָם דִּבְרֵי הַמַּלְאָךְ שֶׁאָמַר וְקָרָאת שְׁמוֹ יִשְׁמָעֵאל, שָׁרְתָה רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ עָלָיו וּקְרָאוֹ יִשְׁמָעֵאל:<br>
**15 [English]**	**and Abram named, etc..** Although Abram had not heard the words of the angel, who said,“And you shall name him Ishmael,” the Holy Spirit rested upon him, and he called him Ishmael. — [from Bereishith Rabbathi, also Mid. Aggadah]



**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַבְרָם בֶּן־שמונים וגו'.** לְשִׁבְחוֹ שֶׁל יִשְׁמָעֵאל נִכְתַּב, לְהוֹדִיעַ שֶׁהָיָה בֶּן י"ג שָׁנָה כְּשֶׁנִּמּוֹל וְלֹא עִכֵּב:<br>
**16 [English]**	**And Abram was eighty-six years old, etc..** This was written in praise of Ishmael, to let us know that he was thirteen years old when he was circumcised, and he did not object. — [Mid. Aggadah]
