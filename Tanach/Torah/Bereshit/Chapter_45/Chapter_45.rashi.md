**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹֽא־יָכֹל יוֹסֵף לְהִתְאַפֵּק לְכֹל הַנִּצָּבִים.** לֹא הָיָה יָכוֹל לִסְבֹּל שֶׁיִּהְיוּ מִצְרִים נִצָּבִים עָלָיו וְשׁוֹמְעִין שֶׁאֶחָיו מִתְבַּיְּשִׁין בְּהִוָּדְעוֹ לָהֶם:<br>
**1 [English]**	**Now Joseph could not bear all those standing.** He could not bear that Egyptians would stand beside him and hear his brothers being embarrassed when he would make himself known to them. [From Tanchuma Vayigash 5]



**2 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׁמַע בֵּית פַּרְעֹֽה.** בֵּיתוֹ שֶׁל פַּרְעֹה, כְּלוֹמַר, עֲבָדָיו וּבְנֵי בֵיתוֹ, וְאֵין זֶה לְשׁוֹן בַּיִת מַמָּשׁ, אֶלָּא כְמוֹ בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, בֵּית יְהוּדָה, מישנ"דה בְּלַעַז:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and the house of Pharaoh heard.** Heb. פַּרְעֹה בֵּית, the house of Pharaoh, namely his servants and the members of his household. This does not literally mean a house, but it is like “the house of Israel” (Ps. 115:12), “the house of Judah” (I Kings 12:21), mesnede in Old French, household. [From Targum Onkelos]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**נִבְהֲלוּ מִפָּנָֽיו.** מִפְּנֵי הַבּוּשָׁה:<br>
**3 [English]**	**they were startled by his presence.** Because of embarrassment. [From Tanchuma Vayigash 5]



**4 [Hebrew]**	**גְּשׁוּ־נָא אֵלַי.** רָאָה אוֹתָם נְסוֹגִים לְאָחוֹר, אָמַר עַכְשָׁו אַחַי נִכְלָמִים, קָרָא לָהֶם בְּלָשׁוֹן רַכָּה וְתַחֲנוּנִים, וְהֶרְאָה לָהֶם שֶׁהוּא מָהוּל (בראשית רבה):<br>
**4 [English]**	**Please come closer.** He saw them drawing backwards. He said, “Now my brothers are embarrassed” (Tanchuma Vayigash 5). He called them tenderly and pleadingly and showed them that he was circumcised (Gen. Rabbah 93:10).



**5 [Hebrew]**	**לְמִחְיָה.** לִהְיוֹת לָכֶם לְמִחְיָה:<br>
**5 [English]**	**to preserve life.** Heb. לְמִחְיָה, to be to you a preserver of life. [From Targum Jonathan]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי־זֶה שְׁנָתַיִם הָֽרָעָב.** עָבְרוּ מִשְּׁנֵי הָרָעָב:<br>
**6 [English]**	**For already two years of famine.** have passed of the [total] years of the famine.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**לְאָב.** לְחָבֵר וּלְפַטְרוֹן:<br>
**8 [English]**	**a father.** A colleague and a protector. [From Gen. Rabbah 93:10]



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽעֲלוּ אֶל־אָבִי.** אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל גְּבוֹהָה מִכָּל הָאֲרָצוֹת:<br>
**9 [English]**	**and go up to my father.** The land of Israel is higher than all [other] lands. [From Kidd. 69a, b]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**פֶּן־תִּוָּרֵשׁ.** דִּלְמָא תִּתְמַסְכַּן, לְשׁוֹן מוֹרִישׁ וּמַעֲשִׁיר:<br>
**11 [English]**	**lest you become impoverished.** Heb. פֶּן-תִּוָּרֵשׁ, [which Onkelos renders:] דָּלְמָא תִתְמַסְכַּן, lest you become impoverished, [which is] an expression similar to “impoverishes (מוֹרִישׁ) and makes rich” (I Sam. 2:7).



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִנֵּה עֵֽינֵיכֶם רֹאוֹת.** בִּכְבוֹדִי, וְשֶׁאֲנִי אֲחִיכֶם שֶׁאֲנִי מָהוּל כָּכֶם, וְעוֹד, כי פי המדבר אליכם בִּלְשׁוֹן הַקֹּדֶשׁ (בראשית רבה):<br>
**12 [English]**	**And behold, your eyes see.** my glory and that I am your brother, for I am circumcised like you, and moreover, that it is my mouth that is speaking to you in the holy tongue (Gen. Rabbah 93:10).

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְעֵינֵי אָחִי בִנְיָמִין.** הִשְׁוָה אֶת כֻּלָּם יַחַד לוֹמַר, שֶׁכְּשֵׁם שֶׁאֵין לִי שִׂנְאָה עַל בִּנְיָמִין אָחִי, שֶׁהֲרֵי לֹא הָיָה בִמְכִירָתִי, כָּךְ אֵין בְּלִבִּי שִׂנְאָה עֲלֵיכֶם:<br>
**12 [English]**	**as well as the eyes of my brother Benjamin.** He compared them all together, saying that “just as I harbor no hatred against my brother Benjamin, for he did not participate in selling me, neither do I have any hatred in my heart against you.” [from Meg. 16b]



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּפֹּל עַל־צַוְּארֵי בנימין אָחִיו וַיֵּבְךְּ.** עַל שְׁנֵי מִקְדָּשׁוֹת שֶׁעֲתִידִין לִהְיוֹת בְּחֶלְקוֹ שֶׁל בִּנְיָמִין וְסוֹפָן לֵחָרֵב:<br>
**14 [English]**	**And he fell on his brother Benjamin’s neck and wept.** for the two sanctuaries which were destined to be in Benjamin’s territory and would ultimately be destroyed. [From Meg. 16b]

**14 [Hebrew]**	**ובנימין בָּכָה עַל־צַוָּארָֽיו.** עַל מִשְׁכַּן שִׁילֹה שֶׁעָתִיד לִהְיוֹת בְּחֶלְקוֹ שֶׁל יוֹסֵף וְסוֹפוֹ לֵחָרֵב (שם):<br>
**14 [English]**	**and Benjamin wept on his neck.** for the Tabernacle of Shiloh, which was destined to be in Joseph’s territory yet would ultimately be destroyed. [From Meg. 16b, Gen. Rabbah 93:12]



**15 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַחֲרֵי כֵן.** מֵאַחַר שֶׁרָאוּהוּ בוֹכֶה וְלִבּוֹ שָׁלֵם עִמָּהֶם:<br>
**15 [English]**	**and afterwards.** After they saw him weeping and that he was wholehearted with them.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**דִּבְּרוּ אֶחָיו אִתּוֹ.** שֶׁמִּתְּחִלָּה הָיוּ בוֹשִׁים מִמֶּנּוּ:<br>
**15 [English]**	**his brothers spoke with him.** whereas previously they had felt shame before him.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַקֹּל נִשְׁמַע בֵּית פַּרְעֹה.** כְּמוֹ בְּבֵית פַּרְעֹה, וְזֶהוּ לְשׁוֹן בַּיִת מַמָּשׁ:<br>
**16 [English]**	**And the voice was heard [in] Pharaoh’s house.** Heb. בֵּית פַּרְעֹה, equivalent to בְּבֵית פַּרְעֹה, and this is an expression [denoting] an actual house. [From Targum Onkelos]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**טַֽעֲנוּ אֶת־בְּעִירְכֶם.** תְּבוּאָה:<br>
**17 [English]**	**load up your beasts.** with grain.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־טוּב אֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם.** אֶרֶץ גֹּשֶׁן, נִבָּא וְאֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ מַה נִּבָּא, סוֹפָם לַעֲשׂוֹתָהּ כִּמְצוּלָה שֶׁאֵין בָּהּ דָּגִים:<br>
**18 [English]**	**the best of the land of Egypt.** [I.e.,] the land of Goshen. He prophesied but did not know what he was prophesying. They (the Israelites) would eventually make it (Egypt) like the depths of the sea, which have no fish. [From Avoth d’Rabbi Nathan, second version, ch. 43; Ber. 9b]

**18 [Hebrew]**	**חֵלֶב הָאָֽרֶץ.** כָּל חֵלֶב לְשׁוֹן מֵיטַב הוּא:<br>
**18 [English]**	**the fat of the land.** Heb. חֵלֶב הָאָרֶץ. Every [instance of] חֵלֶב is an expression meaning the best. [From Targum Onkelos]



**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַתָּה צֻוֵּיתָה.** מִפִּי, לוֹמַר לָהֶם:<br>
**19 [English]**	**And you [Joseph] have been commanded.** By me to say to them. [From Targum Jonathan]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**זֹאת עֲשׂוּ.** כָּךְ אֱמֹר לָהֶם, שֶׁבִּרְשׁוּתִי הוּא:<br>
**19 [English]**	**Do this.** So shall you say to them, that it is in my power [to provide for you].



**23 [Hebrew]**	**שָׁלַח כְּזֹאת.** כַּחֶשְׁבּוֹן הַזֶּה, וּמַהוּ הַחֶשְׁבּוֹן? עשרה חמרים וגו':<br>
**23 [English]**	**he sent the following.** Heb. כְּזֹאת, lit., like this, [meaning:] according to this amount. And what is the amount? Ten he-donkeys, etc.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**מִטּוּב מִצְרָיִם.** מָצִינוּ בַתַּלְמוּד שֶׁשָּׁלַח לוֹ יַיִן יָשָׁן שֶׁדַּעַת זְקֵנִים נוֹחָה הֵימֶנּוּ, וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה גְּרִיסִין שֶׁל פּוּל:<br>
**23 [English]**	**of the best of Egypt.** We find in the Talmud (Meg. 16b) that he sent him aged wine because elderly people find contentment with it. [I.e., the fact that wine improves with age often affords contentment to the elderly.] According to the Midrash Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 94:2 on verse 18), however, this refers to pounded beans [which have a soothing effect on a troubled spirit].

**23 [Hebrew]**	**בָּר וָלֶחֶם.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ:<br>
**23 [English]**	**grain, bread.** As the Targum renders.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**וּמָזוֹן.** לִפְתָּן:<br>
**23 [English]**	**and [other] food.** Things eaten with bread.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**אַל־תִּרְגְּזוּ בַּדָּֽרֶךְ.** אַל תִּתְעַסְּקוּ בִּדְבַר הֲלָכָה שֶׁלֹּא תִרְגַּז עֲלֵיכֶם הַדֶּרֶךְ, דָּבָר אַחֵר אַל תַּפְסִיעוּ פְסִיעָה גַסָּה וְהִכָּנְסוּ בַחַמָּה לָעִיר; וּלְפִי פְשׁוּטוֹ שֶׁל מִקְרָא יֵשׁ לוֹמַר, לְפִי שֶׁהָיוּ נִכְלָמִים, הָיָה דוֹאֵג, שֶׁמָּא יָרִיבוּ בַדֶּרֶךְ עַל דְּבַר מְכִירָתוֹ, לְהִתְוַכֵּחַ זֶה עִם זֶה וְלוֹמַר עַל יָדְךָ נִמְכַּר, אַתָּה סִפַּרְתָּ לָשׁוֹן הָרָע עָלָיו, וְגָרַמְתָּ לָנוּ לִשְׂנֹאתוֹ:<br>
**24 [English]**	**Do not quarrel on the way.** Heb. אַל-תִּרְגְזוּ בַּדָּרֶךְ. Do not engage in a halachic discussion lest the way cause you to stray. Another explanation: Do not walk with large steps, and enter the city while the sun is shining (Ta’anith 10b). According to the simple meaning of the verse, we can say that since they were ashamed, he (Joseph) was concerned that they would perhaps quarrel on the way about his being sold, debating with one another, and saying, “Because of you he was sold. You slandered him and caused us to hate him.”



**26 [Hebrew]**	**וְכִי־הוּא משֵׁל.** וַאֲשֶׁר הוּא מוֹשֵׁל:<br>
**26 [English]**	**and [they told him] that he ruled.** Heb. וְכִי הוּא משֵׁל, and that he rules.

**26 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּפָג לִבּוֹ.** נֶחֱלַף לִבּוֹ וְהָלַךְ מִלְּהַאֲמִין, לֹא הָיָה לִבּוֹ פוֹנֶה אֶל הַדְּבָרִים, לְשׁוֹן מְפִיגִין טַעֲמָן בִּלְשׁוֹן מִשְׁנָה וּכְמוֹ מֵאֵין הֲפֻגוֹת (איכה ג'), וְרֵיחוֹ לֹא נָמָר (ירמיהו מ"ח), מְתַּרְגְּמִינַן וְרֵיחֵיהּ לָא פָג:<br>
**26 [English]**	**and his heart changed.** Heb. וַיָפָג לִבָּוֹ. His heart changed and went away from believing. His heart did not turn to [believe] these words. [וַיָפָג is] a term similar to “their taste changes” (מְפִיגִין טַעְמָן) in the language of the Mishnah (Bezah 14a), and “without respite (הֲפֻגוֹת)” (Lam. 3:49). Also “and its bouquet did not change” (Jer. 48: 11) is translated [into Aramaic] as וְרֵיחֵיה לֹא פָג.



**27 [Hebrew]**	**אֵת כָּל־דִּבְרֵי יוֹסֵף.** סִימָן מָסַר לָהֶם בַּמֶּה הָיָה עוֹסֵק כְּשֶׁפֵּרֵשׁ מִמֶּנּוּ – בְּפָרָשַׁת עֶגְלָה עֲרוּפָה, זֶהוּ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר וירא את העגלות אשר שלח יוסף, וְלֹא נֶאֱמַר אֲשֶׁר שָׁלַח פַּרְעֹה:<br>
**27 [English]**	**all of Joseph’s words.** He (Joseph) gave them a sign, viz., in what topic he was engaged when he (Joseph) separated from him (Jacob). [That was] the section dealing with the heifer that was to be beheaded (עֶגְלָה עִרוּפָה) (Deut. 21), and this is what [Scripture] says, “and he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent,” and it (Scripture) does not say, “that Pharaoh had sent.” [from Gen. Rabbah 94:3]

**27 [Hebrew]**	**וַתְּחִי רוּחַ יַֽעֲקֹב.** שָׁרְתָה עָלָיו שְׁכִינָה, שֶׁפֵּרְשָׁה מִמֶּנּוּ:<br>
**27 [English]**	**and the spirit of…Jacob was revived.** The Shechinah, which had separated from him [because of his grief], rested upon him [once again]. [From Avoth d’Rabbi Nathan , ch. 30, Targum Onkelos , Targum Jonathan]



**28 [Hebrew]**	**רַב.** רַב לִי עוֹד שִׂמְחָה וְחֶדְוָה הוֹאִיל וְעוֹד יוֹסֵף בְּנִי חַי:<br>
**28 [English]**	**Enough! My son Joseph is still alive.** I have enough happiness and joy, since my son Joseph is still alive. [From Targum Onkelos, Targum Jonathan]
