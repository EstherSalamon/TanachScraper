**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּֽהְיוּ חַיֵּי שָׂרָה מֵאָה שָׁנָה וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה וְשֶׁבַע שָׁנִים.** לְכָךְ נִכְתַּב שָׁנָה בְּכָל כְּלָל וּכְלָל, לוֹמַר לְךָ שֶׁכָּל אֶחָד נִדְרָשׁ לְעַצְמוֹ, בַּת ק' כְּבַת כ' לְחֵטְא, מַה בַּת כ' לֹא חָטְאָה, שֶׁהֲרֵי אֵינָהּ בַּת עֳנָשִׁין, אַף בַּת ק' בְּלֹא חֵטְא, וּבַת כ' כְּבַת ז' לְיֹפִי:<br>
**1 [English]**	**And the life of Sarah was one hundred years and twenty years and seven years.** The reason that the word “years” was written after every digit is to tell you that every digit is to be expounded upon individually: when she was one hundred years old, she was like a twenty-year-old regarding sin. Just as a twenty-year-old has not sinned, because she is not liable to punishment, so too when she was one hundred years old, she was without sin. And when she was twenty, she was like a seven-year-old as regards to beauty. — from Gen. Rabbah 58:1]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁנֵי חַיֵּי שָׂרָה.** כֻּלָּן שָׁוִין לְטוֹבָה:<br>
**1 [English]**	**the years of the life of Sarah.** All of them equally good.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**בְּקִרְיַת אַרְבַּע.** עַל שֵׁם אַרְבַּע עֲנָקִים שֶׁהָיוּ שָׁם, אֲחִימָן שֵׁשַׁי וְתַלְמַי וַאֲבִיהֶם. דָּבָר אַחֵר עַל שֵׁם אַרְבַּע זוּגוֹת שֶׁנִקְבְּרוּ שָׁם אִישׁ וְאִשְׁתּוֹ, אָדָם וְחַוָּה, אַבְרָהָם וְשָׂרָה, יִצְחָק וְרִבְקָה, יַעֲקֹב וְלֵאָה (בראשית רבה):<br>
**2 [English]**	**in Kiriath-arba.** lit. the city of the four. So named because of the four giants who were there: Ahiman, Sheshai, Talmai, and their father (Gen. Rabbah from Num. 13:23). Another explanation: Because of the four couples that were buried there, man and wife: Adam and Eve, Abraham and Sarah, Isaac and Rebecca, Jacob and Leah (Pirkei d’Rabbi Eliezer , ch. 20).

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּבֹא אַבְרָהָם.** מִבְּאֵר שֶׁבַע:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and Abraham came.** from Beer-sheba.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**לספוד לְשָׂרָה וְלִבְכֹּתָֽהּ.** וְנִסְמְכָה מִיתַת שָׂרָה לַעֲקֵדַת יִצְחָק לְפִי שֶׁעַל יְדֵי בְּשׂוֹרַת הָעֲקֵדָה, שֶׁנִּזְדַּמֵּן בְּנָהּ לִשְׁחִיטָה וְכִמְעַט שֶׁלֹּא נִשְׁחַט, פָּרְחָה נִשְׁמָתָהּ מִמֶּנָּה וּמֵתָה:<br>
**2 [English]**	**to eulogize Sarah and to bewail her.** The account of Sarah’s demise was juxtaposed to the binding of Isaac because as a result of the news of the “binding,” that her son was prepared for slaughter and was almost slaughtered, her soul flew out of her, and she died. — from Gen. Rabbah 58:5]



**4 [Hebrew]**	**גֵּֽר־וְתוֹשָׁב אָֽנֹכִי עִמָּכֶם.** גֵּר מֵאֶרֶץ אַחֶרֶת וְנִתְיַשַּׁבְתִּי עִמָּכֶם. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה אִם תִּרְצוּ הֲרֵינִי גֵּר, וְאִם לָאו אֶהְיֶה תּוֹשָׁב וְאֶטְּלֶנָּה מִן הַדִּין, שֶׁאָמַר לִי הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא לְזַרְעֲךָ אֶתֵּן אֶת הָאָרֶץ הַזֹּאת:<br>
**4 [English]**	**I am a stranger and an inhabitant with you.** [I am] a stranger from another land, and I have settled among you. [Consequently, I have no ancestral burial plot here (Rashbam, Sforno).] And the Midrash Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 58:6) [states]: If you are willing [to sell me burial property], I am a stranger, but if not, I will be as an inhabitant and will take it legally, for the Holy One, blessed be He, said to me, “To your seed I will give this land” (above 12:7).

**4 [Hebrew]**	**אחוזת קֶבֶר.** אֲחֻזַּת קַרְקַע לְבֵית הַקְּבָרוֹת:<br>
**4 [English]**	**burial property.** the possession of land for a burial place.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־יִכְלֶה.** לֹא יִמְנַע כְּמוֹ לֹא תִכְלָא רַחֲמֶיךָ (תהילים מ') וּכְמוֹ וַיִּכָּלֵא הַגֶּשֶׁם (בראשית ח'):<br>
**6 [English]**	**none…will withhold.** Heb. לא יִכְלֶה, will not withhold, as (Ps. 40:12): “You will not withhold (לא תִכְלָא) Your mercies,” and similarly (above 8:2): “and the rain was withheld (וַיִּכָּלֵא).”



**8 [Hebrew]**	**נַפְשְׁכֶם.** רְצוֹנְכֶם:<br>
**8 [English]**	**your will.** Heb. נַפְשְׁכֶם, equivalent to רְצוֹנְכֶם, your will.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**וּפִגְעוּ־לִי.** לְשוֹן בַּקָּשָׁה כְּמוֹ: אַל תִּפְגְּעִי בִּי (רות א'):<br>
**8 [English]**	**and entreat for me.** Heb. וּפִגְעוּ, an expression of entreaty, as in (Ruth 1: 16): “Do not entreat me” .



**9 [Hebrew]**	**הַמַּכְפֵּלָה.** בַּית וַעֲלִיָּה עַל גַּבָּיו. דָּבָר אַחֵר שֶׁכְּפוּלָה בְּזוּגוֹת (עירובין נ"ג):<br>
**9 [English]**	**double.** A structure with an upper story over it. Another interpretation: [It was called so] because it was doubled with couples (Er. 53a).

**9 [Hebrew]**	**בְּכֶסֶף מָלֵא.** שָׁלֵם, כָּל שָׁוְיָהּ; וְכֵן דָּוִד אָמַר לַאֲרַוְנָה בְּכֶסֶף מָלֵא (דברי הימים א כ"א):<br>
**9 [English]**	**for a full price.** [meaning] its full value. So did David say to Araunah, “for the full price” (I Chron. 21: 24).



**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְעֶפְרוֹן ישֵׁב.** כְּתִיב חָסֵר, אוֹתוֹ הַיּוֹם מִנּוּהוּ שׁוֹטֵר עֲלֵיהֶם; מִפְּנֵי חֲשִׁיבוּתוֹ שֶׁל אַבְרָהָם שֶׁהָיָה צָרִיךְ לוֹ, עָלָה לִגְדֻלָּה:<br>
**10 [English]**	**Now Ephron was sitting.** It ישֵׁב is spelled defectively, [without a“vav.” It can therefore be read יָשַׁב, in the past tense, meaning that he had just sat (Mizrachi, Be’er Yitzchak).] On that very day they had appointed him as an officer over them. Because of the importance of Abraham, who needed him, he rose to an exalted position. — from Gen. Rabbah 58:7]

**10 [Hebrew]**	**לְכֹל בָּאֵי שַֽׁעַר־עִירוֹ.** שֶׁכֻּלָּן בָּטְלוּ מִמְּלָאכְתָּן וּבָאוּ לִגְמֹל חֶסֶד לְשָׂרָה:<br>
**10 [English]**	**of all those who had come into the gate of his city.** For they all left their work and came to pay their respects to Sarah. — from Gen. Rabbah 58:7]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־אֲדֹנִי.** לֹא תִּקְנֶה אוֹתָהּ בְּדָמִים:<br>
**11 [English]**	**No, my lord.** You shall not buy it for money.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**נָתַתִּי לָךְ.** הֲרֵי הוּא כְּמוֹ שֶׁנְּתַתִּיהָ לְךָ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**I have given you.** It is as though I have given it to you.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אַךְ אִם־אַתָּה לוּ שְׁמָעֵנִי.** אַתָּה אוֹמֵר לִי לִשְׁמֹעַ לְךָ וְלִקַּח בְּחִנָּם, אֲנִי אִי אֶפְשִׁי בְּכָךְ אַךְ אִם־אַתָּה לוּ שְׁמָעֵנִי הַלְוַאי וְתִשְׁמָעֵנִי:<br>
**13 [English]**	**But, if only you would listen to me.** You tell me to listen to you and to take it gratis. I do not wish to do that, but “If you would only (לוּ) listen to me,” [meaning] “If only (הַלְוַאי) you would listen to me.” Rashi explains that the word לוּ is equivalent to הַלְוַאי, if only.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**נָתַתִּי.** דני"ש בלע"ז, מוּכָן הוּא אֶצְלִי, וְהַלְוַאי נָתַתִּי לְךָ כְּבָר:<br>
**13 [English]**	**I am giving.** lit. I have given, Donai in Old French. It is ready with me, and I wish that I had already given it to you.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**בֵּינִי וּבֵֽינְךָ.** בֵּין שְׁנֵי אוֹהֲבִים כָּמוֹנוּ מַה הִיא חֲשׁוּבָה לִכְלוּם, אֶלָּא הַנַּח אֶת הַמֶּכֶר וְאֶת מֵתְךָ קְבֹר (בראשית רבה):<br>
**15 [English]**	**between me and you.** Between two friends such as we are, of what importance is it? None! Rather leave the sale and bury your dead.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׁקֹל אַבְרָהָם לְעֶפְרֹן.** חָסֵר וָי"ו; לְפִי שֶׁאָמַר הַרְבֵּה, וַאֲפִלּוּ מְעַט לֹא עָשָׂה (בבא מציעא פ"ז), שֶׁנָּטַל מִמֶּנוּ שְׁקָלִים גְּדוֹלִים שֶׁהֵן קַנְטָרִין, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר עֹבֵר לַסֹּחֵר, שֶׁמִּתְקַבְּלִים בְּשֶׁקֶל בְּכָל מָקוֹם (שם ובכורות נ'), וְיֵשׁ מָקוֹם שֶׁשִּׁקְלֵיהֶן גְּדוֹלִים, שֶׁהֵן קַנְטָרִין צנטינא"רש בלע"ז:<br>
**16 [English]**	**and Abraham weighed out to Ephron.** עֶפְרֹן is spelled without a “vav,” because he promised much but did not do even a little [i.e., he promised the cave as a gift but took a great deal of money for it], for he took from him large shekels, viz. centenaria [worth one hundred smaller shekels], as it is stated: “accepted by the merchant,” i.e., they are accepted as a [full] shekel everywhere, for some places have large shekels, viz. centenaria, centeniers or zenteniyers in Old French, (hundred-unit weights.) - from Gen. Rabbah 58:7, Bech. 50a, B.M. 87a]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּקָם שְׂדֵה עֶפְרוֹן.** תְּקוּמָה הָיְתָה לוֹ, שֶׁיָּצָא מִיַּד הֶדְיוֹט לְיַד מֶלֶךְ; וּפְשׁוּטוֹ שֶׁל מִקְרָא וַיָּקָם הַשָּׂדֶה וְהַמְּעָרָה אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ וְכָל הָעֵץ לְאַבְרָהָם לְמִקְנָה וגו':<br>
**17 [English]**	**the field of Ephron…was established.** Heb. וַיָקָם, lit. it arose. It experienced an elevation, for it left the possession of a simple person [and went] into the possession of a king (Gen. Rabbah 48:8). The simple meaning of the verse is: And the field and the cave that was within it, and all the trees…were established to Abraham as a possession, etc. — from Targum Jonathan ben Uzziel]



**18 [Hebrew]**	**בְּכֹל בָּאֵי שַֽׁעַר־עִירֽוֹ.** בְּקֶרֶב כֻּלָּם וּבְמַעֲמַד כֻּלָּם הִקְנָהוּ לוֹ:<br>
**18 [English]**	**in the presence of all who had come within the gate of his city.** In the midst of them all and in the presence of all he sold it to him.
