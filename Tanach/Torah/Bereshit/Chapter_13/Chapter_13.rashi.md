**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּעַל אַבְרָם וגו' הַנֶּֽגְבָּה.** לָבֹא לִדְרוֹמָהּ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ יִשְֹרָאֵל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁאָמַר לְמַעְלָה הָלוֹךְ וְנָסוֹעַ הַנֶּגְבָּה, לְהַר הַמּוֹרִיָּה; וּמִכָּל מָקוֹם כְּשֶׁהוּא הוֹלֵךְ מִמִּצְרַיִם לְאֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן, מִדָּרוֹם לְצָפוֹן הוּא מְהַלֵּךְ, שֶׁאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם בִּדְרוֹמָהּ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל כְּמוֹ שֶׁמּוֹכִיחַ בַּמַּסָּעוֹת וּבִגְבוּלֵי הָאָרֶץ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**And Abram came up, etc., to the south.** To come to the south of the Land of Israel, as Scripture stated above (12:9): continually traveling southward to Mount Moriah. And in every case when one goes from Egypt to the land of Canaan, he goes from south to north, for the land of Egypt is south of the Land of Israel, as is evidenced by the travels [of the Jews in the desert] and by the boundaries of the Land.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**כָּבֵד מְאֹד.** טָעוּן מַשָּׂאוֹת:<br>
**2 [English]**	**very heavy.** laden with burdens.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֵּלֶךְ לְמַסָּעָיו.** כְּשֶׁחָזַר מִמִּצְרַיִם לְאֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן הָיָה הוֹלֵךְ וְלָן בָּאַכְסַנְיוֹת שֶׁלָּן בָּהֶם בַּהֲלִיכָתוֹ לְמִצְרַיִם, לִמֶּדְךָ דֶּרֶך אֶרֶץ, שֶׁלֹּא יְשַׁנֶּה אָדָם מֵאַכְסַנְיָא שֶׁלּוֹ. דָּבָר אַחֵר בַּחֲזָרָתוֹ פָּרַע הַקָּפוֹתָיו (בראשית רבה):<br>
**3 [English]**	**And he went on his journeys.** When he returned from Egypt to the land of Canaan, he went and lodged in the inns where he had lodged on his way to Egypt. This teaches you etiquette, that a person should not change his lodgings (Arachin 16b). Another explanation (Gen. Rabbah 41: 3): On his return, he paid his debts.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**מִנֶּגֶב.** אֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם בִּדְרוֹמָהּ שֶׁל אֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן:<br>
**3 [English]**	**from the south.** The land of Egypt is south of the land of Canaan.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂה שָׁם בָּרִֽאשֹׁנָה.** וַאֲשֶׁר קָרָא שָׁם אַבְרָם בְּשֵׁם ה' וְגַם יֵשׁ לוֹמַר וַיִּקְרָא שָׁם עַכְשָׁו בְּשֵׁם ה':<br>
**4 [English]**	**that he had made at first, and Abram called there.** And where Abram had called in the name of the Lord. We may also say that it means: and now he called there in the name of the Lord.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**הַֽהֹלֵךְ אֶת־אַבְרָם.** מִי גָרַם שֶׁהָיָה לוֹ זֹאת? הֲלִיכָתוֹ עִם אַבְרָם:<br>
**5 [English]**	**who went with Abram.** What brought about that he had this [wealth]? His going with Abram. [from B.K. 93a]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹֽא־נָשָׂא אֹתָם.** לֹא הָיְתָה יְכוֹלָה לְהַסְפִּיק מִרְעֶה לְמִקְנֵיהֶם, וְלָשׁוֹן קָצָר הוּא, וְצָרִיךְ לְהוֹסִיף עָלָיו, כְּמוֹ וְלֹא נָשָׂא אוֹתָם מִרְעֵה הָאָרֶץ, לְפִיכָךְ כָּתַב וְלֹא נָשָׂא בִּלְשׁוֹן זָכָר:<br>
**6 [English]**	**And…did not bear.** It was unable to supply enough pasture for their cattle, and this is an abbreviated expression, and an additional word is needed. [It is to be explained] as: “And the pasture of the land could not bear them.” Therefore, וְלֹא נָשָׂא is written in the masculine gender.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽיְהִי־רִיב.** לְפִי שְׁהָיוּ רוֹעִים שֶׁל לוֹט רְשָׁעִים וּמַרְעִים בְּהֶמְתָּם בִּשְׂדוֹת אֲחֵרִים, וְרוֹעֵי אַבְרָם מוֹכִיחִים אוֹתָם עַל הַגֶּזֶל, וְהֵם אוֹמְרִים נִתְּנָה הָאָרֶץ לְאַבְרָם, וְלוֹ אֵין יוֹרֵשׁ, וְלוֹט יוֹרְשׁוֹ, וְאֵין זֶה גֶּזֶל, וְהַכָּתוּב אוֹמֵר וְהַכְּנַעֲנִי וְהַפְּרִזִּי אָז יֹשֵׁב בָּאָרֶץ, וְלֹא זָכָה בָהּ אַבְרָם עֲדַיִן (ב"ר):<br>
**7 [English]**	**And there was a quarrel.** Since Lot’s herdsmen were wicked, and they pastured their animals in fields belonging to others, Abram’s herdsmen rebuked them for committing robbery, but they responded, “The land was given to Abram, who has no heir; so Lot will inherit him, and therefore this is not robbery.” But Scripture states: “And the Canaanites and the Perizzites were then dwelling in the land,” and Abram had not yet been awarded its possession. [from Gen. Rabbah 41:5]



**8 [Hebrew]**	**אֲנָשִׁים אַחִים.** קְרוֹבִים, וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה דּוֹמִין בִּקְלַסְתֵּר פָּנִים:<br>
**8 [English]**	**kinsmen.** meaning relatives, and according to the Midrash Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 41:6), they resembled each other in their facial features.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**אִם־הַשְּׂמֹאל וְאֵימִנָה.** בְּכָל אֲשֶׁר תֵּשֵׁב, לֹא אֶתְרַחֵק מִמְּךָ וְאֶעֱמֹד לְךָ לְמָגֵן וּלְעֵזֶר, וְסוֹף דָּבָר הֻצְרַךְ לוֹ, שְׁנֶּאֱמַר וַיִּשְׁמַע אַבְרָם כִּי נִשְׁבָּה אָחִיו וְגוֹ':<br>
**9 [English]**	**if [you go] left, I will go right.** Wherever you dwell, I will not distance myself from you, and I will stand by you as a protector and a helper. And he ultimately needed him, as it is said (below 14:14): “And Abram heard that his kinsman had been captured, etc.”

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵימִנָה.** אַיְמִין אֶת עַצְמִי, כְּמוֹ וְאַשְׂמְאִילָה אַשְׂמְאִיל אֶת עַצְמִי. וְאִם תֹּאמַר הָיָה לוֹ לִנָּקֵד וְאַיְמִינָה, כָּךְ מָצִינוּ בְּמָקוֹם אַחֵר אִם יֵשׁ לְהֵמִין (שמואל ב י"ד) וְאֵין נָקוּד לְהַיְמִין:<br>
**9 [English]**	**I will go right.** [Grammatically, this means] “I will go towards the right,” like וְאַשְׂמְאִילָה, “and I will go towards the left.” Now if you say that it should have been vowelized וְאַיְמִינָה, we find the same in another place (viz., II Sam. 14:19): “if anyone can turn to the right (לַהֵמִין),” and it is not vowelized לְהַיְמִין.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי כֻלָּהּ מַשְׁקֶה.** אֶרֶץ נַחֲלֵי מַיִם:<br>
**10 [English]**	**that it was entirely watered.** A land with streams of water.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**לִפְנֵי שַׁחֵת ה' אֶת־סדום וְאֶת־עֲמֹרָה.** הָיָה אוֹתוֹ מִישׁוֹר:<br>
**10 [English]**	**before the Lord destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.** that plain was:

**10 [Hebrew]**	**כְּגַן־ה'.** לָאִילָנוֹת:<br>
**10 [English]**	**like the garden of the Lord.** for [growing] trees.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**כְּאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם.** לַזְּרָעִים (בראשית רבה):<br>
**10 [English]**	**like the land of Egypt.** for [growing] seeds
(Gen. Rabbah 41:7).

**10 [Hebrew]**	**בֹּֽאֲכָה צֹֽעַר.** עַד צֹעַר. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה דוֹרְשָׁהּ לִגְנַאי, עַל שֶׁהָיוּ שְׁטוּפֵי זִמָּה בָּחַר לוֹ לוֹט בִּשְׁכוּנָתָם:<br>
**10 [English]**	**as you come to Zoar.** Until Zoar. And the Midrash Aggadah interprets it unfavorably: it was because they were lascivious that Lot chose their region for himself (Tractate Horioth 10b).



**11 [Hebrew]**	**כִּכַּר.** מִישׁוֹר, כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**plain.** Heb. כִּכָּר, the plain, as the Targum renders it. Note that according to Rashi, כִּכָּר is not the generic term for a plain, but the proper name of the Plain of the Jordan. See below (14:5).

**11 [Hebrew]**	**מִקֶּדֶם.** נָסַע מֵאֵצֶל אַבְרָם וְהָלַך לוֹ לְמַעֲרָבוֹ שֶׁל אַבְרָם; נִמְצָא נוֹסֵעַ מִמִּזְרָח לְמַעֲרָב. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה הִסִּיעַ עַצְמוֹ מִקַּדְמוֹנוֹ שֶׁל עוֹלָם; אָמַר אִי אֶפְשִׁי לֹא בְּאַבְרָם וְלֹא בֵאלֹהָיו:<br>
**11 [English]**	**from the east.** He traveled from beside Abram and went away to the west of Abram; hence, he travelled from east to west (Targum Jonathan). According to the Midrash Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 41:7), he distanced himself from the Ancient One (מִקַּדְמוֹנוֹ) of the world. He said, “I care neither for Abram nor for his God.”



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֶּֽאֱהַל.** נָטָה אֳהָלִים לְרוֹעָיו וּלְמִקְנֵהוּ עַד סְדוֹם:<br>
**12 [English]**	**and he pitched his tents.** He pitched tents for his herdsmen and his cattle until Sodom.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַנְשֵׁי סדום רָעִים.** וְאַף עַל פִּי כֵן לֹא נִמְנַע לוֹט מִלִּשְׁכֹּן עִמָּהֶם. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ לָמְדוּ מִכָּאן (יומא ל"ח) וְשֵׁם רְשָׁעִים יִרְקָב (משלי י'):<br>
**13 [English]**	**And the people of Sodom were…evil.** Nevertheless, Lot did not hesitate to sojourn with them. And our Rabbis (Yoma 38b) learned from here that (Prov. 10:7) “the name of the wicked shall rot.” [Having mentioned them, the Torah speaks of their evil.]

**13 [Hebrew]**	**רָעִים.** בְּגוּפָם:<br>
**13 [English]**	**evil.** with their bodies.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**וְחַטָּאִים.** בְּמָמוֹנָם (סנה' ק"ט):<br>
**13 [English]**	**and sinful.** with their money. [from Sanh. 109a]

**13 [Hebrew]**	**לה' מְאֹֽד.** יוֹדְעִים רִבּוֹנָם, וּמִתְכַּוְּנִים לִמְרֹד בּוֹ:<br>
**13 [English]**	**very… against the Lord.** They recognized their Master and intended to rebel against Him. [from Sifra, Bechukkothai 2]



**14 [Hebrew]**	**אַֽחֲרֵי הִפָּֽרֶד־לוֹט.** כָּל זְמַן שֶׁהָרָשָׁע עִמּוֹ הָיָה הַדִּבּוּר פּוֹרֵשׁ מִמֵֶּנּוּ:<br>
**14 [English]**	**after Lot had parted.** As long as the wicked man was with him, the Divine speech withdrew from him (and above, when Lot was with him, and it is written (12:7): “And the Lord appeared to Abram,” [we must assume that] at that time, he was righteous, and this is easy to understand). [from Tan. Vayeze 10]



**16 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר אִם־יוּכַל אִישׁ.** כְּשֵׁם שֶׁאִי אֶפְשָׁר לֶעָפָר לְהִמָּנוֹת כָּךְ זַרְעֲךָ לֹא יִמָּנֶה:<br>
**16 [English]**	**that if a man will be able.** Just as it is impossible for the dust to be counted, so will your seed not be counted. [from Targum Onkelos]



**18 [Hebrew]**	**מַמְרֵא.** שֵׁם אָדָם:<br>
**18 [English]**	**Mamre.** the name of a man. [from Gen. Rabbah 42:8]
