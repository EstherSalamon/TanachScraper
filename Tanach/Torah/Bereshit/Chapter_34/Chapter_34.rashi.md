**1 [Hebrew]**	**בַּת־לֵאָה.** וְלֹא בַת יַעֲקֹב? אֶלָּא עַל שֵׁם יְצִיאָתָהּ נִקְרֵאת בַּת לֵאָה, שֶׁאַף הִיא יַצְאָנִית הָיְתָה (בראשית רבה), שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר וַתֵּצֶא לֵאָה לִקְרָאתוֹ (וְעָלֶיהָ מָשְׁלוּ הַמָּשָׁל כְּאִמָּהּ כְּבִתָּהּ):<br>
**1 [English]**	**the daughter of Leah.** And not the daughter of Jacob? However, because of her going out she was called the daughter of Leah, since she (Leah) too was in the habit of going out, as it is said: “and Leah came forth toward him” (above 30:16). [from Tanchuma Vayishlach 7] (And concerning her, they devise the proverb: Like mother like daughter.) - [Gen. Rabbah 80:1]



**2 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׁכַּב אֹתָהּ.** כְּדַרְכָּהּ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**lay with her.** in a natural way. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:5]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְעַנֶּֽהָ.** שֶׁלֹּא כְדַרְכָּהּ (בראשית רבה):<br>
**2 [English]**	**and violated her.** Heb. וַיְעַנֶהָ, lit., and afflicted her. [I.e. he was intimate with her] in an unnatural way. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80: 5]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־לֵב הנערה.** דְּבָרִים הַמִּתְיַשְּׁבִים עַל הַלֵּב; רְאִי, אָבִיךְ בְּחֶלְקַת שָׂדֶה קְטַנָּה כַּמָּה מָמוֹן בִּזְבֵּז, אֲנִי אַשִּׂיאֵךְ וְתִקְנֶה הָעִיר וְכָל שְׂדוֹתֶיהָ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**and spoke to the girl’s heart.** [I.e. he spoke] seductive words, “Look how much money your father squandered for a small parcel of land. I will marry you, and you will acquire the city and all its fields.” - [from Gen. Rabbah 80:7]



**7 [Hebrew]**	**וְכֵן לֹא יֵֽעָשֶֽׂה.** לְעַנּוֹת אֶת הַבְּתוּלוֹת, שֶׁהָאֻמּוֹת גָּדְרוּ עַצְמָן מִן הָעֲרָיוֹת עַל יְדֵי הַמַּבּוּל (בראשית רבה):<br>
**7 [English]**	**and such ought not to be done.** to violate virgins, for the nations (the general population) had refrained from illicit relationships because of the Flood. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:7]



**8 [Hebrew]**	**חָֽשְׁקָה.** חָפְצָה:<br>
**8 [English]**	**has a liking.** He desires.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**מֹהַר.** כְּתֻבָּה:<br>
**12 [English]**	**marriage settlement.** Kethubah. — [from Mechilta Mishpatim, Nezikin 17]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**בְּמִרְמָה.** בְּחָכְמָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**with cunning.** Heb. בְּמִרְמָה, with cunning. — [from Targum Onkelos]

**13 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר טִמֵּא.** הַכָּתוּב אוֹמֵר שֶׁלֹּא הָיְתָה רְמִיָּה, שֶׁהֲרֵי טִמֵּא אֶת דִּינָה אֲחֹתָם (בראשית רבה):<br>
**13 [English]**	**because [after all] he had defiled.** Scripture states that this was not trickery because [after all] he had defiled their sister Dinah. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:8]



**14 [Hebrew]**	**חֶרְפָּה הִוא.** שֶׁמֶץ פְּסוּל הוּא אֶצְלֵנוּ; הַבָּא לְחָרֵף חֲבֵרוֹ הוּא אוֹמֵר לוֹ עָרֵל אַתָּה אוֹ בֶּן עָרֵל. חֶרְפָּה בְּכָל מָקוֹם גִּדּוּף:<br>
**14 [English]**	**for that is a disgrace to us.** To us, it is a defect. If someone wishes to insult another person, he says, “You are uncircumcised,” or “[You are] the son of an uncircumcised person.” Wherever חֶרְפָּה is mentioned, it is an [expression of] insult.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**נֵאוֹת לָכֶם.** נִתְרַצֶּה לָכֶם, לְשׁוֹן וַיֵּאֹתוּ (מלכים ב י"ב):<br>
**15 [English]**	**we will consent to you.** Heb. נֵאוֹת. We will consent to you, an expression like (וַיֵאֹתוּ הַכֹּהִנִים), “And (the priests) consented,” (in connection with Jehoiada) (II Kings 12:9).

**15 [Hebrew]**	**לְהִמֹּל.** לִהְיוֹת נִמּוֹל. אֵינוֹ לְשׁוֹן לִפְעֹל אֶלָּא לְשׁוֹן לְהִפָּעֵל:<br>
**15 [English]**	**will be circumcised.** Heb. לְהִמֹל, lit., to be circumcised. This is not in the active voice but in the passive.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְנָתַנּוּ.** נוּ"ן שְׁנִיָּה מֻדְגֶּשֶׁת לְפִי שֶׁהִיא מְשַׁמֶּשֶׁת בִּמְקוֹם שְׁתֵּי נוּנִי"ן, וְנָתַנְנוּ:<br>
**16 [English]**	**Then we will give.** Heb. וְנָתַנוּ. The second “nun” is punctuated with a “dagesh,” because it serves instead of two “nuns,” [like] וְנָתַנְנוּ.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־בְּנֹֽתֵיכֶם נִקַּח־לָנוּ.** אַתָּה מוֹצֵא בַּתְּנַאי שֶׁאָמַר חֲמוֹר לְיַעֲקֹב וּבִתְשׁוּבַת בְּנֵי יַעֲקֹב לַחֲמוֹר, שֶׁתָּלוּ הַחֲשִׁיבוּת בִּבְנֵי יַעֲקֹב לִקַּח בְּנוֹת שְׁכֶם אֶת שֶׁיִּבְחֲרוּ לָהֶם, וּבְנוֹתֵיהֶם יִתְּנוּ לָהֶם לְפִי דַעְתָּם, דִּכְתִיב וְנָתַנּוּ אֶת בְּנֹתֵינוּ – לְפִי דַעְתֵּנוּ, וְאֶת בְּנֹתֵיכֶם נִקַּח לָנוּ – כְּכָל אֲשֶׁר נַחְפֹּץ; וּכְשֶׁדִּבְּרוּ חֲמוֹר וּשְׁכֶם בְּנוֹ אֶל יוֹשְׁבֵי עִירָם הָפְכוּ הַדְּבָרִים, אֶת בְּנֹתָם נִקַּח לָנוּ לְנָשִׁים וְאֶת בְּנֹתֵינוּ נִתֵּן לָהֶם, כְּדֵי לְרַצּוֹתָם שֶׁיֵּאוֹתוּ לְהִמּוֹל:<br>
**16 [English]**	**and we will take your daughters for ourselves.** You find in the stipulation that Hamor suggested to Jacob, and in the reply of Jacob’s sons to Hamor, that they [Hamor and Shechem] attached importance to Jacob’s sons, [allowing them] to take whomever of the daughters of Shechem they would choose for themselves, and their daughters they would give to them [the people of Shechem], as they (the sons of Jacob) wished, for it is written: “Then we will give you our daughters,” as we wish, “and we will take your daughters for ourselves,” however we wish. But, when Hamor and his son Shechem spoke to their townsmen, they reversed the matter, [and said (verse 21),] “We will take their daughters for ourselves as wives, and our daughters we will give them,” in order to appease them (the townsmen), that they would consent to be circumcised.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁלֵמִים.** בְּשָׁלוֹם וּבְלֵב שָׁלֵם:<br>
**21 [English]**	**These men are peaceful with us.** Heb. שְׁלֵמִים. With peace and sincerity.

**21 [Hebrew]**	**וְהָאָרֶץ הִנֵּה רַֽחֲבַת־יָדַיִם.** כְּאָדָם שֶׁיָּדוֹ רְחָבָה וּוַתְרָנִית; כְּלוֹמַר, אַל תַּפְסִידוּ כְּלוּם – פְּרַקְמַטְיָא הַרְבֵּה בָּאָה לְכָאן וְאֵין לָהּ קוֹנִים:<br>
**21 [English]**	**and the land-behold it is spacious enough.** [The metaphor is that of] a person whose hand is wide and generous. That is to say: You will not lose anything [if they trade here]. Plenty of merchandise comes here, and there is no one to purchase it.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**בְּהִמּוֹל.** בִּהְיוֹת נִמּוֹל:<br>
**22 [English]**	**by…being circumcised.** Heb. בְּהִמוֹל, by being circumcised.



**23 [Hebrew]**	**אַךְ נֵאוֹתָה לָהֶם.** לְדָבָר זֶה, וְעַל יְדֵי כֵן יֵשְׁבוּ אִתָּנוּ:<br>
**23 [English]**	**But let us consent to them.** regarding this matter, and thereby, they will dwell with us.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁנֵֽי־בְנֵֽי־יַֽעֲקֹב.** בָּנָיו הָיוּ, וְאַף עַל פִּי כֵן נָהֲגוּ עַצְמָן שִׁמְעוֹן וְלֵוִי כִּשְׁאָר אֲנָשִׁים שֶׁאֵינָם בָּנָיו, שֶׁלֹּא נָטְלוּ עֵצָה הֵימֶנּוּ (בראשית רבה):<br>
**25 [English]**	**Jacob’s two sons.** They were his sons, but nevertheless, Simeon and Levi conducted themselves like other people, who were not his sons, for they did not take counsel with him. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:10]

**25 [Hebrew]**	**אֲחֵי דִינָה.** לְפִי שֶׁמָּסְרוּ עַצְמָן עָלֶיהָ נִקְרְאוּ אַחֶיהָ:<br>
**25 [English]**	**Dinah’s brothers.** Since they risked their lives for her, they were called her brothers. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:10]

**25 [Hebrew]**	**בֶּטַח.** שֶׁהָיוּ כוֹאֲבִים וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה בְּטוּחִים הָיוּ עַל כֹּחוֹ שֶׁל זָקֵן (בראשית רבה):<br>
**25 [English]**	**with confidence.** Because they (the townsmen) were in pain. The Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 80:10) says: They were confident in the strength of the elder (Jacob).



**27 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־הַחֲלָלִים.** לְפַשֵּׁט הַחֲלָלִים:<br>
**27 [English]**	**upon the slain.** to strip the slain. [from Targum Onkelos]



**29 [Hebrew]**	**חֵילָם.** מָמוֹנָם וְכֵן עָשָׂה לִי אֶת הַחַיִל הַזֶּה (דברים ח'), וְיִשְׂרָאֵל עֹשֶׂה חָיִל (במדבר כ"ד), וְעָזְבוּ לַאֲחֵרִים חֵילָם (תהילים מ"ט):<br>
**29 [English]**	**And all their wealth.** Heb. חֵילָם, their money, and similarly; “amassed this wealth (הַחַיִל) for me” (Deut. 8:17); “and Israel amasses wealth (חָיִל)” (Num. 24: 18); “and leave over their possessions (חֵילָם) to others” (Ps. 49:11).

**29 [Hebrew]**	**שָׁבוּ.** לְשׁוֹן שְׁבִיָּה, לְפִיכָךְ טַעֲמוֹ מִלְּרָע:<br>
**29 [English]**	**they captured.** Heb. שָׁבוּ, a term denoting captivity. Therefore, the accent is on the latter syllable. — [from Targum Onkelos]



**30 [Hebrew]**	**עֲכַרְתֶּם.** לְשׁוֹן מַיִם עֲכוּרִים, אֵין דַּעְתִּי צְלוּלָה עַכְשָׁו (ברכות כ"ה). וְאַגָּדָה, צְלוּלָה הָיְתָה הֶחָבִית וַעֲכַרְתֶּם אוֹתָהּ (בראשית רבה) – מָסֹרֶת הָיְתָה בְּיַד כְּנַעֲנִים שֶׁיִּפְּלוּ בְּיַד בְּנֵי יַעֲקֹב, אֶלָּא שֶׁהָיוּ אוֹמְרִים עַד אֲשֶׁר תִּפְרֶה וְנָחַלְתָּ אֶת הָאָרֶץ (שמות כ"ג), לְפִיכָךְ הָיוּ שׁוֹתְקִין:<br>
**30 [English]**	**You have troubled me.** Heb. עִכַרְתֶּם, an expression denoting murky water [meaning that] now my mind is not clear [without worry]. The Aggadah (Gen. Rabbah 80: 12) [explains]: The barrel was clear, but you have made it murky. (I.e., I saw our way clear to co-exist with the Canaanites, but now you have spoiled the possibility to do so.) The Canaanites had a tradition that they would fall into the hands of Jacob’s sons, but they said [that this would only happen], “Until you are fruitful and inherit the land” (Exod. 23:30). Therefore, they have been silent.

**30 [Hebrew]**	**מְתֵי מִסְפָּר.** אֲנָשִׁים מוּעָטִים:<br>
**30 [English]**	**few in number.** few men.



**31 [Hebrew]**	**הַֽכְזוֹנָה.** הֶפְקֵר:<br>
**31 [English]**	**like a harlot.** Abandoned. — [from Gen. Rabbah 80:12]

**31 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־אֲחוֹתֵֽנוּ.** יָת אֲחָתָנָא:<br>
**31 [English]**	**our sister.** [Onkelos renders] יָת לַאֲחָתָנָא, our sister.
