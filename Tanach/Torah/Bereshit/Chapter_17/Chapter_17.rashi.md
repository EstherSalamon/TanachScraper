**1 [Hebrew]**	**שׁדי.** אֲנִי הוּא שֶׁיֵּשׁ דַּי בֶּאֱלֹהוּתִי לְכָל בְּרִיָּה, לְפִיכָךְ הִתְהַלֵּךְ לְפָנַי וְאֶהְיֶה לְךָ לֶאֱלוֹהַּ ולְפַטְרוֹן; וְכֵן כָּל מָקוֹם שֶׁהוּא בַּמִּקְרָא פֵּרוּשׁוֹ כָּךְ: דַּי שֶׁלּוֹ, וְהַכֹּל לְפִי הָעִנְיָן:<br>
**1 [English]**	**I am the Almighty God.** Heb. שַׁדַּי - I am He Whose Godliness suffices for every creature. [שֶׁ that, דַּי is sufficient]. Therefore, walk before Me, and I will be your God and your Protector, and wherever it (this name) appears in Scripture, it means “His sufficiency,” but each one is [to be interpreted] according to the context. — [from Gen. Rabbah 46:3]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְהַלֵּךְ לְפָנַי.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ פְּלַח קֳדָמַי, הִדַּבֵּק בַּעֲבוֹדָתִי:<br>
**1 [English]**	**walk before Me.** As the Targum renders: “Serve Me, cleave to My service.”

**1 [Hebrew]**	**וֶֽהְיֵה תָמִֽים.** אַף זֶה צִוּוּי אַחַר צִוּוּי, הֱיֵה שָׁלֵם בְּכָל נִסְיוֹנוֹתַי; וּלְפִי מִדְרָשׁוֹ הִתְהַלֵּךְ לְפָנַי בְּמִצְוַת מִילָה, וּבַדָּבָר הַזֶּה תִהְיֶה תָּמִים, שֶׁכָּל זְמַן שֶׁהָעָרְלָה בְךָ אַתָּה בַּעַל מוּם לְפָנַי. דָּבָר אַחֵר וֶהְיֵה תָמִים, (עַכְשָׁו אַתָּה חָסֵר ה' אֵבָרִים, ב' עֵינַיִם, ב' אָזְנַיִם, וְרֹאשׁ הַגְּוִיָּה) שֶׁאוֹסִיף לְךָ אוֹת עַל שִׁמְךָ, וְיִהְיוּ מִנְיַן אוֹתִיּוֹתֶיךָ רמ"ח כְּמִנְיַן אֵבָרֶיךָ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**and be perfect.** This too is one command following another command: be perfect in all My trials (Mid. Ps. 119:3), i.e., “Walk before Me” with faith and honesty, and also be perfect in all My trials. [Mizrachi] According to its midrashic interpretation, walk before Me refers to the commandment of circumcision, and thereby, you will be perfect, for as long as the foreskin is upon you, I consider you imperfect (Gen. Rabbah 46:1). Another explanation: “and be perfect” - Now you are missing [control over] five organs: two eyes, two ears, and the male organ. I will add a letter to your name, and the numerical value of your letters [of your name] will be 248, corresponding to the number of your organs (Tan. Lech Lecha 16, Ned. 32b).



**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶתְּנָה בְרִיתִי.** בְּרִית שֶׁל אַהֲבָה וּבְרִית הָאָרֶץ לְהוֹרִישָׁהּ לְךָ עַל יְדֵי מִצְוָה זוֹ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**And I will place My covenant.** A covenant of love and the covenant of the land, to give it to you as a heritage through [your fulfillment of] this commandment. — [from Gen. Rabbah 46:9]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּפֹּל אַבְרָם עַל־פָּנָיו.** מִמּוֹרָא הַשְּׁכִינָה; שֶׁעַד שֶׁלֹּא מָל לֹא הָיָה בוֹ כֹחַ לַעֲמֹד וְרוּח הַקֹּדֶשׁ נִצֶּבֶת עָלָיו, וְזֶהוּ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בְּבִלְעָם נֹפֵל וּגְלוּי עֵינָיִם, בִּבְרַיְתָא דְרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר:<br>
**3 [English]**	**And Abram fell upon his face.** from fear of the Shechinah, for as long as he was uncircumcised, he did not have the strength to stand when the Divine Presence stood over him, and that is what is said concerning Balaam (Num. 24:4): “who falls and his eyes are open” (Num. Rabbah 12:8). I found this in the Baraitha of Rabbi Eliezer (Pirkei d’Rabbi Eliezer ch. 29).



**5 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי אַב־הֲמוֹן גּוֹיִם.** לְשׁוֹן נוֹטָרִיקוֹן שֶׁל שְׁמוֹ; וְרֵי"ש שֶׁהָיְתָה בוֹ בַּתְּחִלָּה, שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה אָב אֶלָּא לַאֲרָם שֶׁהוּא מְקוֹמוֹ וְעַכְשָׁו אָב לְכָל הָעוֹלָם, לֹא זָזָה מִמְּקוֹמָהּ, שֶׁאַף יוֹ"ד שֶׁל שָׂרַי נִתְרָעֲמָה עַל הַשְּׁכִינָה עַד שֶׁנִּתּוֹסְפָה לִיהוֹשֻׁעַ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר וַיִּקְרָא מֹשֶׁה לְהוֹשֵׁעַ בִּן נוּן יְהוֹשֻׁעַ (במדבר י"ג):<br>
**5 [English]**	**for... the father of a multitude of nations.** אַב הֲמוֹן is an acrostic of his name [i.e., - אב ר הם]. (Gen. Rabbah 46:7). The “resh” that was in it [his name] originally, denoting that he was the father only of Aram, which was his native place, whereas now [he became] the father of the whole world (Ber. 13a): nevertheless the “resh” that was there originally was not moved from its place. For even the “yud” in Sarai’s name complained to the Shechinah until it was added to Joshua, as it is said: (Num. 13:16): “and Moses called Hosea [הוֹשֵׁעַ] the son of Nun, Joshua [יְהוֹשֻׁעַ].” - [from Gen. Rabbah 47:1]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וּנְתַתִּיךָ לְגוֹיִם.** יִשְׂרָאֵל וֶאֱדוֹם, שֶׁהֲרֵי יִשְׁמָעֵאל כְּבָר הָיָה לוֹ, וְלֹא הָיָה מְבַשְּׂרוֹ עָלָיו:<br>
**6 [English]**	**and I will make you into nations.** [This refers to] Israel and Edom, for he already had Ishmael, and He would therefore not be informing him about him.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽהֲקִֽמֹתִי אֶת־בְּרִיתִי.** וּמַה הִיא הַבְּרִית? לִהְיוֹת לְךָ לֵאלֹהִים:<br>
**7 [English]**	**And I will establish My covenant.** And what is that covenant? To be to you for a God.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**לַֽאֲחֻזַּת עוֹלָם.** וְשָׁם אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים, אֲבָל הַדָּר בְּחוּצָה לָאָרֶץ דּוֹמֶה כְמִי שֶׁאֵין לוֹ אֱלוֹהַּ:<br>
**8 [English]**	**for an everlasting possession.** And there I will be to you for a God (Gen. Rabbah 46:9), but if one dwells outside the Holy Land, it is as though he has no God (Keth. 110b).



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַתָּה.** וָי"ו זוֹ מוֹסִיף עַל עִנְיָן רִאשׁוֹן, אֲנִי הִנֵּה בְרִיתִי אִתָּךְ, וְאַתָּה הֱוֵי זָהִיר לְשָׁמְרוֹ, וּמַה הִיא שְׁמִירָתוֹ? זֹאת בְּרִיתִי אֲשֶׁר תִּשְׁמְרוּ הִמּוֹל לָכֶם וְגוֹ':<br>
**9 [English]**	**And you.** Heb. וְאַתָּה. This “vav” connects [this verse] to the preceding matter. “As for Me, behold My covenant is with you,” and you must be careful to observe it. Now what does its observance entail? “This is My covenant, which you shall observe…that every male among you be circumcised.”



**10 [Hebrew]**	**בֵּינִי וּבֵינֵיכֶם וגו'.** אוֹתָם שֶׁל עַכְשָׁו:<br>
**10 [English]**	**between Me and you.** those living now.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וּבֵין זַרְעֲךָ אַֽחֲרֶיךָ.** הָעֲתִידִין לְהִוָּלֵד:<br>
**10 [English]**	**and between your seed.** who are destined to be born.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**הִמּוֹל.** כְּמוֹ לְהִמּוֹל, כְּמוֹ שֶׁאַתָּה אוֹמֵר עֲשׂוֹת, כְּמוֹ לַעֲשׂוֹת:<br>
**10 [English]**	**be circumcised.** Heb. הִמּוֹל, is like לְהִמּוֹל, to circumcise [the infinitive], as you might say עֲשוֹת in place of לַעֲשוֹת, to do.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**וּנְמַלְתֶּם.** כְּמוֹ וּמַלְתֶּם, וְהַנּוּ"ן בּוֹ יְתֵרָה לִיסוֹד הַנּוֹפֵל בּוֹ לִפְרָקִים, כְּגוֹן נ' שֶׁל נוֹשֵׁךְ ונ' שֶׁל נוֹשֵׂא, וּנְמַלְתֶּם, כְּמוֹ וּנְשָׂאתֶם; אֲבָל יִמּוֹל לְשׁוֹן יִפָּעֵל, כְּמוֹ יֵעָשֶׂה, יֵאָכֵל:<br>
**11 [English]**	**And you shall circumcise.** וּנְמַלְתֶּם is like וּמַלְתֶּם, and the “nun” is superfluous, a radical that sometimes appears in it, like the “nun” of נוֹשֵׁךְ and the “nun” of נוֹשֵׂא. וּנְמַלְתֶּם has the same form as וּנְשָׂאתֶם, (i.e., the Kal form). But יִמּוֹל is in the passive form (the Nifal), like יֵעָשֶׂה (it will be done), יֵאָכֵל (it will be eaten).



**12 [Hebrew]**	**יְלִיד בָּיִת.** שֶׁיְּלָדַתּוּ הַשִּׁפְחָה בַּבַּיִת:<br>
**12 [English]**	**one that is born in the house.** whom the maidservant bore in the house.

**12 [Hebrew]**	**מקנת כֶּסֶף.** שֶׁקְּנָאוֹ מִשֶּׁנּוֹלַד:<br>
**12 [English]**	**one that is purchased with money.** whom he bought after he was born.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**הִמּוֹל יִמּוֹל יְלִיד בֵּֽיתְךָ.** כָּאן כָּפַל עָלָיו וְלֹא אָמַר לִשְׁמוֹנָה יָמִים לְלַמֶּדְךָ שֶׁיֵּשׁ יְלִיד בַּיִת נִמּוֹל לְאַחַר שְׁמוֹנָה יָמִים (ס"א לְאֶחָד) כְּמוֹ שֶׁמְּפֹרָשׁ בְּמַסֶּכֶת שַׁבָּת (דף קל"ה ב'):<br>
**13 [English]**	**Those born in the house… shall be circumcised.** Here Scripture repeated it [the commandment to circumcise a slave born in the house;] but did not state [that it is to be] on the eighth day, to teach you that there is a slave born in the house who is circumcised after eight days [other editions: at the age of one day], as is delineated in Tractate Shabbath (135b).



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְעָרֵל זָכָר.** כָּאן לִמֵּד שֶׁהַמִּילָה בְּאוֹתוֹ מָקוֹם שֶׁהוּא נִכָּר בֵּין זָכָר לִנְקֵבָה (בראשית רבה):<br>
**14 [English]**	**And an uncircumcised male.** Here Scripture teaches that circumcision is in that place that distinguishes between male and female.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹֽא־יִמּוֹל.** מִשֶּׁיַּגִּיעַ לִכְלַל עֳנָשִׁין וְנִכְרְתָה; אֲבָל אָבִיו אֵין עָנוּשׁ עָלָיו כָּרֵת, אֲבָל עוֹבֵר בַּעֲשֵׂה:<br>
**14 [English]**	**who will not circumcise.** When he reaches the age when he becomes liable for punishment, then [his soul] will be cut off (Shab. 133b), but his father [who does not circumcise him] is not punishable by “kareth” (spiritual excision), but is guilty of transgressing a positive commandment (Yeb. 70b).

**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְנִכְרְתָה הַנֶּפֶשׁ.** הוֹלֵךְ עֲרִירִי וּמֵת קֹדֶם זְמַנּוֹ (שבת כ"ד):<br>
**14 [English]**	**that soul will be cut off.** He goes childless (Yeb. 55a) and dies prematurely (Moed Katan 28a).



**15 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־תִקְרָא אֶת־שְׁמָהּ שָׂרַי.** דְּמַשְׁמַע שָׂרַי, לִי וְלֹא לְאַחֵרִים, כי שרה סְתָם שְׁמָהּ, שֶׁתְּהֵא שָׂרָה עַל כֹּל (ברכות י"ג):<br>
**15 [English]**	**you shall not call her name Sarai.** which means “my princess,” for me, but not for others. But Sarah, in an unqualified sense, shall be her name, that she will be a princess over all. — [from Ber. 13a]



**16 [Hebrew]**	**וּבֵֽרַכְתִּי אותה.** וּמַה הִיא הַבְּרָכָה? שֶׁחָזְרָה לְנַעֲרוּתָהּ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר הָיְתָה לִּי עֶדְנָה (בר' י"ח):<br>
**16 [English]**	**And I will bless her.** And what is the blessing? That she returned to her youth, as it is said (below 18:12): “My skin has become smooth.” - [from B.M. 87a]

**16 [Hebrew]**	**וּבֵֽרַכְתִּיהָ.** בַּהֲנָקַת שָׁדַיִם כְּשֶׁנִּצְרְכָה לְכָךְ בְּיוֹם מִשְׁתֶּה שֶׁל יִצְחָק, שֶׁהָיוּ מְרַנְּנִין עֲלֵיהֶם שֶׁהֵבִיאוּ אֲסוּפִי מִן הַשּׁוּק וְאוֹמְרִים בְּנֵנוּ הוּא, וְהֵבִיאָה כָל אַחַת בְּנָה עִמָּהּ וּמֵנִקְתָּהּ לֹא הֵבִיאָה, וְהִיא הֵנִיקָה אֶת כֻּלָּם; הוּא שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר הֵינִיקָה בָנִים שָׂרָה, בּרֵאשִׁית רַבָּה רְמָזוֹ בְּמִקְצָת:<br>
**16 [English]**	**and I will bless her.** with breast feeding, when she required it, on the day of Isaac’s feast, for people were murmuring against them, that they had brought a foundling from the street and were saying, “He is our son.” So each one brought her child with her, but not her wet nurse, and she (Sarah) nursed them all. That is what is said (below 21:7): “Sarah has nursed children.” Gen. Rabbah (53:9) alludes slightly to this. — [from B.M. 87a]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּפֹּל אַבְרָהָם עַל־פָּנָיו וַיִּצְחָק.** זֶה תַּרְגּוּם אוֹנְקֵלוֹס, לְשׁוֹן שִׂמְחָה, וַחֲדִי, וְשֶׁל שָׂרָה לְשׁוֹן מָחוֹךְ; לָמַדְתָּ שֶׁאַבְרָהָם הֶאֱמִין וְשָׂמַח וְשָׂרָה לֹא הֶאֱמִינָה וְלִגְלְגָה, וְזֶהוּ שֶׁהִקְפִּיד הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא עַל שָׂרָה וְלֹא הִקְפִּיד עַל אַבְרָהָם:<br>
**17 [English]**	**And Abraham fell on his face and rejoiced.** Heb. וַיִּצְחָק. Onkelos renders this as an expression of joy, וַחֲדִי “and he rejoiced,” but the one [וַתִּצְחָק] in the case of Sarah (below 18:12) [he renders] as an expression of laughter. You learn that Abraham believed and rejoiced, but Sarah did not believe and ridiculed, and for this reason, the Holy One, blessed be He, was angry with Sarah, but was not angry with Abraham.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**הַלְּבֶן.** יֵשׁ תְּמִיהוֹת שֶׁהֵן קַיָּמוֹת, כְּמוֹ הֲנִגְלֹה נִגְלֵיתִי? (שמואל א ב'), הֲרֹאֶה אַתָּה (יח' ח'), אַף זוֹ הִיא קַיֶּמֶת; וְכָךְ אָמַר בְּלִבּוֹ, הֲנַעֲשָׂה חֶסֶד זֶה לְאַחֵר, מַה שֶּׁהַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא עוֹשֶׂה לִי?<br>
**17 [English]**	**Will [a child] be born to on, etc..** There are questions which are positive assertions, like (I Sam. 2:27): הֲנִגְלֹה נִגְלֵיתִי, “Did I appear?” [meaning: “of course I appeared!”]; (II Sam. 15:27): הֲרֹאֶה אַתָּה, “Do you see?” [meaning: “of course you see!”] This too is a positive assertion, and so did he say to himself, “Was such kindness done to anyone else, that the Holy One, blessed be He, is doing for me?”

**17 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִם־שָׂרָה הֲבַת־תִּשְׁעִים שָׁנָה.** הָיְתָה כְדַאי לֵילֵד; וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁדּוֹרוֹת הָרִאשׁוֹנִים הָיוּ מוֹלִידִין בְּנֵי ת"ק שָׁנָה, בִּימֵי אַבְרָהָם נִתְמָעֲטוּ הַשָּׁנִים כְּבָר וּבָא תַּשּׁוּת כֹּחַ לָעוֹלָם, צֵא וּלְמַד מֵעֲשָׂרָה דּוֹרוֹת שֶׁמִּנֹּחַ וְעַד אַבְרָהָם, שֶׁמִּהֲרוּ תּוֹלְדוֹתֵיהֶן בְּנֵי ס' וּבְנֵי ע':<br>
**17 [English]**	**and will Sarah, who is ninety years old.** Shall she be worthy of giving birth? Now although the first generations begot children at the age of five hundred, in Abraham’s time, the years were already lessened, and weakness had come to the world. Go out and learn this from the ten generations from Noah to Abraham, who hastened to beget children at the age of sixty and seventy.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**לוּ יִשְׁמָעֵאל יִֽחְיֶה.** הַלְוַאי שֶׁיִּחְיֶה יִשְׁמָעֵאל; אֵינִי כְדַאי לְקַבֵּל מַתַּן שָׂכָָר כָּזֶה:<br>
**18 [English]**	**If only Ishmael will live.** If only Ishmael will live! I do not deserve to receive such a reward as this.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**יִֽחְיֶה לְפָנֶֽיךָ.** יִחְיֶה בְיִרְאָתֶךָ, כְּמוֹ הִתְהַלֵּךְ לְפָנַי – פְּלַח קֳדָמַי:<br>
**18 [English]**	**will live before You.** [This means]: [“Let him] live in fear of You,” as in (verse 1): “Walk before Me,” [which Onkelos renders:] “Serve Me.” [following Targum Jonathan]



**19 [Hebrew]**	**אֲבָל.** לְשׁוֹן אֲמִתַּת דְּבָרִים, וְכֵן אֲבָל אֲשֵׁמִים אֲנַחְנוּ (בראשית מ"ב), אֲבָל בֵּן אֵין לָהּ (מלכים ב ד'):<br>
**19 [English]**	**Indeed.** אֲבָל is an expression of a confirmation of a statement, and likewise (below 42:21): “Indeed (אֲבָל), we are guilty”; (II Kings 4:14): “Indeed (אֲבָל), she has no son.” - [from Targumim]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְקָרָאתָ אֶת־שְׁמוֹ יִצְחָק.** עַל שֵׁם הַצְּחוֹק, וְיֵשׁ אוֹמָרִים עַל שֵׁם הָעֲשָׂרָה נִסְיוֹנוֹת וצ' שָׁנָה שֶׁל שָׂרָה וח' יָמִים שֶׁנִּמּוֹל, וק' שָׁנָה שֶׁל אַבְרָהָם וַהֲקִמֹתִי אֶת־בְּרִיתִי אִתּוֹ לָמָּה נֶאֱמַר, כְּבָר כְּתִיב וְאַתָּה אֶת בְּרִיתִי תִשְׁמֹר אַתָּה וְזַרְעֲךָ וְגוֹ'? אֶלָּא לְפִי שֶׁאוֹמֵר וַהֲקִמוֹתִי וְגוֹ', יָכוֹל בְּנֵי יִשְׁמָעֵאל וּבְנֵי קְטוּרָה בִּכְלַל הַקִּיּוּם, תַּלְמוּד לוֹמָר וַהֲקִמֹתִי אֶת בְּרִיתִי אִתּוֹ – וְלֹא עִם אֲחֵרִים וְאֶת בְּרִיתִי אקים אֶת־יִצְחָק לָמָּה נֶאֱמַר? אֶלָּא לִמֵּד שֶׁהָיָה קָדוֹשׁ מִבֶּטֶן. דָּבָר אַחֵר אָמַר רַ' אַבָּא, מִכָּאן לָמַד קַל וָחֹמֶר בֶּן הַגְּבִירָה מִבֶּן הָאָמָה; כְּתִיב הִנֵּה בֵּרַכְתִּי אֹתוֹ וְהִפְרֵיתִי אֹתוֹ וְהִרְבֵּיתִי אֹתוֹ, זֶה יִשְׁמָעֵאל, וְקַל וָחֹמֶר וְאֶת בְּרִיתִי אָקִים אֶת יִצְחָק:<br>
**19 [English]**	**and you shall name him Isaac.** Heb. יִצְחָק, because of the rejoicing (צְחוֹק) (Mid. Chaseroth v’Yetheroth). And some say: because of the ten (י) trials, and Sarah’s ninety (צ) years, and the eighth (ח) day on which he was circumcised, and Abraham’s hundred (ק) years. (Pirkei d’Rabbi Eliezer , ch. 32). (Other editions: “And My covenant.” Why is this written? Is it not already written (verse 9): “And you shall keep My covenant, you and your seed, etc.?” But because He said (verse 7): “And I will establish, etc.,” one might think that the sons of Ishmael and the sons of Keturah are included in the establishment [of the covenant]. Therefore, Scripture states: “And I will establish My covenant with him,” and not with others. Now, why does it say [again in verse 21]: “But My covenant I will establish with Isaac?” This teaches us that he was holy from the womb. Another explanation [for the repetition of verse 19]: Said Rabbi Abba: Scripture here derives an a fortiori conclusion regarding the son of the mistress from [what is written regarding] the son of the handmaid. It is written here: “Behold I have blessed him, and I will make him fruitful, and I will multiply him.” This refers to Ishmael. How much more so, “But My covenant I will establish with Isaac!” (Gen. Rabbah 47:5).

**19 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת בְּרִיתִי.** בְּרִית הַמִּילָה תְּהֵא מְסוּרָה לְזַרְעוֹ שֶׁל יִצְחָק:<br>
**19 [English]**	**My covenant.** The covenant of circumcision shall be given over [only] to the seed of Isaac. See Sanh. 59.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁנֵֽים־עָשָׂר נשיאים.** כַּעֲנָנִים יִכְלוּ; כְּמוֹ נְשִׂיאִים וְרוּחַ (משלי כ"ה):<br>
**20 [English]**	**twelve princes.** Heb. נְשִׂיאִים. They will disappear like clouds, as (Prov. 25:14): Clouds (נְשִׂיאִים) and wind. — [from Gen. Rabbah 47:5]



**22 [Hebrew]**	**מֵעַל אַבְרָהָֽם.** לְשׁוֹן נְקִיָּה הוּא כְּלַפֵּי שְׁכִינָה, וְלָמַדְנוּ שֶׁהַצַּדִּיקִים מֶרְכַּבְתּוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם (בראשית רבה):<br>
**22 [English]**	**from above Abraham.** This is a euphemism used in reference to the Shechinah, and we learn that the righteous are the chariot of the Omnipresent. — [from Gen. Rabbah 47:6, 82:6]



**23 [Hebrew]**	**בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם.** בּוֹ בַּיּוֹם שֶׁנִּצְטַוָּה, בַּיּוֹם וְלֹא בַּלַּיְלָה; לֹא נִתְיָרֵא לֹא מִן הַגּוֹיִם וְלֹא מִן הַלֵּצָנִים, וְשֶׁלֹּא יִהְיוּ אוֹיְבָיו וּבְנֵי דּוֹרוֹ אוֹמְרִים אִלּוּ רְאִינוּהוּ לֹא הִנַּחְנוּהוּ לָמוּל וּלְקַיֵּם מִצְוָתוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם:<br>
**23 [English]**	**on that very day.** On the very day that he was commanded (Mid. Ps. 112:2), during the day and not at night. He was afraid neither of the heathens nor of the scorners. [He circumcised in the light of day] so that his enemies and his contemporaries would not say, “Had we seen him, we would not have allowed him to circumcise and to fulfill the commandment of the Omnipresent” (Gen. Rabbah 47:9).

**23 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּמָל.** לְשׁוֹן וַיִּפְעַל:<br>
**23 [English]**	**and he circumcised.** Heb. וַיָמָל, an expression in the וַיִפְעַל form, (the active [kal] form.)



**24 [Hebrew]**	**בְּהִמֹּלוֹ.** בְּהִפָּעֲלוֹ, כְּמוֹ בְּהִבָּרְאָם. נָטַל אַבְרָהָם סַכִּין וְאָחַז בְּעָרְלָתוֹ וְרָצָה
לַחְתֹּךְ וְהָיָה מִתְיָרֵא, שֶׁהָיָה זָקֵן, מֶה עָשָׂה הַקָּדֹושׁ
בָּרוּךְ הוּא, שָׁלַח יָדוֹ וְאָחַז עִמּוֹ, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר : "וְכָרֹות עִמּוֹ
הַבְּרִית“, "לוֹ“ לֹא נֶאֱמַר אֶלָּא "עִמּוֹ“. בְּרֵאשִׁית רַבָּה :<br>
**24 [English]**	**when he was circumcised.** Heb. בְּהִמֹּלוֹ, when it was done to him, like (above 2:4): “when they were created (בְּהִבָּרְאָם).”



**25 [Hebrew]**	**בהמולו אֵת בְּשַׂר עָרְלָתֽוֹ.** בְּאַבְרָהָם לֹא נֶאֱמַר "אֵת", לְפִי שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה חָסֵר אֶלָּא חִתּוּךְ בָּשָׂר, שֶׁכְּבָר נִתְמָעֵךְ עַל יְדֵי תַשְׁמִישׁ; אֲבָל יִשְׁמָעֵאל שֶׁהָיָה יֶלֶד, הֻזְקַק לַחְתֹּךְ עָרְלָה וְלִפְרֹעַ הַמִּילָה, לְכָךְ נֶאֱמַר בּוֹ "אֵת" (בראשית רבה):<br>
**25 [English]**	**when he was circumcised of the flesh of his foreskin.** Concerning Abraham, it does not say אֵת, because he was lacking only the severing of the flesh, because it had already been flattened out by intercourse, but Ishmael, who was a youth, required that the foreskin be severed and the corona be uncovered. Therefore, in his case, it is אֵת. Gen. Rabbah (47:8).



**26 [Hebrew]**	**בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה.** שֶׁמָּלְאוּ לְאַבְרָהָם צ"ט שָׁנָה וּלְיִשְׁמָעֵאל י"ג שָׁנִים נמול אברהם וישמעאל בנו:<br>
**26 [English]**	**On that very day.** when Abraham reached the age of ninety-nine and Ishmael [reached the age of] thirteen, “Abraham was circumcised, and [so was] Ishmael his son.”
