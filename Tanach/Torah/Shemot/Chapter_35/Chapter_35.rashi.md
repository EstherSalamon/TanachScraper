**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּקְהֵל משֶׁה.** לְמָחֳרַת יוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים כְּשֶׁיָּרַד מִן הָהָר, וְהוּא לְשׁוֹן הִפְעִיל, שֶׁאֵינוֹ אוֹסֵף אֲנָשִׁים בְּיָּדַיִם, אֶלָּא הֵן נֶאֱסָפִין עַל פִּי דִּבּוּרוֹ, וְתַרְגּוּמוֹ וְאַכְנֵישׁ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**Moses called… to assemble.** Heb. וַיַּקְהֵל. [He assembled them] on the day after Yom Kippur, when he came down from the mountain. This [word] is a hiph’il [causative] expression [i.e., causing someone to do something], because one does not assemble people with [one’s] hands [i.e., directly], but they are assembled through one’s speech. Its Aramaic translation is וְאַכְנֵשׁ.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים.** הִקְדִּים לָהֶם אַזְהָרַת שַׁבָּת לְצִוּוּי מְלֶאכֶת הַמִּשְׁכָּן, לוֹמַר שֶׁאֵינוֹ דוֹחֶה אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת (מכילתא):<br>
**2 [English]**	**Six days.** He [Moses] prefaced [the discussion of the details of] the work of the Mishkan with the warning to keep the Sabbath, denoting that it [i.e., the work of the Mishkan] does not supersede the Sabbath. -[from Mechilta]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־תְבַֽעֲרוּ אֵשׁ.** יֵשׁ מֵרַבּוֹתֵינוּ אוֹמְרִים הַבְעָרָה לְלָאו יָצָאת, וְיֵשׁ אוֹמְרִים לְחַלֵּק יָצָאת (שבת ע'):<br>
**3 [English]**	**You shall not kindle fire.** Some of our Rabbis say that [the prohibition of] kindling was singled out for a [mere] negative commandment, while others say that it was singled out to separate [all types of labor]. -[from Shab. 70a]



**4 [Hebrew]**	**זֶה הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר־צִוָּה ה'.** לִי לאמר לָכֶם:<br>
**4 [English]**	**This is the word that the Lord has commanded.** me to say to you.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**נְדִיב לִבּוֹ.** עַל שֵׁם שֶׁלִּבּוֹ נוֹדְבוֹ, קָרוּי נְדִיב לֵב. כְּבָר פֵּרַשְׁתִּי נִדְבַת הַמִּשְׁכָּן וּמְלַאכְתּוֹ בִּמְקוֹם צַוָּאתָן:<br>
**5 [English]**	**generous-hearted person.** Heb. נְדִיב לִבּוֹ. Since his heart moved him to generosity, he is called “generous-hearted” (נְדִיב לֵב). I already explained the offering for the Mishkan and its work in the place of their command [Exod. 25 through 34].



**11 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־הַמִּשְׁכָּן.** יְרִיעוֹת הַתַּחְתּוֹנוֹת הַנִּרְאוֹת בְּתוֹכוֹ קְרוּיִים מִשְׁכָּן:<br>
**11 [English]**	**The Mishkan.** The bottom curtains, which appear inside it [the Mishkan], are called Mishkan.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־אָֽהֳלוֹ.** הִיא אֹהֶל יְרִיעוֹת עִזִּים, הֶעָשׂוּי לְגַג:<br>
**11 [English]**	**its tent.** That is the tent [made] of the curtains of goat hair, made for a roof.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־מִכְסֵהוּ.** מִכְסֵה עוֹרוֹת אֵילִים וְהַתְּחָשִׁים:<br>
**11 [English]**	**and its cover.** The cover of ram skins and tachash skins.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵת פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָֽךְ.** פָּרֹכֶת הַמְּחִיצָה; כָּל דָּבָר הַמֵּגֵן, בֵּין מִלְמַעְלָה בֵּין מִכְּנֶגֶד, קָרוּי מָסָךְ וּסְכָךְ, וְכֵן סַכְתָּ בַעֲדוֹ (איוב א'), הִנְנִי סָךְ אֶת דַּרְכֵּךְ (הושע ב'):<br>
**12 [English]**	**and the screening dividing curtain.** Heb. פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ. The dividing curtain, [which serves as a] screen. Anything that protects, whether from above or from the front, is called a screen (מָסָךְ) or a cover (סְכָךְ). Similarly, “You made a hedge (שַׂכְתָּ) about him” (Job 1:10); “behold I will close off (שָׂךְ) your way” (Hos. 2:8).



**13 [Hebrew]**	**לֶחֶם הַפָּנִֽים.** כְּבָר פֵּרַשְׁתִּי, עַל שֵׁם שֶׁהָיוּ לוֹ פָּנִים לְכָאן וּלְכָאן, שֶׁהוּא עָשׂוּי כְּמִין תֵּבָה פְרוּצָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**the showbread.** I already explained (Exod. 25:29) that it was called לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים because it had faces [i.e., surfaces] looking in both directions, for it was made like a type of box, without a cover.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־כֵּלֶיהָ.** מֶלְקָחַיִם וּמַחְתּוֹת:<br>
**14 [English]**	**and its implements.** Its tongs and its scoops.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**נֵֽרֹתֶיהָ.** לוצי"יש בְּלַעַז, בָּזִיכִים שֶׁהַשֶּׁמֶן וְהַפְּתִילוֹת נְתוּנִין בָּהֶן:<br>
**14 [English]**	**its lamps.** Ses luzes, lozes in Old French, spoons in which the oil and the wicks are placed.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵת שֶׁמֶן הַמָּאוֹר.** אַף הוּא צָרִיךְ חַכְמֵי לֵב, שֶׁהוּא מְשֻׁנֶּה מִשְּׁאָר שְׁמָנִים, כְּמוֹ שֶׁמְּפֹרָשׁ בִּמְנָחוֹת (דף פ"ו), מְגַרְגְּרוֹ בְרֹאשׁ הַזַּיִת, וְהוּא כָּתִית וְזַךְ:<br>
**14 [English]**	**and the oil for lighting.** That too required wise-hearted [people] because it was different from other oils, as is explained in Menachoth (86a): he picks it [the olives] at the top of the olive tree, and it is crushed and pure.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**מָסַךְ הַפֶּתַח.** וִילוֹן שֶׁלִּפְנֵי הַמִּזְרָח, שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ שָׁם קְרָשִׁים וְלֹא יְרִיעוֹת:<br>
**15 [English]**	**and the screen of the entrance.** The screen in front of the eastern side, for there were no planks or curtains there.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**אֵת עַמֻּדָיו וְאֶת־אֲדָנֶיהָ.** הֲרֵי חָצֵר קָרוּי כָּאן לְשׁוֹן זָכָר וּלְשׁוֹן נְקֵבָה, וְכֵן דְּבָרִים הַרְבֵּה:<br>
**17 [English]**	**its pillars, and its sockets.** Heb. אֶת-עַמֻּדָיו וְאֶת-אִדָנֶיהָ. Thus “courtyard” (חָצֵר) is referred to here both as masculine and feminine [since עַמֻּדָיו is a masculine possessive and אִדָנֶיהָ is a feminine possessive], and so are many [other] nouns.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־מָסַךְ שַׁעַר הֶֽחָצֵר.** וִילוֹן פָּרוּשׂ לְצַד הַמִּזְרָח עֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה אֶמְצָעִיּוֹת שֶׁל רֹחַב הֶחָצֵר – שֶׁהָיָה חֲמִשִּׁים רֹחַב – וּסְתוּמִין הֵימֶנּוּ לְצַד צָפוֹן ט"ו אַמָּה, וְכֵן לַדָּרוֹם, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר, וַחֲמֵשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה קְלָעִים לַכָּתֵף:<br>
**17 [English]**	**and the screen of the gate of the courtyard.** The screen spread out on the eastern side, [covering] the middle twenty cubits of the width of the courtyard, for it [the courtyard] was fifty cubits wide, and fifteen cubits of it toward the northern side were closed off, and similarly toward the south. As it is said: “The hangings on the shoulder [shall be] fifteen cubits” (Exod. 27:14).



**18 [Hebrew]**	**יִתְדֹת.** לִתְקֹעַ וְלִקְשׁוֹר בָּהֶם סוֹפֵי הַיְרִיעוֹת בָּאָרֶץ, שֶׁלֹּא יָנוּעוּ בָּרוּחַ:<br>
**18 [English]**	**the pegs.** [used] to drive [into the ground] and to tie the ends of the curtains with them into the ground, so that they [the curtains] would not move with the wind.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**מֵֽיתְרֵיהֶֽם.** חֲבָלִים לִקְשֹׁר:<br>
**18 [English]**	**and their ropes.** Heb. מֵיתְרֵיהֶם, ropes [used] to tie [the curtains].



**19 [Hebrew]**	**בִּגְדֵי הַשְּׂרָד.** לְכַסּוֹת הָאָרוֹן וְהַשֻּׁלְחָן, הַמְּנוֹרָה וְהַמִּזְבְּחוֹת בִּשְׁעַת סִלּוּק מַסָּעוֹת:<br>
**19 [English]**	**the meshwork garments.** to cover the ark, the table, the menorah, and the altars when they [the Israelites] would leave for their travels.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־הַנָּשִׁים.** עִם הַנָּשִׁים וּסְמוּכִין אֲלֵיהֶן:<br>
**22 [English]**	**with the women.** Heb. עַל הַנָּשִׁים, lit., [the jewelry was still] on the women. The men came with the women and [stood] near them. (The reason the Targum [Onkelos] left the passage in its simple sense is that he does not render וַיָּבֹאוּ הָאִנָשִׁים as וַאִתוֹ גַבְרַיָא, and the men came, but he renders: וּמַיְתַן, [and the men] brought, meaning that they brought bracelets and earrings while they were still on [i.e., being worn by] the women, as Rashi writes on “spun the goat hair” (verse 26), [which signifies that the women spun the hair while it was still on the goats].)

**22 [Hebrew]**	**חָח.** הוּא תַּכְשִׁיט שֶׁל זָהָב עָגֹל, נָתוּן עַל הַזְּרוֹעַ, וְהוּא הַצָּמִיד:<br>
**22 [English]**	**bracelets.** Heb. חָח. This is a round golden ornament placed on the arm, and it is the צָמִיד.

**22 [Hebrew]**	**וְכוּמָז.** כְּלִי זָהָב הוּא נָתוּן כְּנֶגֶד אוֹתוֹ מָקוֹם לָאִשָּׁה, וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ פֵּרְשׁוּ שֵׁם כּוּמָז, כָּאן מְקוֹם זִמָּה (שבת ס"ד):<br>
**22 [English]**	**and buckles.** Heb. וְכוּמָז. This is a golden ornament placed over a woman’s private parts. Our Rabbis explain the name כּוּמָז as [an acrostic]: כַּאן מְקוֹם זִמָּה, [meaning] here is the place of lewdness. -[from Shab. 64a]



**23 [Hebrew]**	**וְכָל־אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר־נִמְצָא אִתּוֹ.** תְּכֵלֶת אוֹ אַרְגָּמָן אוֹ תוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי אוֹ עוֹרוֹת אֵילִים אוֹ תְּחָשִׁים כֻּלָּם הֵבִיאוּ:<br>
**23 [English]**	**And every man with whom was found.** Blue wool or purple wool or crimson wool or ram skins or tachash skins, all brought [them].



**26 [Hebrew]**	**טָווּ אֶת־הָֽעִזִּֽים.** הִיא הָיְתָה אָמָּנוּת יְתֵרָה, שֶׁמֵּעַל גַּבֵּי הָעִזִּים טוֹוִין אוֹתָם (שבת ע"ד):<br>
**26 [English]**	**spun the goat hair.** This constituted a superior skill, for they [the women] spun it on the backs of the goats. -[from Shab. 74b]



**27 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַנְּשִׂאִם הֵבִיאוּ.** אָמַר רַ' נָתָן: מָה רָאוּ נְשִׂיאִים לְהִתְנַדֵּב בַּחֲנֻכַּת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ בַּתְּחִלָּה וּבִמְלֶאכֶת הַמִּשְׁכָּן לֹא הִתְנַדְּבוּ בַּתְּחִלָּה? אֶלָּא כָּךְ אָמְרוּ נְשִׂיאִים, יִתְנַדְּבוּ צִבּוּר מַה שֶּׁמִּתְנַדְּבִין, וּמַה שֶּׁמְּחַסְּרִים, אָנוּ מַשְׁלִימִין אוֹתוֹ, כֵּיוָן שֶׁהִשְׁלִימוּ צִבּוּר אֶת הַכֹּל – שֶׁנֶּ' וְהַמְּלָאכָה הָיְתָה דַיָּם – אָמְרוּ נְשִׂיאִים מֶה עָלֵינוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת? הביאו את אבני השהם וְגוֹ', לְכָךְ הִתְנַדְּבוּ בַּחֲנֻכַּת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ תְּחִלָּה, וּלְפִי שֶׁנִּתְעַצְּלוּ מִתְּחִלָּה נֶחְסְרָה אוֹת מִשְּׁמָם, וְהַנְּשִׂאִם כְּתִיב (ספרי במדבר מ"ה):<br>
**27 [English]**	**And the princes brought.** Heb. וְהַנְשִׂיאִם. Rabbi Nathan said: What prompted the princes [lit., what did the princes see] to donate for the dedication of the altar first [before the rest of the Israelites] while [in contrast] they did not donate first for the work of the Mishkan? This is what the princes said, “Let the community donate what they will donate, and what[ever] they are missing [i.e., whatever is left to be donated] we will complete.” Since the community completed everything, as it is said: “And the work was sufficient” (Exod. 36:7), the princes said, “What are we to do?” So they brought the shoham stones, etc. Therefore, they brought [donations] first for the dedication of the altar. Since at first they were lazy [i.e., they did not immediately donate], a letter is missing from their name, and וְהַנְשִׂיאִם is written [instead of וְהַנְשִׂיאִים, with additional “yud” s]. [from Num. Rabbah 12:16, Sifrei Num. 7:2, Midrash Chaseroth V’Yetheroth p. 268, Midrash Tanchuma Pekudei 11]



**30 [Hebrew]**	**חוּר.** בְּנָהּ שֶׁל מִרְיָם הָיָה:<br>
**30 [English]**	**Hur.** He was Miriam’s son. -[from Sotah 11b]



**34 [Hebrew]**	**וְאָֽהֳלִיאָב.** מִשֵּׁבֶט דָּן מִן הַיְרוּדִין שֶׁבַּשְּׁבָטִים – מִבְּנֵי הַשְּׁפָחוֹת – וְהִשְׁוָהוּ הַמָּקוֹם לִבְצַלְאֵל לִמְלֶאכֶת הַמִּשְׁכָּן וְהוּא מִגְּדוֹלֵי הַשְּׁבָטִים, לְקַיֵּם מַה שֶּׁנֶּ' וְלֹא נִכַּר שׁוֹעַ לִפְנֵי דָל (איוב ל"ד):<br>
**34 [English]**	**and Oholiab.** of the tribe of Dan, of the lowest of the tribes, of the sons of the handmaidens [Bilhah and Zilpah. Dan was Bilhah’s son]. Yet the Omnipresent compared him [Oholiab] to Bezalel for the work of the Mishkan, and he [Bezalel] was of the greatest of the tribes [Judah], to fulfill what is said: “and a prince was not recognized before a poor man” (Job 34:19). -[from Tanchuma 13]
