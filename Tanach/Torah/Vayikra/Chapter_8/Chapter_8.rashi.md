**2 [English]**	**Take Aaron.** This section was stated seven days before the erection of the Sanctuary, [and should have been stated earlier in Exod. Parashath Pekudei , which discusses the erection and consecration processes. [However], there is no [sequence of] earlier and later events in the Torah [i.e., Scripture does not always follow chronological order].

**2 [Hebrew]**	**קַח אֶת־אַֽהֲרֹן.** קָחֶנּוּ בִדְבָרִים וּמָשְׁכֵהוּ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**Take Aaron.** Take him over with [persuasive] words and attract him. — [Torath Kohanim 8:165]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־פַּר הַֽחַטָּאת וגו'.** אֵלּוּ הָאֲמוּרִים בְּעִנְיַן צַוָּאַת הַמִּלּוּאִים בִּוְאַתָּה תְּצַוֶּה, וְעַכְשָׁיו בְּיוֹם רִאשׁוֹן לַמִּלּוּאִים חָזַר וְזֵרְזוֹ בִשְׁעַת מַעֲשֶֹה:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and the sin-offering bull [and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread].** [Which sin-offering bull, two rams, etc?] These are the ones mentioned in the section dealing with the command of the investitures in [parashath] וְאַתָּה תְּצַוֶּה, (Exod. Chapter 29), and now, on the first day of the investitures, He came back and urged him [in the matter] at the time they were to be put into practice.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**הַקְהֵל אֶל־פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵֽד.** זֶה אֶחָד מִן הַמְּקוֹמוֹת שֶׁהֶחֱזִיק מֻעָט אֶת הַמְרֻבֶּה (ויקרא רבה י'):<br>
**3 [English]**	**Assemble [the entire community] at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.** This is one of the instances where a small [area] accommodated a large [number of people]. — [Lev. Rabbah 10:9]



**5 [Hebrew]**	**זֶה הַדָּבָר.** דְּבָרִים שֶׁתִּרְאוּ שֶׁאֲנִי עוֹשֶׂה לִפְנֵיכֶם, צִוַּנִי הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא לַעֲשׂוֹת, וְאַל תֹּאמְרוּ לִכְבוֹדִי וְלִכְבוֹד אָחִי אֲנִי עוֹשֶֹׁה; כָּל הָעִנְיָן הַזֶּה פֵּרַשְׁתִּי בְּ"וְאַתָּה תְּצַוֶּה":<br>
**5 [English]**	**This is the thing [the Lord has commanded to do].** [Moses said to Israel:] “The things you will see me doing before you, have all been commanded to me by the Holy One, blessed is He, that they be done; so do not say that I am doing them for my own honor or for my brother’s honor!” I have explained the entire passage involving the investitures in [parashath] וְאַתָּה תְּצַוֶּה (Exod. 29).



**8 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־הָֽאוּרִים.** כְּתָב שֶׁל שֵׁם הַמְפֹרָשׁ:<br>
**8 [English]**	**The Urim.** An inscription bearing the explicit Name of God.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּשֶׂם עַל־הַמִּצְנֶפֶת.** פְּתִילֵי תְכֵלֶת הַקְּבוּעִים בַּצִּיץ נָתַן עַל הַמִּצְנֶפֶת, נִמְצָא הַצִּיץ תָּלוּי בַּמִּצְנֶפֶת:<br>
**9 [English]**	**and he placed on the cap.** The sky-blue cords affixed to the showplate he placed over the cap. Thus the showplate was suspended on (עַל) the cap.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּז מִמֶּנּוּ עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ.** לֹא יָדַעְתִּי הֵיכָן נִצְטַוָּה בַּהַזָּאוֹת הַלָּלוּ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**And he sprinkled from it upon the altar.** I do not know where [in Scripture] he was commanded to perform these sprinklings.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּצֹק וַיִּמְשַׁח.** בַּתְּחִלָּה יוֹצֵק עַל רֹאשׁוֹ וְאַחַר כָּךְ נוֹתֵן בֵּין רִיסֵי עֵינָיו וּמוֹשֵׁךְ בְּאֶצְבָּעוֹ מִזֶּה לָזֶה (הוריות י"ב):<br>
**12 [English]**	**And he poured…and anointed [him].** At first, he [Moses] poured [the oil] on his [Aaron’s] head, and afterwards, he placed it between his eyelids, and drew it with his finger, from one [eyelid] to the other. — [Ker. 5b]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּֽחֲבשׁ.** לְשׁוֹן קְשִׁירָה:<br>
**13 [English]**	**and bound them up.** Heb. וַיַּחִבשׁ, an expression denoting binding.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְחַטֵּא אֶת־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ.** חִטְּאוֹ וְטִהֲרוֹ מִזָּרוּת לִכָּנֵס לִקְדֻשָּׁה:<br>
**15 [English]**	**and he purified the altar.** He cleansed and purified it [to convert it] from its alien state, into holiness.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְקַדְּשֵׁהוּ.** בַּעֲבוֹדָה זוֹ:<br>
**15 [English]**	**and sanctified it.** with this service.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**לְכַפֵּר עָלָֽיו.** מֵעַתָּה כָּל הַכַּפָּרוֹת:<br>
**15 [English]**	**to effect atonement upon it.** [i.e., to effect] all atonements from now on.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**הַכָּבֵד.** לְבַד הַכָּבֵד, שֶׁהָיָה נוֹטֵל מְעַט מִן הַכָּבֵד עִמָּהּ:<br>
**16 [English]**	**and the diaphragm of the liver.** Besides the liver, [meaning] that he took a little of the liver along with the diaphragm.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**אֵיל הַמִּלֻּאִים.** אֵיל הַשְּׁלָמִים, שֶׁמִּלּוּאִים לְשׁוֹן שְׁלָמִים, שֶׁמְּמַלְּאִין וּמַשְׁלִימִין אֶת הַכֹּהֲנִים בִּכְהֻנָּתָם:<br>
**22 [English]**	**the ram of the investitures.** Heb. אֵיל הַמִּלֻּאִים. [This expression is equivalent to] אֵיל הַשְּׁלָמִים, “the completion ram” [i.e., the word מִלֻּאִים does not mean “inauguration,” but rather,] it denotes שְׁלָמִים, for these rams filled (מְמַלְּאִים) and completed (מַשְׁלִימִים) the [status of the] kohanim in their kehunah. — [see Torat Kohanim 8:184]



**26 [Hebrew]**	**וְחַלַּת לֶחֶם שֶׁמֶן.** הִיא רְבוּכָה שֶׁהָיָה מַרְבֶּה בָּהּ שֶׁמֶן כְּנֶגֶד הַחַלּוֹת וְהָרְקִיקִין, כָּךְ מְפֹרָשׁ בִּמְנָחוֹת (דף פ"ט):<br>
**26 [English]**	**and one loaf of oily bread.** This refers to the רְבוּכָה, the loaves made by scalding [the dough] with boiling water, [and are called “oily bread,”] because a large quantity of oil was used for it, equal to that used for the loaves and the wafers combined. Thus it is explained in [Tractate] Men. (78a; 89a).



**28 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּקְטֵר הַמִּזְבֵּחָה.** מֹשֶׁה שִׁמֵּשׁ כָּל שִׁבְעַת יְמֵי הַמִּלּוּאִים בְּחָלוּק לָבָן (עבודה זרה ל"ד):<br>
**28 [English]**	**[And Moses…] caused them to [go up in] smoke on the altar.** Moses performed the service throughout all the seven days of investitures, [dressed] in a white robe. — [A.Z. 34a]

**28 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־הָֽעֹלָה.** אַחַר הָעוֹלָה, וְלֹא מָצִינוּ שׁוֹק שֶׁל שְׁלָמִים קָרֵב בְּכָל מָקוֹם חוּץ מִזֶּה:<br>
**28 [English]**	**along with the burnt-offering.** after [he had burnt] the burnt-offering. With the exception of this one, we do not find anywhere [in Scripture], [a case of] a thigh of a peace-offering being offered up [on the altar].



**34 [Hebrew]**	**צִוָּה ה' לַֽעֲשׂת.** כָּל שִׁבְעַת הַיָּמִים; וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ דָרְשׁוּ, לעשת — זֶה מַעֲשֵׂה פָרָה, לכפר — זֶה מַעֲשֵׂה יוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים, וּלְלַמֵּד שֶׁכֹּהֵן גָּדוֹל טָעוּן פְּרִישָׁה קֹדֶם יוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים שִׁבְעַת יָמִים, וְכֵן הַכֹּהֵן הַשּׂוֹרֵף אֶת הַפָּרָה (ספרא):<br>
**34 [English]**	**so the Lord has commanded to do.** on all the seven days [of investitures]. And our Rabbis expounded on the verse as follows: [The word] לַעִשׂת alludes to the procedure involving the “red cow”; (see Num. Chapter 19) [the word] לְכַפֵּר alludes to the service of Yom Kippur. And this comes to teach us that [just as there were seven days of the investitures, so too,] the Kohen Gadol [who performed the service on Yom Kippur,] was required to separate [from his home] seven days before Yom Kippur, and so was the kohen who performed the burning of the “red cow.”



**35 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תָמוּתוּ.** הָא אִם לֹא תַעֲשׂוּ כֵן הֲרֵי אַתֶּם חַיָּבִים מִיתָה:<br>
**35 [English]**	**So that you will not die.** But if you do not do so, you incur the death penalty.



**36 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּעַשׂ אַֽהֲרֹן וּבָנָיו.** לְהַגִּיד שִׁבְחָן, שֶׁלֹא הִטּוּ יָמִין וּשְֹמֹאל:<br>
**36 [English]**	**And Aaron and his sons did [all the things].** To tell their praise, namely, that they did not deviate to the right or to the left.
