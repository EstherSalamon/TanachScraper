**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמֶר ה' אֶל־אַֽהֲרֹן.** לְמֹשֶׁה אָמַר שֶׁיֹּאמַר לְאַהֲרֹן, לְהַזְהִירוֹ עַל תַּקָּנַת יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁלֹּא יִכָּנְסוּ לַמִּקְדָּשׁ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**The Lord said to Aaron.** [God did not say directly to Aaron but] He said [this] to Moses to say to Aaron (Sifrei Korach 17) and caution him regarding regulations for the [benefit of the] Israelites, that they should not enter the Sanctuary.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**אַתָּה וּבָנֶיךָ וּבֵית־אָבִיךָ.** הֵם בְּנֵי קְהָת אֲבִי עַמְרָם:<br>
**1 [English]**	**You, your sons and your father’s house.** They are the sons of Kohath, who was Amram’s father. — [Midrash Aggadah]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**תִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־עֲוֹן הַמִּקְדָּשׁ.** עֲלֵיכֶם אֲנִי מַטִּיל עֹנֶשׁ הַזָּרִים שֶׁיֶּחֶטְאוּ בְעִסְקֵי הַדְּבָרִים הַמְקֻדָּשִׁים הַמְּסוּרִים לָכֶם, הוּא הָאֹהֶל וְהָאָרוֹן וְהַשֻּׁלְחָן וּכְלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ — אַתֶּם תֵּשְׁבוּ וְתַזְהִירוּ עַל כָּל זָר הַבָּא לִגַּע:<br>
**1 [English]**	**Shall bear the iniquity associated with the Sanctuary.** I impose upon you the punishment of the outsiders who sin regarding the sacred objects entrusted to you; the Tent, the ark, the table, and the sacred vessels. You shall sit and warn any unauthorized person who attempts to touch [the sacred objects].

**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַתָּה וּבָנֶיךָ.** הַכֹּהֲנִים:<br>
**1 [English]**	**and you and your sons.** The kohanim.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**תִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־עֲוֹן כְּהֻנַּתְכֶֽם.** שֶׁאֵינָהּ מְסוּרָה לַלְוִיִּם, וְתַזְהִירוּ הַלְוִיִּם הַשּׁוֹגְגִים שֶׁלֹּא יִגְּעוּ אֲלֵיכֶם בַּעֲבוֹדַתְכֶם:<br>
**1 [English]**	**shall bear the sin associated with your kehunah.** For it is not given over to the Levites. You shall warn the Levites who might inadvertently err, that they may not touch you during your [performance of the] service.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְגַם אַחֶיךָ.** בְּנֵי גֵרְשׁוֹן וּבְנֵי מְרָרִי:<br>
**2 [English]**	**Also… your brethren.** The sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari. — [Midrash Aggadah]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְיִלָּווּ.** וְיִתְחַבְּרוּ אֲלֵיכֶם, לְהַזְהִיר גַּם אֶת הַזָּרִים מִלִּקְרַב אֲלֵיהֶם:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and they shall join you.** They shall join you to warn any outsiders not to approach them.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וִישָֽׁרְתוּךָ.** בִּשְׁמִירַת הַשְּׁעָרִים וּלְמַנּוֹת מֵהֶם גִּזְבָּרִין וַאֲמַרְכָּלִין (ספרי):<br>
**2 [English]**	**and they shall minister to you.** By watching the gates and serving as treasurers and supervisors.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**וְזָר לֹֽא־יִקְרַב אֲלֵיכֶֽם.** אֶתְכֶם אֲנִי מַזְהִיר עַל כָּךְ:<br>
**4 [English]**	**and no outsider shall approach you.** You I am cautioning about this.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא־יִֽהְיֶה עוֹד קֶצֶף.** כְּמוֹ שֶׁהָיָה כְבָר, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (במדבר י"ז) "כִּי יָצָא הַקֶּצֶף" (ספרי):<br>
**5 [English]**	**that there be no more wrath.** As there had been previously, as it says, “for wrath is gone forth” (17: 11). - [Sifrei Korach 11]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**לָכֶם מַתָּנָה נְתֻנִים.** יָכוֹל לַעֲבוֹדַתְכֶם שֶׁל הֶדְיוֹט, תַּלְמוּד לוֹמָר "לַה'", כְּמוֹ שֶׁמְּפֹרָשׁ לְמַעְלָה, לִשְׁמֹר מִשְׁמֶרֶת גִּזְבָּרִין וַאֲמַרְכָּלִין:<br>
**6 [English]**	**They are given to you as a gift.** I might think [that they are given to you] for your mundane work. Therefore, it says, “to the Lord” as explained above, to be responsible for the posts of treasurers and administrators. — [Sifrei Korach 12]



**7 [Hebrew]**	**עֲבֹדַת מַתָּנָה.** בְּמַתָּנָה נְתַתִּיהָ לָכֶם:<br>
**7 [English]**	**the service as a gift.** I have given it to you as a gift.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽאֲנִי הִנֵּה נָתַתִּי לְךָ.** בְּשִׂמְחָה, לְשׁוֹן שִׂמְחָה הוּא זֶה, כְּמוֹ "הִנֵּה הוּא יֹצֵא לִקְרָאתֶךָ וְרָאֲךָ וְשָׂמַח בְּלִבּוֹ" (שמות ד'); מָשָׁל לְמֶלֶךְ שֶׁנָּתַן שָׂדֶה לְאוֹהֲבוֹ, וְלֹא כָתַב וְלֹא חָתַם וְלֹא הֶעֱלָה בְעַרְכָּאִין, בָּא אֶחָד וְעִרְעֵר עַל הַשָּׂדֶה, אָמַר לוֹ הַמֶּלֶךְ כָּל מִי שֶׁיִּרְצֶה יָבֹא וִיעַרְעֵר לְנֶגְדְּךָ, הֲרֵינִי כוֹתֵב וְחוֹתֵם לְךָ וּמַעֲלֶה בְעַרְכָּאִין, אַף כָּאן, לְפִי שֶׁבָּא קֹרַח וְעִרְעֵר כְּנֶגֶד אַהֲרֹן עַל הַכְּהֻנָּה, בָּא הַכָּתוּב וְנָתַן לוֹ כ"ד מַתְּנוֹת כְּהֻנָּה בִּבְרִית מֶלַח עוֹלָם, וְלְכָךְ נִסְמְכָה פָרָשָׁה זוֹ לְכָאן (ספרי):<br>
**8 [English]**	**Behold I have given you.** with joy. It [the word “behold”] is an expression of joy, as in “Behold, he is coming forth toward you, and when he sees you, he will rejoice in his heart” (Exod. 4:14). This may be compared to a king who gave a field to his friend but did not write nor sign [a deed], and did not record it in court. A person came and contested [his ownership] of the field. The king said to him: [It seems that] anyone may come and contest your rights. Behold, I will write and sign [a deed] for you, and record it in court. Here, too, since Korah came and made a claim against Aaron regarding the kehunah, Scripture comes and gives him twenty-four ‘gifts’ of kehunah as an everlasting covenant of salt. This is why this section is placed here [after the rebellion of Korah]. — [Sifrei Korach 18, 19]

**8 [Hebrew]**	**מִשְׁמֶרֶת תְּרֽוּמֹתָי.** שֶׁאַתָּה צָרִיךְ לְשָׁמְרָן בְּטָהֳרָה:<br>
**8 [English]**	**the charge of My gift [offerings].** That you must keep them in a state of purity. - [Bech. 34a]

**8 [Hebrew]**	**לְמָשְׁחָה.** לִגְדֻלָּה (שם):<br>
**8 [English]**	**for distinction.** Heb. לְמָשְׁחָה, for greatness. — [Sifrei Korach 20, Chul. 132b]



**9 [Hebrew]**	**מִן־הָאֵשׁ.** לְאַחַר הַקְטָרַת הָאִשִּׁים:<br>
**9 [English]**	**from the fire.** After the burning of the sacrificial parts.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**כָּל־קָרְבָּנָם.** כְּגוֹן זִבְחֵי שַׁלְמֵי צִבּוּר:<br>
**9 [English]**	**All their offerings.** The communal peace-offerings.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**מִנְחָתָם חַטָּאתָם ואשמתם.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**Their meal-offerings, their sin-offerings, their guilt-offerings.** As the literal meaning indicates.[Sifrei Korach 21]

**9 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר יָשִׁיבוּ לִי.** זֶה גֶּזֶל הַגֵּר (שם; זבחים מ"ד):<br>
**9 [English]**	**what they return to Me.** This refers to stolen property of a proselyte. — [Sifrei Korach 21, Zev. 44b. See Rashi on Num. 5:8]



**10 [Hebrew]**	**בְּקֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים תֹּֽאכְלֶנּוּ וגו'.** לִמֵּד עַל קָדְשֵׁי קֳדָשִׁים שֶׁאֵין נֶאֱכָלִין אֶלָּא בָּעֲזָרָה לְזִכְרֵי כְהֻנָּה:<br>
**10 [English]**	**You shall eat it in the holiest of places….** This teaches us that [sacrifices] of the highest degree of holiness are to be eaten only in the courtyard and [only] by the male kohanim . — [Sifrei Korach 22]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**תְּרוּמַת מַתָּנָם.** הַמּוּרָם מִן הַתּוֹדָה וּמֵהַשְּׁלָמִים וּמֵאֵיל נָזִיר:<br>
**11 [English]**	**what is set aside for their gifts.** That is separated from the thanksgiving offering and from the peace-offering and from the Nazirite ram. - [Sifrei Korach 24]

**11 [Hebrew]**	**לְכָל־תְּנוּפֹת.** שֶׁהֲרֵי אֵלּוּ טְעוּנִין תְּנוּפָה:<br>
**11 [English]**	**All the wavings.** Since these require waving. - [Sifrei Korach 25]

**11 [Hebrew]**	**כָּל־טָהוֹר.** וְלֹא טְמֵאִים; דָּבָר אַחֵר: כָּל טָהוֹר לְרַבּוֹת אִשְׁתּוֹ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**Any [ritually] clean.** And not those who are ritually unclean (Sifrei Korach 25). Another interpretation: Anyone ritually clean, including his [the kohen’s] wife. — - [Sifrei Korach 29]



**12 [Hebrew]**	**רֵֽאשִׁיתָם.** הִיא תְּרוּמָה גְדוֹלָה:<br>
**12 [English]**	**the first.** This refers to terumah gedolah [the kohen’s portion of the produce separated by Israelites].



**18 [Hebrew]**	**כַּֽחֲזֵה הַתְּנוּפָה וּכְשׁוֹק הַיָּמִין.** שֶׁל שְׁלָמִים שֶׁנֶּאֱכָלִין לַכֹּהֲנִים וְלִנְשֵׁיהֶם לִבְנֵיהֶם וּלְעַבְדֵיהֶם לִשְׁנֵי יָמִים וְלַיְלָה אֶחָד, אַף הַבְּכוֹר נֶאֱכָל לִשְׁנֵי יָמִים וְלַיְלָה אֶחָד:<br>
**18 [English]**	**like the breast of the waving and the right thigh.** Of the peace-offering which is eaten by the kohanim, their wives, their children and their slaves for two days and one night. So may the firstborn [animal] be eaten for two days and one night. — [Zev. 57a, Sifrei Korach 42]

**18 [Hebrew]**	**לָּךְ יִֽהְיֶה־.** בָּא רַבִּי עֲקִיבָא וְלִמֵּד, וְהוֹסִיף לְךָ הַכָּתוּב הֲוָיָה אַחֶרֶת, שֶׁלֹא תֹאמַר, כְּחָזֶה וְשׁוֹק שֶׁל תּוֹדָה שֶׁאֵינוֹ נֶאֱכָל אֶלָּא לְיוֹם וָלַיְלָה (זבחים נ"ז):<br>
**18 [English]**	**it shall be yours.** R. Akiva taught: [By repeating the words “shall be yours”] Scripture adds another ‘being,’ so that you should not say [that it is] like the breast and thigh of the thanksgiving offering, which is eaten only for [one] day and [one] night. — [Zev. 57a]



**19 [Hebrew]**	**כֹּל תְּרוּמֹת הַקֳּדָשִׁים.** מֵחִבָּתָהּ שֶל פָּרָשָׁה זוֹ כְּלָלָהּ בַּתְּחִלָּה וּכְלָלָהּ בַּסּוֹף וּפָרַט בָּאֶמְצַע (עי' ספרי):<br>
**19 [English]**	**All the gifts of the holy [offerings].** Because this passage is so cherished, it is generalized at the beginning, generalized at the end, and detailed in the middle. — [Sifrei Korach 43]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**בְּרִית מֶלַח עוֹלָם.** כָּרַת בְּרִית עִם אַהֲרֹן בְּדָבָר הַבָּרִיא וּמִתְקַיֵּם וּמַבְרִיא אֶת אֲחֵרִים (שם):<br>
**19 [English]**	**An eternal covenant of salt.** He enacted a covenant with Aaron, with an object that is wholesome and lasting, and keeps other foodstuffs wholesome. — [Sifrei Korach 43]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**בְּרִית מֶלַח.** כַּבְּרִית הַכְּרוּתָה לַמֶּלַח שֶׁאֵינוֹ מַסְרִיחַ לְעוֹלָם:<br>
**19 [English]**	**covenant of salt.** Like a covenant made with salt, that it should never spoil.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**וְחֵלֶק לֹא יִֽהְיֶה לְךָ בְּתוֹכָם.** אַף בַּבִּזָּה (שם):<br>
**20 [English]**	**and you shall have no portion among them.** Even in the spoils of war. — [Sifrei Korach 45]



**23 [Hebrew]**	**וְהֵם.** הַלְוִיִּם — יִשְׂאוּ עֲוֹנָם שֶׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל, שֶׁעֲלֵיהֶם לְהַזְהִיר הַזָּרִים מִגֶּשֶׁת אֲלֵיהֶם:<br>
**23 [English]**	**and they.** The Levites shall bear the iniquity of the Israelites, for it is their duty to warn outsiders against approaching them.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר יָרִימוּ לה' תְּרוּמָה.** הַכָּתוּב קְרָאוֹ תְּרוּמָה עַד שֶׁיַּפְרִישׁ מִמֶּנּוּ תְּרוּמַת מַעֲשֵׂר:<br>
**24 [English]**	**which they shall set aside for the Lord as a gift.** Heb. תְּרוּמָה. Scripture calls it תְּרוּמָה, a gift, until he separates a gift [for the kohanim] from the tithes [received by the Levite from an Israelite]. — [Sifrei Korach 53]



**27 [Hebrew]**	**וְנֶחְשַׁב לָכֶם תְּרֽוּמַתְכֶם כַּדָּגָן מִן־הַגֹּרֶן.** תְּרוּמַת מַעֲשֵׂר שֶׁלָּכֶם אֲסוּרָה לְזָרִים וְלִטְמֵאִין, וְחַיָּבִין עָלֶיהָ מִיתָה וְחֹמֶשׁ כִּתְרוּמָה גְדוֹלָה שֶׁנִּקְרֵאת רֵאשִׁית דָּגָן מִן הַגֹּרֶן:<br>
**27 [English]**	**Your gift shall be considered for you, as grain from the threshing-floor.** Your gift separated from the tithe is forbidden to outsiders and to ritually unclean [people], and they incur the death penalty and [if eaten unintentionally] and [they are liable to pay] an additional fifth, just as in the case of the gift set aside by Israelites for the kohanim, which is called the first grain from the threshing-floor. — [Midrash Aggadah]

**27 [Hebrew]**	**וְכַֽמְלֵאָה מִן־הַיָּֽקֶב.** תְּרוּמַת תִּירוֹשׁ וְיִצְהָר הַנִּטֶּלֶת מִן הַיְקָבִים:<br>
**27 [English]**	**and the produce of the vat.** Like the gifts of wine and oil taken from the vats.

**27 [Hebrew]**	**מְלֵאָה.** לְשׁוֹן בִּשּׁוּל — תְּבוּאָה שֶׁנִּתְמַלֵּאת:<br>
**27 [English]**	**produce.** Heb. מְלֵאָה, lit., fullness, a term denoting ripening produce which has grown to its full [size].

**27 [Hebrew]**	**יָּֽקֶב.** הוּא הַבּוֹר שֶׁלִּפְנֵי הַגַּת שֶׁהַיַּיִן יוֹרֵד לְתוֹכוֹ, וְכָל לְשׁוֹן יֶקֶב חֲפִירַת קַרְקַע הוּא, וְכֵן "יִקְבֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ" (זכריה י"ד), הוּא יָם אוֹקְיָנוֹס, חֲפִירָה שֶׁחָפַר מַלְכּוֹ שֶׁל עוֹלָם:<br>
**27 [English]**	**vat.** Heb. יֶקֶב. This is the pit in front of the press into which the wine flows. The term יֶקֶב always denotes an excavation in the ground. Similarly, “the pits (יִקְבֵי) of the king” (Zech. 14:10), referring to the ocean-an excavation ‘dug’ by the King of the world. - [Peskita d’Rav Kahana p. 143; see also Song Rabbah 7:3, Mattenoth Kehunnah, Redal]



**28 [Hebrew]**	**כֵּן תָּרִימוּ גַם־אַתֶּם.** כְּמוֹ שֶׁיִּשְׂרָאֵל מְרִימִים מִגָּרְנָם וּמִיִּקְבֵיהֶם, תָּרִימוּ גַּם אַתֶּם מִמַּעֲשֵׂר שֶׁלָּכֶם, כִּי הוּא נַחֲלַתְכֶם:<br>
**28 [English]**	**So shall you too set aside.** Just as the Israelites set aside [a portion] from their threshing-floors and from their wine vats [for the kohanim], so shall you, too, set aside [a portion] from your tithes, for that is your inheritance.



**29 [Hebrew]**	**מִכֹּל מַתְּנֹתֵיכֶם תָּרִימוּ אֵת כָּל־תְּרוּמַת ה'.** בִּתְרוּמָה גְדוֹלָה הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר, שֶׁאִם הִקְדִּים לֵוִי אֶת הַכֹּהֵן בַּכְּרִי וְקִבֵּל מַעְשְׂרוֹתָיו קֹדֶם שֶׁיִּטֹּל כֹּהֵן תְּרוּמָה גְדוֹלָה מִן הַכְּרִי, צָרִיךְ לְהַפְרִישׁ הַלֵּוִי מִן הַמַּעֲשֵׂר תְּחִלָּה אֶחָד מֵחֲמִשִּׁים לִתְרוּמָה גְדוֹלָה, וְיַחֲזֹר וְיַפְרִישׁ תְּרוּמַת מַעֲשֵׂר (ספרי):<br>
**29 [English]**	**From all that is given to you, you shall set aside all God’s gifts.** Scripture refers to terumah gedolah [the gift set aside by the Israelites for the kohanim]. If the Levite preceded the kohen to the bin and accepted his tithes before the kohen took his terumah gedolah from the storage bin, the Levite must first separate one fiftieth from the tithe as terumah gedolah and then again separate another gift from the tithe. — [Shab. 127b]



**30 [Hebrew]**	**בַּֽהֲרִֽימְכֶם אֶת־חֶלְבּוֹ מִמֶּנּוּ.** לְאַחַר שֶׁתָּרִימוּ תְרוּמַת מַעֲשֵׂר מִמֶּנּוּ:<br>
**30 [English]**	**When you separate its choicest part.** After you have separated a gift from the tithes.

**30 [Hebrew]**	**וְנֶחְשַׁב.** הַמּוֹתָר לַלְוִיִּם חֻלִּין גְּמוּרִין:<br>
**30 [English]**	**it shall be considered.** The remainder shall be for the Levites and contain no sanctity whatsoever.

**30 [Hebrew]**	**כִּתְבוּאַת גֹּרֶן לישראל.** , שֶׁלֹּא תֹאמַר הוֹאִיל וּקְרָאוֹ הַכָּתוּב תְּרוּמָה — שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "כִּי אֶת מַעְשַׂר בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר יָרִימוּ לַה' תְּרוּמָה" — יָכוֹל יְהֵא כֻלּוֹ אָסוּר, תַּלְמוּד לוֹמָר "וְנֶחְשַׁב לַלְוִיִּם כִּתְבוּאַת גֹּרֶן", מַה שֶּׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל חֻלִּין, אַף שֶׁל לֵוִי חֻלִּין (עי' ספרי):<br>
**30 [English]**	**As produce from the threshing-floor.** For the Israelites. So that you should not say: Since Scripture calls it תְּרוּמָה, “a gift,” as it says, “For the tithes of the children of Israel, which they shall set aside for the Lord as a gift” (18:24), one might think it is completely forbidden [i.e., that it would retain its sanctity]. Thus, Scripture tells us that it shall be considered to the Levites like the produce of the threshing-floor; just as that of the Israelites is non-sacred [after the terumah gift has been set aside from it], so is that of the Levites non-sacred. — [Sifrei Korach 70]



**31 [Hebrew]**	**בְּכָל־מָקוֹם.** אֲפִלּוּ בְּבֵית הַקְּבָרוֹת (שם):<br>
**31 [English]**	**Anywhere.** Even in a cemetery. — [Sifrei Korach 71, Yev. 86b]



**32 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹֽא־תִשְׂאוּ עָלָיו חֵטְא וגו'.** הָא אִם לֹא תָרִימוּ תִּשְׂאוּ חֵטְא:<br>
**32 [English]**	**You shall not bear any sin on account of it.** However, if you do not set aside [a portion], you will bear a sin. — [Yev. 89b, Rashi]

**32 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹֽא־תָמֽוּתוּ.** הָא אִם תְּחַלְּלוּ תָּמוּתוּ:<br>
**32 [English]**	**So that you shall not die.** However, if you do profane it, you shall die. — [Bech. 26b]
