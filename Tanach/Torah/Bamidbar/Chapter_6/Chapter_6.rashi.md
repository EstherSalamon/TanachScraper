**2 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי יַפְלִא.** יַפְרִישׁ; לָמָּה נִסְמְכָה פָרָשַׁת נָזִיר לְפָרָשַׁת סוֹטָה? לוֹמַר לְךָ שֶׁכָּל הָרוֹאֶה סוֹטָה בְקִלְקוּלָהּ יַזִּיר עַצְמוֹ מִן הַיַּיִן, שֶׁהוּא מֵבִיא לִידֵי נִאוּף (סוטה ב'):<br>
**2 [English]**	**who sets himself apart.** Heb. כִּי יַפְלִא, sets himself apart. Why is the section dealing with the nazirite juxtaposed to the section of the adulterous woman? To tell us that whoever sees an adulteress in her disgrace should vow to abstain from wine, for it leads to adultery. — [Sotah 2a]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**נֶדֶר נָזִיר.** אֵין נְזִירָה בְכָל מָקוֹם אֶלָּא פְּרִישָׁה, אַף כָּאן שֶׁפֵּרֵשׁ מִן הַיַּיִן:<br>
**2 [English]**	**a nazirite vow.** Heb. נָזִיר [The term] נְזִירָה everywhere [in Scripture] means only separation; here too [the nazirite] separates himself from wine. - [Sifrei Naso 1:87]

**2 [Hebrew]**	**לְהַזִּיר לה'.** לְהַבְדִּיל עַצְמוֹ מִן הַיַּיִן לְשֵׁם שָׁמַיִם:<br>
**2 [English]**	**to abstain for the sake of the Lord.** To separate himself from wine for the sake of Heaven. — [Ned. 9b, Sifrei Naso 1:84]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**מִיַּיִן וְשֵׁכָר.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ "מֵחֲמַר חֲדַת וְעַתִּיק", שֶׁהַיַּיִן מְשַׁכֵּר כְּשֶׁהוּא יָשָׁן:<br>
**3 [English]**	**from new wine and aged wine.** Heb. מִיַיִּן וְשֵׁכָר. As Targum [Onkelos] renders:“From new wine and aged wine,” for when wine has been aged, it intoxicates מְשַׁכֵּר.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְכָל־מִשְׁרַת.** לְשׁוֹן צְבִיעָה בְּמַיִם וְכָל מַשְׁקֶה; וּבִלְשׁוֹן מִשְׁנָה יֵשׁ הַרְבֵּה, אֵין שׁוֹרִין דְּיוֹ וְסַמָּנִים (שבת י"ז), נָזִיר שֶׁשָּׁרָה פִתּוֹ (נזיר ל"ד):<br>
**3 [English]**	**anything in which grapes have been steeped.** Heb. מִשְׁרַת עֲנָבִים. [The word מִשְׁרַת is] an expression denoting steeping in water, or any other liquid. In the language of the Mishnah, there are many [such examples]: We may not steep (אֵין שׁוֹרִין) ink or dye [in water on the eve of Sabbath] (Shab. 17b); a nazirite who steeped (שֶׁשָּׁרָה) his bread in wine (Nazir 34b).



**4 [Hebrew]**	**חַרְצַנִּים.** הֵם הַגַּרְעִינִין:<br>
**4 [English]**	**seeds.** Heb. חַרְצַנִּים. They are the kernels. - [Sifrei Naso 1:93]

**4 [Hebrew]**	**זָג.** הֵם קְלִפוֹת שֶׁמִּבַּחוּץ, שֶׁהַחַרְצַנִּים בְּתוֹכָן כְּעִנְבָּל בְּזוּג:<br>
**4 [English]**	**skins.** Heb. זָג, the outer shells, for the seeds are inside, like the clapper in a bell (זוּג).



**5 [Hebrew]**	**קָדֹשׁ יִֽהְיֶה.** הַשֵּׂעָר שֶׁלּוֹ, לְגַדֵּל הַפֶּרַע שֶׁל שְׂעַר רֹאשׁוֹ (ספרי; תענית י"א):<br>
**5 [English]**	**it shall be sacred.** [That is,] his hair; he must let the growth of the hair of his head flourish.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**פֶּרַע.** נָקוּד פַּתָּח קָטָן אַף שֶׁהוּא דָּבוּק לִשְׂעַר רֹאשׁוֹ — פֶּרַע שֶׁל שֵׂעָר, וּפֵרוּשׁ שֶׁל פֶּרַע, גִּדּוּל שֶׁל שֵׂעָר, וְכֵן "אֶת רֹאשׁוֹ לֹא יִפְרָע" (ויקרא כ"א), וְאֵין קָרוּי פֶּרַע פָּחוֹת מִשְּׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם (ספרי; סנהדרין כ"ב):<br>
**5 [English]**	**growth.** Heb. פֶּרַע. [The word] is vowelized with a small “pattach” [known as “segol”] because it is [a construct state and] attached to the phrase“the hair of his head.” [The meaning is:] A growth of hair, and the word פֶּרַע means to allow the hair to grow [wild]. Similarly [we find],“He shall not allow his head to grow freely (לֹא יִפְרָע)” (Lev. 21:10). Any growth [of hair] less than thirty days is not considered פֶּרַע.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**כֹּל יְמֵי נִזְרוֹ קָדשׁ הוּא.** זוֹ קְדֻשַּׁת הַגּוּף, מִלִּטַּמֵּא לְמֵתִים (ספרי):<br>
**8 [English]**	**For the entire duration of his abstinence, he is holy.** This [refers to] the sanctification of the body, against contamination by [contact with] the dead.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**בְּפֶתַע.** זֶה אֹנֶס:<br>
**9 [English]**	**unexpectedly.** Heb. בְּפֶתַע. This is an unavoidable occurrence.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**פִּתְאֹם.** זֶה שׁוֹגֵג, וְיֵ"א פֶּתַע פִּתְאֹם דָּבָר אֶחָד הוּא – מִקְרֶה שֶׁל פִּתְאוֹם:<br>
**9 [English]**	**suddenly.** This refers to an unintentional [defilement] (Sifrei Naso 1:110). Some say that [the words] פֶּתַע פִּתְאֹם are a single phrase [denoting one idea, namely], “a sudden incident.” [Perhaps Rashi is alluding to Onkelos or to Menachem (Machbereth Menachem p.147). See Leket Bahir].

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְכִֽי־יָמוּת מֵת עָלָיו.** בָּאֹהֶל שֶׁהוּא בוֹ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**If someone in his presence dies.** In the tent in which he is located. - [Midrash Lekach Tov]

**9 [Hebrew]**	**בְּיוֹם טָֽהֳרָתוֹ.** בְּיוֹם הַזָּאָתוֹ, אוֹ אֵינוֹ אֶלָּא בַשְּׁמִינִי שֶׁהוּא טָהוֹר לְגַמְרֵי, תַּלְמוּד לוֹמָר "בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי", אִי שְׁבִיעִי יָכוֹל אֲפִלּוּ לֹא הִזָּה, תַּלְמוּד לוֹמָר "בְּיוֹם טָהֳרָתוֹ" (ספרי):<br>
**9 [English]**	**on the day of his purification.** On the day he is to be sprinkled, or perhaps only on the eighth day, when he becomes completely clean? [Therefore] Scripture states, “on the seventh day.” But if on the seventh, I might think that [his head must be shaved] even if he was not sprinkled. So Scripture [also] states,“on the day of his purification.” - [Sifrei Naso 1:113]



**10 [Hebrew]**	**וּבַיּוֹם הַשְּׁמִינִי יָבִא שְׁתֵּי תֹרִים.** לְהוֹצִיא אֶת הַשְּׁבִיעִי, אוֹ אֵינוֹ אֶלָּא לְהוֹצִיא אֶת הַתְּשִׁיעִי ? קָבַע זְמַן לַקְּרֵבִין וְקָבַע זְמַן לַמַּקְרִיבִין, מַה קְּרֵבִין הִכְשִׁיר שְׁמִינִי וּמִשְּׁמִינִי וָהָלְאָה, אַף מַקְרִיבִין שְׁמִינִי וּמִשְּׁמִינִי וָהָלְאָה (שם):<br>
**10 [English]**	**And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves.** This [is meant] to exclude the seventh [day], or perhaps it is meant to exclude only the ninth [day]? It [Scripture] designates a time for the sacrifices and it designates a time for those offering them. Just as it validates the eighth [day] and from the eighth [day] onwards for sacrifices, so those who offer the sacrifices may do so on the eighth [day] and from the eighth [day] onwards. - [Sifrei Naso 1:116]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**מֵֽאֲשֶׁר חָטָא עַל־הַנָּפֶשׁ.** שֶׁלֹּא נִזְהַר מִטֻּמְאַת הַמֵּת, רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר הַקַּפָּר אוֹמֵר, שֶׁצִּעֵר עַצְמוֹ מִן הַיַּיִן (ספרי; נזיר י"ט):<br>
**11 [English]**	**for sinning by coming into contact with the dead.** Heb. מֵאֲשֶׁר חָטָא עַל הַנֶּפֶשׁ, lit., for sinning concerning the body, meaning that he did not take precautions against becoming defiled by the dead. Rabbi Eleazar Hakappar says: He afflicted himself [by abstaining] from wine, [thus, he sinned against his own body]. — [Nazir 19a, B.K. 91b, Ta’anith 11a, Sifrei Naso 1:18, and other places]

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְקִדַּשׁ אֶת־רֹאשׁוֹ.** לַחֲזֹר וּלְהַתְחִיל מִנְיַן נְזִירוּתוֹ:<br>
**11 [English]**	**and he shall sanctify his head.** By beginning again the count of his naziriteship. - [Sifrei Naso 1:119]



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִזִּיר לה' אֶת־יְמֵי נִזְרוֹ.** יַחֲזֹר וְיִמְנֶה נְזִירוּתוֹ כְּבַתְּחִלָּה:<br>
**12 [English]**	**He shall consecrate to the Lord the period of his abstinence.** He shall start counting his naziriteship again from the beginning. — [Sifrei Naso 1:119]

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַיָּמִים הָרִֽאשֹׁנִים יִפְּלוּ.** לֹא יַעֲלוּ מִן הַמִּנְיָן:<br>
**12 [English]**	**The previous days shall be canceled.** They shall not count. — [Targum Onkelos]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**יָבִיא אֹתוֹ.** יָבִיא אֶת עַצְמוֹ, וְזֶה אֶחָד מִשְּׁלֹשָׁה אֶתִים שֶׁהָיָה רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל דּוֹרֵשׁ כֵּן, כַּיּוֹצֵא בוֹ "וְהִשִּׂיאוּ אוֹתָם עֲוֹן אַשְׁמָה" (ויקרא כ"ב) — אֶת עַצְמָם, כַּיּוֹצֵא בוֹ "וַיִּקְבֹּר אֹתוֹ בַגַּי" (דברים ל"ד) — הוּא קָבַר אֶת עַצְמוֹ:<br>
**13 [English]**	**he shall present himself.** Heb. יָבִיא אֹתוֹ, lit.,“he shall bring him,” i.e., he shall bring himself. This [word אֹתוֹ“himself”] is one of the three [cases of the word] אֶת which Rabbi Ishmael expounded in this way [as being reflexive]. Similarly,“thereby bringing upon themselves (אוֹתָם) to bear iniquity and guilt” (Lev. 22:16) - [“ אוֹתָם ” meaning] themselves. Similarly,“He buried him (אֹתוֹ) in the ravine” (Deut. 34:6) he [Moses] buried himself. — [Sifrei Naso 1:124]



**15 [Hebrew]**	**וּמִנְחָתָם וְנִסְכֵּיהֶֽם.** שֶׁל עוֹלָה וּשְׁלָמִים; לְפִי שֶׁהָיוּ בִכְלָל, וְיָצְאוּ לִדּוֹן בְּדָבָר חָדָשׁ — שֶׁיִּטָּעֲנוּ לֶחֶם — הֶחֱזִירָן לִכְלָלָן, שֶׁיִּטָּעֲנוּ נְסָכִים כְּדִין עוֹלָה וּשְׁלָמִים (ספרי):<br>
**15 [English]**	**with their meal-offerings and libations.** Of the burnt offerings and peace offerings [but not of the sin-offering]. Since they were included in the general rule [requiring libations] but were then singled out to be the subject of a new case, namely, that they required bread, [Scripture] returns them to the general rule [by stating that] they require libations, as is the universal law for all burnt offerings and peace offerings. - [See Sifrei Naso 1:127]

**15 [Hebrew]**	**חלות מַצּוֹת וּרְקִיקֵי מַצּוֹת.** עֶשֶׂר מִכָּל מִין:<br>
**15 [English]**	**unleavened loaves and unleavened wafers.** Ten of each kind. — [Men. 77b; Rambam , Mishnah Comm ., Men. 7:2; Mishneh Torah , Neziruth 8:1, Kesef Mishneh]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**זֶבַח שְׁלָמִים לה' עַל סַל הַמַּצּוֹת.** יִשְׁחַט אֶת הַשְּׁלָמִים עַל מְנָת לְקַדֵּשׁ אֶת הַלֶּחֶם (עי' מנחות מ"ו):<br>
**17 [English]**	**a peace offering to the Lord, along with the basket of unleavened cakes.** He slaughters the [ram of] the peace offering with the intention of sanctifying the bread. — [Men. 46b]

**17 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת־מִנְחָתוֹ וְאֶת־נִסְכּֽוֹ.** שֶׁל אַיִל:<br>
**17 [English]**	**Its meal offering with its libation.** [I.e.,] the ram’s.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**וְגִלַּח הַנָּזִיר פֶּתַח אוהל מוֹעֵד.** יָכוֹל יְגַלֵּחַ בָּעֲזָרָה, הֲרֵי זֶה דֶּרֶךְ בִּזָּיוֹן, אֶלָּא וְגִלַּח הַנָּזִיר לְאַחַר שְׁחִיטַת הַשְּׁלָמִים שֶׁכָּתוּב בָּהֶן "וּשְׁחָטוֹ פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד":<br>
**18 [English]**	**The nazirite shall shave…at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.** I might think that he should shave in the courtyard, but this would be degrading [for the courtyard]. Rather, “the nazirite shall shave” after the peace offering has been slaughtered, regarding which it is written, “and slaughter it at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting” (Lev. 3:2). - [Nazir 45a, Sifrei Naso 1:128]

**18 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר־תַּחַת זֶבַח הַשְּׁלָמִֽים.** תַּחַת הַדּוּד שֶׁהוּא מְבַשְּׁלָן בּוֹ, לְפִי שֶׁשַּׁלְמֵי נָזִיר הָיוּ מִתְבַּשְּׁלִין בָּעֲזָרָה, שֶׁצָּרִיךְ לִטֹּל הַכֹּהֵן הַזְּרוֹעַ אַחַר שֶׁנִּתְבַּשְּׁלָה וּלְהָנִיף לִפְנֵי ה' (עי' נזיר מ"ה):<br>
**18 [English]**	**which is under the peace offering.** [I.e.,] under the pot in which he cooks it. For the nazirite’s peace offering was cooked in the courtyard, since the kohen had to take the foreleg after it had been cooked and wave it before the Lord.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**הַזְּרֹעַ בְּשֵׁלָה.** לְאַחַר שֶׁנִּתְבַּשְּׁלָה (חולין פ"ח):<br>
**19 [English]**	**the cooked foreleg.** After it has been cooked.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**קֹדֶשׁ הוּא לַכֹּהֵן.** הַחַלָּה וְהָרָקִיק וְהַזְּרוֹעַ תְּרוּמָה הֵן לַכֹּהֵן:<br>
**20 [English]**	**it is consecrated to the kohen.** The loaf, the wafer, and the foreleg are donations for the kohen.

**20 [Hebrew]**	**עַל חֲזֵה הַתְּנוּפָה.** מִלְּבַד חָזֶה וְשׁוֹק הָרְאוּיִים לוֹ מִכָּל שְׁלָמִים מוּסָף עַל שַׁלְמֵי נָזִיר הַזְּרוֹעַ הַזֶּה, לְפִי שֶׁהָיוּ שַׁלְמֵי נָזִיר בִּכְלָל, וְיָצְאוּ לִדּוֹן בַּדָּבָר הֶחָדָשׁ — לְהַפְרָשַׁת זְרוֹעַ — הֻצְרַךְ לְהַחֲזִירָן לִכְלָלָן לִדּוֹן אַף בְּחָזֶה וְשׁוֹק (ספרי):<br>
**20 [English]**	**along with the breast of the waving, etc..** Besides the breast and thigh due him from all peace offerings, this foreleg is added to the nazirite peace offerings. [This is] because the nazirite peace offerings were included in the general rule, but were then singled out to determine something new-setting apart the foreleg. [Thus,] it was necessary to return them to the general rule so that they are subject to [the gifts of] the breast and the thigh as well. — [Sifrei Naso 1: 134]



**21 [Hebrew]**	**מִלְּבַד אֲשֶׁר תַּשִּׂיג יָדוֹ.** שֶׁאִם אָמַר הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר עַל מְנָת לְגַלֵּחַ עַל מֵאָה עוֹלוֹת וְעַל מֵאָה שְׁלָמִים, כְּפִי נִדְרוֹ אֲשֶׁר יִדֹּר כֵּן יַעֲשֶֹה:<br>
**21 [English]**	**in addition to that which is within his means.** [For example,] if he said,“I am hereby a nazirite on the condition that I shall shave [my hair] with one hundred burnt offerings and with one hundred peace offerings” - according to the vow that he vows, so shall he do added to the law of his naziriteship.

**21 [Hebrew]**	**מוּסָף עַל תּוֹרַת נִזְרוֹ.** עַל תּוֹרַת הַנָּזִיר, מוּסָף וְלֹא יֶחְסַר, שֶׁאִם אָמַר הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר חָמֵשׁ נְזִירוֹת עַל מְנָת לְגַלֵּחַ עַל שָׁלֹשׁ בְּהֵמוֹת הַלָּלוּ, אֵין אֲנִי קוֹרֵא בוֹ "כַּאֲשֶׁר יִדֹּר כֵּן יַעֲשֶֹה" (שם):<br>
**21 [English]**	**In addition to the law of his naziriteship.** Heb. תּוֹרַת נִזְרוֹ means he may add to the law of his naziriteship but not omit anything. If he said, “I am hereby a nazirite five times over on condition that I shave with [only] these three animals,” I do not apply to him [the rule],“According to the vow that he vows, so shall he do.” - [Sifrei Naso 1:137]



**23 [Hebrew]**	**אָמוֹר.** כְּמוֹ זָכוֹר, שָׁמוֹר, בְּלַעַז דישנ"ט:<br>
**23 [English]**	**saying to them.** Heb. אָמוֹר. [The infinitive] as in זָכוֹר,“remembering” (Exod. 20:8), and שָׁמוֹר, “keeping” (Deut. 5:12); in French, disant.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**אָמוֹר לָהֶֽם.** שֶׁיִּהְיוּ כֻלָּם שׁוֹמְעִים (שם):<br>
**23 [English]**	**saying to them.** So that they can all hear- [Sifrei Naso 1:143]

**23 [Hebrew]**	**אָמוֹר.** מָלֵא — לֹא תְבָרְכֵם בְּחִפָּזוֹן וּבַהֲלוּת, אֶלָּא בְכַוָּנָה וּבְלֵב שָׁלֵם (תנחומא):<br>
**23 [English]**	**saying.** The word אָמוֹר is written in its full form [i.e., with a “vav”], indicating that they should not bless them hastily or in a hurried manner, but with concentration and with wholeheartedness. - [Midrash Tanchuma Naso 10]



**24 [Hebrew]**	**יְבָֽרֶכְךָ.** שֶׁיִּתְבָּרְכוּ נְכָסֶיךָ:<br>
**24 [English]**	**May [the Lord] bless you.** that your possessions shall be blessed. - [Midrash Tanchuma Naso 10, Sifrei Naso 1:144]

**24 [Hebrew]**	**וְיִשְׁמְרֶֽךָ.** שֶׁלֹּא יָבֹאוּ עָלֶיךָ שׁוֹדְדִים לִטֹּל מָמוֹנְךָ; שֶׁהַנּוֹתֵן מַתָּנָה לְעַבְדּוֹ אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לְשָׁמְרוֹ מִכָּל אָדָם, וְכֵיוָן שֶׁבָּאִים לִסְטִים עָלָיו וְנוֹטְלִין אוֹתָהּ מִמֶּנּוּ, מַה הֲנָאָה יֵשׁ לוֹ בְּמַתָּנָה זוֹ? אֲבָל הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא, הוּא הַנּוֹתֵן, הוּא הַשּׁוֹמֵר; וְהַרְבֵּה מִדְרָשִׁים דָּרְשׁוּ בוֹ בְּסִפְרֵי:<br>
**24 [English]**	**and watch over you.** that no thieves shall attack you and steal your money. For when one gives his servant a gift, he cannot protect it from all other people, so if robbers come and take it from him, what benefit has he [the servant] from this gift? As for the Holy One, blessed be He, however, He is the One who [both] gives and protects (Midrash Tanchuma Naso 10). There are many expository interpretations in the Sifrei .



**25 [Hebrew]**	**יָאֵר השם פָּנָיו אֵלֶיךָ.** יַרְאֶה לְךָ פָּנִים שׂוֹחֲקוֹת, פָּנִים צְהֻבּוֹת:<br>
**25 [English]**	**May the Lord cause His countenance to shine to you.** May He show you a pleasant, radiant countenance. - [Midrash Tanchuma Naso 10, Sifrei Naso 1:144]

**25 [Hebrew]**	**וִֽיחֻנֶּֽךָּ.** יִתֵּן לְךָ חֵן (תנחומא):<br>
**25 [English]**	**and favor you.** May He grant you favor - [Sifrei Naso 1:144]



**26 [Hebrew]**	**יִשָּׂא ה' פָּנָיו אֵלֶיךָ.** יִכְבֹּשׁ כַּעֲסוֹ:<br>
**26 [English]**	**May the Lord raise His countenance toward you.** by suppressing His wrath. - [Sifrei Naso 1:144,]



**27 [Hebrew]**	**וְשָׂמוּ אֶת־שְׁמִי.** יְבָרְכוּם בַּשֵּׁם הַמְפֹרָשׁ (ספרי):<br>
**27 [English]**	**They shall bestow My Name.** They shall bless them with the Explicit Name. - [Sifrei Naso 1:144, Num. Rabbah 11:4, 8]

**27 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽאֲנִי אֲבָֽרֲכֵֽם.** לְיִשְׂרָאֵל, וְאַסְכִּים עִם הַכֹּהֲנִים; דָּבָר אַחֵר, וַאֲנִי אֲבָרְכֵם — לַכֹּהֲנִים (חולין מ"ט):<br>
**27 [English]**	**so that I will bless them.** [I.e.,] the Israelites, and endorse the [blessing of the] kohanim. Another interpretation: “I will bless them”-that is, the kohanim. - [Chul. 49a]
