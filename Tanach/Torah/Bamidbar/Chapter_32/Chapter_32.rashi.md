**3 [Hebrew]**	**עֲטָרוֹת וְדִיבֹן וגו'.** מֵאֶרֶץ סִיחוֹן וְעוֹג הָיוּ:<br>
**3 [English]**	**Ataroth, Dibon….** They were [part] of the land belonging to Sihon and Og.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**הַֽאַחֵיכֶם.** לְשׁוֹן תְּמִיהָה הוּא:<br>
**6 [English]**	**Shall your brethren.** This [’hey’] denotes a question.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**וְלָמָּה תניאון.** תָּסִירוּ וְתַמְנִיעוּ לִבָּם מֵעֲבֹר, שֶׁיִּהְיוּ סְבוּרִים שֶׁאַתֶּם יְרֵאִים לַעֲבֹר מִפְּנֵי הַמִּלְחָמָה וְחֹזֶק הֶעָרִים וְהָעָם:<br>
**7 [English]**	**Why do you discourage.** You turn aside and dissuade their hearts from crossing, for they will think that you are afraid to cross because of the war and the strength of the cities and the people.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**מִקָּדֵשׁ בַּרְנֵעַ.** כָּךְ שְׁמָהּ, וּשְׁתֵּי קָדֵשׁ הָיוּ:<br>
**8 [English]**	**from Kadesh-barnea.** This was its name; there were two places called Kadesh [one Kadesh-barnea and one Kadesh unmodified].



**12 [Hebrew]**	**הַקְּנִזִּי.** חוֹרְגוֹ שֶׁל קְנַז הָיָה וְיָלְדָה לוֹ אִמּוֹ שֶׁל כָּלֵב אֶת עָתְנִיאֵל (סוטה י"א):<br>
**12 [English]**	**The Kenizite.** He [Caleb] was the stepson of Kenaz, to whom Caleb’s mother bore Othniel [see Jud. 3:9]. — [Sotah 11b]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְנִעֵם.** וַיְטַלְטְלֵם, מִן "נָע וָנָד" (בראשית ד):<br>
**13 [English]**	**He made them wander.** He moved them about from place to place, as in נָע וָנָד “a wanderer and an exile” (Gen. 4:12).



**14 [Hebrew]**	**לִסְפּוֹת.** כְּמוֹ "סְפוּ שָׁנָה עַל שָׁנָה" (ישעיהו כ"ט), "עֹלוֹתֵיכֶם סְפוּ" וְגוֹ' (ירמיהו ז'), לְשׁוֹן תּוֹסֶפֶת:<br>
**14 [English]**	**to add.** לִסְפּוֹת like “add (סְפוּ) year to year” (Isa. 29:1), and “add (סְפוּ) your burnt offerings” (Jer. 7:21); it denotes addition.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**נִבְנֶה לְמִקְנֵנוּ פֹּה.** חָסִים הָיוּ עַל מָמוֹנָם יוֹתֵר מִבְּנֵיהֶם וּבְנוֹתֵיהֶם, שֶׁהִקְדִּימוּ מִקְנֵיהֶם לְטַפָּם. אָמַר לָהֶם מֹשֶׁה "לֹא כֵן, עֲשׂוּ הָעִקָּר עִקָּר וְהַטָּפֵל טָפֵל, בְּנוּ לָכֶם תְּחִלָּה עָרִים לְטַפְּכֶם וְאַחַר כֵּן גְּדֵרוֹת לְצֹאנְכֶם" (תנחומא):<br>
**16 [English]**	**We shall build sheepfolds for our livestock here.** They were more concerned about their possessions than about their sons and daughters, since they mentioned their livestock before [mentioning] their children. Moses said to them, “Not so! Treat the fundamental as a fundamental, and the matter of secondary importance as a matter of secondary importance. First ‘build cities for your children,’ and afterwards 'enclosures for your sheep’” (verse 24) - [Mid. Tanchuma Mattoth 7]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽאֲנַחְנוּ נֵֽחָלֵץ חֻשִׁים.** נִזְדַּיֵּן מְהִירִים, כְּמוֹ "מַהֵר שָׁלָל חָשׁ בַּז" (ישעיהו ח'), "יְמַהֵר יָחִישָׁה" (שם ה'):<br>
**17 [English]**	**We will then arm ourselves quickly.** We will speedily arm ourselves, as in, “the booty speeds, the spoil hastens (חָשׁ)” (Isa. 8:3); “Let Him hurry, let Him hasten (יָחִישָׁה)” (ibid. 5:19).

**17 [Hebrew]**	**לִפְנֵי בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.** בְּרָאשֵׁי גְּיָסוֹת, מִתּוֹךְ שֶׁגִּבּוֹרִים הָיוּ, שֶׁכֵּן נֶאֱמַר בְּגָד (דברים ל"ג) "וְטָרַף זְרוֹעַ אַף קָדְקֹד"; וְאַף מֹשֶׁה חָזַר וּפֵרֵשׁ לָהֶם בְּאֵלֶּה הַדְּבָרִים (דברים ג') "וָאֲצַו אֶתְכֶם בָּעֵת הַהִוא וְגוֹ' חֲלוּצִים תַּעַבְרוּ לִפְנֵי אֲחֵיכֶם בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל כָּל בְּנֵי חָיִל", וּבִירִיחוֹ כְּתִיב (יהושע ו') "וְהֶחָלוּץ עוֹבֵר (הֹלֵךְ) לִפְנֵיהֶם" — זֶה רְאוּבֵן וְגָד שֶׁקִּיְּמוּ תְנָאָם:<br>
**17 [English]**	**before the children of Israel.** At the head of the troops, because they were mighty warriors, for concerning Gad it says, “tearing the arm [of his prey, together] with the head” (Deut. 33:20). Moses too, explained this to them a second time in [the portion of] Eleh Hadevarim: “And I commanded you at that time saying, “…pass over, armed, before your brothers, the children of Israel, all who are warriors” (ibid. 3:18), and concerning Jericho it is written, “the armed force went ahead of them” (Josh. 6:13). These were [the tribes of] Gad and Reuben, who were fulfilling their condition.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**וְיָשַׁב טַפֵּנוּ.** בְּעוֹדֵנוּ אֵצֶל אַחֵינוּ:<br>
**17 [English]**	**Our children will reside.** while we are still with our brethren.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**בְּעָרֵי הַמִּבְצָר.** שֶׁנִּבְנֶה עַכְשָׁו:<br>
**17 [English]**	**in the fortified cities.** which we shall build now.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן וָהָלְאָה.** בָּעֵבֶר הַמַּעֲרָבִי:<br>
**19 [English]**	**on the other side of the Jordan and beyond.** On the western bank.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי בָאָה נַּֽחֲלָתֵנוּ.** כְּבָר קִבַּלְנוּהָ בָּעֵבֶר הַמִּזְרָחִי:<br>
**19 [English]**	**for our inheritance has come to us.** We have already received it on the eastern side.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**לְצֹנַֽאֲכֶם.** תֵּבָה זוֹ מִגִּזְרַת "צֹנֶה וַאֲלָפִים כֻּלָּם" (תהלים ח') שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ אָלֶ"ף מַפְסִיק בֵּין נוּ"ן לַצָּדִ"י, וְאָלֶ"ף שֶׁבָּא כָאן אַחַר הַנּוּ"ן בִּמְקוֹם הֵ"א שֶׁל צֹנֶה הוּא; מִיסוֹדוֹ שֶׁל רַ' מֹשֶׁה הַדַּרְשָׁן לָמַדְתִּי כֵן:<br>
**24 [English]**	**for your sheep.** Heb. לְצֹנַאֲכֶם. This word is cognate with, “Flocks (צֹנֶה) and cattle, all of them” (Ps. 8:8), in which there is no א separating the נ from the צ. The א that appears here after the נ [in the word לְצֹנַאֲכֶם is in place of the ה in the word צֹנֶה. I learned this from the commentary of R. Moshe Hadarshan [the preacher].

**24 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַיֹּצֵא מִפִּיכֶם תַּֽעֲשֽׂוּ.** לְגָבוֹהַּ, שֶׁקִּבַּלְתֶּם עֲלֵיכֶם לַעֲבֹר לַמִּלְחָמָה עַד כִּבּוּשׁ וְחִלּוּק, שֶׁמֹּשֶׁה לֹא בִּקֵּשׁ מֵהֶם אֶלָּא "וְנִכְבְּשָׁה … וְאַחַר תָּשׁוּבוּ" וְהֵם קִבְּלוּ עֲלֵיהֶם "עַד הִתְנַחֵל", הֲרֵי הוֹסִיפוּ לְהִתְעַכֵּב שֶׁבַע שֶׁחָלְקוּ, וְכֵן עָשׂוּ:<br>
**24 [English]**	**and what has proceeded from your mouth you shall do.** for the sake of the Most High [God], for you have undertaken to cross over for battle until [the completion of] conquest and the apportionment [of the Land]. Moses had asked of them only “and… will be conquered before the Lord, afterwards you may return,” (verse 22), but they undertook, “until… has taken possession” (verse 18). Thus, they added that they would remain seven years while it was divided, and indeed they did so (see Josh. 22).



**25 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמֶר בְּנֵי־גָד.** כֻּלָּם כְּאִישׁ אֶחָד:<br>
**25 [English]**	**The descendants of Gad spoke.** Heb. וַיֹּאמֶר [in the singular form, indicating that] they all [spoke] as one person. — [Jonathan ben Uzziel]



**28 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְצַו לָהֶם.** כְּמוֹ עֲלֵיהֶם, וְעַל תְּנָאָם מִנָּה אֶלְעָזָר וִיהוֹשֻׁעַ, כְּמוֹ "ה' יִלָּחֵם לָכֶם" (שמות י"ד):<br>
**28 [English]**	**commanded… concerning them.** Heb. לָהֶם, like עִלֵיהֶם, concerning them, and concerning [the fulfillment of] their condition, he appointed Eleazar and Joshua, as in, “the Lord will fight for you (לָכֶם) ” (Exod. 14:14) [not “to you”].



**32 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִתָּנוּ אֲחֻזַּת נַֽחֲלָתֵנוּ.** כְּלוֹמַר בְּיָדֵינוּ וּבִרְשׁוּתֵנוּ תְהִי אֲחֻזַּת נַחֲלָתֵנוּ מֵעֵבֶר הַזֶּה:<br>
**32 [English]**	**and then we shall have the possession of our inheritance.** That is to say, the possession of our inheritance on this side [of the Jordan] will be in our hands and under our ownership.



**36 [Hebrew]**	**עָרֵי מִבְצָר וְגִדְרֹת צֹֽאן.** זֶה סוֹף פָּסוּק מוּסָב עַל תְּחִלַּת הָעִנְיָן — וַיִּבְנוּ בְנֵי גָד אֶת הֶעָרִים הַלָּלוּ לִהְיוֹת עָרֵי מִבְצָר וְגִדְרֹת צֹאן:<br>
**36 [English]**	**fortified cities and sheepfolds.** This last part of the verse relates to the beginning of the passage, “The descendants of Gad built” these cities as fortified cities and sheepfolds.



**38 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־נְבוֹ וְאֶת־בַּעַל מְעוֹן מֽוּסַבֹּת שֵׁם.** נְבוֹ וּבַעַל מְעוֹן שְׁמוֹת עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה הֵם, וְהָיוּ הָאֱמוֹרִיִּים קוֹרִים עָרֵיהֶם עַל שֵׁם עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה שֶׁלָּהֶם, וּבְנֵי רְאוּבֵן הֵסֵבּוּ אֶת שְׁמָם לְשֵׁמוֹת אֲחֵרִים, וְזֶהוּ מוסבת שם — נְבוֹ וּבַעַל מְעוֹן מוּסַבּוֹת לְשֵׁם אַחֵר:<br>
**38 [English]**	**Nebo and Baal-Meon, their names having been changed.** Nebo and Baal Meon were names of pagan deities, and the Amorites named their towns after their deities, and the descendants of Reuben changed their names to other names. This is the meaning of “their names having been changed”-Nebo and Baal-Meon, changed to another name.

**38 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־שִׂבְמָה.** בָּנוּ שִׂבְמָה, וְהִיא שְׂבָם הָאֲמוּרָה לְמַעְלָה:<br>
**38 [English]**	**and Sibmah.** They built Sibmah, which is identical with Sebam mentioned earlier (verse 3).



**39 [Hebrew]**	**וַיּוֹרֶשׁ.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ "וְתָרִיךְ", שֶׁתֵּבַת ריש מְשַׁמֶּשֶׁת שְׁתֵּי מַחֲלָקוֹת, לְשׁוֹן יְרֻשָּׁה וּלְשׁוֹן הוֹרָשָׁה שֶׁהוּא טֵרוּד וְתֵרוּךְ:<br>
**39 [English]**	**driving out.** As the Targum [Onkelos] renders, וְתָרִיךְ‏, and drove out, for the word רִישׁ can be used in two [different] ways, in the sense of יְרוּשָׁה, ‘inheritance,’ or in the sense of הוֹרָשָׁה ‘driving out,’ meaning to expel or oust. — [Machbereth Menachem p. 167]



**41 [Hebrew]**	**חַוֹּֽתֵיהֶם.** כַּפְרָנֵיהוֹן:<br>
**41 [English]**	**their hamlets.** Heb. חַוֹּתֵיהֶם, [Onkelos renders,] כַּפְרָנֵיהוֹן, their hamlets.

**41 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקְרָא אֶתְהֶן חַוֹּת יָאִֽיר.** לְפִי שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ לוֹ בָנִים קְרָאָם בִּשְׁמוֹ לְזִכָּרוֹן:<br>
**41 [English]**	**and called them the hamlets of Jair.** Since he had no children, he named them after himself, as a memorial.



**42 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקְרָא לָה נֹבַח.** "לָה" אֵינוֹ מַפִּיק הֵ"א, וְרָאִיתִי בִיסוֹדוֹ שֶׁל רַ' מֹשֶׁה הַדַּרְשָׁן לְפִי שֶׁלֹּא נִתְקַיֵּם לָהּ שֵׁם זֶה לְפִיכָךְ הוּא רָפֶה, שֶׁמַּשְׁמָעוּת מִדְרָשׁוֹ כְּמוֹ לֹא; וּתְמֵהַנִי מַה יִּדְרֹשׁ בִּשְׁתֵּי תֵבוֹת הַדּוֹמוֹת לָהּ "וַיֹּאמֶר לָה בֹעַז" (רות ב'), "לִבְנוֹת לָה בַיִת" (זכריה ה'):<br>
**42 [English]**	**and called it Nobah.** Heb. לָה. [The ‘hey’ in] לָה is not a ‘mappik’ [aspirate ‘hey’ since there is no dot in the] ה [thus indicating that it is silent, contrary to the general rule]. I saw in the commentary of R. Moshe Hadarshan [as follows]: Since this name did not remain permanently, it is [a] silent [letter], so that it [the word לָה] can be expounded as לֹא, ‘not.’ But I wonder how he would expound two words similar to this, namely, “Boaz said to her (לָה)” (Ruth 2:14); “to build her (לָה) a house” (Zech. 5:11).
