**1 [Hebrew]**	**כִּֽי־תֵצֵא לַמִּלְחָמָה.** סָמַךְ הַכָּתוּב יְצִיאַת מִלְחָמָה לְכָאן, לוֹמַר לְךָ שֶׁאֵין מְחֻסַּר אֵבֶר יוֹצֵא לַמִּלְחָמָה (עי' ספרי); דָּבָר אַחֵר, לוֹמַר לְךָ אִם עָשִׂיתָ מִשְׁפַּט צֶדֶק, אַתָּה מֻבְטָח שֶׁאִם תֵּצֵא לַמִּלְחָמָה אַתָּה נוֹצֵחַ, וְכֵן דָוִד הוּא אוֹמֵר (תהילים קי"ט) עָשִׂיתִי מִשְׁפָּט וָצֶדֶק בַּל תַּנִּיחֵנִי לְעוֹשְׁקָי (תנחומא):<br>
**1 [English]**	**When you go out to war.** Scripture juxtaposes the departure for war alongside this [“eye for eye etc.”] to teach us that a person with a missing limb does not go out to war. - [Sifrei] Another explanation: It teaches that if you execute righteous judgment, you can be sure that when you depart for war you will be victorious. Similarly, David says, “I performed justice and righteousness; do not leave me to my oppressors” (Ps. 119: 121). - [Tanchuma]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־אֹֽיְבֶךָ.** יִהְיוּ בְּעֵינֶיךָ כְּאוֹיְבִים, אַל תְּרַחֵם עֲלֵיהֶם כִּי לֹא יְרַחֲמוּ עָלֶיךָ:<br>
**1 [English]**	**against your enemies.** Let them be in your eyes as enemies; have no pity on them, for they will have no pity upon you.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**סוּס וָרֶכֶב.** בְּעֵינַי כֻּלָּם כְּסוּס אֶחָד, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר (שופטים ו') וְהִכִּיתָ אֶת מִדְיָן כְּאִישׁ אֶחָד, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר (שמות ט"ו) כִּי בָא סוּס פַּרְעֹה (עי' ספרי):<br>
**1 [English]**	**horse and chariot.** In My eyes, they are all like one horse. Similarly, it says, “and you shall strike Midian as one man.” (Jud. 6:16) And similarly, it says, “When Pharaoh’s horse… came [into the sea]” (Exod. 15:19). - [Sifrei]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**עַם רַב מִמְּךָ.** בְּעֵינֶיךָ הוּא רַב, אֲבָל בְּעֵינַי אֵינוֹ רַב (שם):<br>
**1 [English]**	**a people more numerous than you.** In your eyes, they are numerous, but in My eyes, they are not numerous. — [Sifrei]



**2 [Hebrew]**	**כְּקָֽרָבְכֶם אֶל־הַמִּלְחָמָה.** סָמוּךְ לְצֵאתְכֶם מִן הַסְּפָר – מִגְּבוּל אַרְצְכֶם:<br>
**2 [English]**	**when you approach the battle.** When you are on the point of leaving the border.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְנִגַּשׁ הַכֹּהֵן.** הַמָּשׁוּחַ לְכָךְ, וְהוּא הַנִּקְרָא "מְשׁוּחַ מִלְחָמָה":<br>
**2 [English]**	**that the kohen shall come near.** This refers to the kohen anointed for this purpose, and he is called “the one anointed for war.”

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְדִבֶּר אֶל־הָעָֽם.** בִּלְשׁוֹן הַקֹּדֶשׁ (סוטה מ"ב):<br>
**2 [English]**	**and speak to the people.** in the Holy Tongue. — [Sotah 42a]



**3 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל.** אֲפִלּוּ אֵין בָּכֶם זְכוּת אֶלָּא קְרִיאַת שְׁמַע בִּלְבַד, כְּדַאי אַתֶּם שֶׁיּוֹשִׁיעַ אֶתְכֶם:<br>
**3 [English]**	**“Hear, O Israel”.** Even if you have no merit other than the reading of the Shema, you are worthy that He [God] save you. — [Sotah 42b]

**3 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־אֹֽיְבֵיכֶם.** אֵין אֵלּוּ אַחֵיכֶם, שֶׁאִם תִּפְּלוּ בְיָדָם אֵינָם מְרַחֲמִים עֲלֵיכֶם, אֵין זוֹ כְּמִלְחֶמֶת יְהוּדָה עִם יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (דברי הימים ב' כ"ח ט"ו) "וַיָּקֻמוּ הָאֲנָשִׁים אֲשֶׁר נִקְּבוּ בְשֵׁמוֹת וַיַּחֲזִיקוּ בַשִּׁבְיָה וְכָל מַעֲרֻמֵּיהֶם הִלְבִּישׁוּ מִן הַשָּׁלָל וַיַּלְבִּישׁוּם וַיַּנְעִילוּם וַיַּאֲכִילוּם וַיַּשְׁקוּם וַיְסֻכוּם וַיְנַהֲלוּם בַּחֲמֹרִים לְכָל כּוֹשֵׁל וַיְבִיאוּם יְרֵחוֹ עִיר הַתְּמָרִים אֵצֶל אֲחֵיהֶם וַיָּשׁוּבוּ שֹׁמְרוֹן", אֶלָּא עַל אוֹיְבֵיכֶם אַתֶּם הוֹלְכִים, לְפִיכָךְ הִתְחַזְּקוּ לַמִּלְחָמָה (ספרי; סוטה מ"ב):<br>
**3 [English]**	**against your enemies.** These are not your brothers, for if you fall into their hands, they will not have pity on you; this is not like the war of Judah with Israel, of which the verse states, “And the men, who were mentioned by name, rose up and took hold of the captives, and clothed all their nakedness from the spoils, and they dressed them and shod them, and fed them and gave them to drink, and anointed them, and led them on donkeys, every feeble one, and they brought them to Jericho, the city of the palms, beside their brothers, and they returned to Samaria” (II Chron. 28:15). You, however, are going against your enemies; therefore strengthen yourselves for battle. — [Sifrei ; Sotah 42a]

**3 [Hebrew]**	**אַל־יֵרַךְ לְבַבְכֶם אַל־תִּֽירְאוּ וְאַל־תַּחְפְּזוּ וְאַל־תַּֽעַרְצוּ.** אַרְבַּע אַזְהָרוֹת כְּנֶגֶד אַרְבָּעָה דְבָרִים שֶׁמַּלְכֵי הָאֻמּוֹת עוֹשִׂים, מְגִיפִין בִּתְרִיסֵיהֶם כְּדֵי לְהַקִּישָׁן זֶה לָזֶה כְּדֵי לְהַשְׁמִיעַ קוֹל שֶׁיַּחְפְּזוּ אֵלּוּ שֶׁכְּנֶגְדָן וְיָנוּסוּ, וְרוֹמְסִים בְּסוּסֵיהֶם וּמַצְהִילִין אוֹתָם לְהַשְׁמִיעַ קוֹל שַׁעֲטַת פַּרְסוֹת סוּסֵיהֶם, וְצוֹוְחִין בְּקוֹלָם, וְתוֹקְעִין בַּשּׁוֹפָרוֹת וּמִינֵי מַשְׁמִיעֵי קוֹל:<br>
**3 [English]**	**Let your hearts not be faint; you shall not be afraid, you shall not be alarmed, and you shall not be terrified.** Four warnings, corresponding to four practices in which the kings of the nations engage [during battle]: They hold their shields close together to strike them against one another, thereby producing a loud noise to alarm those confronting them, so that they should flee; they stamp [the ground heavily] with their horses and make them neigh, sounding the beating of their horses’ hoofs, and they shout loudly and blow horns and [other] kinds of noisy instruments.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**אַל־יֵרַךְ לְבַבְכֶם.** מִצַּהֲלַת סוּסִים:<br>
**3 [English]**	**Let your hearts not be faint.** Because of the neighing of the horses;

**3 [Hebrew]**	**אַל־תִּֽירְאוּ.** מֵהֲגָפַת הַתְּרִיסִין:<br>
**3 [English]**	**you shall not be afraid.** of the noise made by the fastening of the shields;

**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַל־תַּחְפְּזוּ.** מִקּוֹל הַקְּרָנוֹת:<br>
**3 [English]**	**and you shall not be alarmed.** At the sounds of the horns;

**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַל־תַּֽעַרְצוּ.** מִקּוֹל הַצְּוָחָה (שם):<br>
**3 [English]**	**and you shall not be terrified.** By the noise of the shouting. — [Sifrei ; Sotah 42a]



**4 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי ה' אֱלֹֽהֵיכֶם וגו'.** הֵם בָּאִים בְּנִצְחוֹנוֹ שֶׁל בָּשָׂר וָדָם וְאַתֶּם בָּאִים בְּנִצְחוֹנוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם. פְּלִשְׁתִּים בָּאוּ בְנִצְחוֹנוֹ שֶׁל גָּלְיָת, מֶה הָיָה סוֹפוֹ? נָפַל וְנָפְלוּ עִמּוֹ:<br>
**4 [English]**	**For the Lord, your God….** They are coming with the victory of flesh and blood, whereas you approach with the victory of the Omnipresent. The Philistines came with the victory of Goliath-What was his end? He fell, and they fell with him.

**4 [Hebrew]**	**הַֽהֹלֵךְ עִמָּכֶם.** זֶה מַחֲנֵה הָאָרוֹן (סוטה מ"ב):<br>
**4 [English]**	**Who goes with you.** This refers to the camp of the ark. — [Sotah 42a]



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא חֲנָכוֹ.** וְלֹא דָּר בּוֹ, חִנּוּךְ לְשׁוֹן הַתְחָלָה:<br>
**5 [English]**	**[What man is there who has built a new house] and has not [yet] inaugurated it.** i.e., has not yet lived in it. The term חִנּוּךְ denotes beginning.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִישׁ אַחֵר יַחְנְכֶֽנּוּ.** וְדָבָר שֶׁל עָגְמַת נֶפֶשׁ הוּא זֶה:<br>
**5 [English]**	**[Lest he die in the war,] and another man inaugurate it.** This would be a source of great grief.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא חִלְּלוֹ.** לֹא פְּדָאוֹ בַשָּׁנָה הָרְבִיעִית, שֶׁהַפֵּרוֹת טְעוּנִין לְאָכְלָן בִּירוּשָׁלַיִם אוֹ לְחַלְּלָן בְּדָמִים וְלֶאֱכֹל הַדָּמִים בִּירוּשָׁלַיִם:<br>
**6 [English]**	**[And what man is there that has planted a vineyard,] and has not [yet] redeemed it.** Heb. וְלֹא חִלְּלוֹ. He has not redeemed the vineyard in the fourth year [of its growth], for the fruits [of the fourth year] have to be eaten in Jerusalem or redeemed [by exchanging them] for money, and to eat [food purchased with] the money in Jerusalem.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**פֶּן־יָמוּת בַּמִּלְחָמָה.** יָשׁוּב פֶּן יָמוּת, שֶׁאִם לֹא יִשְׁמַע לְדִבְרֵי הַכֹּהֵן כְּדַאי הוּא שֶׁיָּמוּת (ספרי):<br>
**7 [English]**	**lest he die in the war.** He should return lest he die, for if he does not obey the kohen, he deserves to die. — [Sifrei]



**8 [Hebrew]**	**וְיָֽסְפוּ הַשֹּֽׁטְרִים.** לָמָּה נֶאֱמַר כָּאן ויספו? מוֹסִיפִין זֶה עַל דִּבְרֵי הַכֹּהֵן, שֶׁהַכֹּהֵן מְדַבֵּר וּמַשְׁמִיעַ מִן שמע ישראל עַד להושיע אתכם, וּמִי הָאִישׁ וְשֵׁנִי וּשְׁלִישִׁי כֹּהֵן מְדַבֵּר וְשׁוֹטֵר מַשְׁמִיעַ, וְזֶה שׁוֹטֵר מְדַבֵּר וְשׁוֹטֵר מַשְׁמִיעַ (סוטה מ"ג):<br>
**8 [English]**	**And the officers shall continue.** Why does it say here: “shall continue” [lit. shall add]? They add this [statement] to the words of the kohen, for the kohen speaks and announces aloud to the people from “Hear, O Israel” (verse 3) until “to save you,” (end of verse 4) while “What man is there” (verse 5), and the second and third one [with the same beginning (verses 6 and 7)], the kohen speaks, and an officer announces aloud [to the people]. This verse, however, an officer speaks, and an officer announces aloud. - [Sotah 43a] According to several incunabula editions of Rashi, “a kohen announces aloud.” [See Yosef Hallel.]

**8 [Hebrew]**	**הַיָּרֵא וְרַךְ הַלֵּבָב.** רַבִּי עֲקִיבָא אוֹמֵר כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ, שֶׁאֵינוֹ יָכוֹל לַעֲמֹד בְּקִשְׁרֵי הַמִּלְחָמָה וְלִרְאוֹת חֶרֶב שְׁלוּפָה; רַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי אוֹמֵר הַיָּרֵא מֵעֲבֵרוֹת שֶׁבְּיָדוֹ, וּלְכָךְ תָּלְתָה לוֹ תוֹרָה לַחֲזֹר עַל בַּיִת וְכֶרֶם וְאִשָּׁה, לְכַסּוֹת עַל הַחוֹזְרִים בִּשְׁבִיל עֲבֵרוֹת שֶׁבְּיָדָם שֶׁלֹּא יָבִינוּ שֶׁהֵם בַּעֲלֵי עֲבֵרָה, וְהָרוֹאֵהוּ חוֹזֵר אוֹמֵר שֶׁמָּא בָּנָה בַיִת אוֹ נָטַע כֶּרֶם אוֹ אֵרַשׂ אִשָּׁה (סוטה מ"ד):<br>
**8 [English]**	**[What man is there] who is fearful and fainthearted.** Rabbi Akiva says: [This verse is to be understood] according to its apparent meaning, that he cannot stand in the closed ranks of battle and look upon a drawn sword. Rabbi Jose the Galilean says that [it means] one who is afraid of his sins [that they will cause him to fall in war, as he is unworthy], and therefore, the Torah gives him the excuse of attributing his return home because of a house, a vineyard, or a wife, to cover up for those who return because of their sins, so that people should not understand that they are sinners. [Consequently,] one who sees this person returning would say, “Perhaps he has built a house, or planted a vineyard, or betrothed a woman.” - [Sotah 44a]



**9 [Hebrew]**	**שָׂרֵי צְבָאוֹת.** שֶׁמַּעֲמִידִין זַקָּפִין מִלִּפְנֵיהֶם וּמִלְּאַחֲרֵיהֶם וְכַשִּׁילִין שֶׁל בַּרְזֶל בִּידֵיהֶם וְכָל מִי שֶׁרוֹצֶה לַחֲזֹר הָרְשׁוּת בְּיָדוֹ לְקַפֵּחַ אֶת שׁוֹקָיו; זַקָּפִין – בְּנֵי אָדָם עוֹמְדִין בִּקְצֵה הַמַּעֲרָכָה לִזְקֹף אֶת הַנּוֹפְלִים וּלְחַזְּקָם בִּדְבָרִים, שׁוּבוּ אֶל הַמִּלְחָמָה וְלֹא תָנוּסוּ, שֶׁתְּחִלַּת נְפִילָה נִיסָה (ספרי; סוטה מ"ד):<br>
**9 [English]**	**[They shall appoint] officers of the legions.** This means that they place (זַקָּפִין) guards in front of them and behind them, with iron arrows in their hands, and if anybody attempted to retreat, the guard had the authority to strike his legs. זַקָּפִין are people who stand at the edge of the battle array to pick up (לִזְקֹף) the fallen and to encourage them with words: “Return to the battle and do not flee, for flight is the beginning of defeat.” - [Sifrei , Sotah 44a]



**10 [Hebrew]**	**כִּֽי־תִקְרַב אֶל־עִיר.** בְּמִלְחֶמֶת הָרְשׁוּת הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר, כְּמוֹ שֶׁמְּפֹרָשׁ בָּעִנְיָן "כֵּן תַּעֲשֶׂה לְכָל הֶעָרִים הָרְחֹקֹת וְגוֹ'" (ספרי):<br>
**10 [English]**	**When you approach a city [to wage war against it].** Scripture is speaking of an optional war, as is explicit in the context of this section (verse 15), “Thus you will do to all the cities which are very far away [from you]….” - [Sifrei]



**11 [Hebrew]**	**כָּל־הָעָם הַנִּמְצָא־בָהּ.** אֲפִלּוּ אַתָּה מוֹצֵא בָהּ מִשִּׁבְעָה אֻמּוֹת שֶׁנִּצְטַוִּיתָ לְהַחֲרִימָם, אַתָּה רַשַּׁאי לְקַיְּמָם (שם):<br>
**11 [English]**	**all the people found therein [shall become tributary].** Even if you find in it people of the seven nations, whom you have been commanded to exterminate, you are permitted to spare their lives. — [Sifrei]

**11 [Hebrew]**	**לָמַס וַֽעֲבָדֽוּךָ.** עַד שֶׁיְּקַבְּלוּ עֲלֵיהֶם מִסִּים וְשִׁעְבּוּד (שם):<br>
**11 [English]**	**tributary [to you], and they shall serve you.** [You shall fight them] until they accept upon themselves both tribute and bondage. — [Sifrei]



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְאִם־לֹא תַשְׁלִים עִמָּךְ וְעָֽשְׂתָה עִמְּךָ מִלְחָמָה.** הַכָּתוּב מְבַשֶּׂרְךָ שֶׁאִם לֹא תַשְׁלִים עִמְּךָ סוֹפָהּ לְהִלָּחֵם בְּךָ אִם תַּנִּיחֶנָּה וְתֵלֵךְ (שם):<br>
**12 [English]**	**But if it does make no peace with you, and it wages war against you.** Scripture is informing you that if it does not make peace with you, then, if you let it be and go away, [this city] will ultimately wage war against you.

**12 [Hebrew]**	**וְצַרְתָּ עָלֶֽיהָ.** אַף לְהַרְעִיבָהּ וּלְהַצְמִיאָהּ וְלַהֲמִיתָהּ מִיתַת תַּחֲלוּאִים (שם):<br>
**12 [English]**	**you shall besiege it.** Even to starve it out, and cause them to [die of] thirst and to kill [the inhabitants of the city] through diseases. — [Sifrei]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**וּנְתָנָהּ ה' אֱלֹהֶיךָ בְּיָדֶךָ.** אִם עָשִׂיתָ כָּל הָאָמוּר בָּעִנְיָן, סוֹף שֶׁה' נוֹתְנָהּ בְּיָדֶךָ (שם):<br>
**13 [English]**	**and the Lord your God will deliver it into your hands.** If you have done all that is stated in this section, the Lord will ultimately deliver it into your hands. — [Sifrei]



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַטַּף.** אַף טַף שֶׁל זְכָרִים, וּמָה אֲנִי מְקַיֵּם "וְהִכִּיתָ אֶת כָל זְכוּרָהּ"? בַּגְּדוֹלִים (שם):<br>
**14 [English]**	**And the children [… you shall take for yourself].** Even the male children. But, how then, am I to explain “and you shall strike all its males”? (verse 13) It refers to adult males. — [Sifrei]



**17 [Hebrew]**	**כַּֽאֲשֶׁר צִוְּךָ.** לְרַבּוֹת אֶת הַגִּרְגָּשִׁי (שם):<br>
**17 [English]**	**as [the Lord your God] has commanded you.** [This is] to include the Girgashites [the seventh nation, not mentioned here, but you were commanded to destroy them]. — [Sifrei]



**18 [Hebrew]**	**לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר לֹֽא־יְלַמְּדוּ.** הָא אִם עָשׂוּ תְשׁוּבָה וּמִתְגַיְּרִין אַתָּה רַשַּׁאי לְקַבְּלָם (שם):<br>
**18 [English]**	**so that they will not teach you to do [like all] their abominations].** But if they repent and wish to convert, you are allowed to accept them. — [Sifrei]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**רַבִּים.** שְׁלוֹשָׁה, מִכָּאן אָמְרוּ אֵין צָרִין עַל עַיָּרוֹת שֶׁל גּוֹיִם פָּחוֹת מִשְּׁלוֹשָׁה יָמִים קֹדֶם לַשַּׁבָּת, וְלִמֵּד שֶׁפּוֹתֵחַ בְּשָׁלוֹם שְׁנַיִם אוֹ שְׁלוֹשָׁה יָמִים, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר (שמואל ב א') וַיֵּשֶׁב דָּוִד בְּצִקְלָג יָמִים שְׁנָיִם (ספרי); וּבְמִלְחֶמֶת הָרְשׁוּת הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר:<br>
**19 [English]**	**many.** [this means] three [days]. From here our Rabbis derived [the ruling that] the siege of a heathen city may not be initiated less than three days before the Sabbath (Sifrei , Shab. 19a), and this verse teaches us that the offer of peace (verse 10) must be repeated for two or three days. Similarly, it says: “that David dwelt in Ziklag for two days” (II Sam. 1:1). Scripture is speaking here of an optional war. — [Sifrei]

**19 [Hebrew]**	**כִּֽי־הָֽאָדָם עֵץ הַשָּׂדֶה.** הֲרֵי כִּי מְשַׁמֵּשׁ בִּלְשׁוֹן דִּלְמָא, שֶׁמָּא הָאָדָם עֵץ הַשָּׂדֶה לְהִכָּנֵס בְּתוֹךְ הַמָּצוֹר מִפָּנֶיךָ לְהִתְיַסֵּר בְּיִסּוּרֵי רָעָב וְצָמָא כְּאַנְשֵׁי הָעִיר? לָמָּה תַּשְׁחִיתֶנּוּ?:<br>
**19 [English]**	**Is the tree of the field a man, [to go into the siege before you]?.** The word כִּי here means “perhaps:” Is the tree of the field perhaps a man who is to go into the siege by you, that it should be punished by the suffering of hunger and thirst like the people of the city? Why should you destroy it?



**20 [Hebrew]**	**עַד רִדְתָּֽהּ.** לְשׁוֹן רִדּוּי, שֶׁתְּהֵא כְּפוּפָה לְךָ:<br>
**20 [English]**	**until its submission.** Heb. רִדְתָּהּ, an expression of רִדּוּי, subjugation, [meaning] that it becomes submissive to you.
