**2 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא תֹסִפוּ.** כְּגוֹן חָמֵשׁ פָּרָשִׁיּוֹת בַּתְּפִלִּין, חֲמֵשֶׁת מִינִין בַּלּוּלָב, וְחָמֵשׁ צִיצִיּוֹת, וְכֵן "לֹא תִגְרְעוּ" (ע' ספרי פ' ראה):<br>
**2 [English]**	**Do not add.** for instance, by inserting five sections into the tefillin [instead of four], by using five species for the [commandment of] lulav [on Succoth] instead of four], or by attaching five fringes [instead of four]. And so too, וְלֹא תִגְרְעוּ nor diminish [from it i.e., three instead of four].



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וּשְׁמַרְתֶּם.** זוֹ מִשְׁנָה:<br>
**6 [English]**	**And you shall keep [them].** This refers to study.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽעֲשִׂיתֶם.** כְּמַשְׁמָעוֹ (שם):<br>
**6 [English]**	**and do [them].** [To be interpreted] according to its apparent meaning.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי הִוא חָכְמַתְכֶם וּבִינַתְכֶם וגו'.** בָּזֹאת תֵּחָשְׁבוּ חֲכָמִים וּנְבוֹנִים לְעֵינֵי הָעַמִּים:<br>
**6 [English]**	**for that is your wisdom and your understanding [in the eyes of the peoples].** Through this you will be considered wise and understanding in the eyes of the peoples.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**חֻקִּים וּמִשְׁפָּטִים צַדִּיקִם.** הֲגוּנִים וּמְקֻבָּלִים:<br>
**8 [English]**	**just statutes and ordinances.** צַדִּיקִם means worthy and acceptable ones.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**רַק הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ … פֶּן־תִּשְׁכַּח אֶת־הַדְּבָרִים.** אָז, כְּשֶׁלֹּא תִשְׁכְּחוּ אוֹתָם וְתַעֲשׂוּם עַל אֲמִתָּתָם, תֵּחָשְׁבוּ חֲכָמִים וּנְבוֹנִים, וְאִם תְּעַוְּתוּ אוֹתָם מִתּוֹךְ שִׁכְחָה, תֵּחָשְׁבוּ שׁוֹטִים:<br>
**9 [English]**	**But beware…lest you forget the things.** Only then, when you do not forget them, and will [therefore] do them in their proper manner, will you be considered wise and understanding, but if you distort them because of forgetfulness, you will be considered fools.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**יוֹם אֲשֶׁר עָמַדְתָּ.** מוּסָב עַל מִקְרָא שֶׁלְּמַעְלָה מִמֶּנּוּ אשר ראו עיניך – יום אשר עמדת בחרב אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתָ הַקּוֹלוֹת וְאֶת הַלַּפִּידִים:<br>
**10 [English]**	**the day you stood.** This refers back to the preceding verse :“which your eyes saw” [on] the day that you stood at Horeb, where you saw the thunder and the torches.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**יִלְמְדוּן.** יֵלְפוּן – לְעַצְמָם:<br>
**10 [English]**	**that they may learn.** Heb. יִלְמְדוּן The Targum [Onkelos] renders: יֵלְפוּן, they may learn for themselves.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**יְלַמֵּדוּן.** יְאַלְּפוּן – לַאֲחֵרִים:<br>
**10 [English]**	**that they may teach.** Heb. יְלַמֵּדוּן the Targum [Onkelos] renders: יְאַלְפוּן, that they may teach others.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֹתִי צִוָּה ה' לְלַמֵּד אֶתְכֶם.** תּוֹרָה שֶׁבְּעַל פֶּה:<br>
**14 [English]**	**And the Lord commanded me at that time to teach you.** the Oral Law.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**סָמֶל.** צוּרָה:<br>
**16 [English]**	**form.** Heb. סָמֶל, meaning “form.”



**19 [Hebrew]**	**וּפֶן־תִּשָּׂא עֵינֶיךָ.** לְהִסְתַּכֵּל בַּדָּבָר וְלָתֵת לֵב לִטְעוֹת אַחֲרֵיהֶם:<br>
**19 [English]**	**And lest you lift up your eyes.** to gaze at this thing and to set your heart to stray after them.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר חָלַק ה'.** לְהָאִיר לָהֶם (מגילה ט'); דָּבָר אַחֵר לֶאֱלוֹהוֹת, לֹא מְנָעָן מִלִּטְעוֹת אַחֲרֵיהֶם אֶלָּא הֶחֱלִיקָם בְּדִבְרֵי הַבְלֵיהֶם לְטָרְדָם מִן הָעוֹלָם, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר (תהלים ל"ו) "כִּי הֶחֱלִיק אֵלָיו בְּעֵינָיו לִמְצֹא עֲוֹנוֹ לִשְׂנֹא" (עבודה זרה נ"ה):<br>
**19 [English]**	**which the Lord… assigned.** to illuminate for them [all peoples]. (Meg. 9b) Another explanation: Which God assigned to them as deities; He did not prevent them from erring after them; rather, He caused them to slip, [i.e., to err], with their futile speculations, in order to drive them out of the world. Similarly, it says: “He [God] smoothed the way for him in his eyes to find his iniquity to hate [him]” (Ps. 36:3) (Avodah Zarah 55a).



**20 [Hebrew]**	**מִכּוּר.** כּוּר הוּא כְלִי שֶׁמְּזַקְּקִים בּוֹ אֶת הַזָּהָב:<br>
**20 [English]**	**out of the iron crucible.** Heb. מִכּוּר. A כּוּר is a vessel in which gold is refined.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְאַנַּף־.** נִתְמַלֵּא רֹגֶז:<br>
**21 [English]**	**was angry.** Heb. הִתְאַנַּף, [The hithpa’el conjugation denotes that] He became filled with wrath.

**21 [Hebrew]**	**עַל־דִּבְרֵיכֶם.** עַל אוֹדוֹתֵיכֶם, עַל עִסְקֵיכֶם:<br>
**21 [English]**	**because of you.** Heb. עַל-דִּבְרֵיכֶם, because of you, on your account.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי אָֽנֹכִי מֵת וגו' אֵינֶנִּי עֹבֵר.** מֵאַחַר שֶׁמֵּת מֵהֵיכָן יַעֲבֹר? אֶלָּא אַף עַצְמוֹתַי אֵינָם עוֹבְרִים (ספרי במדבר כ"ז):<br>
**22 [English]**	**For I will die… I will not cross.** Since he was to die, how could he cross? But rather he meant: even my bones will not cross (Sifrei on Numbers 27:12).



**23 [Hebrew]**	**תְּמוּנַת כֹּל.** תְּמוּנַת כָּל דָּבָר:<br>
**23 [English]**	**the likeness of anything.** Heb. תְּמוּנַת כֹּל, the likeness of anything.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר צִוְּךָ ה'.** אֲשֶׁר צִוְּךָ שֶׁלֹּא לַעֲשׂוֹת:<br>
**23 [English]**	**which the Lord… commanded you.** Which He commanded you not to make.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**אֵל קַנָּֽא.** מְקַנֵּא לִנְקֹם, אנפר"מנט בְּלַעַז, מִתְחָרֶה עַל רָגְזוֹ לְהִפָּרַע מֵעוֹבְדֵי עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה:<br>
**24 [English]**	**a zealous God.** Zealous to wreak vengeance, in Old French, anprenemant, zealous anger. He burns in His anger to exact retribution from idol worshippers.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**וְנֽוֹשַׁנְתֶּם.** רָמַז לָהֶם שֶׁיִּגְלוּ מִמֶּנָּה לְסוֹף שְׁמוֹנֶה מֵאוֹת וַחֲמִשִּׁים וּשְׁתַּיִם שָׁנָה כְּמִנְיַן "וְנוֹשַׁנְתֶּם", וְהוּא הִקְדִּים וְהִגְלָם לְסוֹף שְׁמוֹנֶה מֵאוֹת וַחֲמִשִּׁים, וְהִקְדִּים שְׁתֵּי שָׁנִים לִ"וְנוֹשַׁנְתֶּם", כְּדֵי שֶׁלֹּא יִתְקַיֵּם בָּהֶם כִּי אָבֹד תֹּאבֵדוּן, וְזֶהוּ שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (דניאל ט') "וַיִּשְׁקֹד עַל הָרָעָה וַיְבִיאֶהָ עָלֵינוּ כִּי צַדִּיק ה' אֱלֹהֵינוּ" – צְדָקָה עָשָׂה עִמָּנוּ שֶׁמִּהֵר לַהֲבִיאָהּ שְׁתֵּי שָׁנִים לִפְנֵי זְמַנָּהּ (סנהדרין ל"ח; גיטין פ"ח):<br>
**25 [English]**	**and you will be long established.** Heb. וְנוֹשַׁנְתֶּם. He hinted to them that they would be exiled from it at the end of 852 years, the gematria, numerical value, of the word וְנוֹשַׁנְתֶּם, but He exiled them earlier, at the end of 850 years. He did this two years earlier than the numerical value of וְנוֹשַׁנְתֶּם in order that the prophecy about them should not be fulfilled “that you shall utterly perish.”(verse 26) This is the meaning of what is said: “And the Lord ‘hastened’ with the evil and brought it upon us, for the Lord our God is charitable (צַדִּיק)” (Dan. 9:14). He was charitable with us for He hastened to bring it [the exile] two years before its time (San. 38a; Gittin 88a).



**26 [Hebrew]**	**הַֽעִידֹתִי בָכֶם.** הִנְנִי מַזְמִינָם לִהְיוֹת עֵדִים שֶׁהִתְרֵיתִי בָכֶם:<br>
**26 [English]**	**I call as witness against you [… heaven and earth].** I hereby summon them to be witnesses that I have warned you.



**28 [Hebrew]**	**וַֽעֲבַדְתֶּם־שָׁם אֱלֹהִים.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ, מִשֶּׁאַתֶּם עוֹבְדִים לְעוֹבְדֵיהֶם כְּאִלּוּ אַתֶּם עוֹבְדִים לָהֶם:<br>
**28 [English]**	**And there you will worship gods.** As the Targum [Onkelos] explains: Since you serve those who worship them [idols], it is as though you [yourselves] serve them [i.e., the idols].



**31 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא יַרְפְּךָ.** מִלְּהַחֲזִיק בְּךָ בְּיָדָיו, וּלְשׁוֹן לֹא יַרְפְּךָ לְשׁוֹן לֹא יַפְעִיל הוּא – לֹא יִתֵּן לְךָ רִפְיוֹן, לֹא יַפְרִישׁ אוֹתְךָ מֵאֶצְלוֹ, וְכֵן "אֲחַזְתִּיו וְלֹא אַרְפֶּנּוּ" (שיר השירים ג') שֶׁלֹּא נִנְקַד אֶרְפֶּנּוּ; כָּל לְשׁוֹן רִפְיוֹן מוּסָב עַל לְשׁוֹן מַפְעִיל וּמִתְפַּעֵל, כְּמוֹ "הַרְפֵּה לָהּ" (מלכים ב ד') – תֵּן לָהּ רִפְיוֹן, "הֶרֶף מִמֶּנִּי" (דברים ט') – הִתְרַפֵּה מִמֶּנִּי:<br>
**31 [English]**	**He will not let you loose.** He will not let go of you with His hands. The expression לֹא יַרְפְּךָ means that He will not cause something, i.e., He will not cause you looseness. He will not separate you from [being] near Him. Similarly, “I held him fast, and I would not let him loose (אַרְפֶּנּוּ)” (Song of Songs 3:4), which is not vocalized אֲרַפֶּנּוּ [which would mean to heal]. The term רִפְיוֹן “letting slack” always adopts the hif’il [causative conjugation, that is, causing someone else רִפְיוֹן] or the hithpa’el [reflexive conjugation, that is, causing oneself רִפְיוֹן]. For example: (II Kings 4:2), הַרְפֵּה לָהּ, let her be, means literally “give her looseness [i.e., an example of causing to others]”; (Deut. 9:14) הֶרֶף מִמֶּנִּי, let Me be, means literally “Make yourself loose from me [i.e., an example of causing looseness to oneself].”



**32 [Hebrew]**	**לְיָמִים ראשונים.** עַל יָמִים רִאשׁוֹנִים:<br>
**32 [English]**	**regarding the early days.** Heb. לְיָמִים רִאשׁוֹנִים [the“lammed” of לְיָמִים here means] regarding the early days.

**32 [Hebrew]**	**וּלְמִקְצֵה הַשָּׁמַיִם.** וְגַם שְׁאַל לְכָל הַבְּרוּאִים אֲשֶׁר מִקָּצֶה אֶל קָצֶה, זֶהוּ פְּשׁוּטוֹ; וּמִדְרָשׁוֹ, מְלַמֵּד עַל קוֹמָתוֹ שֶׁל אָדָם שֶׁהָיְתָה מִן הָאָרֶץ עַד הַשָּׁמַיִם, וְהוּא הַשִּׁעוּר עַצְמוֹ אֲשֶׁר מִקָּצֶה אֶל קָצֶה (סנהדרין ל"ח):<br>
**32 [English]**	**and from one end of the heavens.** And also ask of all the creatures from one end [of the heavens] to the other end. This is its simple meaning, but its midrashic explanation is: [This] teaches [us] about Adam’s height, that it was from the earth to the heavens, and that this is the very same measurement as from one end of the heavens to the other end (San. 38b).

**32 [Hebrew]**	**הֲנִֽהְיָה כַּדָּבָר הַגָּדוֹל הַזֶּה.** וּמַהוּ הַדָּבָר הַגָּדוֹל, השמע עם וגו':<br>
**32 [English]**	**whether there was anything like this great thing.** And what is this great thing? “Did ever a people hear,” etc.



**34 [Hebrew]**	**אוֹ הֲנִסָּה אֱלֹהִים.** הֲכִי עָשָׂה נִסִּים שׁוּם אֱלוֹהַּ , :<br>
**34 [English]**	**Or has any god performed miracles.** Heb. הֲנִסָּה אלֹהִים. Has any god performed miracles (נִסִּים)?

**34 [Hebrew]**	**לָבוֹא לָקַחַת לוֹ גוֹי וגו'.** כָּל הֵהִי"ן הַלָּלוּ תְּמִיהוֹת הֵן, לְכָךְ נְקוּדוֹת הֵן בַּחֲטָף פַּתָּח: הֲנִהְיָה, הֲנִשְׁמַע, הֲשָׁמַע, הֲנִסָּה:<br>
**34 [English]**	**to come and take him a nation….** All the letters “hey” are in the interrogative form. Therefore, they are vocalized with a chataf patach הִנִהְיָה has there been? הִנִשְׁמַע has it been heard? הִשָמַע did there hear? הֲנִסָּה did… perform miracles?

**34 [Hebrew]**	**בְּמַסֹּת.** עַל יְדֵי נִסְיוֹנוֹת הוֹדִיעָם גְּבוּרוֹתָיו, כְּגוֹן "הִתְפָּאֵר עָלַי" (שמות ח') אִם אוּכַל לַעֲשׂוֹת כֵּן, הֲרֵי זֶה נִסָּיוֹן:<br>
**34 [English]**	**with trials.** Through tests, He let them know His might, for example: “[and Moses said to Pharaoh] 'Boast of your superiority over me [to fix a time]'” (Exodus 8:5), whether I am able to do so. This is a test.

**34 [Hebrew]**	**בְּאֹתֹת.** בְּסִימָנִין, לְהַאֲמִין שֶׁהוּא שְׁלוּחוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם, כְּגוֹן "מַה זֶּה בְיָדֶךָ" (שם ד'):<br>
**34 [English]**	**with signs.** Heb. בְּאֹתֹת. With signs, so that they should believe that he [Moses] was the messenger of the Omnipresent, as, e.g., “What is that in your hand?” (Exod. 4:2)

**34 [Hebrew]**	**וּבְמֽוֹפְתִים.** הֵם נִפְלָאוֹת, שֶׁהֵבִיא עֲלֵיהֶם מַכּוֹת מֻפְלָאוֹת:<br>
**34 [English]**	**and with wonders.** Heb. וּבְמוֹפְתִים. These are wonders, [meaning] that God brought upon them [the Egyptians] wondrous plagues.

**34 [Hebrew]**	**ובמלחמה.** בים, שנאמר (שם יד כה) כי ה' נלחם להם:<br>
**34 [English]**	**and with war.** At the Red Sea, as it is said: “because the Lord is fighting for them” (Exod. 14:25).



**35 [Hebrew]**	**הָרְאֵתָ.** כְּתַרְגּוּמוֹ "אִתְחֲזֵיתָא"; כְּשֶׁנָּתַן הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא אֶת הַתּוֹרָה פָּתַח לָהֶם שִׁבְעָה רְקִיעִים, וּכְשֵׁם שֶׁקָּרַע אֶת הָעֶלְיוֹנִים כָּךְ קָרַע אֶת הַתַּחְתּוֹנִים, וְרָאוּ שֶׁהוּא יְחִידִי, לְכָךְ נֶאֱמַר "אַתָּה הָרְאֵתָ לָדַעַת":<br>
**35 [English]**	**You have been shown.** Heb. הָרְאֵתָ As the Targum [Onkelos] renders it: אִתְחִזֵיתָא, you have been shown. When the Holy One, blessed is He, gave the Torah, He opened for Israel the seven heavens, and just as He tore open the upper regions, so did He tear open the lower regions, and they saw that He is One. Accordingly, it is stated, “You have been shown, in order to know [that the Lord He is God-there is none else besides Him].”



**37 [Hebrew]**	**וְתַחַת כִּי אָהַב.** וְכָל זֶה תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר אָהַב:<br>
**37 [English]**	**And because He loved.** And all this was because He loved [your forefathers].

**37 [Hebrew]**	**וַיּוֹצִֽאֲךָ בְּפָנָיו.** כְּאָדָם הַמַּנְהִיג בְּנוֹ לְפָנָיו, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "וַיִּסַּע מַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים הַהֹלֵךְ וְגוֹ' וַיֵּלֶךְ מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶם" (שם). דָּבָר אַחֵר, ויוצאך בפניו – בִּפְנֵי אֲבוֹתָיו, כְּמָה שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר "נֶגֶד אֲבוֹתָם עָשָׂה פֶלֶא" (תהילים ע"ח); וְאַל תִּתְמַהּ עַל שֶׁהִזְכִּירָם בִּלְשׁוֹן יָחִיד, שֶׁהֲרֵי כְתָבָם בִּלְשׁוֹן יָחִיד – ויבחר בזרעו אחריו:<br>
**37 [English]**	**and He brought you out… before Him.** like a man who leads his son before him, as it is stated (Exod. 14:19), “Then the angel of the Lord who had been going, who had been going [in front of the Israelite camp,] moved and went behind them.” Another explanation: And He brought you out before him-before his forefathers, as it is said: “Before their forefathers, He wrought wonders” (Ps. 78:12). And do not be astonished by the fact that [Scripture] refers to them in the singular [using בְּפָנָיו instead of בִּפְנֵיהֶם], for it has already written about them in the singular, “And chose their seed (בְּזַרְעוֹ) after them (אַחֲרָיו),” [lit. his. seed after him].



**38 [Hebrew]**	**מִמְּךָ מִפָּנֶיךָ.** סָרְסֵהוּ וְדָרְשֵׁהוּ: לְהוֹרִישׁ מִפָּנֶיךָ גּוֹיִם גְּדוֹלִים וַעֲצוּמִים מִמְּךָ:<br>
**38 [English]**	**from before you [nations greater and stronger] than you.** Heb. מִמְּךָ מִפָּנֶי‏ךָ, lit. than you from before you. The verse can be explained by transposing it: to drive out from before you מִפָּנֶי‏ךָ‏, nations greater and stronger than you מִמְּךָ.

**38 [Hebrew]**	**כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּֽה.** כַּאֲשֶׁר אַתָּה רוֹאֶה הַיּוֹם:<br>
**38 [English]**	**as this day.** As you see today.



**41 [Hebrew]**	**אָז יַבְדִּיל.** נָתַן לֵב לִהְיוֹת חָרֵד לַדָּבָר שֶׁיַּבְדִּילֵם, וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵינָן קוֹלְטוֹת עַד שֶׁיֻּבְדְּלוּ אוֹתָן שֶׁבְּאֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן, אָמַר מֹשֶׁה מִצְוָה שֶׁאֶפְשָׁר לְקַיְּמָהּ אֲקַיְּמֶנָּה (מכות י'):<br>
**41 [English]**	**Then [Moses] decided to separate.** Heb. אָז יַבְדִּיל [The future form יַבְדִּיל instead of the past form הִבְדִּיל is to be explained]: Moses set his heart to hasten to [implement] the matter to separate them. And even though they were not to serve as cities of refuge until those of the land of Canaan would be separated, Moses said, “Any commandment that is possible to fulfill, I will fulfill” (Makkoth 10a).

**41 [Hebrew]**	**בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן מִזְרְחָה שָֽׁמֶשׁ.** בְּאוֹתוֹ עֵבֶר שֶׁבְּמִזְרָחוֹ שֶׁל יַרְדֵּן:<br>
**41 [English]**	**on the side of the Jordan towards the sunrise.** On that side which is on the east of the Jordan.

**41 [Hebrew]**	**מִזְרְחָה שָֽׁמֶשׁ.** לְפִי שֶׁהוּא דָּבוּק, נְקוּדָה רֵי"שׁ בַּחֲטָף, מִזְרָח שֶׁל שֶׁמֶשׁ – מְקוֹם זְרִיחַת הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ:<br>
**41 [English]**	**towards the sunrise.** Heb. מִזְרְחָה שָׁמֶשׁ Because the word מִזְרְחָה is in the construct state, the “reish” is punctuated with a “chataf” (vocal “sh’va”), the meaning being, “the rising of the sun,” i.e., the place of the sunrise.



**44 [Hebrew]**	**וְזֹאת הַתּוֹרָה.** זוֹ שֶׁהוּא עָתִיד לְסַדֵּר אַחַר פָּרָשָׁה זוֹ:<br>
**44 [English]**	**And this is the teaching.** This one which he is about to set down after this chapter.



**45 [Hebrew]**	**אֵלֶּה הָֽעֵדֹת אֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר.** הֵם הֵם אֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּר בְּצֵאתָם מִמִּצְרַיִם, חָזַר וּשְׁנָאָהּ לָהֶם בְּעַרְבוֹת מוֹאָב:<br>
**45 [English]**	**These are the testimonies… which [Moses] spoke.** They are the very same ones that he spoke when they went out of Egypt, and he taught it to them again in the plains of Moab.



**47 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן.** שֶׁהוּא בַּמִּזְרָח, שֶׁהָעֵבֶר הַשֵּׁנִי הָיָה בַּמַּעֲרָב:<br>
**47 [English]**	**who were on the side of the Jordan.** which is in the east, because the other side was in the west. [That means on the side opposite the western side.]
