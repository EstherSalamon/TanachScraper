**1 [Hebrew]**	**אֵת כָּל־הַדָּבָר.** קַלָּה כַּחֲמוּרָה (שם):<br>
**1 [English]**	**Everything [that I command you].** less significant [commandments] as well as more significant ones. — [Sifrei]

**1 [Hebrew]**	**תִשְׁמְרוּ לַֽעֲשׂוֹת.** לִתֵּן לֹא תַעֲשֶׂה עַל עֲשֵׂה הָאֲמוּרִים בַּפָּרָשָׁה, שֶׁכָּל הִשָּׁמֶר לְשׁוֹן לֹא תַעֲשֶׂה הוּא, אֶלָּא שֶׁאֵין לוֹקִין עַל הִשָּׁמֶר שֶׁל עֲשֵׂה (מכות י"ג):<br>
**1 [English]**	**that you shall be careful to do.** [This statement] attaches a negative commandment to the positive commandments mentioned in this parashah, for wherever הִשָּׁמֵר is used, it is an expression of a negative commandment. Lashes, however, are not incurred for [infringement of a prohibition when] the term הִשָּׁמֵר is attached to a positive commandment.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־תֹסֵף עָלָיו.** חֲמִשָּׁה טֹטָפוֹת, חֲמִשָּׁה מִינִין בַּלּוּלָב, אַרְבַּע בְּרָכוֹת לְבִרְכַּת כֹּהֲנִים (ספרי):<br>
**1 [English]**	**You shall neither add to it.** [e.g., placing] five chapters in tefillin [instead of four], or [using] five species for the lulav [instead of four], or [reciting] four blessings [instead of three] for the “blessing of the kohanim.” - [Sifrei]



**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְנָתַן אֵלֶיךָ אוֹת.** בַּשָּׁמַיִם, כָּעִנְיָן שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר בְּגִדְעוֹן (שופטים ו') "וְעָשִׂיתָ לִּי אוֹת" וְאוֹמֵר "יְהִי נָא חֹרֶב אֶל הַגִּזָּה" וְגוֹ':<br>
**2 [English]**	**[If there will arise among you a prophet…] and he gives you a sign.** Heb. אוֹת, [meaning a sign] in the heavens, as it is stated in the case of Gideon [who said to the angel]: “then show me a sign (אוֹת)” (Jud. 6:17), and then it says [further], “let it be dry only upon the fleece [and upon all the ground let there be dew]” (Jud. 6:39).

**2 [Hebrew]**	**אוֹ מוֹפֵֽת.** בָּאָרֶץ:<br>
**2 [English]**	**or a wonder.** Heb. מוֹפֵת, [meaning a sign] on the earth. (other texts [of Rashi read as follows]:

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וְנָתַן אֵלֶיךָ אוֹת.** בשמים דכתיב והיו לאותות ולמועדים:<br>
**2 [English]**	**and he gives you a sign.** in the heavens, as is written, “and they [i.e., the sun, the moon, and the stars] shall be for signs (לְאֹתֹת) and for seasons” [Gen. 1: 14].

**2 [Hebrew]**	**אוֹ מוֹפֵֽת.** בארץ דכתיב אם טל יהיה על הגזה לבדה ועל כל הארץ חורב)
, אַף עַל פִּי כֵן לֹא תִשְׁמַע לוֹ, וְאִם תֹּאמַר מִפְּנֵי מָה נוֹתֵן לוֹ הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא מֶמְשָׁלָה לַעֲשׂוֹת אוֹת? כי מנסה ה' אלהיכם אתכם (ספרי; עי' סנהדרין צ'):<br>
**2 [English]**	**or a wonder.** on the earth, as is written, “If there will be dew on the fleece only, and upon all the ground, dry” [Jud. 6:39]). Even so, you shall not listen to him. But if you say, “Why then does the Holy One, blessed is He, give him the power to perform a sign? [Scripture replies,] ”for the Lord, your God, is testing you [… whether you really love the Lord your God]" (verse 4). - [Sifrei; San. 90a]



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת־מצותיו תִּשְׁמֹרוּ.** תּוֹרַת מֹשֶׁה:<br>
**5 [English]**	**keep His commandments.** the Torah of Moses.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וּבְקֹלוֹ תִשְׁמָעוּ.** בְּקוֹל הַנְּבִיאִים:<br>
**5 [English]**	**heed His voice.** the voice of the prophets.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֹתוֹ תַֽעֲבֹדוּ.** בְּמִקְדָּשׁוֹ (ספרי):<br>
**5 [English]**	**worship Him.** in His sanctuary. — [Sifrei]

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וּבוֹ תִדְבָּקֽוּן.** הִדַּבֵּק בִּדְרָכָיו – גְּמֹל חֲסָדִים, קְבֹר מֵתִים, בַּקֵּר חוֹלִים, כְּמוֹ שֶׁעָשָׂה הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא (סוטה י"ד):<br>
**5 [English]**	**and cleave to Him.** Cleave to His ways: bestow kindness, bury the dead, and visit the sick, just as the Holy One, blessed is He, did. — [Sotah 14a]



**6 [Hebrew]**	**סָרָה.** דָּבָר הַמּוּסָר מִן הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה וְלֹא נִבְרָא וְלֹא צִוִּיתִיו לְדַבֵּר כֵּן, דישטו"רנורא בְּלַעַז:<br>
**6 [English]**	**[Because he spoke] falsehood.** Heb. סָרָה, lit. something turned away, removed from the world, which neither existed nor was created, and which I never commanded him to say; destodre in Old French, to distort.

**6 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַפֹּֽדְךָ מִבֵּית עֲבָדִים.** אֲפִלּוּ אֵין לוֹ עָלֶיךָ אֶלָּא שֶׁפְּדָאֲךָ — דַּיּוֹ (ספרי):<br>
**6 [English]**	**and Who redeemed you from the house of bondage.** Even if God had no [other claim] on you other than that He had redeemed you, it would be sufficient [to demand your obedience]. — [Sifrei]



**7 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי יְסִֽיתְךָ.** אֵין הֲסָתָה אֶלָּא גֵּרוּי, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ש"מ כ"ו) אִם ה' הֱסִיתְךָ בִּי, אנסיטר"א בְּלַעַז, שֶׁמַּשִּׂיאוֹ לַעֲשׂוֹת כֵּן:<br>
**7 [English]**	**If [your brother…] tempts you.** The term הֲסָתָה always denotes inciting as it is said, אִם ה׳ הֶסִיתְךָ, “If the Lord has incited you against me” (I Sam. 26:19); ametra in Old French, will incite, that he tempts him to do so.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**אָחִיךָ.** מֵאָב:<br>
**7 [English]**	**your brother.** from the father[’s side].

**7 [Hebrew]**	**בֶן־אִמֶּךָ.** מֵאֵם:<br>
**7 [English]**	**the son of your mother.** from the mother[’s side].

**7 [Hebrew]**	**חֵיקֶךָ.** הַשּׁוֹכֶבֶת בְּחֵיקְךָ וּמְחֻקָּה בְּךָ, אפקיי"דא בְּלַעַז, וְכֵן (יחזקאל מ"ג), "וּמֵחֵיק הָאָרֶץ" – מִיסוֹד הַתָּקוּעַ בָּאָרֶץ:<br>
**7 [English]**	**of your embrace.** The one that lies in your embrace and is attached (מְחֻקָּה) to you; afichiede in Old French, bound, attached (Greenberg), afichier in Old French, to attach (Gukovitzki). This is similar to וּמֵחֵיק הָאָרֶץ (Ezek. 43:14), which refers to the base [of the altar] that is affixed firmly in the ground.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר כְּנַפְשְׁךָ.** זֶה אָבִיךָ, פֵּרֵשׁ לְךָ הַכָּתוּב אֶת הַחֲבִיבִין לְךָ, קַל וָחֹמֶר לַאֲחֵרִים:<br>
**7 [English]**	**[Or your friend,] who is as your own soul.** This refers to your father. Scripture enumerates those [persons] who are dear to you; how much more so [must you apply this] to others [and not spare them]!

**7 [Hebrew]**	**בַּסֵּתֶר.** דִּבֵּר הַכָּתוּב בַּהוֹוֶה, שֶׁאֵין דִּבְרֵי מֵסִית אֶלָּא בַּסֵּתֶר, וְכֵן שְׁלֹמֹה הוּא אוֹמֵר (משלי ז') "בְּנֶשֶׁף בְּעֶרֶב יוֹם בְּאִישׁוֹן לַיְלָה וַאֲפֵלָה":<br>
**7 [English]**	**[If your brother…tempts you] in secret.** Scripture speaks of what commonly [occurs, but does not exclude the other case, i.e., one who entices publicly], for usually the one who entices speaks clandestinely. Similarly, Solomon [describing an episode of enticement,] says: “In the twilight, in the evening time, in the blackness of night and the darkness” (Prov. 7:9). - [Sifrei]

**7 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר לֹא יָדַעְתָּ אַתָּה וַֽאֲבֹתֶֽיךָ.** דָּבָר זֶה גְּנַאי גָּדוֹל הוּא לְךָ – שֶׁאַף הָאֻמּוֹת אֵין מַנִּיחִין מַה שֶּׁמָּסְרוּ לָהֶם אֲבוֹתֵיהֶם, וְזֶה אוֹמֵר לְךָ עֲזֹב מַה שֶּׁמָּסְרוּ לְךָ אֲבוֹתֶיךָ (ספרי):<br>
**7 [English]**	**[Let us go and worship other gods,] which neither you nor your forefathers have known.** This would be a great disgrace for you, for not even the heathen nations forsake what their forefathers have handed down to them. Yet this [man] says to you, “Abandon what your forefathers have handed down to you!” - [Sifrei]



**8 [Hebrew]**	**הַקְּרֹבִים אֵלֶיךָ אוֹ הָֽרְחֹקִים.** לָמָּה פֵּרֵט קְרוֹבִים וּרְחוֹקִים? אֶלָּא כָּךְ אָמַר הַכָּתוּב: מִטִּיבָן שֶׁל קְרוֹבִים אַתָּה לָמֵד טִיבָן שֶׁל רְחוֹקִים, כְּשֵׁם שֶׁאֵין מַמָּשׁ בַּקְּרוֹבִים, כָּךְ אֵין מַמָּשׁ בָּרְחוֹקִים (סנהדרין ס"א):<br>
**8 [English]**	**Let us go and worship other gods…] whether near to you or far [from you].** Why did [Scripture] specify [gods] that are “near… or far”? Rather, this is what Scripture says: Through [knowing] the character of those [gods] close to you, you learn the character of those far from you; just as those close to you are devoid of substance, so are those far from you devoid of substance. — [San. 61b; Sifrei]

**8 [Hebrew]**	**מִקְצֵה הָאָרֶץ.** זוֹ חַמָּה וּלְבָנָה וּצְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם שֶׁהֵן מְהַלְּכִין מִסּוֹף הָעוֹלָם וְעַד סוֹפוֹ (ספרי):<br>
**8 [English]**	**[gods… far from you,] from one end of the earth [to the other end of the earth].** This refers to the sun, the moon, and the legion of heavens [i.e., the stars], which extend from one end of the world to the other. — [Sifrei]



**9 [Hebrew]**	**לֹֽא־תֹאבֶה לוֹ.** לֹא תְהֵא תָאֵב לוֹ, לֹא תֶּאֱהָבֶנּוּ, לְפִי שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ויקרא י"ט) וְאָהַבְתָּ לְרֵעֲךָ כָּמוֹךָ – אֶת זֶה לֹא תֶאֱהַב:<br>
**9 [English]**	**You shall not desire him.** Heb. לֹא-תֹאבֶה. You shall feel no longing (תאב) towards him; do not love him (לֹא תֹאהֲבֶנוּ)). Since it is said, “You shall love your fellow man as yourself” (Lev. 19:18), [therefore it is necessary to tell us that] this person, [however,] you shall not love.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תִשְׁמַע אֵלָיו.** בְּהִתְחַנְּנוֹ עַל נַפְשׁוֹ לִמְחֹל לוֹ, לְפִי שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (שמות כ"ג) עָזֹב תַּעֲזֹב עִמּוֹ – לָזֶה לֹא תַעֲזֹב:<br>
**9 [English]**	**and do not hearken to him.** when he pleads for his life, that you should pardon him. [It is necessary to state this] because it says, “[If you see the donkey of your enemy] you shall surely help him” (Exod. 23:5); this person, [however,] you shall not help.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תָחוֹס עֵֽינְךָ עָלָיו.** לְפִי שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר (ויקרא י"ט) לֹא תַעֲמֹד עַל דַּם רֵעֶךָ – עַל זֶה לֹא תָחֹס:<br>
**9 [English]**	**neither shall you pity him.** [It is necessary to state this] because it says, “you shall not stand idly by the blood of your fellow man” (Lev. 19:16). This person [however] you shall not pity. — [Sifrei]

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תַחְמֹל.** לֹא תַהֲפֹךְ בִּזְכוּתוֹ:<br>
**9 [English]**	**[neither shall you…] have mercy upon him.** Do not search for merits in his favor.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תְכַסֶּה עָלָיו.** אִם אַתָּה יוֹדֵעַ לוֹ חוֹבָה אֵינְךָ רַשַּׁאי לִשְׁתֹּק (ספרי):<br>
**9 [English]**	**nor shall you shield him.** If you are aware of something that will condemn him, you are not permitted to remain silent. — [Sifrei]



**10 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי הָרֹג תַּֽהַרְגֶנּוּ.** אִם יָצָא מִבֵּית דִּין זַכַּאי הַחֲזִירֵהוּ לְחוֹבָה, יָצָא מִבֵּית דִּין חַיָּב אַל תַּחֲזִירֵהוּ לִזְכוּת (שם):<br>
**10 [English]**	**But you shall surely kill him.** (If he has left the court, acquitted, have him returned for conviction;) if [on the other hand,] he has left the court convicted, do not return him for acquittal. — [Sifrei]

**10 [Hebrew]**	**יָֽדְךָ תִּֽהְיֶה־בּוֹ בָרִֽאשׁוֹנָה.** מִצְוָה בְּיַד הַנִּסָּת לַהֲמִיתוֹ. לֹא מֵת בְּיָדוֹ, יָמוּת בְּיַד אֲחֵרִים, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר ויד כל העם וגו' (עי' ספרי):<br>
**10 [English]**	**Your hand shall be the first against him [to put him to death].** The one who was tempted [to idolatry by this person] has the duty to put him to death. [However] if he did not die by his hand, he must die by the hand of others, as is stated (in this verse), “and afterwards the hand of all the people, etc.” - [Sifrei]



**13 [Hebrew]**	**לָשֶׁבֶת שָׁם.** פְּרָט לִירוּשָׁלַיִם שֶׁלֹּא נִתְּנָה לְדִירָה (שם):<br>
**13 [English]**	**[If you will hear in one of your cities which the Lord, your God, gives you] to dwell therein.** [The words “to dwell therein” are added] to exclude Jerusalem, which was not apportioned as a dwelling place [for a particular tribe]. — [Sifrei]

**13 [English]**	**If you hear… saying.** Heb. לֵאמֹר, lit. to say. [If you hear people] saying thus:



**14 [Hebrew]**	**אֲנָשִׁים.** וְלֹא נָשִׁים:<br>
**14 [English]**	**men.** But not women.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**בְּנֵֽי־בְלִיַּעַל.** בְּלִי עֹל, שֶׁפָּרְקוּ עֻלּוֹ שֶׁל מָקוֹם (שם):<br>

**14 [Hebrew]**	**יֽשְׁבֵי עִירָם.** וְלֹא יוֹשְׁבֵי עִיר אַחֶרֶת, מִכָּאן אָמְרוּ אֵין נַעֲשֵׂית עִיר הַנִּדַּחַת עַד שֶׁיַּדִּיחוּהָ אֲנָשִׁים וְעַד שֶׁיִּהְיוּ מַדִּיחֶיהָ מִתּוֹכָהּ (סנהדרין קי"א):<br>
**14 [English]**	**the inhabitants of their city.** But not the inhabitants of another city. From here [our Rabbis] said: A city is not considered a “wayward city” (עִיר הַנִדַּחַת) unless men (אֲנָשִׁים) lead it astray, and unless those who lead it astray are from within it. — [San. 111b]



**15 [Hebrew]**	**וְדָֽרַשְׁתָּ וְחָֽקַרְתָּ וְשָֽׁאַלְתָּ הֵיטֵב.** מִכָּאן לָמְדוּ שֶׁבַע חֲקִירוֹת מֵרִבּוּי הַמִּקְרָא, כָּאן יֵשׁ ג' – דְּרִישָׁה וַחֲקִירָה וְהֵיטֵב, וְשָׁאַלְתָּ אֵינוֹ מִן הַמִּנְיָן, וּמִמֶּנּוּ לָמְדוּ בְּדִיקוֹת – וּבְמָקוֹם אַחֵר הוּא אוֹמֵר (דברים י"ט) "וְדָרְשׁוּ הַשּׁוֹפְטִים הֵיטֵב", וְעוֹד בְּמָקוֹם אַחֵר הוּא אוֹמֵר (שם י"ז) "וְדָרַשְׁתָּ הֵיטֵב", לָמְדוּ 'הֵיטֵב' 'הֵיטֵב' לִגְזֵרָה שָׁוָה, לִתֵּן הָאָמוּר שֶׁל זֶה בָּזֶה (סנהדרין מ'):<br>
**15 [English]**	**Then you shall inquire, investigate, and ask thoroughly.** Heb. וְדָרַשְׁתָּ וְחָקַרְתָּ וְשָׁאַלְתָּ הֵיטֵב. From this verse [in combination with others as explained], our Rabbis learned [the law of] the “seven investigations” [in a court of law], since Scripture uses many expressions [of investigation]. Here there are three expressions: דְרִישָׁה, חֲקִירָה, and הֵיטֵב [i.e., “inquiry,” “investigation,” and “thoroughly”]. The term וְשָׁאַלְתָּ, “ask,” is not counted as one [of the seven investigations (חֲקִירוֹת)] and the cross-examinations (בְּדִיקוֹת) [regarding accompanying circumstances] are derived from it. [So far, we have three expressions.] And elsewhere [in the case of collusive witnesses,] it says: 
וְדָרְשׁוּ הַשּׁוֹפְטִים הֵיטֵב, “And the judges shall inquire thoroughly” (Deut. 19:18). [This adds two more expressions, דְרִישָׁה and הֵיטֵב, to make five altogether.] And still elsewhere [in the case of idolatry,] it says: וְדָרַשְׁתָּ הֵיטֵב, “And you shall inquire thoroughly” (Deut. 17:4) [thus adding two more expressions, דְרִישָׁה and הֵיטֵב, to make up a final total of seven investigatory expressions demonstrating the law of “seven investigations”]. And our Rabbis learned a גְּזֵירָה שָׁוָה [i.e., an inference from an identical word in two passages, which teaches us that the laws in one passage apply to the other passage as well] from [the words] הֵיטֵב הֵיטֵב, to place what is stated in one context in the other context. [I.e., it is accounted as if all seven written in the context of the “wayward city,” as well as in the cases of collusive witnesses and individuals who worshipped idols. I.e., all the seven expressions can be connected together to make seven investigations.] - [San. 40a; Sifrei] [The judges would ask the witnesses the following seven questions: 1) In what shemitah cycle was the crime committed? 2) In what year? 3) In what month? 4) In what day of the month? 5) On what day [of the week]? 6) In what hour? 7) In what place? (San. 5:1)]

**16 [English]**	**You shall surely strike down.** Heb. הַכֵּה תַכֶּה. [The double expression is used to explain that] if you cannot execute the death penalty that is prescribed for them, put them to death in some other manner. — [Sifrei ; B.M 31b]

**17 [English]**	**[and burn with fire the city…] for the Lord your God.** [i.e.,] for His Name and for His sake.
