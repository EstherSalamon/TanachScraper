**1**	Then the word of the Lord came to me, saying:

**2**	"Son of man, what will become of the vine-tree out of all [other] trees-the branch that was among the trees of the forest?

**3**	Will wood be taken from it to be used for work, or will they take from it a peg upon which to hang any utensil?

**4**	Behold, [if] it were given to the fire to be consumed, and both its ends the fire consumed, and [if] its inside were charred-is it fit for work?

**5**	Behold, when it was whole, it could not be used for work; how much less when fire has consumed it and it has become charred, will it any longer be used for work?

**6**	Therefore, so said the Lord God: As a vine-tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given over to the fire to be consumed, have I placed the dwellers of Jerusalem.

**7**	And I shall set My countenance against them; From the fire they have emerged, but the fire will devour them, and you will know that I am the Lord when I direct My countenance against them.

**8**	I shall make the land desolate because they have trespassed grievously," says the Lord God.
