**2**	**I will totally destroy.** An expression of destruction, as in (Jud. 18:25) “and you will lose your life,” mentioned regarding Micah. The “aleph” is defective in the second word, for it should have been written אֶאֱסֹף There are many [defective alephs] like these, as in (Isa. 13:20) “no Arab shall pitch his tent there,” which is like יֶאֱהַל.



**3**	**and the stumbling blocks with the wicked.** They are the pagan deities.



**4**	**the remnant of Baal.** that there shall be no remnant of its remembrance.

**4**	**the name of the idolatrous priests with the ancillary priests.** Their worshippers with their priests.



**5**	**who swear by the Lord.** and go back and swear by their king, their pagan deity. They make the pagan deity the main one, saying, “If you do not believe the oath by God, I swear to you by the pagan deity.”



**7**	**He has invited His guests.** He prepared His armies for the appointed day.



**8**	**gentile garb.** Ornaments of the idols.



**9**	**who leaps over the threshold.** Jonathan renders: who follows the customs of the Philistines, who would not step on the threshold of Dagon, as it is stated (I Sam. 5:5): “Therefore, the priests of Dagon… do not tread, etc.”



**10**	**from the fish gate.** Jonathan renders: from the gate of the fish; there was a gate in Jerusalem called by that name.

**10**	**from the second gate.** from the gate of the fowl, which was second to it [the fish gate].



**11**	**you inhabitants of Maktesh.** The Kidron Valley, which is as deep as a mortar. This is according to Targum Jonathan.

**11**	**from the fish gate.** This is Acre, situated in the bosom of the sea, where many fish are caught.

**11**	**from the Mishneh.** This is Lod, which is second to Jerusalem.

**11**	**from the hills.** This is Sepphoris, which is situated at the tops of the mountains like a bird.

**11**	**[11] inhabitants of Maktesh.** This is Tiberias, which is deeper than all of [the rest of] the land of Israel.

**11**	**for the entire people of Canaan is cut off.** Jonathan renders: For the entire nation, whose deeds resemble the deeds of the people of the land of Canaan, has been broken.

**11**	**all those laden with silver.** Jonathan renders: All those wealthy with property, laden with a burden of silver and gold; as in (Prov. 27:3) “The weight of stone, the burden of sand” an expression of a burden.



**12**	**I will search Jerusalem with candles.** I will judge their iniquities strictly.

**12**	**who are settled.** float on their lees. [As in] (II Kings 6:6) “And the axe blade floated,” rendered in Aramaic: וּקְפָא פַרְזְלָה.

**12**	**who say in their heart, “The Lord shall do neither good…”.** to those who obey His will.

**12**	**nor harm.** to those who transgress His will.



**14**	**wherein the mighty man cries bitterly.** The mighty man will cry out with a bitter voice.



**15**	**a day of ruin.** , an expression of desolation.

**15**	**and desolation.** A greater degree of desolation.



**17**	**And I will bring distress upon men.** Upon Israel, who is called “man.”

**17**	**and their flesh.** their flesh. In Arabic, flesh is called al laham.

**17**	**like dung.** dung.
