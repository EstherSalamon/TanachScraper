**4**	**shall be raised on [their] side.** [Jonathan renders:] on the flanks, the flanks of the kings, they will be raised.



**5**	**Then you shall see and be radiant.** Heb. וְנָהַרְתָּ, from נְהוֹרָה, [Aramaic for light,] then you shall see and be radiant [from Jonathan].

**5**	**and your heart shall be startled and become enlarged.** And your heart shall wonder and become enlarged.

**5**	**for the abundance of the west shall be turned over to you.** for the abundance of the west shall be turned over to you [after Jonathan].

**5**	**the wealth of the nations.** The possessions of the nations [after Jonathan].



**6**	**A multitude.** Heb. שִׁפְעַת, A multiplicity.

**6**	**the young camels of Midian.** Heb. בִּכְרֵי. [Jonathan renders:] הוֹגְנֵי. They are young camels. Comp. (Jer. 2:23) “a swift young camel (בִּכְרָה).”

**6**	**and Ephah.** They, too, are of the sons of Midian. Comp. (Gen. 25:4) “Ephah and Epher.”



**7**	**the rams of Nebaioth.** Heb. אֵילֵי, rams of Nebaioth [after Jonathan].



**9**	**as in the beginning.** Like ‘as in the beginning,’ meaning in the days of Solomon, like the matter that is stated (I Kings 10:22): “For the king had at sea ships of Tarshish, etc.; once in three years, the ships of Tarshish would come, etc.” Tarshish is the name of the sea.

**9**	**in the name of the Lord your God.** that is called upon you, for they will hear a report of Him and the name of His might, and come.

**9**	**for He has glorified you.** He has given you glory.



**10**	**and in my grace.** Because I favored you; in old French, en mon apayemant.



**11**	**And they shall open your gates always.** Heb. וּפִתְּחוּ. This is an expression of opening in the strong conjugation (פִּעֵל), since their opening is a perpetual opening, a constant opening. Just as שַׁבֵּר is an expression of breaking, so is פִּתְּחוּ an expression of opening. Tresoverts in O.F.



**13**	**box trees, firs, and cypresses.** Species of trees of the forest of Lebanon.



**14**	**Zion of the Holy One of Israel.** [Lit. Zion the Holy One of Israel. Jonathan renders:] Zion desired by the Holy One of Israel, Zion of the Holy One of Israel.



**16**	**and the breast of kings.** Heb. וְשֹׁד, an expression of breasts (שָׁדַיִם) and ‘you shall suck’ proves it.



**17**	**Instead of the copper.** that they took from you.

**17**	**and I will make your officers peace.** [Jonathan renders:] And I will make your officers peace and your rulers with righteousness

**17**	**your officers.** Your appointed officers. Our Rabbis stated: The officers who came upon you in your exile and the rulers who pressed you will be counted for you as peace and charity (Baba Bathra 9a). [That is, the money they have exacted from you will be counted as charity.]



**19**	**You shall no longer have.** You shall not require the light of the sun.



**20**	**neither… be gathered in.** Heb. יֵאָסֵף, an expression similar to (Joel 2:10) “gathered in (אָסְפוּ) their brightness.” Gathered in their light.



**21**	**in which I will glory.** That I will glory with them. Pourvanter in French.



**22**	**in its time I will hasten it.** If they are worthy, I will hasten it; if they are not worthy, it will be in its time.
