**1**	**and to those who brought up Ahab’s children.** lit. to those who brought up Ahab.



**2**	**and the chariots and the horses… are with you.** and they have strength to wage war.



**4**	**the two kings.** Joram and Ahaziah.



**6**	**Now the king’s sons are seventy men. They are with the elders of the city….** All this was written in the letter, “And I know their count seventy men and they are with the elders of the city who are bringing them up.”



**7**	**.**



**8**	**two piles.** [Heb. צִבֻּרִים]. Synonymous with חֳמָרִים in Heb. and דְּגוֹרִין in Aramaic (monticules in French, mounds).



**9**	**You are righteous.** You consider yourselves all righteous, and me you consider wicked since I slew the king. Now look, behold I revolted against my master and slew him. Now who slew all these? But from these you shall know then that it is a decree of the King upon them, and he who slays is righteous and fulfills the commandment of the Omnipresent.



**12**	**the meeting place of the shepherds.** Jonathan [renders]: the house of the gathering of the shepherds.

**12**	**meeting.** [Heb. עקד], an expression of binding, the place where the shepherds assemble.



**13**	**.**



**15**	**and he said to him.** [i.e.,] Jehu [said to Jehonadeb].

**15**	**“Is your heart right.** and fully committed in what I am doing, as my heart is fully with your heart?”

**15**	**“Indeed it is. Give me your hand.”.** Hold it and pick me up to you to the chariot.



**19**	**with cunning.** [Heb. בְּעָקְבָה] with [guile, an expression of] (Gen. 27:36) וַיַעְקְבֵנִי, “he beguiled me.”



**20**	**Proclaim an assembly.** Announce an assembly, that they assemble in the temple of the Baal.



**22**	**over the wardrobe.** Jonathan rendered: to the one appointed over the wardrobe, chests and boxes in which they wrap and store the vestments of the Baal worshippers.



**24**	**his life shall be forfeit for his life.** [i.e.,] the life of the guard shall be forfeit for the life of the escapee.



**25**	**cast them out.** Jonathan [renders]: and the couriers and the mighty warriors cast cadavers, [i.e.,] they cast the Baal worshippers down dead.



**27**	**for a privy.** [the] Kethib is לִמְחֹרָאוֹת, an expression meaning a privy, and likewise, (Isaiah 36:12) “to eat their excrement, חוריהם.”



**29**	**However,… from the sins of Jeroboam….** for fear that the kingdom return to the house of David, as Jeroboam had feared.



**30**	**And the Lord said to Jehu.** through Jonah.

**30**	**your descendants of the fourth generation.** four generations as reward for your having destroyed the house of Ahab, which reigned for four generations: Omri, Ahab, Ahaziah, and Jehoram.



**32**	**to be vexed with Israel.** [Heb. לְקַצוֹת,] like (Gen. 27:46) בְחַיַי קַצְתִּי, “I am vexed with my life.”
