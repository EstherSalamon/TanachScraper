**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַנּשֶׁה.** הוּא יְהוֹרָם בֶּן אַחְאָב, שֶׁהָיָה מַלְוֵהוּ בְּרִבִּית מַה שֶּׁזָּן אֶת הַנְּבִיאִים בִּימֵי אָבִיו, בְּמִדְרַשׁ רַבִּי תַּנְחוּמָא. לְכָךְ נֶאֱמַר: וַיַּךְ אֶת יְהוֹרָם בֵּין זְרוֹעָיו, שֶׁפָּשְׁטוּ לִטֹּל רִבִּית.<br>
**1 [English]**	**and the creditor.** He was Jehoram the son of Ahab, who would lend him with interest what he used to sustain the prophets in his father’s time. In R. Tanhuma’s Midrash (Mishpatim 9): Therefore it is stated (below 9:24): “And he struck Jehoram between his arms which stretched out to take interest.”

**4 [English]**	**And you shall close the door.** It affords respect to the miracle if it comes about in secret.

**4 [English]**	**and the full one you shall carry away.** from before you, and you shall place another vessel in its place to fill it, but the jug of oil you shall not move from its place, for the Holy One, Blessed be He, is making it as a spring, and it is not customary for a spring to move from its place. **29 [English]**	**do not greet him.** [lit. do not bless him.] Do not ask him about his welfare. He, however, did not do so. Instead, to every inquirer he would say, “My master sent me to revive the dead.”

**39 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְפַלַּח.** וַיְבַקַּע.<br>
**39 [English]**	**and diced them.** split them.



**42 [Hebrew]**	**מִבַּעַל שָׁלִשָׁה.** שֵׁם מְדִינָה. וְיוֹנָתָן תִּרְגֵּם: מֵאַרְעָא דָּרוֹמָא. 'בַּעַל' לְשׁוֹן 'מִישׁוֹר', וְהַרְבֵּה בַּמִּקְרָא.<br>
**42 [English]**	**from Baal Shalishah.** the name of a province. And Jonathan rendered: from the southland. “Baal” is an expression meaning a plain. There are many such expressions in the Bible.

**42 [Hebrew]**	**לֶחֶם בִּכּוּרִים.** בְּפֶסַח, שֶׁהַתְּבוּאָה מְבַכֶּרֶת.<br>
**42 [English]**	**bread of the first fruits.** It was during Passover, when the grain becomes ripe.

**42 [Hebrew]**	**בְּצִקְלֹנוֹ.** בִּלְבוּשֵׁיהּ.<br>
**42 [English]**	**in their shells.** Jonathan [renders]: in their garments. [The shell is compared to a garment of the grain].

**42 [Hebrew]**	**לָעָם.** לַתַּלְמִידִים שֶׁהָיָה זָן.<br>
**42 [English]**	**to the people.** to the students whom he sustained.



**43 [Hebrew]**	**מַה אֶתֵּן זֶה.** כָּל לֶחֶם וְלֶחֶם.<br>
**43 [English]**	**How will I give this.** [i.e.,] each bread.
