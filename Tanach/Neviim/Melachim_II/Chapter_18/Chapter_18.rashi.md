**1 [Hebrew]**	**בִּשְׁנַת שָׁלשׁ.** לְמִרְדּוֹ שֶל הוֹשֵׁעַ.<br>
**1 [English]**	**in the third year.** of Hoshea’s rebellion.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקְרָא לוֹ נְחֻשְׁתָּן.** לְשׁוֹן בִּזָּיוֹן, כְּלוֹמַר מַה צֹּרֶךְ בָּזֶה, אֵינוֹ אֶלָּא נְחַשׁ נְחשֶׁת.<br>
**4 [English]**	**and he called it Nehushtan.** a pejorative, as though to say, “Why is this necessary? It is nothing but a copper serpent.”



**7 [Hebrew]**	**יַשְׂכִּיל.** יַצְלִיחַ.<br>
**7 [English]**	**he succeeded.** [Heb. יַשְׂכִּיל] He succeeded.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**קִצַּץ.** אֶת הַדְּלָתוֹת שֶׁהָיָה שֶל זָהָב.<br>
**16 [English]**	**stripped.** the doors, which were of gold.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת הָאֹמְנוֹת.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: וְיַת סְקוֹפַיָּא, אִיסְקוּפוֹת.<br>
**16 [English]**	**and the thresholds.** This follows Targum.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת תַּרְתָּן וְאֶת רַב סָרִיס.** לָמַדְנוּ בְּסֵדֶר עוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא בָאוּ שְׁלָשְׁתָּם יַחַד, אֶלָּא רַבְשָׁקֵה לְבַדּוֹ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁכָּתַב בְּסֵפֶר יְשַׁעְיָהוּ וְתַרְתָּן וְרַב סָרִיס בָּאוּ בִּשְׁלִיחוּת שְׁנִיָּה, כְּשֶׁבָּאָה לוֹ שְׁמוּעָה עַל תִּרְהָקָה מֶלֶךְ כּוּשׁ, וַיִּשְׁמַע וַיִּשְׁלַח מַלְאָכִים אֶל חִזְקִיָּהוּ וְגוֹ'.<br>
**17 [English]**	**Tartan and Rabsaris.** We learned in Seder Olam (?) that the three of them did not come together, but Rabshakeh came alone, as the prophet wrote in the Book of Isaiah (36:2), and Tartan and Rabsaris came on a second mission, when he received word concerning Tarhakah, king of Cush, “and he heard and sent emissaries to Hezekiah…” (Is. 37:9).

**17 [Hebrew]**	**בִּתְעָלַת.** פוסקיר"ה בְּלַעַ"ז, בְּרֵכָה שֶׁעוֹשִׂין לְבֵיבְרֵי דָגִים.<br>
**17 [English]**	**near the conduit.** fosed (fosse) in French. A ditch, a trench, a moat.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**שְׂדֵה כוֹבֵס.** שָׂדֶה שֶׁהַכּוֹבְסִין שׁוֹטְחִין בּוֹ בְּגָדִים, וְכֵן תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: חֲקָל מַשְׁטַח קַצְרַיָּא.<br>
**17 [English]**	**the washer’s field.** A field where the washers stretch out garments. So did Jonathan render, “a field where the washers stretch out.”



**18 [Hebrew]**	**הַמַּזְכִּיר.** אֵיזֶה מִשְׁפָּט בָּא לִפְנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ רִאשׁוֹן, שֶׁיִּפְסְקֶנּוּ רִאשׁוֹן.<br>
**18 [English]**	**the recorder.** which judgment came first before the king, that he should first adjudicate.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**אָמַרְתָּ אַךְ דְּבַר שְׂפָתַיִם וְגוֹ'.** אָמַרְתָּ עַד הֵנָּה לֹא אֶעֱבֹד לְמֶלֶךְ אַשּׁוּר, אַךְ דְּבַר שְׂפָתַיִם הָיָה, כָּל זְמַן שֶׁלֹּא יֵצֵא מִמְּקוֹמוֹ לָבֹא עָלֶיךָ, לֹא הֻצְרַכְתָּ לְעֵצָה וּגְבוּרָה, אֲבָל עַכְשָׁיו שֶׁיָּצָא וּבָא, עֵצָה וּגְבוּרָה אַתָּה צָרִיךְ לַמִּלְחָמָה הַזֹּאת, עַתָּה אֱמֹר עַל מִי בָטַחְתָּ.<br>
**20 [English]**	**You have said but words of the lips….** Until now you have said, “I will not pay tribute to the king of Assyria.” It was but words of the lips. As long as he did not leave his place to march upon you, you did not require counsel and might. Now, however, that he has left [his place] and come upon you, you require counsel and might for this war. Now tell upon whom you have depended?



**21 [Hebrew]**	**וּבָא בְּכַפּוֹ.** כְּשֶׁאָדָם נִסְמָךְ עַל מִשְׁעֶנֶת קָנֶה רָצוּץ שֶׁנִּשְׁבַּר בְּיָדוֹ, בָּאִין רָאשֵׁי הַקְּרוּמִיּוֹת וְנוֹקְבִין אֶת כַּפּוֹ.<br>
**21 [English]**	**and it will go into his palm.** When a person supports himself on a support of a splintered reed, which has broken in his hand, the ends of the scaly envelope will come and puncture his palm.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**הֲלוֹא הוּא אֲשֶׁר הֵסִיר חִזְקִיָּהוּ וְגוֹ'.** יֵשׁ לִלְמֹד מִכָּאן, דְּרַבְשָׁקֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל מוּמָר הָיָה, שֶׁהָיָה מוֹדֶה שֶׁהַקָּבָּ"ה אֱלוֹהַּ, אֶלָּא שֶׁרְצוֹנוֹ לַעֲבֹד עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.<br>
**22 [English]**	**Is He not the one Whose high places and altars Hezekiah has removed….** It can be deduced from here that Rabshakeh was an apostate Jew, for he admits that the Holy One, Blessed be He, is God, but His will is that we worship idols.



**23 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְעָרֶב נָא.** הִתְפָּאֵר עָלָיו, בּוֹא בְּעֵרָבוֹן, עַל מְנָת אִם תּוּכַל לִתֵּן לְךָ רוֹכְבִים עַל אַלְפַּיִם סוּסִים.<br>
**23 [English]**	**wager now.** boast over him. Enter a wager on the condition that if you are able to supply riders for two thousand horses.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְעָרֶב.** גיי"ר בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**23 [English]**	**wager.** gajjer in O.F.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֵיךְ תָּשִׁיב אֵת פְּנֵי פַחַת אַחַד עַבְדֵי אֲדֹנִי.** שֶׁהֲרֵי כָּל הַקָּטָן שֶׁבָּהֶם, שַׂר עַל אַלְפַּיִם אִישׁ, וְאַתָּה אֵינְךָ מַשִּׂיג לְאַלְפַּיִם אִישׁ, לָמַדְנוּ מִכָּאן עַל רָאשֵׁי גְּיָסוֹת מֵאָה וּשְׁמוֹנִים וַחֲמִשָּׁה אֶלֶף שֶׁנָּפְלוּ עִם סַנְחֵרִיב, הַקָּטָן שֶׁבָּהֶם, שַׂר עַל אַלְפַּיִם אִישׁ שֶׁעִמּוֹ.<br>
**24 [English]**	**And how can you repulse one captain of… my master’s servants.** for the smallest of them is captain over two thousand men, and you cannot obtain two thousand men. We deduce from here concerning the heads of the armies, who totaled 185,000, who fell with Sennacherib, that the smallest of them was captain over two thousand men that were with him.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**הֲמִבַּלְעֲדֵי ה'.** וְכִי שֶׁלֹּא בִּרְשׁוּת בָּאתִי, כְּבָר נִתְנַבֵּא יְשַׁעְיָה: בִּימֵי אָחָז אָבִיךָ יָבִיא ה' עָלֶיךָ וְעַל עַמְּךָ וְגוֹ' אֶת מֶלֶךְ אַשּׁוּר.<br>
**25 [English]**	**is it with other than the Lord.** Did I come without permission? Isaiah already prophesied in the days of Ahaz your father (Is. 7:17), “The Lord shall bring upon you and upon your people… the king of Assyria.”

**25 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַשְׁחִיתָהּ.** אֶת זוֹ הוֹסִיף מִשֶּׁלּוֹ.<br>
**25 [English]**	**and destroy it.** This he added of his own.



**26 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי שֹׁמְעִים אֲנָחְנוּ.** אָנוּ בְּנֵי פַּלָּטִין, וּמַכִּירִין בְּלָשׁוֹן אֲרַמִּית וּבִשְׁאָר לְשׁוֹנוֹת, וְאַל תְּדַבֵּר אֵלֵינוּ יְהוּדִית, שֶׁכָּל הָעָם מַכִּירִין בּוֹ, וְאֵין אָנוּ רוֹצִים שֶׁיִּשְׁמְעוּ.<br>
**26 [English]**	**for we understand.** We are residents of the palace, and understand the Aramaic language and other languages. Do not speak to us Judean, which all the people understand, and we do not want them to hear.

**26 [Hebrew]**	<br>
**26 [English]**	**within the hearing of the people.** For all the people understand Judean, and they are frightened by your words. Since he said to them, “Say now to Hezekiah,” they thought that he did not come to frighten the people. Since Rabshakeh was an apostate Jew, they thought that even though his master’s orders were incumbent upon Rabshakeh [to observe], but his heart was attracted to his family and he would take pity upon them.



**27 [Hebrew]**	**לֶאֱכֹל אֶת צוֹאָתָם.** בְּרָעָב הַמָּצוֹר.<br>
**27 [English]**	**to eat their dung.** in the hunger [brought about] by the siege.

**27 [Hebrew]**	**חוֹרֵיהֶם.** רְעִי הַיּוֹצֵא דֶּרֶךְ חוֹר פִּי הַטַּבַּעַת. וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ פֵּרְשׁוּ: לְכַנּוֹתָם לָשׁוֹן נָאָה צוֹאָתָם.<br>
**27 [English]**	**their dung.** [The] kethib [reads] חֹרֵיהֶם - dung that is excreted through the orifice of the anus. Our Rabbis explained to euphemize and read, צוֹאָתָם, i.e., their dung.

**27 [Hebrew]**	**מֵימֵי שִׁינֵיהֵם.** צוֹאָה לֶחָה, שִׁנֵּי דְּכַרְכְּשָׁא דְּקַיְמָא אַתְּלַת שִנֵּי. אוֹ לְשׁוֹן גָּלָל, שֶׁבָּא עַל יְדֵי לְעִיסַת הַשִּׁנַּיִם.<br>
**27 [English]**	**their urine.** [According to] kethib, שִׁינֵיהֶם, [however, we explain it as] loose excrement, the glands of the rectum, which is held by three glands. Alternatively, an expression of excrement which comes about through the chewing of the teeth.



**31 [Hebrew]**	**עֲשׂוּ אִתִּי בְרָכָה.** שְׁאִילַת שָׁלוֹם, כְּמוֹ: וַיְבָרֶךְ יַעֲקֹב אֶת פַּרְעֹה.<br>
**31 [English]**	**Make peace with me.** [Lit. make a blessing with me - an expression of] a greeting, like (Gen. 47:7) “and Jacob greeted Pharaoh.”



**32 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל אֶרֶץ כְּאַרְצְכֶם.** כֵּן דַּרְכִּי, לְהַעֲבִיר אֶת הָאֻמּוֹת מִמְּדִינָה לִמְדִינָה, הָיָה לוֹ לוֹמַר אֶל אֶרֶץ טוֹבָה מִשֶּׁלָּכֶם, שֶׁהֲרֵי לְפַתּוֹתָם בָּא, אֶלָּא שֶׁיָּדַע שֶׁיַּכִּירוּ שֶׁדְּבָרָיו שֶׁקֶר.<br>
**32 [English]**	**to a land like your land.** So is my wont to repatriate the nations from province to province. He should have said, “to a land better than yours,” since he came to entice them, but he knew that they would recognize that his statement was false.

**32 [Hebrew]**	**אֶרֶץ דָּגָן וְתִירוֹשׁ.** זוֹ אַפְרִיקִי.<br>
**32 [English]**	**a land of grain and wine.** This is Africa.



**34 [Hebrew]**	**הֵנַע וְעִוָּה.** אוֹתָם מֶלֶךְ אַשּׁוּר, הֶחֱרִיבָם וְהִגְלָם.<br>
**34 [English]**	**He exiled them and twisted them.** [Heb. וְעִוָה הֵנַע.] The King of Assyria [exiled them and twisted] them; [i.e.,] he destroyed them and exiled them.

**34 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי הִצִּילוּ אֶת שֹׁמְרוֹן.** וְהַשּׁוֹמְרוֹנִים הָיוּ עוֹבְדִים אֶת אֱלֹהֵי אֲרָם שֶׁהָיוּ שְׁכֵנֵיהֶם.<br>
**34 [English]**	**Now, did they save Samaria.** and the inhabitants of Samaria worshipped the gods of the Arameans, who were their neighbors.



**37 [Hebrew]**	**קְרוּעֵי בְגָדִים.** עַל שֶׁשָּׁמְעוּ גִּדּוּפֵי הַשֵּׁם.<br>
**37 [English]**	**with torn garments.** because they heard blasphemies of the Name of God.
