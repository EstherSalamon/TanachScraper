**1 [Hebrew]**	**וְנַעֲמָן שַׂר צְבָא.** נִסִּים שֶׁנַּעֲשׂוּ עַל יְדֵי אֱלִישָׁע, מְסַדֵּר וְהוֹלֵךְ.<br>
**1 [English]**	**Now, Naaman the general.** He goes on to recount the miracles that were performed through Elisha.

**1 [Hebrew]**	**תְּשׁוּעָה לַאֲרָם.** הוּא מָשַׁךְ בַּקֶּשֶׁת לְתֻמּוֹ, וְהָרַג אֶת אַחְאָב.<br>
**1 [English]**	**the victory to Aram.** He drew back his bow innocently and killed Ahab.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**יָצְאוּ גְדוּדִים.** כְּשֶׁהוֹלְכִין מֵאָה אוֹ מָאתַיִם מֵעַצְמָן לִשְׁלֹל כַּאֲשֶׁר יִמְצְאוּן, הוּא קָרוּי גְּדוּד.<br>
**2 [English]**	**went out in bands.** When they go in groups of one hundred or two hundred by themselves, to plunder whatever they find, that is called a band.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**נַעֲרָה קְטַנָּה.** רִיבָה קְטַנָּה, מֵעִיר נַעֲרוֹן.<br>
**2 [English]**	**a young girl.** Heb. naarah ketannah, a young girl from the town of Naaran.

**2 [Hebrew]**	**וַתְּהִי לִפְנֵי.** וַהֲוַת מְשַׁמְּשָׁא קֳדָם אִתַּת נַעֲמָן.<br>
**2 [English]**	**who ministered to.** [lit. and she was before Naaman’s wife.] Jonathan [renders]: and she was serving before Naaman’s wife.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**אַחֲלֵי אֲדֹנִי.** לִפְנֵי הַנָּבִיא. לְשׁוֹן וַיְחַל משֶׁה, בַּקָּשׁוֹת כָּל הַמִּתְפַּלְּלִים עָלָיו יִהְיוּ, שֶׁיָּבוֹא הַנֵּס לִפְנֵי הַנָּבִיא.<br>
**3 [English]**	**My master’s supplications.** before the prophet Heb. אֲחֲלֵי, an expression similar to וַיְחַל מֹשֶה, “and Moses prayed” (Ex. 32:11). The supplications of all those who pray for him, shall be that the miracle shall come before the prophet.

**3 [Hebrew]**	**אַחֲלֵי.** שוהיידמנ"ס בְּלַעַ"ז, כְּלוֹמַר זוֹ הִיא בַּקָּשָׁה שֶׁהוּא צָרִיךְ לָהּ.<br>
**3 [English]**	**the supplications.** אַחֲלֵי I.e., to say that this is the supplication that he requires.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּבֹא.** נַעֲמָן.<br>
**4 [English]**	**And he came.** [i.e.,] Naaman [came]

**4 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּגֵּד לַאדֹנָיו.** מֶלֶךְ אֲרָם.<br>
**4 [English]**	**and told his master.** the king of Aram.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקַּח בְּיָדוֹ.** מִנְחָה לַנָּבִיא.<br>
**5 [English]**	**and he took in his possession.** as a gift to the prophet.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְשֵׁשֶׁת אֲלָפִים זָהָב.** זְהוּבִים.<br>
**5 [English]**	**and six thousand gold pieces.** [lit.] and six thousand gold.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**לֵאמֹר וְעַתָּה כְּבוֹא הַסֵּפֶר הַזֶּה וְגוֹ'.** הַסֵּפֶר אָמַר לוֹ, וְעַתָּה כְּבוֹא הַסֵּפֶר הַזֶּה וְגוֹ'.<br>
**6 [English]**	**saying.** [“And now when this letter comes…5”]-The letter said to him, “And now, when this letter…”

**6 [Hebrew]**	**וַאֲסַפְתּוֹ מִצָּרַעְתּוֹ.** 'אֲסֵפָה' בִּמְצֹרָע הִיא לְשׁוֹן רְפוּאָתוֹ, כִּי בְּהִתְרַפּאוֹתוֹ הוּא נֶאֱסָף אֶל תּוֹךְ בְּנֵי אָדָם, וּבְחָלְיוֹ הַכֹּל בְּדֵלִין הֵימֶנּוּ.<br>
**6 [English]**	**and you shall cure him of his zaraath.** [lit. and you shall gather him in from his zaraath.] Gathering, as applied to the mezora, is the expression of his cure, for when he is cured, he is gathered in among people, whereas during his illness, everyone stays away from him.



**7 [Hebrew]**	**הַאֱלֹהִים אָנִי.** הֵ"א נָקוּד פַּתָּ"ח, לְלַמֵּד שֶׁהוּא בִּלְשׁוֹן תְּמִיהָה.<br>
**7 [English]**	**Do I have power from God.** [after] Jonathan. [Lit. Am I God?] The ‘heh’ at the beginning of the word הַאֱלֹהִים, is voweled with a ‘pathah’ to indicate that it is a question.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**מִתְאַנֶּה.** מְבַקֵּשׁ תּוֹאֲנָה לְהִתְגָּרוֹת בִּי.<br>
**7 [English]**	**he is looking for a pretext.** He is looking for a pretext to engage in strife with me.

**7 [Hebrew]**	**מִתְאַנֶּה.** לְשׁוֹן עֲלִילוֹת דְּבָרִים.<br>
**7 [English]**	**he is looking for a pretext.** [Heb. מִתְאַנֶה] an expression of pretext.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**הִנֵּה אָמַרְתִּי.** סָבוּר הָיִיתִי, אֵלַי יֵצֵא הַנָּבִיא וִידַבֵּר עִמִּי וְיִרְאֶה אֶת הַחֹלִי.<br>
**11 [English]**	**Here I thought.** [I thought that] the prophet would come out and speak with me, and see the ailment.

**11 [Hebrew]**	**וְהֵנִיף יָדוֹ אֶל הַמָּקוֹם.** הַצָּרַעַת.<br>
**11 [English]**	**and he would raise his hand toward the spot.** of the] zaraath.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אָבִי.** כְּמוֹ אֲדוֹנִי.<br>
**13 [English]**	**Master.** [lit. father].

**13 [Hebrew]**	**הֲלוֹא תַעֲשֶׂה.** וְכִי לֹא תַעֲשֵׂהוּ, אֲפִלּוּ אָמַר לְךָ דְּבַר טֹרַח.<br>
**13 [English]**	**would you not do it?.** Would you not do it even if he ordered you to do something requiring exertion?

**13 [Hebrew]**	**וְאַף כִּי.** קַל וָחֹמֶר שֶׁאָמַר לְךָ דָּבָר קַל, רְחַץ וּטְהָר.<br>
**13 [English]**	**and surely.** by a fortiori conclusion, since he said to you to do an easy thing, [viz.] immerse yourself and become clean.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**בְרָכָה.** מִנְחַת שָׁלוֹם שֶל הַקְבָּלַת פָּנִים, שֶׁשּׁוֹאֵל תַּלְמִיד אוֹ עֶבֶד בִּשְׁלוֹם הָרַב, שולד"ו בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**15 [English]**	**a gift.** [lit. a blessing], a gift of peace, of greeting, when a disciple or a slave greets his master (sallud in O.F., a greeting).



**16 [Hebrew]**	**אִם אֶקָּח.** שֶׁדְּמֵי עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה מְעֹרָבִין בּוֹ.<br>
**16 [English]**	**I will not accept.** for the price of idols was included in it.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**וָלֹא.** לְשׁוֹן בַּקָּשָׁה, לְשׁוֹן הַלְוַאי.<br>
**17 [English]**	**Now, if only.** וָלֹא, an expression of a request, an expression of הַלְוַאי.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**יֻתַּן נָא.** מֵאֲדָמָה זוֹ מֵאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁהִיא קְדוֹשָׁה, מַשָּׂא שְׁנֵי פְּרָדִים, וְאֶשָּׂאֶנָּה לְעִירִי וְאֶעֱשֶׂה אוֹתָהּ מִזְבֵּחַ.<br>
**17 [English]**	**Now… be given.** from this soil of the land of Israel, which is holy, a load of earth as carried by a team of mules, and I will carry it off to my city, and I will make it into an altar.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**בֵית רִמּוֹן.** שֵׁם עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.<br>
**18 [English]**	**Beth-Rimmon.** the name of a pagan deity.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֵיתִי.** עַל כָּרְחִי, כְּשֶׁשּׁוֹחֶה אֲדוֹנִי וְהוּא נִשְׁעָן עַל יָדִי.<br>
**18 [English]**	**and I will prostrate myself.** against my will when my master bows, since he leans on my hand.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**כִּבְרַת אָרֶץ.** שֵׁם מִדָּה שֶל קַרְקַע, כְּמוֹ: צִמְדֵי כֶרֶם, ארפינ"ט בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**19 [English]**	**some distance.** [Heb.] kivrath eretz, the name of a land measure, like (Is. 5:10) “ten acres of vineyard.” (arpent in French), an old French measure of land, equivalent to about an acre.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**מְאוּמָה.** חָסֵר אָלֶ"ף, לְפִי שֶׁהָיְתָה לְקִיחָה זוֹ לְמוּם.<br>
**20 [English]**	**something.** Heb. מאומה The “aleph” is missing, since this taking was responsible for his being stricken with a blemish. מוּם [in Heb.].



**21 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּפֹּל.** וְאִתְרְכִין.<br>
**21 [English]**	**and he leaned over.** Jonathan. [Lit. and he fell.]



**23 [Hebrew]**	**הוֹאֵל.** הִשָּׁבַע שֶׁשְּׁלַחֲךָ.<br>
**23 [English]**	**Please.** swear that he sent you.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**בִּשְׁנֵי חֲרִטִים.** מִינֵי בְּגָדִים וְסוּדָרִין, כְּמוֹ: הַמִּטְפָּחוֹת וְהָחֲרִיטִים, כָּךְ חִבְּרוֹ מְנַחֵם. אֲבָל יוֹנָתָן תִּרְגֵּם זֶה 'פַּלְדָּסִין', וְאֶת שֶׁבְּסֵפֶר יְשַׁעְיָהוּ תִּרְגֵּם 'מְחַכַּיָּא'. וְהַפּוֹתְרִין אוֹמְרִין: בִּשְׁנֵי חֲרִיטֵי, בִּשְׁנֵי כִּיסִין אֲרֻכִּים, פירדיי"ש בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**23 [English]**	**in two pockets.** Heb. חֲרִטִים types of garments and kerchiefs, like (Is. 3:22) “and the kerchiefs and the ‘haritim.’” Menahem classified it thus (p. 94). Jonathan, however, translated this as ‘paldesin,’ sheets, (see Targum Jud. 14) and the one in the book of Isaiah as ‘mahachaia,’ a plate covering the genitals. The exegetes say that בִּשְׁנֵי חֲרִטִים, [means] ‘in two long pockets’ brids in O.F.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל שְׁנֵי נְעָרָיו.** שֶׁל נַעֲמָן.<br>
**23 [English]**	**his two servants.** [i.e.,] Naaman’s.

**23 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׂאוּ לְפָנָיו.** לִפְנֵי גֵּחֲזִי.<br>
**23 [English]**	**and they carried them before him.** before Gehazi.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּבֹא אֶל הָעֹפֶל.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: לַאֲתַר כָּסֵי.<br>
**24 [English]**	**And he came to a secret place.** Jonathan renders: to a hidden place.

**24 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקַּח מִיָּדָם.** מִיַּד שְׁנֵי נַעֲרֵי נַעֲמָן.<br>

**24 [English]**	**and he dismissed the men.** lest Elisha see them.
