**1**	**Now, Naaman the general.** He goes on to recount the miracles that were performed through Elisha.

**1**	**the victory to Aram.** He drew back his bow innocently and killed Ahab.



**2**	**went out in bands.** When they go in groups of one hundred or two hundred by themselves, to plunder whatever they find, that is called a band.

**2**	**a young girl.** Heb. naarah ketannah, a young girl from the town of Naaran.

**2**	**who ministered to.** [lit. and she was before Naaman’s wife.] Jonathan [renders]: and she was serving before Naaman’s wife.



**3**	**My master’s supplications.** before the prophet Heb. אֲחֲלֵי, an expression similar to וַיְחַל מֹשֶה, “and Moses prayed” (Ex. 32:11). The supplications of all those who pray for him, shall be that the miracle shall come before the prophet.

**3**	**the supplications.** אַחֲלֵי I.e., to say that this is the supplication that he requires.



**4**	**And he came.** [i.e.,] Naaman [came]

**4**	**and told his master.** the king of Aram.



**5**	**and he took in his possession.** as a gift to the prophet.

**5**	**and six thousand gold pieces.** [lit.] and six thousand gold.



**6**	**saying.** [“And now when this letter comes…5”]-The letter said to him, “And now, when this letter…”

**6**	**and you shall cure him of his zaraath.** [lit. and you shall gather him in from his zaraath.] Gathering, as applied to the mezora, is the expression of his cure, for when he is cured, he is gathered in among people, whereas during his illness, everyone stays away from him.



**7**	**Do I have power from God.** [after] Jonathan. [Lit. Am I God?] The ‘heh’ at the beginning of the word הַאֱלֹהִים, is voweled with a ‘pathah’ to indicate that it is a question.

**7**	**he is looking for a pretext.** He is looking for a pretext to engage in strife with me.

**7**	**he is looking for a pretext.** [Heb. מִתְאַנֶה] an expression of pretext.



**11**	**Here I thought.** [I thought that] the prophet would come out and speak with me, and see the ailment.

**11**	**and he would raise his hand toward the spot.** of the] zaraath.



**13**	**Master.** [lit. father].

**13**	**would you not do it?.** Would you not do it even if he ordered you to do something requiring exertion?

**13**	**and surely.** by a fortiori conclusion, since he said to you to do an easy thing, [viz.] immerse yourself and become clean.



**15**	**a gift.** [lit. a blessing], a gift of peace, of greeting, when a disciple or a slave greets his master (sallud in O.F., a greeting).



**16**	**I will not accept.** for the price of idols was included in it.



**17**	**Now, if only.** וָלֹא, an expression of a request, an expression of הַלְוַאי.

**17**	**Now… be given.** from this soil of the land of Israel, which is holy, a load of earth as carried by a team of mules, and I will carry it off to my city, and I will make it into an altar.



**18**	**Beth-Rimmon.** the name of a pagan deity.

**18**	**and I will prostrate myself.** against my will when my master bows, since he leans on my hand.



**19**	**some distance.** [Heb.] kivrath eretz, the name of a land measure, like (Is. 5:10) “ten acres of vineyard.” (arpent in French), an old French measure of land, equivalent to about an acre.



**20**	**something.** Heb. מאומה The “aleph” is missing, since this taking was responsible for his being stricken with a blemish. מוּם [in Heb.].



**21**	**and he leaned over.** Jonathan. [Lit. and he fell.]



**23**	**Please.** swear that he sent you.

**23**	**in two pockets.** Heb. חֲרִטִים types of garments and kerchiefs, like (Is. 3:22) “and the kerchiefs and the ‘haritim.’” Menahem classified it thus (p. 94). Jonathan, however, translated this as ‘paldesin,’ sheets, (see Targum Jud. 14) and the one in the book of Isaiah as ‘mahachaia,’ a plate covering the genitals. The exegetes say that בִּשְׁנֵי חֲרִטִים, [means] ‘in two long pockets’ brids in O.F.

**23**	**his two servants.** [i.e.,] Naaman’s.

**23**	**and they carried them before him.** before Gehazi.



**24**	**And he came to a secret place.** Jonathan renders: to a hidden place.

**24**	**and he took them from their hands.** from the hands of Naaman’s two servants.

**24**	**and he dismissed the men.** lest Elisha see them.



**26**	**Is it time to take the silver.** to become wealthy and to buy with it clothing and olive trees…
