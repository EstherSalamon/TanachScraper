**32 [English]**	**nor shall he advance upon it wlth a shield.** He shall not set a shield before it. [In Isaiah 37:33, Rashi adds,] Since the Aramaic translation of “before” is קֳדָם.

**32 [Hebrew]**	**סֹלְלָה.** צִבּוּר אֲדָמָה לְהַגְבִּיהַּ תֵּל לַעֲמֹד עָלָיו לְהִלָּחֵם עַל הָעִיר, וְעַל שֵׁם שֶׁהוּא כּוֹבְשׁוֹ וְחוֹבְטוֹ בְּעֵצִים וּמַקָּבוֹת כְּדֵי שֶׁיְּהֵא נָדוֹשׁ וְכָבוּשׁ בְּחֹזֶק, קוֹרֵהוּ סוֹלְלָה, לְשׁוֹן: סֹלּוּ סֹלּוּ הַמְסִלָּה.<br>
**32 [English]**	**a siege mound.** [Heb. סֹלְלָה,] a heap of earth to raise a mount upon which to stand to embattle the city. Since one presses it down and beats it with sticks and sledge hammers, so that it be trodden and pressed down with strength, it is called סֹלְלָה, an expression similar to (Is. 62:10) “Beat down, beat down the highway.” [Heb. סֹלוּ סֹלוּ הַמְסִלָה.]



**35 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהִי בַּלַּיְלָה הַהוּא.** כְּשֶׁשָּׁב סַנְחֵרִיב מֵעַל כּוּשׁ, וּבָא עַד נֹב שֶׁהָיְתָה סְמוּכָה לִירוּשָׁלַיִם.<br>
**35 [English]**	**And it came to pass on that night.** when Sennacherib returned from [warring] with Cush, and he came as far as Nob, which was near Jerusalem.

**35 [Hebrew]**	**מֵאָה שְׁמוֹנִים וַחֲמִשָּׁה אָלֶף.** כֻּלָּם רָאשֵׁי גְּיָסוֹת.<br>
**35 [English]**	**one hundred eighty five thousand.** All of them were heads of companies.



**36 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֵּשֶׁב בְּנִינְוֵה.** הִיא רֹאשׁ לְאֶרֶץ אַשּׁוּר.<br>
**36 [English]**	**and dwelt in Nineveh.** That is the capital of the land of Assyria. [In Isaiah 37:37, Rashi adds,] as it is said (Gen. 10:11), “From that land Ashur came out, and he built Nineveh…”



**37 [Hebrew]**	**בֵּית נִסְרֹךְ אֱלֹהָיו.** נֶסֶר מִתֵּבָתוֹ שֶׁל נֹחַ.<br>
**37 [English]**	**the temple of Nisroch his god.** a board from Noah’s ark.

**37 [Hebrew]**	**הִכֻּהוּ בַחֶרֶב.** שָׁמְעוּ אוֹתוֹ שֶׁאָמַר לְשָׁחְטָן לְפָנָיו אִם יַצִּילוּהוּ, שֶׁלֹּא יַהַרְגוּהוּ שָׂרֵי הַמַּלְכוּת שֶׁמֵּתוּ בְנֵיהֶם עַל יָדוֹ.<br>
**37 [English]**	**slew him.** They heard him that he said he would slaughter them before him if they (sic) (he- kara) [would save him so that the princes of the kingdom, whose sons perished because of him, would not kill him].
