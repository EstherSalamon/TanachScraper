**3 [English]**	**at the entrance of the gate.** as it is said (Lev. 13:46) “He shall live alone; outside the camp shall be his habitation.”

**6 [English]**	**had caused the Aramean camp to hear.** It seemed to them as though they were hearing.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**וּמְצָאָנוּ עָווֹן.** מִתְחַיְּבִין אָנוּ לַמַּלְכוּת.<br>
**9 [English]**	**We will incur guilt.** We will be held guilty by the throne.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**כַּאֲשֶׁר הֵמָּה.** כְּמוֹ שֶׁהָיוּ מְלֵאִים מִתְּחִלָּתָם, לֹא הוֹצִיאוּ מַה שֶּׁבְּתוֹכָם.<br>
**10 [English]**	**as they were.** as they were full at the beginning; they did not take out what was in them.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי רְעֵבִים אֲנַחְנוּ.** וּתְאֵבִים לָצֵאת אֶל הַשָּׁלָל וְאֶל הַמָּזוֹן.<br>
**12 [English]**	**that we are hungry.** and long to go out to the loot and to the food.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁאֲרוּ בָהּ.** בְּתוֹךְ הָעִיר, שֶׁלֹּא מֵתוּ בָּרָעָב.<br>
**13 [English]**	**that are left there.** within the city, that did not die of hunger.

**13 [Hebrew]**	**הִנָּם כְּכָל הֲמוֹן יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁאֲרוּ בָהּ.** אִם יֹאמְרוּ מְסֻכָּנִין הֵם שֶׁלֹּא יַהַרְגֵם אַרָם, הֲרֵי הִנָּם בָּעִיר הַזֹּאת בְּסַכָּנַת הָרָעָב כִּשְׁאָר כָּל הֲמוֹן יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁאֲרוּ בָהּ, וְאִם יָמוּתוּ הִנָּם כְּכָל הֲמוֹן יִשְׂרָאֵל, אֲשֶׁר תָּמּוּ בָּרָעָב.<br>
**13 [English]**	**Behold, they are like all the multitude of Israel that are left there.** If they will say that they are imperiled lest the Arameans kill them, behold they are in this city in peril of famine like the rest of all the multitude of Israel that are left there, and if they die, they are like all the multitude of Israel that have perished from hunger.

**15 [English]**	**in their haste.** in their hurry to flee.

**17 [English]**	**upon whose hand he leaned.** on the day before, when he came to Elisha’s house.
