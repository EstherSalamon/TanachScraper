**1 [Hebrew]**	**וִיהוֹרָם בֶּן אַחְאָב.** אַף פָּרָשָׁה זוֹ, בִּשְׁבִיל לְהַגִּיד נֵס שֶל מֵישָׁע מֶלֶךְ מוֹאָב שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂה עַל יְדֵי אֱלִישָׁע נִכְתְּבָה כָּאן, שֶׁמָּנָה בְּאֵלִיָּהוּ שְׁמוֹנָה נִסִּים, וּבֶאֱלִישָׁע שִׁשָּׁה עָשָׂר, לְקַיֵּם: פִּי שְׁנַיִם בְּרוּחֲךָ אֵלַי, וּכְתָבָן כֻּלָּם זוֹ בְּצַד זוֹ, כָּךְ רָאִיתִי בִּשְׁלשִׁים וּשְׁתַּיִם מִדּוֹת דְּרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר בְּנוֹ דְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי, וַחֲצִיַּת יַרְדֵּן שֶׁל אֱלִישָׁע, כִּשְׁתַּיִם דְּשֶׁל אֵלִיָּהוּ.<br>
**1 [English]**	**And Jehoram the son of Ahab.** This section too was written here to tell the miracle of Mesha the king of Moab, which was performed through Elisha, for Scripture enumerated for Elijah eight miracles and for Elisha sixteen, to fulfill “a double portion of your spirit to me” (above 2:9), and he wrote them all one juxtaposed to the other. I saw this in the Thirty-two Methods of R. Eliezer the son of R. Jose the Calilean. Now the dividing of the Jordan by Elisha is counted like two miracles of Elijah.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**רַק לֹא כְאָבִיו וּכְאִמּוֹ.** שֶׁהוֹסִיפוּ אֶת הַבַּעַל, לְהַכְעִיס.<br>
**2 [English]**	**however, not like his father and mother.** who added the worship of the Baal in order to provoke God.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**רַק בְּחַטֹּאות יָרָבְעָם וְגוֹ'.** דָּבֵק. וְכֵן כָּל מַלְכֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, מִדְּאָגָה שֶׁלֹּא תָשׁוּב הַמַּמְלָכָה לְבֵית דָּוִד אִם יַעֲלוּ לָרֶגֶל, לְכָךְ עָבְדוּ אֶת הָעֲגָלִים.<br>
**3 [English]**	**Only to the sins of Jeroboam… did he cling.** And so [did] all kings of Israel, for fear lest the kingdom return to the house of David if they would perform the pilgrimage on festivals. They, therefore, worshipped the calves.



**4 [Hebrew]**	**נֹקֵד.** מָרֵי גֵיתִין.<br>
**4 [English]**	**a sheepman.** Jonathan [however, renders נֹקֵד as] an owner of livestock.

**4 [Hebrew]**	**אֵילִים צָמֶר.** דִּיכְרִין דְּרָעֲיָא, עִם צִמְרָם.<br>
**4 [English]**	**rams with their wool.** Jonathan [renders:] pasture-fed rams, i.e., with their wool.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**דֶּרֶךְ מִדְבַּר אֱדוֹם.** כִּי גַּם מֶלֶךְ אֱדוֹם יֵלֵךְ עִמָּנוּ.<br>
**8 [English]**	**by way of the Desert of Edom.** for the king of Edom, too, will go with us.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**לָתֵת אוֹתָם וְגוֹ'.** שֶׁיָּמוּתוּ בַּצָּמָא.<br>
**10 [English]**	**to deliver them….** for they will die of thirst.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר יָצַק מַיִם עַל יְדֵי אֵלִיָּהוּ.** בְּהַר הַכַּרְמֶל, וּבִיצִיקָתוֹ נַעֲשׂוּ אֶצְבְּעוֹתָיו כְּמַעְיְנוֹת מַיִם, עַד שֶׁנִּתְמַלְּאָה הַתְּעָלָה, וְרָאוּי הוּא לֵעָשׂוֹת לָנוּ נֵס עַל יָדוֹ בְּמַיִם.<br>
**11 [English]**	**who poured water on Elijah’s hands.** on Mt. Carmel, and with his pouring, his fingers became like springs of water, until the ditch filled up, and it is fitting for a miracle to be performed for us through him concerning water.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אַל כִּי קָרָא ה' וְגוֹ'.** בְּבָעוּ לָא תִּדְכַּר חוֹבִין רִשְׁעָתָא הַהִיא, בָּעֵי עֲלָנָא רַחֲמִין, אֲרֵי זַמִּין ה' וְכוּ'.<br>
**13 [English]**	**Don’t [say that], for the Lord has summoned….** Jonathan [paraphrases thus]: Please do not mention the sins of that wicked woman. Beg mercy for us, for the Lord has summoned…



**15 [Hebrew]**	**קְחוּ לִי מְנַגֵּן.** מִפְּנֵי הַכַּעַס נִסְתַּלְּקָה שְׁכִינָה מִמֶּנּוּ.<br>
**15 [English]**	**fetch me a musician.** Because of his anger, the Shechinah left him.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**גֵּבִים גֵּבִים.** בּוֹרוֹת מְלֵאִים מַיִם.<br>
**16 [English]**	**full of pools.** Pits full of water.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**וְנָקַל זֹאת.** וְעוֹד נֵס זֶה נָקֵל וּמוּעָט בְּעֵינֵי ה', וְהוֹסִיף לְהַפְלִיא אֶתְכֶם נֵס אַחֵר, וְנָתַן אֶת מוֹאָב בְּיֶדְכֶם.<br>
**18 [English]**	**And this will be slight.** And furthermore, this miracle is slight and insignificant in the eyes of the Lord, and He will proceed to act wondrously with you [and perform] another miracle, and He will deliver Moab into your hands.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְכָל עֵץ טוֹב תַּפִּילוּ.** אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: לֹא תַשְׁחִית אֶת עֵצָהּ, כָּאן הִתִּיר לָכֶם, שֶׁהִיא אֻמָּה בְּזוּיָה וְנִקְלֵית לְפָנָיו, וְכֵן הוּא אוֹמֵר: לֹא תִדְרשׁ שְׁלוֹמָם וְטוֹבָתָם, אֵלּוּ הָאִילָנוֹת הַטּוֹבִים שֶׁבָּהֶם.<br>
**19 [English]**	**and you shall fell every good tree.** Even though it is stated (Deut. 20:19): “You shall not destroy its trees,” here He permitted it for you, for this is a contemptible and insignificant nation before Him. And so [Scripture] states (Deut. 23:7): “You shall not seek their welfare and their good.” These are the good trees that are among them.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**הַחֶלְקָה הַטּוֹבָה.** שָׂדֶה זְרוּעָה, קומצ"י בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**19 [English]**	**good field.** A sown field (Koumici in O.F.).



**20 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהִי בַבֹּקֶר.** כַּעֲלוֹת הַמִּנְחָה. הַמַּיִם הִתְחִילוּ לֵירֵד מִן הַשָּׁמַיִם בַּבֹּקֶר בְּאֶרֶץ אֱדוֹם, וְכַעֲלוֹת הַמִּנְחָה בָּאִין שׁוֹטְפִין וּבָאִין לְאוֹתוֹ הַנַּחַל, לְפִי שֶׁאָמַר: לֹא תִרְאוּ רוּחַ וְלֹא תִרְאוּ גֶשֶׁם, בְּמִדְרַשׁ רַבִּי תַּנְחוּמָא.<br>
**20 [English]**	**And it was in the morning when the meal offering was offered up.** The water began falling from heaven in the morning in the land of Edom, and at the time of offering up the meal offering, it came surging into that valley, since the prophet had said, “You shall not see wind neither shall you see rain” (v. 17). In R. Tanhuma’s Midrash.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**עַל הַגְּבוּל.** עַל הַסְּפָר, שֶׁקּוֹרִין מרק"א.<br>
**21 [English]**	**at the border.** at the border that is called marche [in French].



**22 [Hebrew]**	**אֲדֻמִּים כַּדָּם.** כֵּן דֶּרֶךְ הַמַּיִם, כְּשֶׁהַשֶּׁמֶשׁ זוֹרַחַת עֲלֵיהֶם בַּבֹּקֶר, מַאֲדִימוֹת.<br>
**22 [English]**	**red as blood.** Such is the nature of water; when the sun shines on it in the morning, it appears red.

**22 [Hebrew]**	**מִנֶּגֶד.** מֵרָחוֹק, וְהֵם לֹא רָאוּ בְּאוֹתוֹ הַנַּחַל מַיִם מֵעוֹלָם, לְפִיכָךְ הָיוּ סְבוּרִין שֶׁהוּא דָם.<br>
**22 [English]**	**a distance.** at a distance. Now, since they had never seen water in that valley, they, therefore, thought that it was blood.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**וְהֶעָרִים יַהֲרֹסוּ.** הָיוּ הוֹרְסִין.<br>
**25 [English]**	**And they demolished the cities.** [i.e.,] they were demolishing the cities.

**25 [Hebrew]**	**יַשְׁלִיכוּ אִישׁ אַבְנוֹ.** שֶׁהָיוּ נוֹטְלִין הָאֲבָנִים מִן הַחוֹמוֹת, עַד הִשְׁאִיר כָּל אַבְנֵי הַקִּירוֹת בַּקִּיר לַעֲמֹד עַל חַרְסִית שֶׁלָּהֶן הוּא הַטִּיט, כְּלוֹמַר שֶׁלֹּא הָיְתָה עוֹד אֶבֶן בַּחוֹמָה, וְאַחֲרֵי זֹאת וַיָּסֹבּוּ הַקַּלָּעִים וַיַּכּוּ אֶת הַנִּשְׁאָר בַּחֶלְקָה, שֶׁלֹּא נִשְׁבְּרָה הַחוֹמָה, וְהִפִּילוּהָ.<br>
**25 [English]**	**and the catapultists surrounded and struck it.** and each one threw his stone for they would take stones from the walls until they left all the stones of the walls in the wall with only their clay, i.e., their mortar; that is to say that there were no more stones in the wall. And afterwards the catapultists surrounded that which remained in the field where the wall was not broken, and broke it down.



**27 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּקַּח אֶת בְּנוֹ הַבְּכוֹר.** בִּפְסִיקְתָּא דְּפָרָשַׁת שְׁקָלִים נִדְרָשׁ: שֶׁשָּׁאַל אֶת עֲבָדָיו מַה טִּיבָהּ שֶל אֻמָּה זוֹ שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂה לָהֶם נִסִּים כָּאֵלֶּה, אָמְרוּ לוֹ: אֲבִיהֶם אַבְרָהָם בֵּן יָחִיד הָיָה לוֹ, אָמַר לוֹ הַקָּבָּ"ה הַקְרִיבֵהוּ לְפָנַי, וְרָצָה לְהַקְרִיבוֹ לְהַקָּבָּ"ה, אָמַר לָהֶם: אַף אֲנִי בֵּן בְּכוֹר יֵשׁ לִי, אֵלֵךְ וְאַקְרִיבֶנּוּ לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.<br>
**27 [English]**	**And he took his firstborn son.** In the Pesikta of the section of Shekalim it is expounded that he asked his servants ([in] Pesikta [and] Yalkut: his astrologers), “What is the character of this nation, that miracles such as these were performed for them?” They replied, “Their forefather, Abraham, had an only son. The Holy One, Blessed be He, said to him, Sacrifice him before Me, and he wanted to sacrifice him to the Holy One, Blessed be He.” He said to them, “I too have a first born son. I will go and sacrifice him to the gods.”

**27 [Hebrew]**	**עַל הַחֹמָה.** חָסֵר וָא"ו, שֶׁהָיָה עוֹבֵד לַחַמָּה.<br>
**27 [English]**	**on the wall.** Heb. חֹמָה. The ‘vav’ is missing, for he worshipped the sun, חַמָה [i.e., he sacrificed his son to the sun].

**27 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהִי קֶצֶף גָּדוֹל.** שֶׁנִּזְכְּרוּ עֲוֹנוֹתֵיהֶם, שֶׁאַף הֵם עוֹבְדִין לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה, וְאֵינָן רְאוּיִין לְנֵס.<br>
**27 [English]**	**and there was great wrath.** For their iniquities were remembered, that they too worship pagan deities and are not worthy of miracles.
