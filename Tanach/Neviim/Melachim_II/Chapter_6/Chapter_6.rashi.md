**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמְרוּ בְנֵי הַנְּבִיאִים.** נִסִּים שֶׁנַּעֲשׂוּ לוֹ מְסַדֵּר וְהוֹלֵךְ. רַבּוֹתֵינוּ אָמְרוּ: מִכָּאן שֶׁהָיָה גֵּיחֲזִי דּוֹחֶה הַתַּלְמִידִים מִלְּפָנָיו, וּכְשֶׁנִּטְרַד בָּאוּ תַּלְמִידִים רַבִּים, וְנִדְחַק הַמָּקוֹם מֵהֶם.<br>
**1 [English]**	**And the disciples of the prophets said.** He continues to enumerate the miracles that were performed for him (sic). Our Sages deduced from here that Gehazi would reject the pupils from before him, and when he was banished, many pupils came, and the place became too cramped for them.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**הוֹאֶל.** הִתְרַצֶּה.<br>
**3 [English]**	**be willing.** Heb. הואל.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת הַבַּרְזֶל.** הַנְּסֹרֶת.<br>

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְהוּא שָׁאוּל.** שְׁאִלְתִּיו, וְאֵין לִי מִמַּה לְשַׁלֵּם.<br>
**5 [English]**	**it is borrowed.** I borrowed it, and I have not the wherewithal to pay.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וַיָּצֶף.** צָף עַל הַמַּיִם.<br>
**6 [English]**	**floated.** floated on the water.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**פְּלֹנִי אַלְמֹנִי.** כָּסֵי וְטָמִיר, פְּלוֹנִי, לְשׁוֹן: כִּי יִפָּלֵא 'יִתְכַּסֵּי'.<br>
**8 [English]**	**hidden, secret.** Jonathan [renders]: covered and hidden. פְּלֹנִי is an expression of (Deut. 17:8) “If it be concealed” (יִפָּלֵא).

**8 [Hebrew]**	**אַלְמוֹנִי.** מִבְּלִי שֵׁם, שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה רוֹצֶה לְהִתְגַּלּוֹת.<br>
**8 [English]**	**secret.** without a name, for he did not want it to be revealed.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**תַּחֲנֹתִי.** שָׁם אֶחֱנֶה, וְאֶאֱרֹב אֶת מֶלֶךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל אוֹ אֶת גְּדוּדָיו הָעוֹבְרִים לִשְׁלֹל בְּאַרְצִי דֶּרֶךְ אוֹתוֹ הַמָּקוֹם.<br>
**8 [English]**	**I will encamp.** There I will encamp and ambush the king of Israel or his bands who pass to plunder in my land through that place.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**מֵעֲבֹר הַמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה.** מִלֶּכֶת שָׁם.<br>
**9 [English]**	**of passing this place.** [i.e.,] from going there.

**9 [Hebrew]**	**נְחִתִּים.** חוֹנִים.<br>
**9 [English]**	**are encamped.** Heb. נְחִתִּים.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׁלַח מֶלֶךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל.** הָיָה שׁוֹלֵחַ וְרוֹאֶה אִם אֱמֶת הוּא.<br>
**10 [English]**	**And the king of Israel sent.** He would send and see whether it was true.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִזְהִירֹה.** שֶׁהִזְהִיר אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים מֵעֲבֹר שָׁם.<br>
**10 [English]**	**and cautioned him.** concerning which the man of God had cautioned him from passing.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא אַחַת וְלֹא שְׁתָּיִם.** עָשָׂה לוֹ כָּךְ, כִּי אִם פְּעָמִים רַבּוֹת.<br>
**10 [English]**	**not once and not twice.** did he do so for him, but many times.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**עַל הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה.** מִי הוּא מְגַלֶּה סוֹדוֹ.<br>
**11 [English]**	**about this matter.** who was revealing his secret.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**אֵיכֹה.** הֵיכָן הוּא.<br>
**13 [English]**	**where he is.** where he is.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר אִתָּנוּ.** בְּעֶזְרָתֵנוּ.<br>
**16 [English]**	**who are with us.** [i.e.,] who are on our side (helping us).



**18 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמַר הַךְ נָא וְגוֹ'.** עַל הַקְּלָלָה לֹא הִזְכִּיר אֶת הַשֵּׁם, אֲבָל עַל הַפְּקִיחָה הִזְכִּיר אֶת הַשֵּׁם עַל שְׁתֵּיהֶן, עַל שֶל נַעַר וְעַל שֶל גְּדוּד.<br>
**18 [English]**	**and said, Please strike….** On the curse he did not mention the Name [of God,] but on opening the eyes he mentioned the Name [of God] on both of them, concerning [opening] the attendant’s [eyes], and concerning [opening the eyes] of the troop.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**סַנְוֵרִים.** חֹלִי שֶׁל שִׁמָּמוֹן, רוֹאֶה וְאֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ מַה הוּא רוֹאֶה.<br>
**18 [English]**	**with blindness.** a sickness of confusion; one sees, yet does not know what he sees.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא זֹה הָעִיר.** שֶׁהַנָּבִיא בְּתוֹכָהּ, וֶאֱמֶת אָמַר לָהֶם, שֶׁכְּבָר יָצָא מִמֶּנָּה.<br>
**19 [English]**	**nor is this the city.** wherein the prophet is [found]. Now he told them the truth since he had already left it.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**הַאֲשֶׁר שָׁבִיתָ וְגוֹ'.** וְכִי דַּרְכְּךָ לַהֲרֹג אוֹתָם שֶׁאַתָּה מֵבִיא שִׁבְיָה.<br>
**22 [English]**	**Do you slay those you have captured with your sword and with your bow.** Is it your custom to slay one whom you bring into captivity (and destroy them)?



**23 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּכְרֶה.** לְשׁוֹן תִּקּוּן סְעוּדָה.<br>
**23 [English]**	**And he prepared.** Heb. וַיִכְרֶה an expression of preparation of a meal.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**דִּבְיוֹנִים.** זֶבֶל הַזָּב מִן הַיּוֹנִים.<br>
**25 [English]**	**dove’s dung.** [Heb.] divyonim; dung that flows from the doves.



**26 [Hebrew]**	**הוֹשִׁיעָה אֲדֹנִי הַמֶּלֶךְ.** כְּסָבוּר שֶׁשּׁוֹאֶלֶת מְזוֹנוֹת.<br>
**26 [English]**	**“Save me, my lord the king!”.** He thought she was asking for food.



**29 [Hebrew]**	**וַתַּחְבִּא אֶת בְּנָהּ.** מֵת, וְרוֹצָה לְאָכְלוֹ לְבַדָּהּ.<br>
**29 [English]**	**But she hid her son.** dead, and wants to eat him alone.



**30 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּרְא הָעָם.** דֶּרֶךְ הַקֶּרַע, אֶת הַשַּׂק תַּחַת בְּגָדָיו.<br>
**30 [English]**	**and the people saw.** the sackcloth under his garments through the tear.



**31 [Hebrew]**	**אִם יַעֲמֹד רֹאשׁ אֱלִישָׁע.** שֶׁהַיְכֹלֶת בְּיָדוֹ לְבַקֵּשׁ רַחֲמִים.<br>
**31 [English]**	**If Elisha the son of Shaphat’s head remain.** for he is able to beg for mercy.



**32 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּשְׁלַח אִישׁ מִלְּפָנָיו.** הַמֶּלֶךְ שָׁלַח לְהָרְגוֹ, וּבְטֶרֶם יָבֹא אוֹתוֹ הַמַּלְאָךְ אֶל אֱלִישָׁע, וְהוּא נִגְלָה לוֹ בְּרוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ, וְאָמַר אֶל הַזְּקֵנִים: וְהִנֵּה הַמַּלְאָךְ יוֹרֵד אֵלָיו וְהַמֶּלֶךְ אַחֲרָיו.<br>
**32 [English]**	**and he sent a man from before him.** The king sent [a man] to kill him, and when that messenger had not yet come to Elisha, [and he was] revealed to him through the divine spirit, and he said to the elders…. and behold the messenger was coming down to him and the king was after him.



**33 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמֶר.** הַמֶּלֶךְ.<br>
**33 [English]**	**And he said.** The king [said]

**33 [Hebrew]**	**הִנֵּה זֹאת הָרָעָה מֵאֵת ה'.** זֹאת אַחַת מִן הַקְּלָלוֹת שֶׁקִּלֵּל עַל יְדֵי משֶׁה: וְאָכַלְתָּ פְּרִי בִטְנְךָ בְּמָצוֹר וּבְמָצוֹק וְגוֹ'.<br>
**33 [English]**	**Behold, this calamity is from the Lord.** This is one of the curses that He cursed through Moses, (Deut. 28:53) “And you will eat the fruit of your womb… during the siege and in the straitness…”

**33 [Hebrew]**	**מָה אוֹחִיל לַה' עוֹד.** לְהוֹשִׁיעַ, הֲלֹא לֹא יוֹעִיל.<br>
**33 [English]**	**What more can I hope for from the Lord?.** To save? It will surely not avail.
