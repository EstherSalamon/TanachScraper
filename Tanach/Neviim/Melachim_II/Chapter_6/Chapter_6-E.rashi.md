**1**	**And the disciples of the prophets said.** He continues to enumerate the miracles that were performed for him (sic). Our Sages deduced from here that Gehazi would reject the pupils from before him, and when he was banished, many pupils came, and the place became too cramped for them.

**5**	**it is borrowed.** I borrowed it, and I have not the wherewithal to pay.



**6**	**floated.** floated on the water.



**8**	**hidden, secret.** Jonathan [renders]: covered and hidden. פְּלֹנִי is an expression of (Deut. 17:8) “If it be concealed” (יִפָּלֵא).

**8**	**secret.** without a name, for he did not want it to be revealed.

**8**	**I will encamp.** There I will encamp and ambush the king of Israel or his bands who pass to plunder in my land through that place.



**9**	**of passing this place.** [i.e.,] from going there.

**9**	**are encamped.** Heb. נְחִתִּים.



**10**	**And the king of Israel sent.** He would send and see whether it was true.

**10**	**and cautioned him.** concerning which the man of God had cautioned him from passing.

**10**	**not once and not twice.** did he do so for him, but many times.



**11**	**about this matter.** who was revealing his secret.



**13**	**where he is.** where he is.



**16**	**who are with us.** [i.e.,] who are on our side (helping us).



**18**	**and said, Please strike….** On the curse he did not mention the Name [of God,] but on opening the eyes he mentioned the Name [of God] on both of them, concerning [opening] the attendant’s [eyes], and concerning [opening the eyes] of the troop.

**18**	**with blindness.** a sickness of confusion; one sees, yet does not know what he sees.



**19**	**nor is this the city.** wherein the prophet is [found]. Now he told them the truth since he had already left it.



**22**	**Do you slay those you have captured with your sword and with your bow.** Is it your custom to slay one whom you bring into captivity (and destroy them)?



**23**	**And he prepared.** Heb. וַיִכְרֶה an expression of preparation of a meal.



**25**	**dove’s dung.** [Heb.] divyonim; dung that flows from the doves.



**26**	**“Save me, my lord the king!”.** He thought she was asking for food.



**29**	**But she hid her son.** dead, and wants to eat him alone.



**30**	**and the people saw.** the sackcloth under his garments through the tear.



**31**	**If Elisha the son of Shaphat’s head remain.** for he is able to beg for mercy.



**32**	**and he sent a man from before him.** The king sent [a man] to kill him, and when that messenger had not yet come to Elisha, [and he was] revealed to him through the divine spirit, and he said to the elders…. and behold the messenger was coming down to him and the king was after him.

**33**	**Behold, this calamity is from the Lord.** This is one of the curses that He cursed through Moses, (Deut. 28:53) “And you will eat the fruit of your womb… during the siege and in the straitness…”

**33**	**What more can I hope for from the Lord?.** To save? It will surely not avail.
