**1 [Hebrew]**	**בַּסְעָרָה.** בְּרוּחַ סְעָרָה.<br>
**1 [English]**	**in a whirlwind.** [lit. in a storm],] i.e., in a storm wind.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**שֵׁב נָא פֹה.** חָפֵץ הָיָה לִדְחוֹתוֹ מֵחֲמַת עֲנָוָה, שֶׁלֹּא יִרְאֶה בְּהִלָּקְחוֹ.<br>
**2 [English]**	**Stay here now.** He wished to drive him away because of his [Elijah’s] humility, so that he would not see him being taken away.



**3 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת אֲדֹנֶיךָ.** וְלֹא אֲדוֹנֵנוּ, מְלַמֵּד שֶׁהָיוּ שְׁקוּלִים כְּאֵלִיָּהוּ.<br>
**3 [English]**	**your master.** but not our master. This teaches us that they were equal to Elijah.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּגְלֹם.** כְּרָכָהּ, כְּדֵי שֶׁתְּהֵא נוֹחָה לְהַכּוֹת בָּהּ.<br>
**8 [English]**	**and rolled it up.** He rolled it up in order that it would be easy to strike with it.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּגְלֹם.** וְלֹא כְּרִיכָה מַמָּשׁ, אֶלָּא קוֹלֵט, שֶׁקּוֹרִין פייגד"ו בְּלַעַ"ז.<br>
**8 [English]**	**and he rolled it.** not exactly rolling but gathering together, which is called koulir in O.F.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**הִקְשִׁיתָ לִשְׁאוֹל.** אִי אֶפְשָׁר לָתֵת לְךָ יוֹתֵר מִמַּה שֶּׁיֵּשׁ לִי בְּיָדִי.<br>
**10 [English]**	**You have made a difficult request.** It is impossible to give you more than I have in my possession.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**אִם תִּרְאֶה אֹתִי לֻקַּח מֵאִתָּךְ.** אָז אוּכַל לַעֲשׂוֹת לְךָ יוֹתֵר וְיוֹתֵר.<br>
**10 [English]**	**If you see me taken away from you.** then I will be able to do for you more and more.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**יְהִי לְךָ כֵן וְאִם אַיִן לֹא יִהְיֶה.** שֶׁאֵין הַיְכֹלֶת בְּיָדִי.<br>
**10 [English]**	**it will be so to you, and if not, it will not be.** for I have not the ability.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**אָבִי אָבִי רֶכֶב יִשְׂרָאֵל.** רַבִּי רַבִּי דְּטָב לְהוֹן לְיִשְׂרָאֵל בִּצְלוֹתֵהּ מֵרְתִיכִין וּפָרָשִׁין.<br>
**12 [English]**	**My father! My father! The chariots of Israel.** Jonathan [renders:] My master! My master! Who benefited Israel with his prayer more than chariots and riders.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**אַיֵּה ה' אֱלֹהֵי אֵלִיָּהוּ.** שֶׁהֻבְטַחְתִּי שֶׁתִּשְׁרֶה בִי פִּי שְׁנַיִם בְּרוּחוֹ אֵלַי.<br>
**14 [English]**	**Where is the Lord, the God of Elijah?.** that I was promised [who promised me: Kara] that a double portion of his spirit would rest upon me.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**אַף הוּא וַיַּכֶּה אֶת הַמַּיִם.** אַף הוּא, בְּפִרְקֵי רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר, בְּנוֹ שֶל רַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי: גְּדוֹלָה חֲצִיַּת אֱלִישָׁע כִּפְלַיִם כְּשֶׁל אֵלִיָּהוּ, שֶׁבִּתְחִלָּה זְכוּת שְׁנֵיהֶם, וְעַכְשָׁיו זְכוּתוֹ יְחִידִי.<br>
**14 [English]**	**He too struck the water.** He too. In the Chapters of Rabbi Eliezer the son of Rabbi Jose the Galilean, [should read: In the Thirty-two Methods of Rabbi Eliezer the son of Rabbi Jose the Galilean. Shem Ephraim], Elisha’s splitting [the Jordan] was doubly as great as Elijah’s, for in the beginning there was the merit of them both, while here was his merit alone.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**פֶּן נְשָׂאוֹ רוּחַ ה'.** אֶפְשָׁר אֶמֶשׁ אָמְרוּ לוֹ: הֲיָדַעְתָּ כִּי הַיּוֹם ה' לוֹקֵחַ אֶת אֲדוֹנֶיךָ, וְעַכְשָׁיו נֶעְלָם מֵהֶם הֵיכָן הוּא, מְלַמֵּד, מִיּוֹם שֶׁנִּגְנַז אֵלִיָּהוּ הָלְכָה וְנִסְתַּלְּקָה רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ מִן הַנְּבִיאִים, וְשׁוּב לֹא הָיְתָה רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ מְרֻבָּה בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל.<br>
**16 [English]**	**Perhaps a wind from the Lord has carried him off.** Is it possible that on the day before, they said to him, “Do you know that today the Lord will take your master from you?” (v. 5) And now they did not know where he was? This teaches us that since the day when Elijah was hidden, the holy spirit departed from the prophets, and the holy spirit was no longer widespread throughout Israel.



**17 [Hebrew]**	**עַד בּשׁ.** נִתְבַּיֵּשׁ מֵהֶם, שֶׁלֹּא יֹאמְרוּ אֵינוֹ רוֹצֶה לְהַקְבִּיל פְּנֵי רַבּוֹ, לְפִי שֶׁנָּטַל גְּדֻלָּתוֹ אֵינוֹ רוֹצֶה שֶׁיָּשׁוּב, כָּל זֶה רָאִיתִי בַּתּוֹסֶפְתָּא דְּסוֹטָה.<br>
**17 [English]**	**until he was ashamed.** He was ashamed because of them, lest they say that he does not want to go to meet his teacher. Since he took over his high position, he does not want him to return. All this I saw in Tosefta Sotah (12:5).



**19 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַמַּיִם רָעִים.** וּמֵחֲמַת הַמַּיִם הָאָרֶץ מְשַׁכֶּלֶת אֶת יוֹשְׁבֶיהָ לְקָבְרָם, אִם כֵּן מַהוּ: וּמוֹשַׁב הָעִיר טוֹב, מִכָּאן אָמְרוּ: חֵן מָקוֹם עַל יוֹשְׁבָיו.<br>
**19 [English]**	**but the water is bad.** and because of the water, the land causes people to die, to send them to their graves. If so, what is the meaning of: “the city is a good place to live in?” From here we deduce that a place has charm for its inhabitants.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל מוֹצָא הַמַּיִם.** שֶׁהָיוּ נוֹבְעִים מִשָּׁם.<br>
**21 [English]**	**to the source of the water.** from where the water flowed.

**21 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּשְׁלֶךְ שָׁם מֶלַח.** וַהֲלֹא מֶלַח דָּבָר הַמְקַלְקֵל אֶת הַמַּיִם הוּא, אֶלָּא נֵס בְּתוֹךְ נֵס.<br>
**21 [English]**	**and threw salt there.**



**23 [Hebrew]**	**וּנְעָרִים קְטַנִּים.** הַמְנֹעָרִין מִן הַמִּצְוֹת.<br>
**23 [English]**	**and some little boys.** Heb. וּנְעָרִים, people empty [of any observance of commandments].

**23 [Hebrew]**	**עֲלֵה קֵרֵחַ.** עֲלֵה מִכָּאן, שֶׁהִקְרַחְתָּ עָלֵינוּ אֶת הַמָּקוֹם, שֶׁעַד עַכְשָׁיו הָיִינוּ מִשְׂתַּכְּרִין לְהָבִיא מַיִם מְתוּקִים מֵרָחוֹק, וּמִתְפַּרְנְסִין בְּכָךְ, וּמִשֶּׁהִמְתִּיקוּ אִבְּדוּ פַּרְנָסָתָן, כָּךְ מְפֹרָשׁ בְּסוֹטָה.<br>
**23 [English]**	**Go away, baldy.** Go away from here, for you have made the place bald for us, for until now we would hire ourselves out to bring sweet water from a distance, and we would earn our livelihood thereby. And when the water became sweet, they lost their livelihood. Thus it is explained in Sotah (46b).



**24 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּרְאֵם.** רָאָה שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶם וְלֹא בְּזַרְעָם לַחְלוּחִית שֶל טוֹבָה.<br>
**24 [English]**	**and saw them.** He saw that neither in them nor in their descendants would there be any ‘sap’ of good deeds.
