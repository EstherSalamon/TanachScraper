**10**	**through His servants the prophets.** Nahum and Habakkuk, but since Manasseh was wicked, they were not referred to with his name to explain that they were in his time.



**12**	**will tingle.** The root is צלל, an expression similar to מְצִלְתַּיִם, cymbals (tentir in French), to ring, resound.



**13**	**and the plumb line.** That is the line upon which the builders of a wall tie a lead weight to make the wall exact.

**13**	**as one wipes a dish.** as one cleans a dish.
