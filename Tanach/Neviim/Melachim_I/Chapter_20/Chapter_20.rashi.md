**39 [Hebrew]**	**אִם הִפָּקֵד יִפָּקֵד.** אִם יִבְרַח, לְשׁוֹן: לֹא נִפְקַד מִמֶּנּוּ אִישׁ, לְשׁוֹן חִסָּרוֹן.<br>
**39 [English]**	**if he will be missing.** If he escapes, an expression similar to (Num. 31: 49) “and no man was missing from us (ולא נפקדו);” an expression of a lack.



**40 [Hebrew]**	**וְהוּא אֵינֶנּוּ.** בָּרַח לוֹ.<br>
**40 [English]**	**and he was gone.** He ran away.

**40 [Hebrew]**	**אַתָּה חָרַצְתָּ.** אוֹ נֶפֶשׁ, אוֹ כִּכַּר כֶּסֶף.<br>
**40 [English]**	**you have pronounced it.** Either your life or a talent of silver.



**42 [Hebrew]**	**אִישׁ חֶרְמִי.** אִישׁ מִלְחַמְתִּי, לְשׁוֹן חֵירוּם, וּלְשׁוֹן חֵירוּם נוֹפֵל בְּמִלְחָמָה, וְכֵן: עַל אֱדוֹם תֵּרֵד וְעַל עַם חֶרְמִי. וּמִדְרַשׁ אַגָּדָה כַּמָּה מְצוּדוֹת וַחֲרָמִים פָּרַשְׂתִּי לְךָ עַד שֶׁנָּפַל בְּיָדְךָ.<br>
**42 [English]**	**the man whom I designated for destruction.** the man of My war, an expression of חרם, strife. Now, the expression of strife fits with war, and similarly, (Is. 34:5) “… on Edom it will descend and on the nation with whom I war (חרמי).” [Thus we render, “the man with whom I war.” [According to the] Midrash Aggadah: Many traps and nets have I spread for you until he fell into your hand.



**43 [Hebrew]**	**סָר וְזָעֵף.** (תַּרְגּוּם:) נְסִיס וְדַוָּי, רוּחוֹ סָרָה מִמֶּנּוּ.<br>
**43 [English]**	**sad and upset.** Heb. סר וזעף. [Jonathan renders:] נסים ודוי, his spirit turned away from him. [These are expressions of sadness.] His spirit turned away from him.
