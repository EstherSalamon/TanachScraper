**10 [Hebrew]**	**קָטָנִּי עָבָה.** אֶצְבַּע קְטַנָּה שֶׁלִּי.<br>
**10 [English]**	**My little finger is thicker than my father’s loins.** Heb. קטני, my little finger.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**עַקְרַבִּים.** אשגלנטי"ר בְּלַעַ"ז (עֵץ וֶרֶד–הַבָּר שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ קוֹצִים), שֶׁעוֹקְצִין כְּעַקְרָב.<br>
**11 [English]**	**scorpions.** aiguillon in French, a goad or sting [called so] because they sting like scorpions.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**רְאֵה בֵיתְךָ.** בֵּית הַמִּקְדָּשׁ שֶׁבָּנִיתָ, לְךָ לְבַדְּךָ יִהְיֶה.<br>
**16 [English]**	**Now see your house.** The Temple that you built, will be for you alone.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**אֲדוֹרָם.** הוּא אֲדוֹנִירָם.<br>
**18 [English]**	**And King Rehoboam sent Adoram.** He was Adoniram [mentioned above in 5:28].



**21 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת שֵׁבֶט בִּנְיָמִן.** הָעוֹמְדִים עַל הַגְּבוּל, שֶׁיְּהוּדָה וּבִנְיָמִין הָיָה גְּבוּלָם סָמוּךְ זֶה לָזֶה.<br>
**21 [English]**	**and the tribe of Benjamin.** Who were situated on the border, for the boundaries of Judah and Benjamin were adjacent.



**27 [Hebrew]**	**אִם יַעֲלֶה הָעָם הַזֶּה וְגוֹ'.** אֵין יְשִׁיבָה בָּעֲזָרָה אֶלָּא לְמַלְכֵי בֵּית דָּוִד בִּלְבַד, וְיִהְיֶה הוּא יוֹשֵׁב וַאֲנִי עוֹמֵד, וּבְמוֹצָאֵי שְׁבִיעִית בִּזְמַן הַקְהֵל, יִקְרָא אֶת פָּרָשַׁת הַמֶּלֶךְ, שֶׁהוּא רֹאשׁ בִּמְדִינָתוֹ, וַאֲנִי כִּשְׁאָר הָעָם, נִמְצֵאתִי בָּזוּי.<br>
**27 [English]**	**When this people goes.** Sitting in the Temple court is not permitted except for the kings of the house of David only. Therefore, he [Rehoboam] will be seated and I will be standing. Moreover, in the year following the Sabbatical Year, at the time of the Assembly, he will read the portion relating to the king, for he is the king of his province, whereas I will be like the rest of the people, and will, therefore, find myself degraded.



**30 [Hebrew]**	**עַד דָּן.** בְּסוֹף גְּבוּל הָאָרֶץ.<br>
**30 [English]**	**even until Dan.** at the end of the boundary of the land.



**33 [Hebrew]**	**בַּחֹדֶשׁ הַשְּׁמִינִי.** דָּרַשׁ לָהֶם הוּא חֹדֶשׁ הָאָסִיף, וּבוֹ רָאוּי הֶחָג לִהְיוֹת.<br>
**33 [English]**	**in the eighth month.** He expounded to them that this was the month of ingathering, and was, therefore, the proper time for the festival to be [celebrated].

**33 [Hebrew]**	**בָּדָא מִלִּבּוֹ.** לְשׁוֹן כָּזָב, קונטרובי"ר בְּלַעַ"ז:<br>
**33 [English]**	**that he had fabricated from his heart.** Heb. אשר בדא מלבו, an expression of falsehood (controver in O.F., to invent).
