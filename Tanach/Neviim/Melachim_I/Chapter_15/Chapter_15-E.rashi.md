**1**	**And in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam.** One year after Rehoboam’s death. Now in the twentieth year of Jeroboam, Asa reigned. The third year of Nadab [Abijam] is counted for King Asa as a year, and for him it is also counted.



**12**	**and he abolished the adulterers.** Jonathan renders: And he abolished the prostitutes.



**13**	**from being queen.** Heb. מגבירה, [lit. from queen,] from being queen.

**13**	**for she had made a frightful image.** Our Rabbis stated: [מפלצת is a combination of] מפליא ליצנותא, it carried lasciviousness to an extreme. She made for it a phallic symbol, and she would copulate with it every day.



**14**	**The high places he did not remove.** The private high places upon which they had become accustomed to sacrifice to Heaven since Shiloh had been destroyed until the Temple was built, when the high places were permissible, were not removed now. Even though they were forbidden when the Temple was built and the people were punished by premature death because of it.



**22**	**without exception.** [lit. no one was exempt.] Even a bridegroom concerning whom it is stated (Deut. 24:5) “He shall be free for his house one year.”



**25**	**two years.** the second and the third year of Asa.



**33**	**During the third year of Asa, king of Judah, Baasha the son of Ahijah, reigned.** The third year of Asa is counted for Nadab and Baasha.
