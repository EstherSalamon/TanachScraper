**1 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהִי אַחַר הַדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה.** עוֹד אַחֶרֶת לְקָרֵב מִיתָתוֹ.<br>
**1 [English]**	**And it was after these happenings.** [lit. after these things,] still another incident to bring his death close.



**2 [Hebrew]**	**מְחִיר זֶה.** הַכֶּרֶם הַזֶּה.<br>
**2 [English]**	**its worth.** [lit. the worth of this, i.e.,] the worth of this vineyard.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל הַזְּקֵנִים.** זִקְנֵי אַשְׁמַאי, סָבֵי דְבַהֲתָא.<br>
**8 [English]**	**to the elders.** wicked and ignorant elders, elders of shame.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**קִרְאוּ צוֹם.** וּבְיוֹם צוֹם דַּרְכָּם לִבְדֹּק בַּעֲבֵרוֹת שֶׁבְּיָדָם.<br>
**9 [English]**	**Proclaim a fast.** On a fast day it was their custom to search for the sins in their hands.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**בֵּרַךְ נָבוֹת.** חִלּוּף שֶׁל קְלָלָה, וְתַרְגּוּם: גָּדִיף נָבוֹת קֳדָם ה' וּמַלְכָּא לָט.<br>
**13 [English]**	**Naboth cursed.** Heb. ברך, [lit. blessed, a euphemism for קללה,] curse. The Targum renders: Naboth blasphemed against God and cursed the king.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**קוּם רֵשׁ.** יֵשׁ מֵחֲכָמִים אוֹמְרִים: הֲרוּגֵי מַלְכוּת נִכְסֵיהֶם לַמֶּלֶךְ, וְיֵשׁ מֵהֶן אוֹמְרִים: בֶּן אֲחִי אָבִיו הָיָה, וְאוֹתוֹ וְאֶת בָּנָיו הָרַג, וְרָאוּי לְיָרְשׁוֹ.<br>
**15 [English]**	**Get up and take possession.** Some of our Sages say that the property of those executed by the king belong to the king. And some of them say that he [Ahab] was the son of his [Naboth’s] father’s brother. He killed him and his children [as well], and was therefore fit to inherit his [property].



**20 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְמַכֶּרְךָ.** מָכַרְתָּ אֶת עַצְמְךָ לְהַכְעִיס אֶת בּוֹרַאֲךָ. הִתְמַכֶּרְךָ כְּמוֹ: וְהִתְמַכַּרְתֶּם שָׁם לְאוֹיְבֶיךָ.<br>
**20 [English]**	**you sold yourself.** You sold yourself to anger your Creator. Heb. התמכרך, you sold yourself [reflexive form] like (Deut. 28:68) והתמכרתם, “and you will sell yourselves there to your enemies.”



**23 [Hebrew]**	**בְּחֵל יִזְרְעֶאל.** בְּבִקְעַת יִזְרְעֶאל, בְּחֵל, תַּרְגּוּמוֹ שֶׁל גַּיְא.<br>
**23 [English]**	**in the valley of Jezreel.** Heb. חל, the Aramaic translation of valley.



**25 [Hebrew]**	**רַק לֹא הָיָה כְאַחְאָב.** הַכָּתוּב מֵעִיד עָלָיו, שֶׁלֹּא הָיָה בַּמְּלָכִים כְּמוֹתוֹ, שֶׁיָּרָבְעָם וְכָל אֲשֶׁר אַחֲרָיו, עָבְדוּ אֶת הָעֲגָלִים מִיִּרְאָה, אִם יַעֲלֶה הָעָם יְרוּשָׁלַיִם, תָּשׁוּב הַמַּמְלָכָה לְבֵית דָּוִד, וְזֶה הוֹסִיף הַבַּעַל וְהָאֲשֵׁרָה לְהַכְעִיס.<br>
**25 [English]**	**But there was none like Ahab.** The Scripture testifies concerning him that there was none among the kings like him, for Jeroboam and all those after him worshipped the calves out of fear that if the people would go up to Jerusalem, the kingdom would return to the house of David, but this one [Ahab] added the Baal and the Asherah to anger God.

**25 [Hebrew]**	**הִתְמַכֵּר.** נִמְכַּר לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה. רָאִיתִי בִּירוּשַׁלְמִי חִיאֵל בֵּית הָאֱלִי שׁוֹשְׁבִינוֹ הָיָה, וּבְכָל יוֹם שָׁם דָּמָיו וְנָתַן לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.<br>
**25 [English]**	**sold himself.** was sold to idolatry. I saw in Yerushalmi that Hiel was his counselor, and every day he [Hiel] would estimate his [Ahab’s] value and he [Ahab] would give it to the pagan deities.



**27 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְהַלֵּךְ אַט.** יָחֵף.<br>
**27 [English]**	**and put sackcloth on his flesh, and he fasted.** he walked barefoot.



**29 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא אָבִיא הָרָעָה.** רָעַת בֵּיתוֹ לֹא אָבִיא בְּיָמָיו, אֲבָל גְּזֵרַת יָלֹקּוּ הַכְּלָבִים אֶת דָּמוֹ, אִי אֶפְשָׁר לָבֹא בִּימֵי בְּנוֹ, אֶלָּא עָלָיו.<br>
**29 [English]**	**I will not bring the disaster.** i.e., the disaster of his house I will not bring in his days, but the decree that the dogs will lick his blood is impossible to come in the days of his son, but upon him.
