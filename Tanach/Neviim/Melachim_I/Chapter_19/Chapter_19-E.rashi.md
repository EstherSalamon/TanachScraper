**9**	**to the cave.** That is the cleft of the rock where Moses stood (Ex. 33:22).



**10**	**They have torn down Your altars.** The private high places that were built in the Name of Heaven, for the altar of the Temple was in Jerusalem.



**11**	**and a great… wind.** A camp of angels of the wind.

**11**	**an earthquake.** A camp of angels of earthquake.



**12**	**a fire.** A camp of angels of fire.

**12**	**a still small sound.** A voice of those praising quietly, but concerning the prophets of the nations, Scripture states: (Job 4:16) “Silence and a voice I hear”; there was silence to the praising, but I heard the voice coming out of the silence, retentissement in O.F., resounding, but no one heard the voice itself.



**13**	**he wrapped his face.** Jonathan renders: כריך אפוהי, and so Scripture says: “wrapped in a garment (לוטה בשמלה)” (I Sam. 21:10).



**16**	**you shall anoint to be prophet in your stead.** I do not want your prophecy since you plead for the prosecution of My children.



**17**	**Elisha will kill.** We do not find that Elisha killed anyone except the forty-two children through the bears in Jericho (II Kings 2:24).



**20**	**Go, return.** from following me.

**20**	**for what have I done to you.** that you should follow me.



**21**	**he cooked the meat for them.** Heb. בִּשְׁלָם הַבָּשָׂר. The mem of bishelam [is usually indicative of the direct object, to be rendered,] he cooked them. [In this case, however, since the object is] habassar, [the meat, we render:] he cooked for them the meat, so Dunash and Menahem (Machbereth Menahem, p. 48) explain, and so did Jonathan render this in two words. [‘bashilu lehon,’ he cooked for them.] I say, however, that it is unnecessary to divide it into two words [i.e., to add the preposition to]. Now what is the meaning of בִּשְׁלָם הַבָּשָׂר? He cooked the two oxen, their flesh; [i.e., he cooked them, viz. their flesh.] For the yoke is no fewer than two. [Therefore the plural suffix is used.]

**21**	**and he gave the people.** Out of great joy, he made a feast.
