**5 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה לִי.** שֶׁהֶרְאָה הַסֵּפֶר שֶׁשָּׁלַח דָּוִד בְּיַד אוּרִיָּה. (במדרש רבי תנחומא שם)<br>
**5 [English]**	**You also know what Joab the son of Zeruiah did to me.** by showing them the letter which David had sent through Uriah.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**דְּמֵי מִלְחָמָה בְּשָׁלֹם.** שֶׁהָיָה לָהֶם שָׁלוֹם עִמּוֹ, וְלֹא הָיוּ נִשְׁמָרִים מִמֶּנּוּ.<br>
**5 [English]**	**And shed the blood of war in peace.** Since they were at peace with him, they were not on guard against him.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**בַּחֲגֹרָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר בְּמָתְנָיו.** שֶׁחָגַר חַרְבּוֹ מְצֻמֶּדֶת עַל מָתְנָיו, שֶׁלֹּא כְּדֶרֶךְ הַחוֹגְרִים, בְּצֵאתוֹ לִקְרַאת עֲמָשָׂא, וּפִיהָ לְמַטָּה כְּדֵי שֶׁתִּפֹּל, כְּמָה שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: וְהוּא יָצָא וַתִּפֹּל, וּכְשֶׁנָּפְלָה וּנְטָלָהּ, כְּסָבוּר עֲמָשָׂא שֶׁלֹּא נְטָלָהּ אֶלָּא לְהַגְבִּיהָהּ מִן הָאָרֶץ, וְלֹא נִשְׁמַר מֵהַחֶרֶב אֲשֶׁר בְּיַד יוֹאָב.<br>
**5 [English]**	**And he put the blood of war upon his girdle that was about his loins.** He had girded his sword, attached to his loins, not in the customary manner of those girding swords, and he went out to meet Amasa. He turned the opening downward so that it should easily fall out of the sheath to the ground as it says (Samuel II 20:8) “and he went out towards Amasa and it fell out.” As soon as it fell, Joab [nonchalantly] retrieved it, and held it ready in his hand. Amasa presumed he retrieved it solely to pick it up from the ground, and was not alert to guard himself against an attack from the sword which was in Joab’s hand.

**5 [Hebrew]**	**וּבְנַעֲלוֹ אֲשֶׁר בְּרַגְלָיו.** אֶת אַבְנֵר הָרַג בְּעָרְמָה, שֶׁשְּׁאָלוֹ גִּדֶּמֶת הֵיאָךְ חוֹלֶצֶת, כְּמָה שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: וַיַּטֵּהוּ יוֹאָב אֶל תּוֹךְ הַשַּׁעַר לְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ 'בַשֶּׁלִי', לְשׁוֹן 'שַׁל' נְעָלֶיךָ.<br>
**5 [English]**	**and in his shoes that (were) on his feet.**



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תוֹרֵד שֵׂיבָתוֹ בְּשָׁלֹם שְׁאֹל.** אַל תַּנִּיחֵהוּ לָמוּת מִיתַת עַצְמוֹ וְלִפֹּל בְּגֵיהִנֹּם.<br>
**6 [English]**	**And do not let his hoary head go down to the grave in peace.** Do not let him die a natural death [since this will minimize his worldly punishment] and cause him to fall into purgatory for torturous purification.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**קְלָלָה נִמְרֶצֶת.** מְפֹרֶשֶׁת, כְּמוֹ: מַה יַּמְרִיצְךָ; (שם ו כה): וּמַה נִּמְרְצוּ, כְּמוֹ מַה נִּמְלְצוּ.<br>
**8 [English]**	**Who cursed me with a grievous curse.** [Others maintain] נִמְרֶצֶת means clear and unquestionable as (we find) in (Job 16:3): “what will bring clarity to you (ימריצך),” and (Job 6:25): “how clear (נמרצו) are right words,” the same as נִמְלְצוּ a derivation of the word מְלִיצָה, an interpretation or clarification.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי אִישׁ חָכָם אָתָּה.** תֵּן לִבְּךָ לִמְצֹא לוֹ עֲוֹן מָוֶת בִּשְׁבִיל דָּבָר אַחֵר, וְזֶהוּ: וְיָדַעְתָּ אֵת אֲשֶׁר תַּעֲשֶׂה לּוֹ.<br>
**9 [English]**	**For you are a wise man.** Turn your attention to find for him an iniquity justifying a death penalty in another matter.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַתִּכֹּן מַלְכֻתוֹ מְאֹד.** אַף עַל הָעֶלְיוֹנִים.<br>
**12 [English]**	**And his kingdom was firmly established.** He was also master and ruler of all heavenly creatures and beings.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**לְדַבֶּר לוֹ.** לְדַבֵּר בִּשְׁבִילוֹ, בִּשְׁבִיל אֲדוֹנִיָּהוּ.<br>
**19 [English]**	**to speak to him.** to speak on his behalf; on behalf of Adoniahu.

**19 [Hebrew]**	**לְאֵם הַמֶּלֶךְ.** אָמְרוּ רַבּוֹתֵינוּ (בבא בתרא צא ב): לְאִמָּהּ שֶׁל מַלְכוּת, הִיא רוּת.<br>
**19 [English]**	**for the king’s mother.** Our Sages said, however, “the king’s mother” is a reference to Ruth.



**22 [Hebrew]**	**וְשַׁאֲלִי לוֹ אֶת הַמְּלוּכָה.** מִשֶּׁיִּשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בְּשַׁרְבִיטוֹ שֶׁל מֶלֶךְ, הוּא תְּחִלַּת הַשְּׂרָרָה.<br>
**22 [English]**	**… ask for him the kingdom also.** From the moment a commoner uses the scepter of the king, that is the beginning of authority.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**עָשָׂה לִי בַּיִת.** בֵּית הַמְּלוּכָה, כַּאֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּר בְּיַד נָתָן.<br>
**24 [English]**	**… Who has established me.** “… and Who has made me a house” a house of royalty as he had spoken through Nathan the prophet.



**26 [Hebrew]**	**עֲנָתֹת לֵךְ.** מִשָּׁם הָיָה.<br>
**26 [English]**	**Go to Anathoth to your own fields….** He was from there.

**26 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי אִישׁ מָוֶת אָתָּה.** שֶׁמָּרַדְתָּ בְּמַלְכוּת אָבִי בִּהְיוֹתְךָ עִם אֲדוֹנִיָּהוּ, לְהַמְלִיכוֹ שֶׁלֹּא מִדַּעְתּוֹ.<br>
**26 [English]**	**For you are deservant of death.** Because you rebelled against the kingdom of my father when you were with Adoniahu to appoint him king without the knowledge of my father.

**26 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי נָשָׂאתָ אֶת אֲרוֹן.** בִּבְרֹחַ אָבִי מִפְּנֵי אַבְשָׁלוֹם.<br>
**26 [English]**	**Because you did bear the Ark of the Lord God before David my father.** as my father was escaping from before Absalom.

**26 [Hebrew]**	**וְכִי הִתְעַנִּיתָ.** עִמּוֹ בְּאוֹתָהּ הַצָּרָה, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: כִּי אָמְרוּ הָעָם רָעֵב וְעָיֵף וְצָמֵא בַּמִּדְבָּר.<br>
**26 [English]**	**And because you were afflicted.** with him in those very same afflictions, as it says (Samuel II 17:29) “for they said the people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness.”



**27 [Hebrew]**	**אֶת אֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר עַל בֵּית עֵלִי.** וַהֲקִימֹתִי לִי כֹּהֵן נֶאֱמָן וְגוֹ', וְהִבַּטְתָּ צַר מָעוֹן וְגוֹ', וּמִשֶּׁחָרְבָה שִׁילֹה, לֹא הָיָה בַּיִת חָשׁוּב לַעֲבוֹדָה לְהַקָּבָּ"ה, וְעַתָּה כְּשֶׁיִּבָּנֶה בֵּית הַבְּחִירָה, מִלֵּא הַקָּבָּ"ה אֶת דְּבָרוֹ לְגָרֵשׁ אֶת בְּנֵי עֵלִי.<br>
**27 [English]**	**Which He spoke concerning the house of Eli.** [Scripture predicts the end of the house of Eli:] “And I will raise Me up a faithful priest” (Samuel I 2:35) or “And you shall see a rival in My habitation” (Samuel I 2:32). From the time of the destruction of Shilo, there was no house worthy of the service to the Holy One Blessed be He and now that the Temple will be built, the Holy One Blessed be He fulfilled His word to banish the children of Eli.



**28 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַשְּׁמֻעָה בָּאָה עַד יוֹאָב.** שְׁמוּעַת מִצְוַת דָּוִד שֶׁצִּוָּה לְהָרְגוֹ, וְיָדַע שֶׁשְּׁלֹמֹה שׂוֹנְאוֹ.<br>
**28 [English]**	**And the tidings came to Joab.** the report of David’s charge, that he had commanded to kill him, and he [also] knew that Solomon disliked him.

**28 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי יוֹאָב נָטָה אַחֲרֵי אֲדֹנִיָּה וְאַחֲרֵי אַבְשָׁלוֹם לֹא נָטָה.** סוֹף דָּבָר, אֲבָל בִּקֵשׁ לִנְטוֹת, אֶלָּא נִתְיָרֵא שֶׁעֲדַיִן לַחְלוּחִית שֶׁל דָּוִד קַיֶּמֶת.<br>
**28 [English]**	**For Joab turned after Adoniahu, and after Absalom he did not turn.** The end of the matter was [that Joab did not follow him], but he did seek to turn after him, but for the fact that he was fearful, because the vigor of David’s might was still present.



**30 [Hebrew]**	**וַיֹּאמֶר לֹא.** שְׁתַּיִם לֹא אֲקַבֵּל עָלַי, קְלָלוֹת שֶׁקִּלְלַנִי דָּוִד אָבִיךָ בְּמִיתַת אַבְנֵר: יָחוּלוּ עַל רֹאשׁ יוֹאָב, יְקַבְּלֵם שְׁלֹמֹה עָלָיו, וְאַחַר כָּךְ אֵצֵא.<br>
**30 [English]**	**And he said “No…”.** Two punishments I will not accept upon myself. The curses that your father David cursed me at the death of Abner, “They shall fall upon the head of Joab,” let Solomon accept them upon himself, and afterward I will come out.



**31 [Hebrew]**	**עֲשֵׂה כַּאֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר.** אֲנִי מְקַבְּלָם עָלַי, לְכָךְ נִדְבְּקוּ כָּל אֵלֶּה בְּזֶרַע דָּוִד, עֻזִּיָּה הָיָה מְצֹרָע, אָסָא מַחֲזִיק בַּפֶּלֶךְ, יְהוֹיָקִים נָפַל בַּחֶרֶב, וַחֲסַר לֶחֶם צִדְקִיָּהוּ.<br>
**31 [English]**	**Do as he said.** I will accept the curses upon myself. Therefore, all these [curses] became attached to the offspring of David. Uzziah was a leper (Chronicles II 26:19), Assa leaned on a staff (Kings I 15:23), Jehoiakim fell by the sword (Jeremiah 22:19), and one who lacked bread was Zedekiah (Kings II 25:30).



**34 [Hebrew]**	**בְּבֵיתוֹ בַּמִּדְבָּר.** שֶׁהָיָה בֵּיתוֹ הֶפְקֵר כַּמִּדְבָּר, לַעֲנִיִּים.<br>
**34 [English]**	**And he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.** His home was ownerless and unrestricted as a wilderness for all the destitute and needy to use at their convenience.



**36 [Hebrew]**	**וְלֹא תֵצֵא מִשָּׁם.** כְּדֵי שֶׁיְּהֵא מָצוּי לוֹ, וְלִמְצֹא לוֹ חֵטְא מָוֶת.<br>
**36 [English]**	**And do not go forth.** in order that he be accessible to him and that he might find him guilty of a crime deserving of death.



**42 [Hebrew]**	**וָאָעִד בְּךָ.** לְשׁוֹן הַתְרָאָה.<br>
**42 [English]**	**And forewarned you.** Heb. ואעד, an expression referring to the legal warning given to the offender before committing the offense.



**43 [Hebrew]**	**אֵת שְׁבֻעַת ה'.** אֲשֶׁר הִשְׁבַּעְתִּיךָ בַּה'.<br>
**43 [English]**	**And why have you nor kept the oath of the Lord.** which I made you swear in the name of the Lord?



**46 [Hebrew]**	**וְהַמַּמְלָכָה נָכוֹנָה וְגוֹ'.** כְּלוֹמַר, לֹא נֶעֱנַשׁ עַל כָּל אֵלֶּה.<br>
**46 [English]**	**And the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon.** that is to say, he was not punished for any of these deeds.
