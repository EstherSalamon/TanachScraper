**3 [Hebrew]**	**וְנִקֻּדִים.** מִין קְלָיוֹת, מְיַבְּשִׁין בְּתַנּוּר וְעוֹשִׂין אוֹתָן קֶמַח לַעֲשׂוֹת מִמֶּנּוּ מַאֲכָל, שֶׁקּוֹרִין לוֹ שְׁתִיתָא בִּלְשׁוֹן גְּמָרָא.<br>
**3 [English]**	**biscuits.** Heb. ונקדים. A type of parched grain, dried in an oven. They make flour from it to make it into a dish known in the language of the Gemara as ‘shethitha.’



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וְהִיא מִתְנַכֵּרָה.** כְּאִלּוּ אֵינָהּ זֹאת.<br>
**5 [English]**	**she was disguised.** as though she were not this one.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**שָׁלוּחַ אֵלַיִךְ.** מֵאֵת הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא בִּשְׁלִיחוּת קָשָׁה.<br>
**6 [English]**	**I am sent to you.** from the Holy One Blessed be He with a harsh message.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר.** תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן: יָדַע מַדָּע, מֵשִׁית בְּקִירוֹת לִבּוֹ.<br>
**10 [English]**	**male child.** Jonathan renders: One who knows knowledge; one who places [wisdom or counsel] into the walls of his heart.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**עָצוּר וְעָזוּב.** עָצוּר שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ מַחֲזִיק יָד, כְּמוֹ: זֶה יַעְצֹר בְּעַמִּי, וְעָזוּב, שֶׁאֵין לוֹ מַחֲזִיק בַּיָּד.<br>
**10 [English]**	**those who are restrained and those who are free.** Heb. עצור ועזוב. עצור is one who has someone restraining his hand, similar to “This one shall rule over (יעצור) My people” (Sam. 9:17). ועזוב means one who has no one restraining his hand.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**כַּאֲשֶׁר יְבַעֵר.** הַמְבַעֵר אֶת הַמַּאֲכָל בְּפִיו, לַעֲשׂוֹת גָּלָל, כָּךְ אֲבָעֵר אַחֲרָיו עַד תֻּמּוֹ.<br>
**10 [English]**	**as one expunges.** As the one who [consumes food to make] it eventually into dung, so will I expunge after him until he is completely consumed.



**13 [Hebrew]**	**דָּבָר טוֹב.** שֶׁבִּטֵּל מִשְׁמַרְתּוֹ, שֶׁהוֹשִׁיבוֹ אָבִיו שׁוֹמֵר שֶׁלֹּא יַעֲלֶה אִישׁ לָרֶגֶל, וּבִטֵּל מִשְׁמַרְתּוֹ וְעָלָה.<br>
**13 [English]**	**a good deed.** For he absented his watch, for his father had stationed him as a sentry so that no man would go up on the pilgrimage festivals. Instead, he absented his watch and he himself went up.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**אֲשֶׁר יַכְרִית אֶת בֵּית יָרָבְעָם זֶה הַיּוֹם וּמֶה גַּם עַתָּה.** הַנּוֹלָדִים לוֹ הַיּוֹם, וַאֲשֶׁר עֲתִידִין לְהִוָּלֵד לוֹ מֵעַתָּה, כָּךְ תִּרְגֵּם יוֹנָתָן.<br>
**14 [English]**	**who will cut off the house of Jeroboam this day, and also those who will be born from now on..** [Those] who are already born to him this day, and those who are destined to be born to him from now on. So did Jonathan render.



**21 [Hebrew]**	**הָעִיר אֲשֶׁר בָּחַר ה'.** וְאַף עַל פִּי כֵן:<br>
**21 [English]**	**the city that the Lord had chosen.** Yet, even so…



**22 [Hebrew]**	**וַיַּעַשׂ יְהוּדָה הָרָע.** וְלֹא נָתְנוּ זֶה עַל לִבָּם.<br>
**22 [English]**	**And Judah did what displeased the Lord.** and they did not pay attention to this.

**22 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְקַנְאוּ.** לְשׁוֹן קְנִיטָה.<br>
**22 [English]**	**and they angered Him.** Heb. ויקנאו [usually used in the sense of jealousy or zeal but here is] an expression of angering.



**23 [Hebrew]**	**גַּם הֵמָּה.** כִּשְׁאָר הַשְּׁבָטִים.<br>
**23 [English]**	**And they too.** [like the other] tribes.



**24 [Hebrew]**	**קָדֵשׁ.** נִאוּף.<br>
**24 [English]**	**.**



**25 [Hebrew]**	**שִׁישַׁק.** מָצִינוּ בְּמִדְרַשׁ (שִׁיר הַשִּׁירִים) הוּא פַּרְעֹה נְכֹה, וְנִקְרָא שִׁישַׁק, עַל שֶׁהָיָה שׁוֹקֵק וּמִתְאַוֶּה כָּל יָמָיו לְכִסֵּא הַשֵּׁן שֶׁהָיָה לִשְׁלֹמֹה חֲתָנוֹ, וְעַכְשָׁיו עָלָה וּלְקָחוֹ.<br>
**25 [English]**	**Shishak the king of Egypt.** We find in Midrash Shir Hashirim: This was Pharaoh Neco. He was called Shishak because all his life he longed for (שוקק) the ivory throne that belonged to Solomon, his son-in-law. Now, he went up and took it.



**26 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶת הַכֹּל לָקַח.** אֶת הַכִּסֵּא, הֶחָבִיב מִן הַכֹּל.<br>
**26 [English]**	**and he took everything.** the throne that was dearer to him than everything.



**28 [Hebrew]**	**יִשָּׂאוּם הָרָצִים.** לְפָנָיו לְכָבוֹד, וְאַחַר כָּךְ יְשִׁיבוּם אֶל תָּא שֶׁהָרָצִים עוֹמְדִים שָׁם תָּמִיד.<br>
**28 [English]**	**the runners would carry them.** before him in honor. Afterwards, they would return them to the chamber where the runners were always stationed.
