**1**	**And the Philistines fought with Israel.** This is like a person saying, “Let us return to the earlier topic.”



**11**	**about him.** (אליו, lit., to him) like עליו, about him, i.e., about Saul.



**12**	**and they buried them there.** (Jonathan renders:) And they burned for them as they burn for the kings there. As we learned: (A.Z. 11a, Tos. Sab. ch. 8:9, Tos. San. ch. 4:2) We perform burning at the burial of kings, and it is not considered a custom of the Amorites.
