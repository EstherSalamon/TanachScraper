**1**	**the Bethlehemite.** (בית-הלחמי,) from Bethlehem, and so is the rule of every noun which is composed of two words, e.g., Beth-lehem, Beth-shemesh, Beth-el, Kiriath-arba, if one wishes to add a ‘heh’ (definite article) thereto, he adds it between the two words, e.g., Kiriath-ha’arba (Gen. 35:27), בית-האלי (I Kings 16:34), בית-הלחמי.



**4**	**hurried.** (lit., trembled) hurried out toward him.



**6**	**that he said, “Surely, before the Lord is His anointed.”.** and he said to himself, “Surely, it is true that this one is fitting for the kingdom.”



**7**	**Look not upon his appearance.** upon the beauty of his form.

**7**	**for I have rejected him.** because he is an irascible person, as it is stated: “And Eliab’s wrath was kindled against David” (infra 17:29).

**7**	**for it is not as man sees.** Even though you called yourself ‘seer,’ for you said to Saul, “I am the seer,” (supra 9:19), here I inform you that you do not see.



**9**	**Shammah.** That is Shimeah (mentioned in II Sam. 12:3; II Chron. 2:13).



**11**	**The youngest still remains.** (Heb. שאר, kal conj. active) This is like נשאר (remains, niph’al conj. passive).

**11**	**we will not sit down.** (לא נסב) We will not sit down to eat. Every instance of sitting down to a meal is called הסבה (leaning).



**13**	**And a spirit of the Lord passed.** a spirit of valiance.



**16**	**your servants are before you.** Behold, your servants are before you, who will do your bidding.



**18**	**one of the young men.** The special one of the young men, Doeg the Edomite.

**18**	**who knows how to play.** With his entire essence, he intended to introduce Saul’s evil eye into David, i.e., that he envy him.
