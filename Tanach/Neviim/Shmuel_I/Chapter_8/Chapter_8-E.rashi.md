**3**	**and they turned.** They themselves; (i.e., ‘va’yittu’ is an intransitive verb, of the ‘kal’ or simple conjugation).

**3**	**and they perverted.** Something else; viz. justice. (I. e., ‘va’yattu’ is a transitive verb of the ‘hiph’il’ or causative conjugation.) And our Rabbis said: Samuel’s sons did not sin. They merely did not follow their father’s footsteps. While their father would travel to all places of Israel and judge them in their cities they did not do so, in order to increase the income of their sheriffs and scribes, (who were employed to summon the litigants to trial).
