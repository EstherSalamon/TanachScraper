**3**	**And I troubled the young men.** (Heb. ‘va’yada’) An expression of troubling. The young men who came out with me, I have troubled them to run before me to a hidden, secret place.

**3**	**I troubled.** similar to, “And with them he broke (va’yoda) the men of Succoth” (Jud. 8:16).

**3**	**hidden, secret.** (Heb. ‘ploni almoni.’) ‘Almoni’ is derived from ‘almon’ (a widower), bereft of a name, (as a widower is bereft of his spouse), for I do not wish to mention it, since it is a secret matter.

**3**	**hidden.** (Heb. ‘ploni,’) like: ‘ki yipalei’ (Deut. 17:8), which means ‘will be concealed.’



**4**	**or whatever is found..** If there are not five, give me whatever is found in your possession.



**5**	**but… from women.** With difficulty, I give them to strangers (non-priests) to eat, but it is impossible to give it to unclean people.



**6**	**withheld from us..** lit., withheld to us.

**6**	**and the young men’s garments are hallowed.** i.e., ritually clean.

**6**	**and that is in a manner common.** and the bread, since it has been removed from the table, and they have burned the spoonfuls (of frankincense), it is almost common, since it has been excluded from the prohibition of ‘me’ilah’ (betrayal) from the time it is permitted for the priests.

**6**	**and even if today it would be hallowed in the vessel.** and even if today were the commencement of its sanctity on the table, I should (or perhaps it should read, ‘you would’) be obliged to take it from there and feed us, for a ravenous hunger has seized me, and my life is in danger. Thus it is explained in Men. (95b).



**7**	**to place warm bread.** i. e., other warm bread on the day this was taken away. And our Rabbis said: Warm on the day it was taken - It was as warm on the day it was taken as it was on the day it was arranged.



**8**	**detained before the Lord.** he tarried before the tent of meeting to engage in Torah.

**8**	**the chief of Saul’s shepherds.** the head of the tribunal.



**9**	**ואין יש פה.** And if there is here.

**9**	**urgent.** hurried.



**10**	**wrapped.** (Heb (לוטה wrapped, and so: “and be wrapped (וילט) his face in his mantle” (I Kings 19:13).

**10**	**behind the ephod.** (after the ephod) after he had consulted the Urim and Tummim for him. And so did Jonathan render: after he asked the ephod for him.



**12**	**Is this not David the king of the land?.** They said to Achish, There was a stipulation between us, (supra 17:9) “If he is able to fight with me and kill me, we shall be slaves to you.” You have nothing to do but get off your throne and let him become king of this land.



**14**	**his speech.** his speech.

**14**	**and he feigned insanity.** and he feigned insanity.

**14**	**and he scribbled.** he wrote.

**14**	**his saliva.** (Heb. ‘riro’) his saliva. Anything which drips is called ‘rir,’ and so: “in saliva (b’rir) of health” (Job 6:7), and so: “whether his flesh run (rar)” (Lev. 15:3).



**16**	**Will this one come into my house?.** (הזה)This is a question form. It is therefore punctuated by a “hataf patah” (under the ‘heh’), and (the ‘zain’) is not punctuated by a ‘dagesh.’
