**1**	**Request rain from the Lord.** if you need it.

**1**	**rain-clouds.** Clouds that bring rain

**1**	**for each person, grass in the field.** For one man and for one grass that requires rain.



**2**	**For the teraphim, etc., and the soothsayers envisioned lies, and dreamers.** who misled your forefathers, saying, “I dreamt.” They would encourage [your forefathers] to rebel against the Holy One, blessed be He. They spoke futility, and they spoke vanity, saying “peace” - but there was no peace.

**2**	**Therefore, they traveled.** I, however, am a true prophet for them..

**2**	**they were humbled.** “to humble yourself before Me.” An expression of humility.



**3**	**My wrath is kindled against the shepherds.** Against the kings of Greece.

**3**	**upon the goats.** Against their princes, and so did Jonathan render it: My wrath was against the kings and upon the rulers I visit. The Greeks were likened to goats in (Dan. 8:21), “And the rough he-goat is the king of Greece.” Whoever wishes to explain the matter [otherwise] (not as referring to the kings of Greece) will say that הָעַתּוּדִים is an expression of princes, as in (Isa. 14:9), “It aroused the giants for you, all the chiefs of the earth.”

**3**	**as His majestic horse in battle.** As the horse whose majesty is recognized in battle.



**4**	**Out of them shall come the cornerstone.** Out of them shall come their kings; out of them shall come their princes, and out of them shall come those who wage their wars.



**5**	**And they shall be like mighty men, treading the mire of the streets in battle.** This verse is inverted. It should be understood: And they shall be in battle, walking and treading people as mighty men tread the mire of the streets.

**5**	**treading.** as in (Ps. 60:14), “And He shall tread down our enemies,” and (Jer. 12:10) “They have trampled My field.”

**5**	**and they shall shame the riders of horses.** The house of Judah shall shame the riders of horses who come to wage war with them.



**6**	**And I will strengthen the house of Judah.** in the war against the Greeks.

**6**	**and the house of Joseph I will save.** in the place where they were exiled - in Halah and in Habor in the days of Sennacherib.

**6**	**And I will cause them to settle.** Like וְהוֹשַּׁבְתִּים, an expression of settling.

**6**	**And they shall be as though I had not forsaken them.** As though I had never forsaken them.



**8**	**I will whistle to them.** in the manner of those who whistle as a sign and a signal for those straying on their way to him.

**8**	**and I will gather them.** at the time of the end in the future.

**8**	**for I have redeemed them.** When I will redeem them.

**8**	**And they shall multiply.** in exile as they multiplied in Egypt.



**9**	**And I will sow them.** first among the peoples, as a person sows a seah to bring in many korim.

**9**	**And they shall live with their children.** אֶת-בְנֵיהֶם



**10**	**and the Lebanon.** This is the Temple.

**10**	**and it shall not suffice for them.** Heb. וְלֹא יִמָּצֵא לָהֶם



**11**	**And trouble shall pass through the sea.** And the trouble shall pass through Tyre, which is situated in the midst of the sea, it is the head of Edom.

**11**	**and He shall strike.** The Holy One, blessed be He, Who is the One Who strikes.

**11**	**the waves of the sea.** to sink Tyre.

**11**	**all the depths of the river.** That is, Egypt.
