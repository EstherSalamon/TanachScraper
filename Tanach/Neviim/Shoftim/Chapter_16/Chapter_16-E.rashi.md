**2**	**To the Gazites, saying (Lit.).** To the people of Gaza it was told.

**2**	**And they were quiet.** They conducted themselves quietly.



**3**	**וַיִּסָּעֵם.** And he uprooted them from their place.



**7**	**bowstrings.** I.e., thin cords.



**9**	**and his strength was not known.** He did not have to exert himself to tear them.



**11**	**ropes.** I.e., thick cords.



**13**	**מַחְלְפוֹת.** Locks. Pelous (פלודיש) in O.F.

**13**	**to the roller.** The wood upon which the weaver spreads out the warp. The name of this wood is ensouple in O.F. The roller (is called) ourdissuire in O.F.



**16**	**וַתְּאַלְצֵהוּ.** And she pressed him.



**18**	**And Delilah saw that he had told her.** The words of truth are discernable.



**19**	**and she called to the man.** To the messenger of the lords.



**21**	**And they brought him down to Gaza.** (This was) the location of his first corruption (above v. 1).

**21**	**and he did grind.** With a mill. This was hard labor. However, our Sages explained that which they explained.



**25**	**between the pillars.** Upon which the building was supported.



**26**	**and let me feel.** Heb. וַהֲמִישֵנִי, Palpare (פלפאר) in O.F. as in, (Gen. 27: 12) “Perhaps my father will feel me” (יְמֻשֵּׁנִי).



**28**	**remember me.** Remember the twenty years that I judged Israel and did not tell one of them “Move this stick for me from one place to another.”

**28**	**the vengeance for one of my two eyes.** And the reward for the second eye reserve for me for the world to come, but here pay me the reward for one of them.



**29**	**וַיִּלְפֹּת.** And he grasped. Embrasser (אינברצא) in O.F. as in, (Ruth 3: 8) “And the man trembled and was grasped” (וַיִּלָּפֵת), he was grasped by the arms of the woman.
