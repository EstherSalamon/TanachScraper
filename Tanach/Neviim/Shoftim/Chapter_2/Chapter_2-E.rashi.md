**1**	**And a messenger of the Lord went up.** We learned in Seder Olam that this was Phinehas.

**1**	**to Bochim.** This is the name of the place (see below v.5).

**1**	**I will take you up.** So I intended initially to take you out of Egypt and so I did, on the condition that you drive out my enemies.



**3**	**And I also said.** Now I said in my anger.

**3**	**pokers.** As pokers in your sides with troops and marauders to plunder and spoil.



**6**	**And Joshua had sent away, etc..** This incident actually occurred earlier, but since the Scripture is coming to tell us (v.11) “And they did that which is evil,” it begins with the following: Before Joshua died and they (Israel) accepted before him to serve God, he sent them each to his inheritance, and they served God all his (Joshua’s) days and the days of the elders, etc., and afterwards, “And the children of Israel did evil, etc.”



**7**	**that outlived Joshua.** Heb. הֶאֱרִיכוּ. Lit., their days were lengthened but their years were not, because they were lax with his eulogy. And so it says (v.9) “Mount גָּעַש,” the mountain quaked (געש) over them to kill them (Sab. 105b).



**9**	**in Timnath-Cheres.** It was called so because they erected a picture (תְּמוּנָה) of the sun (חֶרֶס) on his grave as though to say: How unfortunate, the person who stopped the sun (Josh. 10:12) lies in a grave.



**15**	**Wherever they went out, the hand of the Lord was against them for evil.** Concerning Elimelech, Machlon, and Kilyon it was said. In Seder Olam 12.



**16**	**And the Lord raised up judges.** Constantly in each generation, as they are enumerated in this book, judge after judge.



**18**	**And when the Lord raised.** This is in the present tense: And when He raised for them judges, God would be with the judge, etc.

**18**	**for [the Lord] retracted.** He would retract from the evil. So renders Targum Jonathan.

**18**	**because of their cry.** Because of their cry that arose before Him because of their enemies.



**19**	**they did not diminish, etc..** They did not leave over anything for which reason they were reproached through all that befell them.



**22**	**whether they would keep.** The “heh” (of הֲשׁוֹמְרִים is vocalized הֲ because it is an expression of question and testing (i.e., would they keep, etc.).
