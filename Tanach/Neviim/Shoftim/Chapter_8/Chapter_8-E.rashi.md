**2**	**What have I done now in comparison with you?.** Of what importance is what I did at first, compared with what you subsequently did.

**2**	**Are not the gleanings… better.** Are not the gleanings that came to your hand at the end better than the vintage that I and my family picked in the beginning, considering that in your hands the Lord delivered the kings (i. e., the princes).



**3**	**and what was I able.** to do that would be as outstanding as your deed?



**4**	**he crossed, and the three hundred men.** They were crossing the Jordan after Zebah and Zalmunna who had passed earlier (perhaps even prior to the blockade above 7:24) and laid waste to the land of Reuben, Gad and Menasseh. Therefore, he crossed to take revenge against them also.



**5**	**to the men of Succoth.** Who were Israelites.



**6**	**Is the palm of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand.** That you boast that you saved us from Midian?



**7**	**the briers.** Ronzes in O.F.



**10**	**were in Karkor.** The name of a place.



**11**	**by the way of the tent dwellers.** Through the land of Kedar and Arabia, whose people live in the desert in tents, constantly grazing their flock and not residing in houses.

**11**	**on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah.** Which were Israelite cities. However, he only wanted to go there roundabout, so that they should not be detected, and thereby suddenly surprise them.



**13**	**when the sun was still high.** I.e., when the sun had not yet set, while the sun (חֶרֶס) still appears high (לְמַעֲלָה).



**16**	**וַיּוֹדַע בָּהֶם.** And broke with them.



**18**	**One was as the form of the children of the king.** Heb. אֶחָד כְּתֹאַר בְּנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ. Targum Jonathan renders: One among them appeared like the children of the king. However, we can interpret: As you are, so were they, all of you possessing one appearance (כָּמוֹךָ כְמוֹהֶם אֶחָד); since they appeared like the children of kings (כְּתֹאַר בְּנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ).



**20**	**But the young man did not draw his sword.** To kill them, because he was afraid of them.



**21**	**the crescents.** Of gold, lunettes in O.F.



**26**	**הַשַּׂהֲרוֹנִים וְהַנְּטִיפוֹת.** The crescents and the pendants [after Targum Jonathan].



**27**	**And Gideon made it into an Ephod.** To serve as a reminder of the great victory, by showing how immense their army was that the nose rings of the nobles amounted to all this gold.

**27**	**and all Israel went astray after it.** After Gideon’s death.



**33**	**Baal- berith.** This was its name.



**35**	**And they did not do kindness with the house of Jerubbaal.** They killed his sons, as Scripture states below (9: 5).
