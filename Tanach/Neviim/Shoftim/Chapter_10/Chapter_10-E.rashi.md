**1**	**the sons of Dodo.** Heb. דוֹדוֹ, this was his name.



**4**	**עֲיָרִים.** (Targum Jonathan renders) עוּלִין, i.e., colts.

**4**	**And they had thirty cities.** And they had thirty unwalled cities. It therefore states עֲיָרִים which is an expression of unwalled cities (עֲיָירוֹת).



**6**	**the Baalim, etc..** Seven idols are enumerated here.

**6**	**and they forsook the Lord, and did not serve Him.** Even in conjunction with the idols.



**8**	**that year.** When Jair died.

**8**	**(the children of Israel) that were beyond the Jordan.** I.e., The land of Reuben, Gad, and half of Menasseh who were their neighbors.



**11**	**Did (I) not (save you) from the Egyptians.** Seven deliverances are mentioned here, corresponding to the seven heathen gods that they served.



**12**	**and Maon.** This is the name of a nation.



**16**	**and His soul was grieved by the toil.** As if one could say, He did not have the expanse to endure and contain the anguish caused Him by the toil (or suffering) Of Israel.



**17**	**were gathered.** Heb. וַיִּצְּעֲקוּ. They gathered through announcements.
