**3**	**This time I shall be blameless.** Justly will I harm them.



**4**	**and turned.** The tails, tail to tail. וַיִּפֶן implies he himself turned. וַיַּפֶן implies he made others turn. And likewise is conjugated every word whose verb contains a “heh” (at the end which drops such as גלה רבה פנה) as in, (Ex. 1:20) הָעָם וַיִּרֶב (“And the people multiplied”), (Lamen. 2:5) יְהוּדָה וַיֶרֶב בְּבַת (“And He multiplied in Judah grief”), (II Kings 25:21) וַיִּגֶל יְהוּדָה מֵעַל אַדְמָתוֹ (“And Judah exited from his land”), (II Kings 17:6) וַיֶּגֶל אֶת ישְׂרָאֵל אַשּׁוּרָה (“And he exiled Israel to Ashur”) [as per Shem Ephraim].



**6**	**the son-in-law of the Timnite.** The son-in-law of the Philistine who is in Timnah.



**7**	**Are you accustomed to do like this.** Perhaps you are accustomed to do such; to give the wife of one to another?



**8**	**the riders and the foot soldiers.** (Lit., leg on thigh) (Targum Jonathan renders) “riders and foot soldiers.” A horse rider is not supported by his thigh but rather by his leg, with one foot inserted into the metal (stirrup) which hangs from the saddle.

**8**	**in the cleft .** In the cleft (which is a crevice between the protrusions) of the rock of Etam, and similarly, (Isa. 2:21) וּבִסְעִיפֵי הַסְּלָעִים (“The protrusions of the rocks”), (Isa. 10: 33) מְסָעֵף פֻּארָה (“Will cut down its branches”).

**9**	**in Lehi.** (lit., a jaw) This is the name of a place.



**10**	**Why have you come up against us.** Are we not subservient to you?

**10**	**to bind Samson.** I.e., so that you should bind him and deliver him to us.



**13**	**No, however we will bind you.** I.e., We will not kill you, however, we will bind you.



**14**	**A spirit of the Lord.** A spirit of might emanating from God [quoting Targum Jonathan].

**14**	**his bonds .** His bonds.



**15**	**טְרִיָּה.** Moist. I saw in a book of cures that the moisture which issues from a wound is called טְרִיָּה.



**16**	**חֲמוֹר חֲמוֹרָתָיִם.** With the jawbone of a donkey I piled many piles, i.e., with the jawbone of a donkey I heaped them up [after Targum Jonathan].



**19**	**the socket.** The socket within which the tooth rests, thich is shaped like a mortar (מַכְתֶּשֶׁת)

**19**	**En-hakkore.** The fountain (מַעְיָן) that came about through the prayers of the one who called (הַקּוֹרֵא) to God.
