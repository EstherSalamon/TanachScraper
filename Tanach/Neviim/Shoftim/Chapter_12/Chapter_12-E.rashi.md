**1**	**and passed to the north.** They crossed the Jordan (eastward) and went up north to Gilead.



**4**	**because the lowest of Ephraim said, (Of what importance are) you Gileadites.** The lowly of Ephraim belittled Gilead by saying to them, “Of what importance are you Gilead among Ephraim and Menasseh.” So renders Targum Jonathan.



**5**	**And the Gileadites seized the fords….** They established guards at the fords.



**6**	**Say now 'Shibboleth’.** (say) this stream (שִׁבֹּלֶת) of the river will I cross.

**6**	**and he was not prepared to pronounce it properly.** They stumbled in their speech.



**8**	**Ibzan.** This was Boaz (who came from Beth-lehem).
