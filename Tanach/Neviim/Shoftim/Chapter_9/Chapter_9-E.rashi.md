**4**	**and hasty.** פוֹחֲזִים. Hasty in their actions, to do without meditation.



**6**	**by the plain of the stones.** Heb. אֵלוֹן מֻצָּב. Targum Jonathan renders מֵישַׁר קָמָתָא, i.e., a plain (אֵלוֹן) containing monuments (מַצֵּבוֹת).



**8**	**the trees went forth.** This is allegorical.

**8**	**to the olive tree.** Referring to Othniel the son of Kenaz from the tribe of Judah, which is called an olive tree, as it says, (Jer. 11:16) “Like a fresh olive tree, beautiful in form, etc.” (this refers to the cities of Judah mentioned in v. 13 ibid).



**9**	**דִּשְׁנִי.** This is an expression of “fatness.”



**10**	**to the fig tree.** Deborah.



**11**	**my sweetness.** (Referring to) the bee’s honey.



**12**	**to the vine.** Referring to Gideon who descended from Joseph of whom it is said, (Gen. 49:22) “A fruitful son is Joseph; blessed as a vine standing by the water” (as per Onkelos).



**13**	**which causes God… to rejoice.** For the Levites do not recite the sacrificial song of praise but during the libations of wine.



**14**	**To the thorn.** Heb. הָאָטָד, epine in O.F.



**15**	**If in truth.** I.e., because I am fit to rule.



**22**	**And Abimelech ruled.** Forcefully and arrogantly he held his position.



**23**	**an evil spirit.** A spirit of hatred, (טלנט), talent in O.F.



**26**	**Gaal the son of Ebed.** He was from another nation.



**27**	**וַיַּעֲשוּ הִלּולִים.** And they made dances [quoting Targum Jonathan].



**28**	**Who is Abimelech and who is Shechem.** Who is Abimelech that he should rule over Shechem, and who is Shechem to be servants to Abimelech? Was not Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal (הֲלֹא בֶן יַרֻבַּעַל) from Ophrah of the Abiezrites?

**28**	**and Zebul (is he not) his officer.** As for the officer of the city whose name is Zebul, he is (but) an officer of Abimelech; since his master has no right to rule here, neither do his appointees.

**28**	**Serve the men of Hamor.** If you are coming to acquire for yourselves leaders, come and serve the men of Hamor, who was a previous lord over the land.

**28**	**but why should we serve him.** I.e., Abimelech.



**29**	**And he said to Abimelech.** (Indirectly;) and Gaal spoke before Zebul to inform Abimelech (as subsequently evident).

**29**	**Increase your army, and come out.** From the place where you are, and let us see your greatness.



**31**	**in secret.** Heb. בְּתָרְמָה. As in (Gen. 27:35) “בְּמִרְמָה,” and (Jer. 8: 5) “בּתַרְמִית,” i.e., quietly and secretly.



**34**	**in four companies.** Lit. in four heads.



**37**	**from the navel of the earth.** (Targum Jonathan renders) מִן תּוּקְפָא דְאַרְעָא (i. e., from the strength of the land) from its highest mountain. Combine 2 Heb. words

**37**	**plain Meonenim.** the plain of Meonenim.



**42**	**that the people went out to the field.** The people of Shechem.

**42**	**and they told.** The informers told Abimelech that the citizens went out to the field to attend their needs.



**44**	**in the entrance of the gate of the city.** So that no one could enter.



**46**	**forest.** צְרִיחַ in Heb. A forest in which the trees and branches are bent over all around the forest; plissé in O. F.

**46**	**Beth-El-Brith.** That forest belonged to Baal Brith (above v. 4), the name of their idol. Zalmon (v. 48) also had a forest. Some interpret צְרִיחַ as voutes in O.F., which is made in the ground.



**48**	**שוֹכַת עֵצִים.** A bough from a tree.



**53**	**פֶּלַח רֶכֶב.** A piece of the upper millstone which is called a רֶכֶב.

**53**	**and crushed.** וְתָרִץ same as וּתְרוֹצֵץ (a more familiar form).



**54**	**lest they say of me.** Lit. lest they say to me.
