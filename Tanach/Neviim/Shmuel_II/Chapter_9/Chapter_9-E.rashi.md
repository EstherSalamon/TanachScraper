**9**	**have I given to your master’s son.** The king has the right to transfer inheritance as it is stated in I Sam. 8:14 in the laws pertaining to the monarchy: “your fields and your vineyards he will take and give to his servants.”



**11**	**so shall your servant do: as for Mephibosheth, he shall eat at my table.** Zibah said: “so shall your servant do,” and David responded: “as for Mephibosheth, he shall eat at my table.”



**12**	**the inhabitants of Zibah’s house.** his sons and servants.
