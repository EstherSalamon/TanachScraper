**4**	**wayfarer.** The evil inclination is first compared to a traveler who stops by on his way, then to a guest who lodges, and finally to a man, who is master of the household.



**5**	**liable to death.** One who robs the poor is as if he takes his life, for it is written: “the life of the owners he [the thief] takes” (Prov. 1:19).



**6**	**he shall repay fourfold.** This [actually] happened to him, that he was smitten through four children; the child (born to Bathsheba See v. 18.), Amnon (below 13:19), Tamar (below 13:14), and Absalom (below 18:15).



**8**	**and your master’s wives.** Michal the daughter of Saul.

**8**	**I add.** I would add for you.



**14**	**the enemies of the Lord.** This is a euphemism for ‘the Above.’ Now Jonathan translates it as: Because you have opened the mouths of the enemies of God’s nation.



**15**	**became mortally ill.** Heb. ויאנש, an expression of sickness.



**16**	**and he came in and slept lying on the ground.** and he came into the house and slept the night lying on the ground.



**17**	**he did not eat.** Heb. ברה. A synonym for eating similar to: “that I may eat from her hand” (Heb. ואברה מידה, below 13:6).



**25**	**for the Lord’s sake.** who loved him.



**26**	**the royal city.** there were two wall supports in it, an outer and an inner one. The outer one was the royal city, and the inner one was for fortification and support.



**30**	**and the crown of Malkam.** the name of the abomination of the sons of Ammon was Malkam from [the name] Molech.

**30**	**and it was set on David’s head.** Ittai the Gittite renounced it.



**31**	**saws, and under harrows of iron.** It is grooved with numerous indentations and is called lime [in French]. They are types of tortures.

**31**	**saws.** A knife indented with numerous consecutive nicks.

**31**	**harrows.** A threshing sledge. It is grooved with numerous indentations and is called lime [in French].

**31**	**brick kiln.** in the mud of the streets. And so did I translate it: and they dragged them in the streets.
