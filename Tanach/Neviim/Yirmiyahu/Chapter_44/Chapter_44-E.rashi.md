**10**	**They were not crushed.** Their hearts were not crushed because of their evil.



**14**	**only fugitives.** Jeremiah and Baruch returned from there, for Nebuchadnezzar exiled them when the land of Egypt was delivered into his hand in the twenty-seventh year of his reign (Ezek. 29:17). So we learned in Seder Olam (ch. 26).



**19**	**without our husbands.** Without their knowledge.

**19**	**cakes in her image.** Heb. כונים להעצבה, molds in the shape of idols (idols in the shape of a young lady as above ch. 22:28).



**22**	**And He could no longer bear.** Heb. ולא יוּכַל, like ולא יָכוֹל, and He could not.



**30**	**Pharaoh Hophra.** Heb. פרעה חפרע, rendered by Jonathan as פרעֹה תבירה, the broken Pharaoh, and this ‘heth’ serves instead of a ‘heh.’
