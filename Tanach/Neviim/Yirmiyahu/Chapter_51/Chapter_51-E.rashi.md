**1**	**Leb-kamai.** Casdim (Chaldeans) according to cypher of ‘At-bash’ (א-תב ש).

**1**	**the desire of a destroyer.** The desire of the king of Persia and Media, who is a destroyer.

**1**	**the desire.** Heb. רוּחַ (talent in O.F.).



**2**	**and they shall scatter her.** Heb. וְזֵרוּהָ.

**2**	**and empty her land.** Heb. וִיבֹקְקוּ.



**3**	**To… the archer shall bend.** This is an elliptical verse. [The meaning is:] To wherever I say, there the archer shall bend his bow.

**3**	**and he shall not be healed with his coat of mail.** Heb. יִתְעַל וְאַל.



**5**	**For Israel is not widowed.** He has not forgotten him to avenge him.

**5**	**for their land.** of the Chaldeans.



**6**	**Flee.** Israel, from within Babylon.



**7**	**a golden cup.** Until now, Babylon was [a golden cup] in the Lord’s hand with which to intoxicate the whole earth with the wine of wrath.

**7**	**therefore, nations are mad.** They have become drunk and have become mad.



**9**	**her judgment has touched the sky.** Their retribution has reached high up to the sky.



**10**	**“The Lord has brought forth our merits”.** We have been remembered by Him favorably, and the merits of our forefathers have come before Him.



**11**	**Polish.** Heb. הברו, an expression of cleaning.

**11**	**the quivers.** Heb. השלטים, the quivers into which the arrows are placed.



**12**	**for the Lord both planned.** already many days.

**12**	**and did.** His plan. It is customary for Scripture to speak with the word גַם twice, one after the other, and following the latter, comes the former.



**13**	**your end has come.** Heb. קצך, your end.

**13**	**the measure of your violence.** Heb. אַמַת בִּצְעֵךְ, the measure of your violence. Another explanation: אַמַת בִּצְעֵךְ means: the measure of your end. Cf. “When… completes (יִבַצַע)” (Isa. 10:12).



**14**	**by Himself.** (en soi meme in French.)

**14**	**indeed.** Heb. כי אם, an expression of ‘in truth.’

**14**	**I have… filled you with men.** I will fill you with camps of peoples, as many as locusts.

**14**	**and they shall raise a battle-cry against you.** And they shall raise their voice against you.



**17**	**Every man is brutish.** [i.e.,] whoever makes a graven image for a god.

**17**	**molten images.** [As translated], an expression [denoting] molten images.



**18**	**at the time of their visitation.** When the Holy One, blessed be He, visits retribution upon them and upon their worshippers.



**19**	**and the tribe of His inheritance.** Jacob mentioned above at the beginning of the verse.



**20**	**for Me a shatterer.** Heb. מפץ. Until now, I have appointed you as a destroyer and a shatterer of the nations upon whom I decreed exile.



**24**	**And I will recompense Babylon.** After all this, when I complete My vengeance upon the nations through him.



**27**	**prepare.** Heb. קַדְשוֹ.

**27**	**a marshal.** Heb. טפסר, an expression of an army [or an expression of a lord].

**27**	**bristling.** Heb. סמר. (Herisse in French), covered with spikes. Cf. “It made the hair of my flesh stand up like spikes (תְּסַּמֵר)” (Job 4:15).



**29**	**and was in pain.** Heb. ותחל, an expression of חַלְחָלָה pain.



**30**	**failed.** Heb. נשתה, [lit.] jumped. Compare “the hip sinew (הנשה)” (Gen. 33:32), so called because it jumped [was dislocated] from its place.



**33**	**is like a granary at the time it is threshed.** Behold, now it is full, like a granary at the time they have threshed its grain, when it is full of wheat.

**33**	**A little more time.** and she shall he emptied and shall resemble a granary at the time of the beginning of the harvest, when it is empty.



**35**	**the plundering done me and my food.** The cry of violence done me and my food, for they have eaten my food.



**38**	**they growl.** Heb. נערו, an expression of a braying (נוֹעֵר) donkey.



**39**	**in their heat, I will place their wine feasts.** I will intend that their wine feasts shall be in the hot season so that they become drunk.

**39**	**in order that they become joyful and sleep.** Heb. וישנו, and sleep soundly.

**39**	**a perpetual sleep.** Heb. שנתעולם, a perpetual sleep, and so it happened to them; out of their wine feast and drunkenness they were destroyed and slain, and the city was captured, as it is written in Daniel (5:2): “Belshazzar said while he tasted the wine, etc.;” (5:4), “They drank wine, etc.;” (5:30), “On that night, Belshazzar, the king of the Chaldeans, was slain;” (6:1), “And Darius the Mede received the kingship.”



**40**	**like lambs.** Heb. כברים, lambs (and Jonathan renders:) like bulls.



**41**	**Sheshach.** Babylon in the ‘At- bash’ (א-תב ש), cipher.



**42**	**The sea has ascended upon Babylon.** A huge army, like the waters of [the sea].



**44**	**on Bel.** On the deity of Babylon.

**44**	**what he has swallowed.** That all the inhabitants of the land were humbled under him.

**44**	**shall no longer stream.** Heb. יהרו, and shall not gather. Cf. “And all the nations shall stream (ונהרוּ) to it” (Isa. 2:2). Like the waters of the river, all of which go to the sea.



**46**	**because of the rumor that is heard in the land.** This rumor concerning Belshazzar that he was slain.

**46**	**for a rumor shall come one year.** In that very year there will come a rumor that Babylon has been destroyed by itself from heaven as it is stated: “And Babylon, the beauty of the kingdoms the glory of the pride of the Chaldee, shall be like God’s overturning of Sodom and Gemorrah” (Isa. 13:19).

**46**	**and after it in a year.** [In] the second [year].

**46**	**a rumor.** that is good for you [viz.] “So said Cyrus, the king of Persia…, Whoever among you of all His people…” (Ezra 1:2f).

**46**	**and one ruler upon another ruler.** Darius the Mede reigned one year, and in the second year Cyrus the Persian.



**49**	**As Babylon.** was [a cause] for the slain of Israel to fall therein.

**49**	**so in Babylon.** shall fall the slain of all her land.



**50**	**Fugitives from the sword.** Israel, who was exiled into her midst, who escaped the sword of Nebuzaradan.



**51**	**for we have heard reproach.** For the Chaldeans were saying and reproaching us, saying, “The hand of our god is exalted for we have destroyed His house.”



**52**	**the mortally wounded shall groan.** Heb. יאנק. The mortally wounded shall cry. When they kill a person with an arrow or with a spear, and he groans when they remove the spear, that is אנקה.



**53**	**and should she fortify.** Heb. תבצר, an expression of a strong fortress.

**53**	**from Me shall spoilers come upon her.** From Me, from the heavens that are higher than she, shall the spoilers come upon her.



**55**	**the stirring of their voice was uttered.** The sound of their cry is heard.



**58**	**shall be overthrown.** Heb. תתערער, an expression of digging. An expression like “Dig under it, dig under it (עָרוּ), until its foundation” (Psalms 137:7).

**58**	**for much futility.** For much futility which it will bring upon them.



**59**	**Prince of tribute.** [In Jonathan’s Aramaic translation,] שר תקרובתא. Through him, people who brought tribute would gain an audience with the king.
