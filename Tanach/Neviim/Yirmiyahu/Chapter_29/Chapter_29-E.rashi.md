**2**	**and the queen mother.** The mother of the king.



**8**	**mislead.** An expression of enticing. Cf: “The snake misled me (הֹשִיאַנִי)” (Gen. 3:13).



**12**	**And you shall call Me and go.** In My ways. This is an ellipsis.



**16**	**concerning the king.** Zedekiah, who has not been exiled.



**17**	**as the loathsome figs.** Heb. הֹשֹּעָרִים, an expression of, ֹשַעֲרוּרִיָּה, “a disgraceful thing” (Hos. 6:10).



**18**	**and for a reproach.** That they will reproach them, and so is every expression of ירפה in Scripture.



**22**	**whom… burnt.** an expression of “roasted with fire” (Lev. 2:14).



**23**	**and they committed adultery with the wives of their neighbors.** As is explained in Sanhedrin (93a), “So said the Holy One, blessed be He, ‘Lie with my friend.’”

**23**	**and a witness.** an expression of testimony (עֵדוּת).



**24**	**the Nehelamite.** A place name.



**26**	**made you a priest.** He wrote this to Zephaniah.

**26**	**officers.** appointees.

**26**	**the prison.** כִּפָה

**26**	**the stocks.** a tree trunk (ceps in French).



**28**	**Forasmuch as.** For you should rebuke him since he sent to us to Babylon saying, “Your exile there is long.”

**28**	**Forasmuch as.** like עַל-אֹשֶר because of which.
