**2**	**and he shall have his life as prey.** His life shall be saved, as if had “plundered” his soul; just as a man profits from plundering booty, so this person will profit [by saving] his life.



**4**	**since he weakens.** (a lacher in O.F.), an expression of weakness.



**5**	**cannot.** Heb. יוּכַל, like יָכוֹל, since he is weakening the hands of, etc.



**6**	**the pit of Malchiah.** lit. the pit Malchiah.



**7**	**Ebed-melech.** Baruch the son of Neriah.

**7**	**the Cushite.** Zedekiah. Just as a Cushite is different in his skin, so was Zedekiah different in his righteousness, from all his generation (Moed Katan 16b). And there is a Midrash that expounds “the Cushite,” as referring to Baruch (Sifrei ibid.).

**7**	**an officer.** Heb. סריס, a great man.



**9**	**He would have died in his place, etc..** If they had left him in his place, he would have died by himself in the famine, but they have done harm to bring his death closer.



**10**	**thirty men.** Since they were weakened by the famine, all of them were required.



**11**	**worn clouts.** Heb. בלוי סחבות, worn out garments, that are dragged.



**12**	**and the rags.** Heb. והמלחים, an expression of decay. Cf. “shall vanish (נמלחוּ) like smoke” (Isa. 51:6).

**12**	**under your armpits.** So that the ropes should not harm you.

**12**	**armpits.** Heb. אצילות, aisseles in French.



**14**	**the third entrance.** I do not know where it is. Perhaps it is in the Israelites’ Court. The appellation “third” is to say that the hel [the wall surrounding the Temple Court], and the Women’s Court are before it.



**19**	**“I fear”.** Heb. דֹאֵג.

**19**	**lest they deliver me.** [Lest] the Chaldeans [deliver me] into the hands of the Jews, saying, Your king shall be your slave since you have made peace with us first.

**19**	**and mock me.** [i.e.,] the Jews [will mock me] (engregier in O.F.), complain.



**22**	**“Your cohorts”.** [lit. the men of your peace,] the false prophets, who told you that you would not be caught.

**22**	**into the mire.** Heb. בבץ, mire, an expression as in (Job 8:11): “Can the rush shoot up without mire (בצה)?”



**23**	**and this city you shall burn with fire.** It is as though you are burning it with your hands for you are causing it.
