**2**	**and raise a standard.** (Perche in French.) That is a signal for gathering.

**2**	**Bel… Merodach.** The names of the Babylonian deities.



**3**	**a nation… from the north.** [viz.] Persia and Media.



**5**	**are directed hitherward.** Toward this place, Jerusalem, they shall direct their faces when Babylon will be destroyed and they will leave it with the sanction of Cyrus, king of Persia.



**6**	**their shepherds caused them to stray.** Their kings caused them to stray away from Me.

**6**	**mountains… led them astray.** To the mountains, to the idol worship that was on the mountains. שׁוֹבְבוּם (esvayent in O.F.), led astray, their kings led them astray. And Jonathan rendered: their governors plundered them.



**7**	**and the hope of their forefathers.** Jonathan renders: וְסִבּוּר אֲבָהָתְהוֹן, and the hope of their forefathers.



**8**	**Wander out of Babylon.** Accordingly, go out of there.

**8**	**like the he goats before the flocks.** It is customary for the he-goats to go before the flock, at the head of the she-goats.



**9**	**and they shall set themselves in array against her.** They shall array themselves in battle against her.



**11**	**O spoilers of My heritage.** Heb. שֹּׁסֵי.

**11**	**as you become fat.** Heb. תפושו, an expression of fat.

**11**	**a threshing heifer.** Heb. דשא, a heifer that threshes the grain and eats constantly.



**12**	**Your mother.** Your nation.

**12**	**the end of the nations.** Although your beginning was great, your end will be a desert.



**14**	**shoot at her.** Heb. ידו, cast stones at her. Cf. “And they cast (וידוּ) stones at me” (Lam. 3:53).



**15**	**she gave her hand.** like a person who has no strength and stretches forth his hand to whomever he sees, to beg mercy and aid. Cf. "We gave our hand to Egypt" (Lam. 5:6).

**15**	**her foundations have fallen.** Heb. אשיותיה. The strength of her foundations. Cf. “And the foundations (וְאֻשַּׁיָא) shall be fastened” (Ezra 4:12). And in the language of the Mishnah: Those who dig for foundations (לְאֻשִׁין) [Baba Kamma 50a]. Cf. “And strengthen yourselves (והתאוששוּ)” (Isa. 46:8), equivalent to והתחזקוּ, and strengthen yourselves.

**15**	**as she did.** to the city of the Lord.



**16**	**Cut off a sower.** Destroy a king from Babylon and one who grasps a sword in time of slaughter.



**17**	**First… devoured him.** For he exiled the Ten Tribes.

**17**	**broke his bones.** He broke his bones, for he finished the leftovers, Judah and Benjamin. עִצְמוֹ (desossa in French), [broke the bones].



**19**	**and he shall pasture in the Carmel.** An expression of pasture.



**21**	**the rebellious land.** Heb. מרתים, which rebelled against Me and provoked Me.

**21**	**Pekod.** The name of a province. Cf. “Pekod and Shoa and Koa” (Ezekiel 23:23). Jonathan renders [them as] names of provinces.

**21**	**and do according to all that I have commanded you.** You, Cyrus, through Isaiah the prophet.



**23**	**the hammer of the whole earth.** which would shatter and crush everyone.



**24**	**I laid a snare for you.** Heb. יקשתי, an expression of a snare (מוֹקֵש).



**26**	**Come against her from her border.** From the end of the city, commence upon her, that she not know, and she will be taken suddenly.

**26**	**open her gates.** Jonathan renders: אִבּוּלָהָא, her gates, but it has no similar instance [in Scripture]. Menahem (Machbereth p. 14) classifies it as an expression of a manger.

**26**	**thresh her.** Heb. סלוה.

**26**	**stacks.** Granaries.



**27**	**all her bulls.** Her princes. So is the custom of the prophets to compare the princes to mighty bulls and to he-goats.



**29**	**Gather archers against Babylon.** Heb. השמיעו, an expression similar to: “And Saul called the people together (וַיְשַׁמַע)” (I Sam. 15:4). Call out that archers assemble against Babylon. Cf. “His archers (רַבָּיו) surround me” (Job 16:13).



**30**	**Therefore.** An expression of an oath.



**34**	**in order to give rest to the earth.** When Babylon falls, there will be rest and peace for all.



**36**	**upon the imposters.** Heb. הבדים, to her diviners.

**36**	**and they shall become foolish.** with their divining. Similar to this is: “Who frustrates the signs of imposters” (Isa. 44:25), and so did Jonathan render. They are called בּדים because they fabricate (בּוֹדִים) and say false things.



**37**	**the allies.** Heb. הערב. Jonathan renders: סוּמְכותא (garantie in French).



**38**	**and they are mad upon things of horror.** Heb. יתהללו, they shall become mad. Another interpretation: (Pour vanter in French) in order to boast. And so did Jonathan render: משתבּחין, they boast, [i.e.,] with the gods of the giants that they had.



**39**	**martens… with cats.** Jonathan [renders]: And martens shall meet cats. [These are] species of animals found in ruins.



**44**	**Behold, as a lion.** that ascends from the heights of the Jordan, so will the spoiler come to the strong dwelling. So did Jonathan paraphrase: Behold a king with his camp shall ascend upon them like a lion that ascends from the height of the Jordan. We learn that the height of the Jordan is a place of lions. Also, in this manner Jonathan renders: “How will you do in the heights of the Jordan” (12:5): Against the wild beasts of the heights of the Jordan.

**44**	**when I bring the moment.** Heb. ארגיעה, when I bring the time.

**44**	**over her.** Heb. מעליה.

**44**	**and who will appoint Me a time.** for war?



**45**	**the young of the flock.** Persia, the youngest of the sons of Japheth.
