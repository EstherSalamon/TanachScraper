**3 [English]**	**wait for it for…; it shall not delay.** at all after the seventy years. This clause can also be explained to mean: though he tarry, meaning the prophet Jeremiah. Jonathan renders: the prophecy is written and explained in the Book of the Torah. He translated כְּתֹב as כָּתוּב. It has already been alluded to in the Torah (Lev. 26:34): “Then shall the land placate [God concerning] its Sabbaths.” Israel sinned by violating seventy Sabbatical Years, in which they did not release the land, and, corresponding to them, they were exiled therefrom for seventy years. And so it is stated in II Chronicles (36:21): “Until the land placated its Sabbaths; all the days it lay waste, it rested to complete seventy years.” And so you find in Ezekiel (4:5): “And I have given you the years of their iniquity according to the number of days, three hundred and ninety days… (verse 6) I gave you a day for a year.” And Scripture says (verse 4): “And you lie on your left side, etc.” You are found saying that Israel provoked God for three hundred and ninety years until the Ten Tribes were exiled, and the tribe of Judah sinned for forty years, and from the time the Ten Tribes were exiled until the destruction of Jerusalem are the twenty-two years of Manasseh. The rest of Manasseh’s years were spent in repentance, for it is stated concerning him (II Kings 21:2f.) “And he did what was evil, etc.,… as Ahab… had made.” Therefore we count Manasseh’s evil years according to the number of Ahab’s years, and he reigned for twenty-two years. With two years of his son Amon’s reign, and eleven of Jehoiakim’s, and eleven of Zedekiah’s, the evil years total forty-six, and this prophecy was said to Ezekiel in the fifth year of Zedekiah. In any case, we find the time of their sinning four hundred and thirty years, for after this prophecy they tarried there six years. [I.e., the two tribes of Judah and Benjamin remained in the land for six more years] In four hundred years, there are eight Jubilee Years, and in every Jubilee cycle there are seven Release Years, making a total of fifty-six Release Years and eight years consecrated as Jubilee Years making all together sixty-four. In thirty-six years there are five Release Years, totaling sixty-nine consecrated years, and this final Jubilee year is also counted in the number, since it was not completed because of their iniquity. And here, this is what the Holy One, blessed be He, said to Habakkuk: A vision is already written in the Torah, but it is sealed. You write and explain on the tablets, for a vision of this appointed time will yet be revealed.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**וחמס ארץ.** ארץ ישראל:<br>
**17 [English]**	**and the violence of the land.** The land of Israel.

**17 [Hebrew]**	**קריה.** ירושלים:<br>
**17 [English]**	**a city.** Jerusalem.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**מה הועיל.** לבבל פסל:<br>
**18 [English]**	**What did a graven image avail.** Babylon?

**18 [Hebrew]**	**כי בטח.** האדם היוצר ויוצרו של אלוהו זה:<br>
**18 [English]**	**that the maker of his work trusted.** The man who formed it, who is the maker of this god of his.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**עליו.** על יצירתו זאת הוא בוטח שיעזור לו אשר בא לעשות אלילים אלמים:<br>
**18 [English]**	**in it.** He trusts in this creation of his, that it assists him, so he comes to make dumb idols.



**19 [Hebrew]**	**הוא יורה.** בתמיה:<br>
**19 [English]**	**Shall it teach?.** This is a question.



**20 [Hebrew]**	**וה' בהיכל קדשו.** מוכן ליפרע:<br>
**20 [English]**	**But the Lord is in His Holy Temple.** ready to exact retribution.

**20 [Hebrew]**	**הס מפניו כל הארץ.** הס לשון שיתוק ודמימת חורבן:<br>
**20 [English]**	**Silence the whole earth before Him.** הַס is an expression of silencing and the silence of destruction.
