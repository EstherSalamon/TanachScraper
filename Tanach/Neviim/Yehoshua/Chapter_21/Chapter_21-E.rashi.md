**5**	**And the rest of the children of Kohath.** They are the children of Moses, and the children of Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel.

**5**	**from the families of the tribe of Ephraim.** These cities fell to them from the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Ephraim, for they received cities in the inheritance of each tribe, as it is stated: “From the many you shall take many, and from the few you shall take few; each tribe according to its inheritance which they inherit, shall give of its cities to the Levites.”



**9**	**these cities that will be mentioned by name.** Since above he did not mention the names of the cities which they gave them, and below he enumerates them, he, therefore, states: These cities that will be mentioned by name.
