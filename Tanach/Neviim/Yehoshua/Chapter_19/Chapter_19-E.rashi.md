**1**	**And the second lot came out.** i.e., second to that of Benjamin, for Benjamin’s lot was the first of the seven tribes whose messengers Joshua charged with: “And they shall divide it into seven parts,” for Judah and Joseph had already received their parts, as it is stated there: “Judah shall remain in his border on the south.” From here on, he proceeds to enumerate seven lots.



**9**	**too much for them.** lit., more than they, more than was fit for them.



**12**	**Chisloth-tabor.** I believe that this is an expression of “flanks” (כְּסִלִים), flancs in French [as in Lev. III:4], not at its peak, nor at its base, but on its slope near the middle, near the rear, but on the front side, as the flanks are situated in an animal. [Thus, we render: The Flanks of Mt. Tabor.] The place which the Scripture refers to as Aznoth-tabor, is near its peak, in the place of the ears (אָזְנַיִם).



**13**	**on the east of Gath-hepher.** To the east of Gath-hepher, the city of Jonah the son of Amittai.

**13**	**to 'Ittah Kazzin.** The name of the city was Ittah kazzin [not 'Eth kazzin].

**13**	**and from these circled to Neah.** lit., which circles to Neah, i.e., from there the border circled to Neah. Thus Jonathan rendered: And it went out to Rimmon, and from there circled to Neah.



**29**	**to the fortress city of the rock.** fortified cities, strong cities.

**29**	**rock.** Heb. צֹר, an expression of צוּר, a rock.

**29**	**from the lot of Achzib.** lit from the rope of Achzib, from the lot of Chezib.



**35**	**And the fortress cities.** which belonged to Naphtali were: Ziddim, Zer, etc.



**41**	**Zorah, and Eshtaol.** They belonged to Judah, and the lot of the children of Dan fell adjacent to them.



**47**	**And the border of the children of Dan went out from them.** The children of Dan took part [of their inheritance] here, and they also received a lot elsewhere far from their border, with the other tribes intervening between them.

**47**	**and fought against Leshem.** at a later time, in the days of Othniel the son of Kenaz and in the days of Micah’s graven image, as is stated in the book of Judges.

**47**	**Leshem.** identical with Laish mentioned in the Book of Judges.



**51**	**divided for an inheritance.** lit., caused to inherit.
