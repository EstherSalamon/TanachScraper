**1 [Hebrew]**	**לְמָכִיר בְּכוֹר מְנַשֶּׁה.** לְפִיכָךְ נָטַל תְּחִלָּה בִּימֵי משֶׁה בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, וַיְהִי לוֹ הַגִּלְעָד וְהַבָּשָׁן.<br>
**1 [English]**	**for Machir the firstborn of Manasseh.** Therefore, he took first in Moses’ days on the other side of the Jordan, and had Gilead and Bashan.



**5 [Hebrew]**	**וַיִּפְּלוּ חֶבְלֵי מְנַשֶּׁה עֲשָׂרָה.** שִׁשָּׁה לְשִׁשָּׁה בָּתֵּי אָבוֹת הַמְּנוּיִים לְמַעְלָה, וְאַרְבַּע לִבְנוֹת צְלָפְחָד, לֹא שֶׁתְּהֵא כָּל אַחַת בֵּית אָב לְעַצְמָהּ אֶלָּא שֶׁנָּטְלוּ אַרְבָּעָה חֲלָקִים, חֵלֶק שֶׁל אֲבִיהֶם שֶׁהָיָה מִיּוֹצְאֵי מִצְרַיִם, וּלְפִי מִנְיַן הַיּוֹצְאִים מִמִּצְרַיִם נִתְחַלְּקָה הָאָרֶץ וְחִלְּקוּ עִם אֶחָיו בְּנִכְסֵי חֵפֶר אָבִיו, שֶׁהָיָה גַּם הוּא מִיּוֹצְאֵי מִצְרַיִם וְשֶׁהָיָה בְּכוֹר וְנָטַל שְׁנֵי חֲלָקִים, וְעוֹד הָיָה לָהֶם חֵלֶק אֲחִי אֲבִיהֶם שֶׁמֵּת בַּמִּדְבָּר בְּלֹא בָּנִים, וְנָטְלוּ נַחֲלָה בְּחֶלְקוֹ, כָּךְ אָמְרוּ רַבּוֹתֵינוּ בְּבָּבָא בַּתְרָא (קיח א), וְלֹא הוּזְקַק הַכָּתוּב לְהַשְׁמִיעֵנוּ מִנְיַן חֶלְקֵי הַבָּנוֹת, אֶלָּא לְלַמֵּד שֶׁנָּטְלוּ חֵלֶק בְּכוֹרָה וּלְהוֹדִיעַ שֶׁאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל חֵלֶק יְרֻשָּׁה לָהֶם מֻחְזֶקֶת מֵאֲבוֹתֵיהֶם, שֶׁאִילּוּלֵי כֵּן אֵין הַבְּכוֹר נוֹטֵל בָּרָאוּי כְּבַמּוּחְזָק.<br>
**5 [English]**	**And there fell ten portions to Manasseh.** i.e., six for the six fathers’ houses enumerated above, and four for the daughters of Zelophehad, not that each one should be considered an individual father’s house, [for they were five - Kara] but they received four shares: (1) The share of their father who was one of those who left Egypt, and according to the number of those who left Egypt the land was apportioned; (2) and his share with his brothers in the property of Hepher, who was also one of those who left Egypt; and (3) he was the firstborn, and received two portions; and (4) in addition, they had the share of their father’s brother, who died in the wilderness without children, and they received inheritance in his share. Our Rabbis stated this in Baba Bathra. It was not necessary for the scripture to relate to us the number of the daughters’ shares, except to teach us that they received the firstborn share, and to inform us that Eretz Israel was an inherited share which belonged to them from the time of their forefathers, for otherwise, the firstborn does not receive an extra share of that which is fit to come into the father’s possession, as he does in that which the father had already acquired.



**6 [Hebrew]**	**וְאֶרֶץ הַגִּלְעָד.** שֶׁבְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן הָיְתָה לִבְנֵי מְנַשֶּׁה הַנּוֹתָרִים.<br>
**6 [English]**	**and the rest… had the land of Gilead.** i.e., the rest of Manasseh’s sons had the land of Gilead which was on the other side of the Jordan.



**8 [Hebrew]**	**אֶרֶץ תַּפּוּחַ.** הַכְּפָרִים וְהַחֲצֵרִים, וְתַפּוּחַ עַצְמָהּ הָעִיר הָיְתָה לִבְנֵי אֶפְרַיִם.<br>
**8 [English]**	**The land of Tappuah.** i.e., the villages and suburbs, but the city of Tappuah itself, belonged to the children of Ephraim.

**8 [Hebrew]**	**אֶל גְּבוּל מְנַשֶּׁה.** עַל מֶצֶר מְנַשֶּׁה לְסוֹף גְּבוּלוֹ.<br>
**8 [English]**	**on the border of Manasseh.** on the boundary of Manasseh, at the end of his border.



**9 [Hebrew]**	**עָרִים הָאֵלֶּה.** מִתַּפּוּחַ עַד נַחַל קָנָה לְאֶפְרַיִם הָיוּ בְּתוֹךְ עָרֵי מְנַשֶּׁה.<br>
**9 [English]**	**These cities.** from Tappuah to the brook of Kanah belonged to Ephraim among the cities of Manasseh.



**10 [Hebrew]**	**נֶגְבָּה לְאֶפְרַיִם.** אֶפְרַיִם נָטַל חֶלְקוֹ מִדָּרוֹם לְצַד הָאָרֶץ שֶׁבֵּין בְּנֵי יְהוּדָה לִשְׁאָר הַשְּׁבָטִים.<br>
**10 [English]**	**Southward it was Ephraim’s.** Ephraim received his share from the south, toward the land between the children of Judah and the rest of the tribes.

**10 [Hebrew]**	**וְצָפוֹנָה לִמְנַשֶּׁה.** מְנַשֶּׁה נָטַל לְצַד צָפוֹן.<br>
**10 [English]**	**and northward it was Manasseh’s.** Manasseh received the land to the north.



**11 [Hebrew]**	**שְׁלשֶׁת הַנָּפֶת.** (תַּרְגּוּם:) תְּלָתָא פְּלָכִין, וּ'שְׁלשֶׁת הַנָּפֶת' הַנִּזְכָּרִים כָּאן שֶׁל דֹּאר וְשֶׁל עֵין דּוֹר הָיוּ, כְּמוֹ שֶׁאָמוּר לְמַעְלָה בְּסֵפֶר זֶה (יב כג): מֶלֶךְ דּוֹר לְנָפַת דּוֹר, וּבְפָסוּק אַחֵר אוֹמֵר (לעיל יא ב): לְנָפֶת דֹּאר מִיָּם, וְכָאן כָּךְ הוּא אוֹמֵר: וְאֶת יוֹשְׁבֵי דֹּאר וּבְנוֹתֶיהָ שְׁלשֶׁת הַנָּפֶת שֶׁהָיוּ לָהּ.<br>
**11 [English]**	**the three regions.** “three provinces” (Jonathan). The three regions mentioned here were of Dor and 'En-dor, as aforementioned in this book: “The king of Dor of the regions of Dor,” and in another verse the Scripture states: “and in the regions of Dor on the west.” And here the Scripture states: “The inhabitants of Dor and her towns,” which were the three regions that belonged to her.



**12 [Hebrew]**	**וַיּוֹאֶל.** וַיֹּאבֶה.<br>
**12 [English]**	**were content.** Heb. וַיּוֹאֶל, was willing.



**14 [Hebrew]**	**וַיְדַבְּרוּ בְּנֵי יוֹסֵף.** שֵׁבֶט מְנַשֶּׁה.<br>
**14 [English]**	**And the children of Joseph spoke.** i.e., The tribe of Manasseh.

**14 [Hebrew]**	**עַד אֲשֶׁר־עַד־כֹּה בֵּרְכַנִי ה'.** עַד אֲשֶׁר רָאִיתָ שֶׁנִּתְרַבָּה מִנְיָנוֹ מִמִּנְיָן רִאשׁוֹן לְמִנְיָן שֵׁנִי עֶשְׂרִים אֶלֶף וַחֲמֵשׁ מֵאוֹת כְּמִנְיַן 'כֹּה' בְּגִימְטַרְיָה, בְּמִנְיָן רִאשׁוֹן אַתָּה מוֹצֵא בִּמְנַשֶּׁה בְּסֵפֶר בְּמִדְבָּר (א לה): שְׁנַיִם וּשְׁלשִׁים אֶלֶף וּמָאתַיִם, וּבְפִינְחָס (שם כו לד): שְׁנַיִם וַחֲמִשִּׁים אֶלֶף וּשְׂבַע מֵאוֹת. דָּבָר אַחֵר: עַד אֲשֶׁר עַד כֹּה בְּרָכָה נֶאֶמְרָה לְאַבְרָהָם (בראשית טו ה): כֹּה יִהְיֶה זַרְעֲךָ, נִתְקַיְּימָה בִּי, וּלְפִי פְּשׁוּטוֹ: עַד אֲשֶׁר עַד כֹּה עַד אֲשֶׁר כָּךְ, כַּאֲשֶׁר אַתָּה רוֹאֶה.<br>
**14 [English]**	**forasmuch as the Lord has blessed me thus?.** Forasmuch as you have seen that my number has increased from the first count to the second count by 20,500, signified by the numerical value of כֹּה (25). In the first count you find in Manasseh in the book of Numbers 32,200, and in the Sidrah Pinchas, 52,700. Another explanation is: forasmuch as the Lord has blessed me even until כה, the blessing which was bestowed on Abraham: (“So כֹּה) shall be your seed,” was fulfilled in me. And according to peshat, forasmuch as the Lord has blessed me thus, as you see.



**15 [Hebrew]**	**אִם עַם רַב אַתָּה.** יֵשׁ בְּךָ כֹּחַ לִכְרוֹת יְעָרוֹת וּלְפַנּוֹתָהּ, כְּעֵין שֶׁקּוֹרִין אישרטי"ר בְּלַעַז וְשָׁם תִּבְנֶה עָרִים.<br>
**15 [English]**	**If you are a numerous people.** you have strength to cut down forests and to clear them, similar to that which we call essarter in French, and there you shall build cities.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**וּבֵרֵאתָ.** לְשׁוֹן כְּרִיתָה, כְּמוֹ (יחזקאל כג מז): וּבָרֵא אוֹתְהֶן בְּחַרְבוֹתָם, אישרטרא"ש בְּלַעַז.<br>
**15 [English]**	**and cut down.** Heb. וּבֵרֵאתָ, an expression of cutting, like: And they will cut them (וּבָרֵא אוֹתְהָן) with their swords, essarteras in French.

**15 [Hebrew]**	**אָץ.** דָּחוּק.<br>
**15 [English]**	**confined.** Heb. אָץ.



**16 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא יִמָּצֵא לָנוּ הָהָר.** כִּי אָץ הוּא לָנוּ כַּאֲשֶׁר אָמַרְתָּ.<br>
**16 [English]**	**The hill is not enough.** For it is too confined for us, as you said.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**לֹא יִמָּצֵא.** לֹא יַסְפִּיק.<br>
**16 [English]**	**is not enough.** Heb. לֹא יִמָּצֵא.

**16 [Hebrew]**	**וְרֶכֶב בַּרְזֶל.** וּמַה שֶּׁאַתָּה אוֹמֵר לַעֲלוֹת הַיַּעְרָה וְלִכְבּוֹשׁ בְּאֶרֶץ הַפְּרִיזִּי וְהָרְפָאִים, עַם חָזָק הוּא אוֹתוֹ כְּנַעֲנִי וְרֶכֶב בַּרְזֶל לוֹ, וְאַל תִּתְמַהּ אִם קְרָאוֹ לַפְּרִיזִי וּרְפָאִים כְּנַעֲנִי, כִּי כֻּלַּם בְּנֵי כְּנַעַן הָיוּ.<br>
**16 [English]**	**iron chariots.** and what you say, to go up to the forest and to conquer in the land of the Perizzites and the giants [Rephaim], is no easy task, for those Canaanites are a mighty nation and have iron chariots. Do not wonder if the Scripture calls the Perizzites and Rephaim Canaanites, for they were all children of Canaan.



**18 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי הַר יִהְיֶה־לָּךְ.** אוֹתוֹ שֶׁאָמַרְתִּי לְךָ עֲלֵה לְךָ הַיַּעֲרָה.<br>
**18 [English]**	**But the mountain shall be yours.** the one concerning which I said to you, “go up to the forest.”

**18 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי יַעַר הוּא וּבֵרֵאתוֹ.** כִּי יַעַר הוּא וְאֵינוֹ רָאוּי אֶלָּא לְעַם רַב וִיבָרְאוּהוּ וִיפַנּוּהוּ.<br>
**18 [English]**	**for it is a forest, and you shall cut it down.** for it is a forest, and it is only fit for a numerous people, who will cut it down and clear it.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**וּבֵרֵאתוֹ.** אַתָּה שֶׁאַתָּה עַם רַב.<br>
**18 [English]**	**and you shall cut it down.** for you are a great people.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**וְהָיָה לְךָ תֹּצְאֹתָיו כִּי תוֹרִיש אֶת הַכְּנַעֲנִי.** עַל יְדֵי שֶׁאַתָּה עַם רַב.<br>
**18 [English]**	**for you shall drive out the Canaanite.** since you are a great people.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי רֶכֶב בַּרְזֶל לוֹ.** וְאֵין אֶחָד מִשְּׁאָר הַשְּׁבָטִים כְּדַאי לְהִלָּחֵם בּוֹ.<br>
**18 [English]**	**because he has iron chariots.** and none of the other tribes is big enough to wage war with him.

**18 [Hebrew]**	**כִּי חָזָק הוּא.** וְאַתָּה יֵשׁ בְּךָ הַיְכוֹלֶת וְתּוֹרִישֶׁנּוּ, וְרַבּוֹתֵינוּ פֵּירְשׁוּ (בבא בתרא קיח א): עֲלֵה לְךָ הַיַּעְרָה, הַחְבִּיאוּ עַצְמְכֶם בַּיְעָרוֹת שֶׁלֹּא תִּשְׁלוֹט בָּכֶם עַיִן הָרָע.<br>
**18 [English]**	**for he is strong.** and you have the ability, and must drive him out. Our Rabbis explained: Go up to the forest - Hide in the forests lest the evil eye have power over you.
